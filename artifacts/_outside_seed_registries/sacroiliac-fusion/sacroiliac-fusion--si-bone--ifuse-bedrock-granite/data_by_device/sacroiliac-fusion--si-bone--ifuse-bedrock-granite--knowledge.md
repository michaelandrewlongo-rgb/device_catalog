# iFuse Bedrock Granite

**Manufacturer:** Si-Bone
**Category:** Sacroiliac Fusion

## What It Is

Spinopelvic fixation implant designed to address the 24% lumbopelvic fixation failure rate. FDA Breakthrough Device Designation. Features robust cannulated core with large neck and shank designed for spinopelvic stresses, FuSIon 3D microporous lattice surface mimicking cancellous bone, OMNICapture tulip and screw set with negative-rake square threads to mitigate tulip splay and expulsion, and IntelliHarvest macroporous graduated fenestrations for bone self-harvesting.

## Indications

Spinopelvic fixation in adult deformity, revision spine surgery, and cases with concomitant SI joint dysfunction.

## Compatible With

OMNICapture Tulip & Screw Set (included)

## Use Notes

FDA Breakthrough Device Designation. CMS New Technology Add-on Payment (NTAP) eligible for Medicare patients. Use cases include revision with S1/stacked S2AI, congenital deformity revision, adult idiopathic scoliosis.

## Key Differences vs Competitors

- **Primary function**: iFuse Bedrock Granite is specifically designed for spinopelvic fixation to address lumbopelvic fixation failure, while other iFuse devices (iFuse Implant System, iFuse INTRA, iFuse INTRA X, iFuse TORQ) are primarily for sacroiliac joint fusion/stabilization, and iFuse TORQ TNT is for pelvic fragility fractures.
- **Material and surface technology**: Features FuSIon 3D microporous lattice surface and IntelliHarvest macroporous graduated fenestrations for bone self-harvesting, similar to iFuse TORQ's FuSIon 3D surface and IntelliHarvest features, but differs from triangular titanium implants (iFuse Implant System) and allograft implants (iFuse INTRA, iFuse INTRA X).
- **Compatibility and components**: Includes OMNICapture tulip and screw set with negative-rake square threads, specifically compatible with spinopelvic fixation systems, whereas other devices are either standalone SI joint implants or have different compatibility (iFuse Implant System is compatible with iFuse Bedrock Granite as supplemental fixation).
- **FDA designation and payment**: Has FDA Breakthrough Device Designation and CMS NTAP eligibility for Medicare patients, similar to iFuse TORQ TNT's FDA Breakthrough Device Designation, but other devices lack this specified status.
- **Target patient population**: Indicated for spinopelvic fixation in adult deformity, revision spine surgery, and cases with concomitant SI joint dysfunction, contrasting with other devices focused on SI joint dysfunction (iFuse Implant System), minimally invasive SI joint stabilization (iFuse INTRA, iFuse INTRA X, iFuse TORQ), or pelvic fragility fractures (iFuse TORQ TNT).
