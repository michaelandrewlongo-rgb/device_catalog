# Prestige LP Cervical Disc

**Manufacturer:** Medtronic
**Category:** Cervical Disc

## What It Is

Two-piece articulating metal-on-metal artificial cervical disc. First FDA-approved one- and two-level cervical disc with 10-year IDE study data. Proprietary titanium ceramic composite, no nickel/cobalt/chromium.

## Sizing/Specs

One- or two-level disc replacement C3-C7.

## Indications

Reconstruction of disc C3-C7 following discectomy for radiculopathy or myelopathy.

## Use Notes

10yr data: 4.3%% reoperation vs 14.4%% ACDF (two-level). MR Conditional 1.5/4T. PMA P060018.
