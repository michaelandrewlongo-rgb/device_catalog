# Atlantis Vision Elite Anterior Cervical Plate System (Medtronic)

## What It Is

The Atlantis Vision Elite is an anterior cervical plate system for use after discectomy and/or corpectomy with interbody fusion (ACDF or ACCF). It features a low-profile translucent plate design with integrated locking caps that cover the screw heads after insertion, preventing screw backout. The plate uses self-drilling screws in both fixed-angle and variable-angle configurations, with a Nitinol (shape memory alloy) locking cap mechanism. The system supports 1-level through 5-level constructs. Plates are pre-assembled with locking caps and washers. The system is intended for anterior interbody screw fixation from C2 to T1.

## Indications

- Anterior interbody screw fixation of the cervical spine, C2 to T1
- Temporary stabilization of the anterior spine during fusion
- Degenerative disc disease (discogenic origin with degeneration confirmed by history and radiographic studies)
- Trauma (including fractures)
- Tumor
- Deformity (kyphosis, lordosis, scoliosis)
- Failed previous fusion
- Anterior cervical discectomy and fusion (ACDF)
- Anterior cervical corpectomy and fusion (ACCF)

## Contraindications

- Active infection at the operative site
- Severe osteoporosis, osteopenia, or metabolic bone disease limiting screw fixation
- Known allergy to titanium, Nitinol (nickel-titanium), or other implant materials
- Conditions precluding benefit of spinal implant surgery
- Inadequate bone stock for screw purchase
- Mental or neuromuscular disorders creating unacceptable risk of fixation failure or noncompliance
- Skeletal immaturity
- Do not combine titanium/Nitinol components with components from other systems or manufacturers

## Compatible With

- **Cervical interbody cages:** Any ACDF cage (PEEK or titanium) cleared for anterior cervical use. The Atlantis plate provides supplemental fixation over the cage.
- **Corpectomy cages:** Expandable or static corpectomy devices for ACCF reconstruction
- **Bone graft:** Autograft, allograft, or bone graft substitutes within the interbody space
- **Cervical retractor systems:** Standard anterior cervical retractor setups (Caspar-type distraction pins, self-retaining retractors)
- **Imaging:** Intraoperative fluoroscopy (AP and lateral). MRI compatibility has NOT been evaluated for this device (per IFU).
- **Dedicated instrument set:** Medtronic Atlantis Vision Elite reusable instrument set (SPS02121) plus disposable drill bits and plate holding pins

## Use Notes

- Supports 1-level through 5-level constructs (C2 to T1), making it one of the more versatile anterior cervical plate systems for long constructs.
- Nitinol locking caps are pre-assembled on the plate. After all screws are placed, rotate each cap 90 degrees clockwise with the Lock Screwdriver. Caps cover screw heads and mechanically prevent backout.
- Locking caps rotate clockwise ONLY to engage. Counterclockwise rotation unthreads the lock from the plate and is not reversible without plate removal. This is a critical intraoperative detail.
- Plate Holding Pins (through the cannulated locking caps) provide temporary midline fixation during plate positioning without blocking screw holes.
- Self-drilling screws eliminate the need for pre-drilling in standard bone. Self-tapping screws require pilot holes but are preferred in very dense bone.
- The 4.5mm screw is a rescue option when the 4.0mm has stripped bone. Always have rescue screws available.
- Color coding: Grey (4.0mm fixed), Blue (4.5mm fixed), Green (4.0mm variable), Magenta/Purple (4.5mm variable).
- Plate should not overhang adjacent disc spaces (risk of adjacent-segment ossification and dysphagia).
- For multi-level constructs, use variable-angle screws at intermediate vertebral bodies to accommodate screw angulation.
- MRI safety is unknown for this device. The IFU explicitly states the device has not been evaluated for MR environment safety, heating, or migration.

## Key Differences vs Competitors

- **vs. Vectra (DePuy):** Both offer fixed and variable-angle screws. The Atlantis Vision Elite uses Nitinol locking caps (rotate to cover screw heads); the Vectra uses integrated plate-based screw locking. The Atlantis supports up to 5-level constructs with plate lengths up to 110mm. The Atlantis offers self-drilling screws; the Vectra offers cancellous bone screws with tap options.
- **vs. Reflex Hybrid (Stryker):** The Reflex Hybrid uses a rotational screw-locking mechanism with semi-constrained variable-angle screws. The Atlantis uses Nitinol cap locking. The Atlantis has a broader plate length range (19-110mm) for longer constructs. The Reflex Hybrid emphasizes low-profile design.
- **vs. Zero-profile devices (Zero-P, COALITION):** Zero-profile cages with integrated fixation eliminate the anterior plate entirely, reducing dysphagia risk. The Atlantis is a traditional plate system offering broader construct flexibility (up to 5 levels, corpectomy spanning, variable screw angles). Traditional plates remain the standard for multi-level and corpectomy constructs.
- **Unique feature:** Nitinol locking caps provide a visible, mechanical anti-backout mechanism. The caps physically cover the screw heads, making it easy to confirm locking status intraoperatively and on follow-up imaging.

## Relevant Setup

### Pre-operative
- Determine the number of levels and select the appropriate plate length
- An adequate inventory of plate sizes (larger and smaller than expected) should be available at the time of surgery
- Verify all instruments are present and functioning before the case
- Components from different manufacturers must NOT be mixed

### Plate Selection
- Plate should span from the midpoint of the vertebral body above to the midpoint of the vertebral body below the fused segment(s)
- Plate should NOT overhang adjacent disc spaces (risk of adjacent segment ossification)
- For multi-level constructs, use variable-angle screws at the intermediate vertebral bodies to allow angulation

## Use Step-by-Step

### Plate Positioning
1. Complete the discectomy/corpectomy and place the interbody graft or cage.
2. Select the appropriate plate length. The plate should center over the midline and span from mid-body to mid-body.
3. Temporarily fix the plate to the spine using Plate Holding Pins through the cannulated locking caps. This provides midline fixation without interfering with screw holes. Use caution not to turn the locking cap while seating the holding pin.

### Drilling (for self-tapping screws)
4. Insert the appropriate Drill Guide (Fixed or Variable) fully into the screw hole.
5. Select the drill bit of appropriate length. Insert the drill bit through the Drill Guide.
6. Drill to the desired depth. The drill stops at the depth indicated when the stop contacts the top of the Drill Guide.
7. Confirm drill trajectory with intraoperative imaging.
8. Remove the Drill Guide and drill bit.

### Awl Technique (for self-drilling screws)
9. Insert the Universal Awl or Fixed Angle Awl into the screw hole at the appropriate trajectory.
10. Advance the awl with gentle pressure and twisting to create the pilot hole in bone.
11. Remove the awl. Confirm trajectory with fluoroscopy.

### Screw Insertion
12. Load the appropriate screw (fixed or variable angle, self-drilling or self-tapping, 4.0 or 4.5mm) onto the Screwdriver for Insertion (self-holding).
13. Advance the screw until the head is fully seated in the plate and the plate is lagged to the bone.
14. Repeat for all remaining screw holes.
15. Use intraoperative imaging to verify screw position after each screw or after all screws are placed.

### Locking
16. After all screws are inserted and positions confirmed, engage the locking mechanism on each screw hole.
17. Rotate each locking cap 90 degrees clockwise using the 2.5mm Hex Screwdriver (Lock Screwdriver). The cap covers the screw head and prevents backout.
18. Do NOT rotate the locking mechanism counterclockwise, which will unthread the lock from the plate.
19. Confirm that all locking caps are fully engaged and seated.

### Optional Tapping
20. For dense bone, the Tap for Cancellous Bone Screws (4.0mm: 311.402; 4.5mm: 311.404) may be used before screw insertion.

## Pearls / Troubleshooting

- **Use Plate Holding Pins through the cannulated locking caps** to temporarily fix the plate at midline without blocking screw insertion. Do not turn the locking cap while seating the pin.
- **Use Ball Tip Lock Driver** when engaging the locking mechanism from challenging angles. It facilitates conical angulations up to 20 degrees.
- **Engage locking caps CLOCKWISE ONLY.** Counterclockwise rotation will unthread the lock from the plate. This is not reversible without removing the plate.
- **If the locking cap will not close**, ensure the bone screws are fully seated first, then lock. Partially seated screws will prevent cap closure.
- **4.5mm screws serve as emergency rescue** when the 4.0mm screw has stripped the bone. They must thread through the plate; ensure proper drill guides are used to minimize insertion resistance.
- **Verifying screw seating with DTS Guide is difficult** because the tube covers the screw. Align the laser etching on the screwdriver shaft with the top of the DTS Guide tube to confirm the screw is seated.
- **Screws incline slightly anterocaudal to posterocranial** in the cervical spine. Screws must remain in the vertebral body and not penetrate adjacent intervertebral discs.
- **Use intraoperative imaging** to verify screw position. This is especially important for variable-angle screws where trajectory is surgeon-dependent.
- **For long spans or poor bone quality**, consider screws longer than 16mm and/or supplemental posterior fixation.
- **Plate should not overhang adjacent disc spaces.** This is associated with adjacent-level ossification and dysphagia.
- **MRI compatibility unknown.** The Atlantis Anterior Cervical Plate has not been evaluated for safety, heating, migration, or compatibility in the MR environment.

### Explantation
- Use the 2.5mm Hex Screwdriver to rotate locking caps counterclockwise 90 degrees to uncover screw heads.
- Using the same screwdriver, loosen and extract all screws, then remove the plate.
