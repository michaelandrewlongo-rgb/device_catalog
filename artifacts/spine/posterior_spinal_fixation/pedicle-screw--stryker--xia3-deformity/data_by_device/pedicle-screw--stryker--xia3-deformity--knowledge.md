# Xia 3 Spinal System - Deformity (Stryker)

## What It Is

The Xia 3 is a posterior non-cervical pedicle screw and rod system designed primarily for spinal deformity correction. It features a 6-point star screw-to-screwdriver interface, accepts both 5.5mm and 6.0mm diameter rods in the same screw head, and uses a Buttress Thread Blocker (closure mechanism) with a laser-etched titanium design. The system offers polyaxial, monoaxial, uniplanar, biased angle, and closed head screw options, plus laminar hooks and a full suite of deformity correction tools including the SUK DVR (Direct Vertebral Rotation) system. Closure screws (blockers) are available in titanium only.

## Indications

- Degenerative disc disease
- Spondylolisthesis
- Trauma (fracture or dislocation)
- Spinal stenosis
- Curvatures (scoliosis, kyphosis, lordosis)
- Tumor
- Pseudarthrosis
- Failed previous fusion
- Pediatric deformity: spondylolysis/spondylolisthesis, fracture, tumor, pseudarthrosis (with autograft/allograft, posterior approach only)
- Non-cervical spine: anterior/anterolateral and posterior approaches
- Pedicle and non-pedicle fixation

## Contraindications

- Active infection at the operative site
- Severe osteoporosis or osteopenia that would compromise implant fixation
- Patient conditions precluding benefit of spinal fixation (e.g., morbid obesity, pregnancy, mental illness, substance abuse affecting compliance)
- Inadequate tissue coverage of the implant
- Known allergy to titanium, cobalt-chromium, or vitallium alloy
- Any condition that would compromise the ability of the implant to provide stability
- Do not mix titanium and stainless steel in the same construct
- Do not mix cobalt-chromium-molybdenum alloy and stainless steel in the same construct

## Use Notes

- The Xia 3 system is primarily a deformity platform; its uniplanar screws and SUK DVR system are its differentiating features for 3D spinal deformity correction
- Uniplanar screws are fixed in the medial/lateral plane (enabling vertebral rotation) but polyaxial in the cephalad/caudal plane (enabling rod seating); they are essential for DVR and are distinguished by non-anodized surface and machined grooves
- All screws accept both 5.5mm and 6.0mm rods in the same tulip, allowing stiffness optimization without changing screws
- 8 rod reduction options provide flexibility from simple persuaders to reduction uniplanar screws with breakable extended tabs
- Buttress Thread Blocker final torque is 12 Nm; do not exceed. The Torque Wrench arrows align at target torque.
- Do not bend and unbend rods (stress risers). Pre-bent Rad Rods and Max Rad Rods available for significant sagittal correction.
- Biased Angle Screws (up to 9.5mm x 100mm) and Closed Head Screws available for sacral/iliac fixation
- MRI safety has not been evaluated for the Xia 3, Xia 4.5, or Xia Growth Rod Conversion Set

## Key Differences vs Competitors

- **vs Medtronic CD HORIZON / SOLERA:** SOLERA is a broader platform covering degenerative, trauma, tumor, and deformity. Xia 3 is deformity-focused with the SUK DVR system for direct vertebral rotation, which SOLERA lacks. SOLERA integrates with StealthStation navigation and POWEREASE powered instruments; Xia 3 does not have comparable navigation/power integration.
- **vs DePuy Synthes EXPEDIUM VERSE:** VERSE is a versatile open + MIS platform with broad pediatric indications. Xia 3 is more specialized for deformity correction. VERSE has integrated percutaneous MIS capability; Xia 3 is primarily an open system. Xia 3's uniplanar screws and DVR system are unique differentiators for scoliosis correction.
- **vs Globus REVERE:** REVERE uses a non-threaded locking cap (faster closure, no cross-threading). Xia 3 uses the Buttress Thread Blocker. REVERE has the Addition/RESCUE revision extension system. Xia 3 has superior deformity correction instrumentation (DVR, 8 reduction options, uniplanar screws).
- **vs NuVasive Reline:** Reline is an open thoracolumbosacral system for general spinal fixation. Xia 3 offers far deeper deformity instrumentation. Reline does not have uniplanar screws, DVR, or the range of deformity correction tools in the Xia 3 system.

### Screwdriver Assembly (Polyaxial/Monoaxial)
1. Insert Inner Shaft down the Outer Shaft
2. Slide the Sleeve over the Inner Shaft
3. Fully insert the handle quick-connect onto the Screwdriver Shaft
4. Engage tabs on Inner Shaft into screw head saddle
5. Seat Inner Shaft fully, turn Outer Shaft clockwise until threads fully engaged
6. Press "LOCK" button to confirm (ratchet sound)

### Low Profile Screwdriver Assembly
1. Insert Polyaxial Inner Shaft down the Outer Shaft
2. Slide Locking Nut over Inner Shaft (serrated teeth positioned distally)
3. Insert handle quick-connect onto Screwdriver Shaft
4. Engage tabs into screw head saddle
5. Seat and turn Outer Shaft clockwise
6. Depress button on Locking Nut and slide into Outer Shaft to lock

## Use Step-by-Step

### Pedicle Preparation
1. Expose the posterior elements and identify pedicle entry points.
2. Create a starting hole using the Awl at the pedicle entry point (use Sacral Awl for S1).
3. Probe the pedicle channel with the Curved Blunt Probe or Thoracic Pedicle Probe.
4. Sound all four pedicle walls and floor with the Pedicle Feeler to confirm no cortical breach.
5. Tap the pedicle if needed using the Modular Tap.

### Screw Insertion
6. Assemble the Screwdriver (Polyaxial or Monoaxial per screw type).
7. Load the screw onto the screwdriver by engaging the Inner Shaft tabs into the screw saddle, seating fully, and locking the Outer Shaft clockwise.
8. Insert the screw into the pedicle to the desired depth.
9. Disengage the screwdriver: press "UNLOCK," rotate Outer Shaft counterclockwise while holding the handle, release quick-connect, remove Sleeve, then remove Inner Shaft from Outer Shaft.

### Rod Contouring
10. Determine the appropriate rod length using the Rod Template.
11. Cut the rod to length using the Table-Top Rod Cutter if necessary.
12. Contour the rod using French Benders or Tube Benders. Make small incremental bends for even stress distribution. Do not repeatedly contour. Do not bend in the opposite direction.

### Rod Placement and Reduction
13. Place the rod into the screw head grooves using Rod Insertion Forceps or Rod Gripper.
14. If the rod does not seat easily, use one of the 8 reduction options:
    - **Option 1 (Inserter Tube + Universal Tightener):** Align the tube over the implant, insert blocker through the tube using the Universal Tightener. Lower line = blocker at top; upper line = blocker fully seated.
    - **Option 2 (Rod Fork):** Slide the Rod Fork into the lateral grooves and rotate backward to lever the rod into the head.
    - **Option 3 (Persuader):** At "0" connect to implant head; at "1" locked to implant, push rod in; at "2" rod is fully seated. Provides up to 20mm of reduction.
    - **Option 4 (One-Handed Persuader):** Connect with handle in open position, squeeze until handle reaches shaft. Ratchet sound confirms full seating. Up to 20mm reduction.
    - **Option 5 (Lateral Persuader):** For rod not centered over tulip. Squeeze handle to medialize, rotate center tube clockwise until seated. Up to 10mm medialization and 20mm axial reduction.
    - **Option 6 (Reduction Clip):** Low profile, allows multiple clips on adjacent segments. Attach inner sleeve to notches, rotate outer sleeve until rod seated. Up to 10mm reduction.
    - **Option 7 (SUK Reduction Tube):** Attach inner sleeve to tulip notches, rotate outer sleeve. Up to 10mm reduction. Can also be used for direct vertebral rotation.
    - **Option 8 (Reduction Uniplanar Screws):** Screw placed with extended tabs for reduction. After reduction complete, break off tabs using Reduction Screw Tab Remover. Up to 15mm reduction.

### Rod Linkage (Blocker Insertion)
15. Assemble the Blocker onto the Universal Tightener.
16. Insert the Blocker through the Inserter Tube (or Persuader/One-Handed Persuader) into the screw head. Universal Tighteners are NOT for final tightening.

### Deformity Correction (5 options, used independently or in combination)
17. **Rod Rotation:** Contour rod for sagittal plane, insert 90 degrees out of phase, rotate to proper alignment.
18. **Translation:** Use Persuaders at distal and proximal ends of curve apex, translate spine toward rod.
19. **Distraction/Compression:** Distract the concavity, compress the convexity of the deformity. Posterior distraction creates kyphosis; compression creates lordosis.
20. **In Situ Bending:** With closure screws loosely tightened, bend the rod in situ. Take care not to overload the bone-implant interface or notch the rod.
21. **Direct Vertebral Rotation (DVR):** Use Uniplanar Screws with SUK DVR System. Apply cantilever forces via SUK Tubes and Clamps for 3D correction. Distribute load evenly over multiple tubes.

### Final Tightening
22. Place the Anti-Torque Key around the screw head.
23. Place the Torque Wrench through the Anti-Torque Key until guided into the blocker.
24. Line up the two arrows on the Torque Wrench to achieve final tightening torque of **12 Nm**. Do not exceed 12 Nm.

### Cross Connector Placement
25. Measure the cross connector size using the Cross Connector Measuring Device or MAC Caliper.
26. Ensure center nut is loose for smooth insertion. Place using Cross Connector Inserter.
27. Tighten one set screw with the 3.5mm Hex Driver, then the second, return to the first for final tightening.
28. Final tighten center nut with 8mm Hex Driver.

## Pearls / Troubleshooting

- **Universal Tighteners are NOT for final tightening.** They are only for provisional blocker insertion. Always use the Torque Wrench + Anti-Torque Key for final lockdown.
- **12 Nm final torque, do not exceed.** The Torque Wrench has two arrows that align at the target torque.
- **Anti-Torque Key has two functions:** (1) aligns the Torque Wrench with the tightening axis, and (2) maximizes torque needed to lock the assembly. Skipping it risks incomplete locking.
- **Short Universal Tightener cannot be used with the Standard Persuader** or Standard Inserter Tube. Match short-to-short.
- **Uniplanar screws are key for DVR.** They are fixed in the medial/lateral plane (enabling vertebral rotation) but polyaxial in the cephalad/caudal plane (enabling rod seating). Visually distinguished by non-anodized surface and machined grooves on the tulip.
- **Distribute DVR load across multiple tubes.** Do not concentrate all derotation force through a single tube.
- **When rod is forced down while tightening the blocker**, ensure the blocker is fully engaged in the screw head to resist reactive forces during final tightening.
- **Caution with rod position:** Extra care needed if (1) rod is not horizontal in the screw head, (2) rod sits high, or (3) an acute convex/concave bend is present. These increase risk of component failure.
- **Xia 3 screws accept both 5.5mm and 6.0mm rods** in the same tulip. This provides flexibility for stiffness options but verify rod diameter during rod selection to avoid construct mismatch.
- **Rod contouring: do not bend and unbend.** Reverse bending creates stress risers. Pre-bent Rad Rods and Max Rad Rods are available if significant sagittal correction is needed.
- **Do not mix titanium and stainless steel** in the same patient. Cobalt-Chromium-Molybdenum Alloy and Stainless Steel also should not be mixed.
- **MRI status unknown.** The Xia 3, Xia 4.5, and Xia Growth Rod Conversion Set have not been tested for heating or migration in the MR environment.
