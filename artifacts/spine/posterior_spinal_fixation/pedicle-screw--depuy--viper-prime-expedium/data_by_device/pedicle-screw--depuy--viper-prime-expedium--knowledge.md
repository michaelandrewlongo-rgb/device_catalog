# VIPER PRIME Screws / EXPEDIUM Spine System (DePuy Synthes)

## What It Is

The VIPER PRIME is DePuy Synthes' percutaneous MIS pedicle screw platform, part of the broader VIPER Spine System. VIPER PRIME screws are designed specifically for posterior percutaneous approaches with MIS instrumentation. Fenestrated versions (VIPER PRIME with Fenestrations, EXPEDIUM Verse with Fenestrations) allow cement augmentation through a cannulated, fenestrated screw shank. The VIPER and EXPEDIUM systems share the same indications and are cross-compatible.

## Sizing/Specs

### Materials
- ASTM F136 titanium alloy (Ti-6Al-4V)
- ASTM F1537 cobalt-chromium-molybdenum alloy

### Sterilization
- Gamma radiation (sterile packaged, single use)

### Screw Types
- VIPER PRIME Screws (solid, for MIS percutaneous)
- VIPER PRIME Screws with Fenestrations (cannulated/fenestrated, for cement augmentation)
- EXPEDIUM Verse Screws with Fenestrations (cannulated/fenestrated, for open or MIS)

## Indications

**Adult (skeletally mature):**
- Degenerative disc disease
- Spondylolisthesis
- Trauma (fracture or dislocation)
- Spinal stenosis
- Curvatures (scoliosis, kyphosis, lordosis)
- Tumor
- Pseudarthrosis
- Failed previous fusion
- Thoracic, lumbar, and sacral spine
- Noncervical pedicle fixation and nonpedicle fixation
- Posterior percutaneous approach (MIS) or open

**Pediatric:**
- Adolescent idiopathic scoliosis (posterior approach only, with autograft/allograft)

**Fenestrated screws with CONFIDENCE cement:**
- Advanced-stage tumors of the thoracic and lumbar spine where life expectancy is insufficient for fusion
- Restores spinal column integrity without fusion requirement
- Only at levels where structural integrity is not severely compromised

## Contraindications

- Active infection at the operative site
- Severe osteoporosis or osteopenia that would compromise implant fixation
- Patient conditions precluding benefit of spinal fixation (e.g., morbid obesity, pregnancy, mental illness, substance abuse affecting compliance)
- Inadequate tissue coverage of the implant
- Known allergy to titanium or cobalt-chromium alloy
- Any condition that would compromise the ability of the implant to provide stability
- Fever or elevated WBC count suggestive of active systemic infection
- Fenestrated screws with cement: not for use where structural integrity of the vertebral body is severely compromised (risk of cement extravasation)

## Compatible With

- **VIPER Spine System** components (rods, connectors, instruments)
- **EXPEDIUM Spine System** components
- **EXPEDIUM VERSE Spine System** components
- **CONFIDENCE High Viscosity Spinal Cement** (for fenestrated screws only)
- Autograft and/or allograft

## Use Notes

- VIPER PRIME screws are specifically designed for percutaneous posterior approaches; the MIS instrumentation set is a separate kit
- Fenestrated screws can be used with or without cement augmentation
- This 510(k) (K173095) covers the addition of sterile gamma-irradiated packaging; design and materials are identical to earlier cleared versions
- When using fenestrated screws without cement, standard fusion indications apply
- When using fenestrated screws with CONFIDENCE cement, no fusion is expected; use is limited to palliative stabilization in patients with limited life expectancy

## Key Differences vs Competitors

- **vs Medtronic SEXTANT / CD HORIZON:** SEXTANT is Medtronic's MIS percutaneous system, used with CD HORIZON screws. VIPER PRIME is the DePuy Synthes equivalent. VIPER PRIME offers fenestrated cement-augmentation screws (with CONFIDENCE cement) for palliative tumor stabilization, a niche that SEXTANT does not directly address. SEXTANT integrates with StealthStation navigation; VIPER PRIME does not have a dedicated navigation interface.
- **vs Globus CREO MIS:** CREO MIS is Globus's percutaneous system with cannulated guidewire-based screws. VIPER PRIME similarly offers percutaneous placement. CREO uses the non-threaded locking cap (faster closure, no cross-threading). VIPER PRIME uses standard set screws. VIPER PRIME has a fenestrated cement augmentation option that CREO does not.
- **vs Stryker ES2 / Serrato:** Stryker's MIS percutaneous offerings. VIPER PRIME has broader cross-compatibility with the EXPEDIUM and EXPEDIUM VERSE open systems, allowing intraoperative conversion from MIS to open without changing platforms.
- **vs NuVasive Precept:** NuVasive's MIS percutaneous system. Both are cannulated percutaneous designs. VIPER PRIME's fenestrated cement augmentation capability is a differentiator for osteoporotic and tumor cases.
