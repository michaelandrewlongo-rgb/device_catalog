# Neo Pedicle Screw System (Neo Medical)

## What It Is

The Neo Pedicle Screw System is a radically simplified posterior pedicle screw system from Neo Medical (Switzerland). The core innovation is inventory reduction: the traditional 200+ screw size/design matrix is consolidated into only 14 screw references, using high-performance polymer components in the instrumentation. The entire instrument set weighs approximately 1 kg (vs. the typical 70 kg / 7 trays), with only 5 primary instruments. All screws and instruments are provided sterile and single-use. The system supports both open and MIS techniques with the same implant and instrument kits.

## Indications

- Degenerative pathology
- Tumor
- Trauma
- Supports reduction, compression, distraction, and cement augmentation

*Specific FDA-cleared indications depend on regulatory jurisdiction; the catalog is CE-marked (CE 1250). Verify local clearance status.*

## Contraindications

- Active infection at the operative site
- Severe osteoporosis or osteopenia that would compromise implant fixation
- Patient conditions precluding benefit of spinal fixation (e.g., morbid obesity, pregnancy, mental illness, substance abuse affecting compliance)
- Inadequate tissue coverage of the implant
- Known allergy to titanium or implant materials
- Any condition that would compromise the ability of the implant to provide stability
- Patients requiring complex deformity correction with hooks, transverse connectors, or revision connector systems (not available in this platform)

## Compatible With

- Self-contained system; does not reference cross-compatibility with other manufacturers' components
- Cement augmentation via cannulated fenestrated design (compatible with standard PMMA bone cement)

## Use Notes

- The 14-SKU screw inventory is a major logistical advantage for ambulatory surgery centers, traveling surgeons, and facilities with limited storage
- Single-use sterile instruments eliminate reprocessing
- The same screw functions as polyaxial or monoaxial depending on whether the pre-mounted locking clips are engaged
- Intra-operative decision: monoaxial vs. polyaxial, cement vs. no cement, all from the same screw
- Compression and distraction use a single tool (bidirectional)
- Rod measurer included in every instrument kit
- Swiss-manufactured implants with full traceability

## Key Differences vs Competitors

- **vs Medtronic CD HORIZON / SOLERA:** CD HORIZON is a massive multi-sub-system platform with dozens of screw types, hooks, connectors, and rod materials. Neo PSS has 14 screw references total. Neo eliminates reprocessing with single-use sterile instruments; Medtronic uses reusable trays. Neo's 1 kg instrument kit vs Medtronic's ~70 kg / 7 trays is the defining logistical difference. Neo lacks hooks, transverse connectors, and revision options.
- **vs DePuy Synthes EXPEDIUM VERSE:** VERSE offers open + MIS + pediatric indications with broad cross-compatibility across the EXPEDIUM/VIPER ecosystem. Neo is a self-contained system with no cross-manufacturer compatibility. VERSE has far greater system depth; Neo has far simpler logistics.
- **vs Stryker Xia 3:** Xia 3 is designed for complex deformity correction (DVR, uniplanar screws, 8 reduction options). Neo targets straightforward degenerative, trauma, and tumor cases. Neo has no deformity correction instrumentation.
- **vs Globus CREO MIS:** CREO is a percutaneous MIS-specific system. Neo supports both open and MIS with the same screw/instrument kit. Neo's cannulated fenestrated design allows intraoperative cement augmentation; CREO screws are not fenestrated.
- **Key advantage:** Inventory simplification, single-use sterile instruments, and portability make Neo well-suited to ambulatory surgery centers, traveling surgeons, and resource-limited settings. It trades system breadth for logistical efficiency.
