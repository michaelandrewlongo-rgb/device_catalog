# Cascadia TL 3D Interbody System

**Manufacturer:** Stryker
**Category:** Interbody cage / TLIF

## What It Is

Lamellar 3D titanium posterior lumbar interbody cage for transforaminal lumbar interbody fusion (TLIF). Lamellar 3D Titanium Technology incorporates 300-500 um longitudinal channels with transverse windows creating an interconnected lattice for bony integration. Approximately 70% porosity. Reverse hourglass design provides large graft volume. Bulleted nose for ease of insertion. 3-5 um surface roughness for direct bony ongrowth. Roughened titanium surfaces demonstrate increased protein expression vs smooth titanium in bench testing.

Shares the Lamellar 3D Titanium Technology platform with Cascadia Cervical 3D (ACDF) and Mojave PL 3D (PLIF, expandable).

## Sizing/Specs

Full range of implant sizes to accommodate vertebral anatomy (refer to surgical technique guide for sizing matrix).

## Indications

Transforaminal lumbar interbody fusion (TLIF).
