# Mojave PL 3D Expandable Interbody System

**Manufacturer:** Stryker
**Category:** Interbody cage / PLIF system
510(k) K163364 | Class 2 | 2017-03-15

## What It Is

Expandable posterior lumbar interbody fusion device with independent control of anterior and posterior height. Lamellar 3D Titanium Technology with 500 um longitudinal channels and transverse windows for bony integration. Lockable at any desired height and lordosis to restore sagittal balance. Graft chamber allows bone graft packing prior to insertion. Collapsed insertion profile minimizes risk of ligament, nerve root, and endplate damage.

## Sizing/Specs

| Footprint (W × L) | Collapsed Height | Expanded Height |
|---|---|---|
| 11 × 24 mm | 7 mm | 10 mm |
| 11 × 28 mm | 9 mm | 12 mm |
| 11 × 32 mm | 12 mm | 15 mm |

Lordosis adjustable intraoperatively via independent anterior and posterior height controls. Collapsed profile minimizes footprint during insertion; locks at any height/lordosis combination.

## Indications

Posterior lumbar interbody fusion (PLIF).

## Use Notes

All-in-one instrumentation for fast and simple insertion and expansion. Now sold by VB Spine.

## Key Differences vs Competitors

- The Mojave PL 3D Expandable Interbody System is an expandable PLIF device with independent control of anterior and posterior height, allowing for intraoperative lordosis adjustment, unlike the static Tritanium TL Posterior Lumbar Cage or the Cascadia TL 3D Interbody System which are TLIF devices.
- Mojave features Lamellar 3D Titanium Technology with 500 um longitudinal channels and transverse windows for bony integration, similar to Cascadia TL 3D Interbody System (300-500 um channels) but distinct from Tritanium TL's randomized porous structure or CONCORDE LIFT's PEEK-OPTIMA body with titanium endplates.
- Mojave is indicated specifically for Posterior Lumbar Interbody Fusion (PLIF), whereas devices like Cascadia TL 3D, Tritanium TL, CONCORDE LIFT, and Elevate are indicated for Transforaminal Lumbar Interbody Fusion (TLIF) or other approaches.
- The Mojave system offers specific collapsed and expanded height options (e.g., 7mm collapsed to 10mm expanded for 11x24mm footprint), providing precise sizing information not specified for devices like Cascadia TL 3D, which states a "full range of implant sizes".
