# SABLE Expandable Posterior Lumbar Interbody Spacer

**Manufacturer:** Globus Medical
**Category:** Interbody cage

## What It Is

Expandable posterior lumbar interbody spacer with 3D-printed titanium endplates. Inserted at a low collapsed height and expanded to the target height in situ. Lordosis options available at three angles. Bone graft can be delivered via funnel placed down the inserter after cage expansion. 3D-printed endplates facilitate bony integration.

## Key Differences vs Competitors

- Unlike the CONCORDE LIFT and Elevate Expandable Interbody Device which use PEEK with titanium endplates, the SABLE Expandable Posterior Lumbar Interbody Spacer is entirely 3D-printed titanium with 3D-printed endplates.
- The SABLE device offers a broader overall expansion range of 6–18 mm height compared to the Mojave PL 3D Expandable Interbody System which has a maximum expanded height of 15 mm (for the 11x32mm footprint).
- While the RISE TLIF Expandable Lumbar Interbody Spacer and CONCORDE LIFT are indicated for TLIF, the SABLE device is indicated for both TLIF and PLIF approaches.
- The SABLE device allows for bone graft delivery via a funnel after cage expansion, a feature not explicitly detailed for other expandable devices like RISE TLIF or CONCORDE LIFT.
- Unlike the Cascadia TL 3D Interbody System which is a static interbody cage, the SABLE device is an expandable cage, allowing for in-situ height restoration.
