# proGAV 2.0 (Miethke / B. Braun / Aesculap)

## What It Is

The proGAV 2.0 is a posture-dependent, magnetically adjustable CSF shunt system that combines two separate inline units: an adjustable differential pressure unit (disc-shaped, cranial) and a fixed gravitational unit (cylindrical, inline with catheter, using the ShuntAssistant 2.0 mechanism). In the supine position, only the adjustable differential pressure unit is active -- the gravitational unit is fully open. As the patient sits or stands, the gravitational unit activates via a tantalum ball mechanism, increasing total opening pressure to prevent overdrainage. The adjustable unit features Miethke's "Active-Lock" mechanism -- a mechanical brake that prevents unintended valve adjustment from external magnetic fields up to 3T, including MRI.

## Settings

### Adjustable Differential Pressure Unit
- Range: 0 to 20 cmH2O
- Adjustment increments: 1 cmH2O steps
- Factory preset: 5 cmH2O

### Fixed Gravitational Unit (ShuntAssistant 2.0, selected at ordering)
- Available levels: 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, or 35 cmH2O (can also be ordered without gravitational unit)
- Not adjustable after implantation

### Standard Configurations
- FX642T: adjustable 0-20 cmH2O + gravitational 20 cmH2O (children up to 5 years and adults over 65)
- FX643T: adjustable 0-20 cmH2O + gravitational 25 cmH2O (individuals 5-65 years)

### Combined Opening Pressure Examples (non-binding recommendations, all in cmH2O)

| Patient | Adjustable Unit | Gravitational Unit | Combined Supine | Combined Upright |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Newborn/children up to 5 | 5 | 20 | 5 | 25 |
| Children from 5 years | 10 | 25 | 10 | 35 |
| Adults < 5'3" | 5 | 25 | 5 | 30 |

For patients with limited mobility or high BMI, choose a lower gravitational unit level.

## Adjustment Method

### Tools Required ("Soft Touch" Instruments)

1. **proGAV 2.0 Compass** -- Locates the adjustable unit and reads the current DP setting. Open the compass lid and align the integrated template over the valve. Close the compass to read the current pressure on the indicator scale.

2. **proGAV 2.0 Adjustment Tool** -- Magnetic tool that changes the adjustable DP unit setting from 0 to 20 cmH2O. Position the tool so the desired pressure value aligns with the valve inlet connector direction. Center over the valve and press lightly on the adjustable unit with your finger to deactivate the "Active-Lock" mechanism and set the pressure. Releasing finger pressure automatically re-locks the valve.

### Adjustment Procedure
1. Locate the adjustable unit by palpation
2. Place proGAV 2.0 Compass over valve with lid open for localization
3. Close compass to read current DP setting
4. Dial the Adjustment Tool to the desired pressure
5. Align with inlet connector direction, center over valve
6. Press lightly to release Active-Lock, then release to lock at new setting
7. Re-verify with Compass

### Active-Lock Mechanism
- Mechanical brake that holds the rotor in position
- Requires deliberate finger pressure through the skin to release
- Prevents inadvertent pressure changes from external magnetic fields up to 3T
- Provides tactile feedback (click) when released
- Automatically re-engages when finger pressure is removed

## MRI Safety

- **MR Conditional up to 3 Tesla**
- The Active-Lock mechanism prevents unintended valve adjustments by external magnetic fields up to 3T
- The gravitational unit (ShuntAssistant 2.0) is fixed and unaffected by MRI
- **The valve setting is NOT expected to change with MRI** due to the Active-Lock brake mechanism, unlike competitors that use freely rotating magnetic rotors
- Verify setting with proGAV 2.0 Compass before and after MRI as a precaution
- Do not bring Soft Touch Instruments into the MR environment

## Indications

- CSF shunting for hydrocephalus (ventriculoperitoneal)
- Adults and pediatric patients including newborns
- Available in multiple system configurations:
  - Shunt system only (adjustable unit + gravitational unit)
  - With integrated distal catheter (1200 mm)
  - With pediatric Sprung Reservoir (unidirectional flushing for patency checks)
  - With Sprung Reservoir (adult)
  - With pediatric Control Reservoir (unidirectional flushing, ventricular catheter with deflector and stylet)

## Contraindications

- Active or suspected CNS infection in the implantation region
- Implantation near cardiac pacemakers or other active implants (magnets in adjustment tools may interfere)
- Must be implanted parallel to body axis for correct gravitational function
- Not for use with M.blue plus Instruments (different tool system; the proGAV 2.0 uses its own Soft Touch instruments)

## Key Differences vs Competitors

- **Active-Lock mechanism**: Unique mechanical brake prevents MRI-induced setting changes up to 3T. Medtronic Strata valves may change settings after MRI and require post-MRI verification/reprogramming. CERTAS Plus also may change with MRI.
- **Two-unit modular design**: Separate adjustable DP disc + inline gravitational cylinder, vs. M.blue which integrates both in one disc. This means the proGAV 2.0 has a larger footprint but allows the gravitational unit to be placed further along the catheter.
- **1 cmH2O step adjustability**: Finer resolution than Strata (5 discrete performance levels) or CERTAS Plus (8 settings spanning 25-215 mmH2O). Adjustable range is 0-20 cmH2O (narrower than M.blue's 0-40 cmH2O gravitational range, but the proGAV adjusts the DP component while M.blue adjusts the gravitational component).
- **Soft Touch instruments**: Designed for patient comfort during transcutaneous adjustment; spring-loaded compass and adjustment tool with tactile feedback.
- **Extensive pediatric configurations**: Available with pediatric Sprung Reservoir, Control Reservoir, deflectors, and introducing stylets for neonatal/pediatric use.
- **Fixed gravitational unit**: Uses ShuntAssistant 2.0 (6 levels, 10-35 cmH2O), selected at ordering. M.blue allows continuous gravitational adjustment postoperatively.
