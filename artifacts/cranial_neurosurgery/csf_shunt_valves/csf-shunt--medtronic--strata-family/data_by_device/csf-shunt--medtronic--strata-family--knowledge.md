# Strata Family of Adjustable Valves (Medtronic / PS Medical)

## What It Is

The Strata family comprises magnetically adjustable differential pressure CSF shunt valves made by Medtronic (PS Medical). The core mechanism uses a rotor with a magnet and spring system inside a silicone housing with a firm plastic base. CSF flows through the valve when intraventricular pressure exceeds the selected performance level. The family includes three main product lines:

1. **Strata Adjustable Valves** (with siphon control via the patented Delta Chamber)
2. **Strata NSC Valves** (No Siphon Control -- same adjustable mechanism without the Delta Chamber)
3. **Strata NSC LP** (lumboperitoneal variant of Strata NSC)

The Delta Chamber is a normally closed siphon-control device that opens in response to positive ventricular pressure but remains closed when distal (peritoneal) pressure is negative, preventing siphon-driven overdrainage in the upright position.

## Settings

All Strata valves offer 5 performance levels, adjusted magnetically:

| Performance Level | Approximate Opening Pressure (Strata with Delta, 0 cmH2O HP) | Approximate Opening Pressure (Strata with Delta, -50 cmH2O HP) |
|---|---|---|
| 0.5 | ~15-25 mmH2O | ~15-35 mmH2O |
| 1.0 | ~35-55 mmH2O | ~35-55 mmH2O |
| 1.5 | ~70-90 mmH2O | ~70-90 mmH2O |
| 2.0 | ~135-145 mmH2O | ~101-130 mmH2O |
| 2.5 | ~155-167 mmH2O | ~140-167 mmH2O |

- Performance level 1.5 is the factory default setting
- Strata NSC valves (without Delta Chamber) have the same 5 performance levels with similar pressure ranges

### Strata NSC LP Valves
- Same 5 performance levels as Strata NSC
- Designed for subcutaneous lumboperitoneal shunt placement
- Integral lumbar connector for easy attachment of proximal lumbar catheter

## Adjustment Method

### Strata Adjustment Tools
- Handheld magnetic tool set consisting of: Locator Tool, Indicator Tool, and Adjustment Tool
- Pocket-sized, no external power required
- Steps: (1) locate valve with Locator Tool, (2) read current setting with Indicator Tool, (3) adjust to desired performance level with Adjustment Tool
- No X-ray verification required after adjustment -- the tools provide direct confirmation

### StrataVarius Adjustment System
- Handheld electronic adjustment tool with built-in magnet and LCD readout screen
- Powered by two AA batteries
- Portal for valve palpation and magnetic valve adjustment
- Provides numeric interpretation (in mmH2O) of the valve's performance level setting via included Smart Card
- Left- or right-hand use
- Steps: (1) locate and center over valve, (2) read current setting on LCD, (3) adjust magnetically, (4) verify on LCD
- No X-ray confirmation needed

**Note**: The Strata NSC LP valve requires the StrataVarius Adjustment System (REF 45806) for postoperative adjustment and verification.

## MRI Safety

- **MRI-safe to 3.0 Tesla** -- exposure to 3T MRI will not damage the valve
- **However, MRI may change the valve pressure setting**. Immediately after MRI exposure, the valve pressure setting must be checked and reprogrammed if necessary by a neurosurgeon or attending physician.
- This is a critical practical difference vs. Miethke valves (proGAV 2.0 Active-Lock, M.blue) which are designed to resist MRI-induced setting changes.

## Indications

- Management of hydrocephalus in adults and pediatric patients
- Strata Adjustable (with Delta Chamber): VP shunting where siphon-driven overdrainage is a concern
- Strata NSC: VP shunting where siphon control is not needed or where a separate anti-siphon device will be used
- Strata NSC LP: lumboperitoneal shunting for communicating hydrocephalus

## Contraindications

- Active CNS infection
- Standard shunt contraindications per product labeling
- The valve setting may be altered by strong magnetic fields; patients must avoid prolonged close-range magnet exposure outside of clinical settings

## Key Differences vs Competitors

- **Delta Chamber siphon control (Strata Adjustable only)**: Patented normally-closed mechanism that opens only with positive ventricular pressure. Unlike Miethke gravitational valves (M.blue, proGAV 2.0, ShuntAssistant 2.0) which use gravity-activated ball mechanisms, the Delta Chamber uses a pressure-differential approach. It does not require specific body-axis implantation orientation.
- **5 discrete performance levels**: Fewer settings than CERTAS Plus (8 settings), proGAV 2.0 (21 settings in 1 cmH2O steps), or M.blue (continuous 0-40 cmH2O). Simpler but less granular.
- **No X-ray needed for setting verification**: Both the Strata Adjustment Tools and StrataVarius system provide direct magnetic confirmation of setting, unlike some competitors that require X-ray or specialized compass tools.
- **Silicone housing**: Flexible, low-profile compared to titanium-bodied Miethke valves (M.blue, SA 2.0, proGAV 2.0).
- **Setting changes with MRI**: Unlike proGAV 2.0 (Active-Lock resists to 3T) and M.blue (setting confirmed stable through MRI), the Strata setting may change during MRI and must be verified afterward. This is a significant operational burden for patients requiring frequent MRI surveillance.
- **No gravitational unit option**: The Strata family relies on the Delta Chamber for overdrainage protection rather than a gravitational mechanism. The Delta Chamber does not require body-axis-parallel implantation but also does not provide the progressive posture-dependent pressure increase that gravitational valves offer.
- **LP variant available**: The Strata NSC LP is purpose-built for lumboperitoneal shunting with a specialized lumbar connector, similar to the Miethke SA 2.0 LP variants.
- **Wide configuration range**: Available in Regular, Small, and Bur Hole valve sizes; with or without integral peritoneal catheters (90 cm, 120 cm); with or without BioGlide hydrophilic surface modification.
