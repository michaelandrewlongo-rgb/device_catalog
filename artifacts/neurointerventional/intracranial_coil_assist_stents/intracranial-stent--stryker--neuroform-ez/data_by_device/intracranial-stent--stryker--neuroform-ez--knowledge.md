# Neuroform EZ Stent System

**Manufacturer:** Stryker Neurovascular
**Category:** Intracranial Stent

FDA HDE H020002 | Original approval September 11, 2002 (supplements through S064, August 2025) | Class III
**Note: Humanitarian Device Exemption (HDE). IRB approval required prior to use.**

## What It Is

The Neuroform EZ Stent System is a self-expanding, open-cell nitinol intracranial stent pre-loaded on an enhanced Stent Delivery Wire. It is engineered for conformability, flexibility, and accessibility in the neurovasculature. The Neuroform EZ is the current generation of the Neuroform stent platform — among the first intracranial stents to receive FDA authorization for stent-assisted coiling of wide-neck intracranial aneurysms. The "EZ" designation reflects design improvements in deliverability relative to earlier Neuroform generations.

The open-cell nitinol design provides wall apposition in tortuous anatomy and allows post-stent microcatheter re-access through the stent interstices, supporting both jailing and sequential coiling techniques.

This device operates under a Humanitarian Device Exemption (HDE H020002), not a 510(k) clearance. IRB approval is required at each institution prior to patient treatment.

## Indications

Intended for use with embolic coils for the treatment of wide-neck, intracranial, saccular aneurysms arising from a parent vessel with a diameter of ≥2 mm and ≤4.5 mm that are not amenable to treatment with surgical clipping. Wide-neck aneurysms are defined as having a neck >4 mm or a dome-to-neck ratio <2.

*(Source: HDE H020002 Approval Order Statement, confirmed in FDA HDE database)*

## Use Notes

- **HDE device: IRB approval required at each institution before use.** This is a Class III device authorized under a Humanitarian Device Exemption, not 510(k) cleared.
- MR Conditional per FDA UDI registry. Specific MRI parameters (field strength, SAR limits, gradient fields) require the current IFU.
- Pre-sterilized; single use only; do not resterilize.
- Open-cell design allows post-stent microcatheter re-access through stent interstices for sequential coiling or coil addition.
- IFU reference: AP001666 v2.0 (updated January 2025). Available from Stryker representative or stryker.com (US HCP-gated).
- IRB requirement distinguishes this from Atlas — clinical sites must maintain active HDE coverage to use this device.

## Key Differences vs Competitors

- **Regulatory status**: Neuroform EZ operates under a Humanitarian Device Exemption (HDE) requiring IRB approval at each institution, similar to ENTERPRISE 2 and Enterprise VRD, but unlike Neuroform Atlas (which has 510(k) clearance) and LVIS/LVIS Jr. (not specified).
- **Cell design**: Neuroform EZ uses an open-cell nitinol design allowing post-stent microcatheter re-access, contrasting with the closed-cell designs of ENTERPRISE 2, Enterprise VRD, and LVIS/LVIS Jr. (Neuroform Atlas also has open-cell design).
- **Parent vessel diameter range**: Neuroform EZ is indicated for vessels 2.0-4.5 mm, which is broader than ENTERPRISE 2's 2.5-4.0 mm range but similar to LVIS/LVIS Jr. and Neuroform Atlas (2.0-4.5 mm).
- **MRI compatibility**: Neuroform EZ is MR Conditional (specific parameters per IFU), while LEO+ labeling contains no MRI safety information and other devices have not specified MRI status.
- **Deployment features**: Neuroform EZ uses pre-loaded stent delivery wire technology, while ENTERPRISE 2 offers recapture/redeployment capability, LVIS/LVIS Jr. features resheath/recapture before full deployment, and Neuroform Atlas uses unsheathing deployment technique.
