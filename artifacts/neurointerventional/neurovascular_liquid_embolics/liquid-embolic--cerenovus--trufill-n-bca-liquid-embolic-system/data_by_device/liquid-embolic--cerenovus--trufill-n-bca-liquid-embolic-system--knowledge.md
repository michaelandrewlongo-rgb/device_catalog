# TRUFILL n-BCA Liquid Embolic System

**Manufacturer:** Cerenovus (J&J)
**Category:** Liquid embolic

## What It Is

N-butyl cyanoacrylate (n-BCA) liquid embolic system for embolization of cerebral arteriovenous malformations. Provides adjustable polymerization times to match hemodynamic flow rate and customizable tantalum concentration for visualization. Available as kit or procedural set with accessories.

## Sizing/Specs

Kit configurations: 1 Gram Kit (631400), 2 Gram Kit (631500), 1 Gram Procedural Set (632400NA), 2 Gram Procedural Set (632500NA); Kit contents: n-BCA, 10mL Ethiodized Oil, Tantalum Powder 1 Gram, self-piercing cap; Procedural Set adds: 30 mL beaker, 1 mL syringes (x3), 3 mL syringes (x3), 18G blunt fill needle, 21G hypodermic needles (x2), syringe labels

## Indications

Embolization of cerebral arteriovenous malformations (AVMs).

## Compatible With

Prowler EX Microcatheter, CEREBASE DA Guide Sheath, ENTERPRISE 2 Vascular Reconstruction Device; Does not require expensive detachable tip microcatheters or dual lumen balloon catheters

## Use Notes

Complication rates numerically lower than Onyx LES but not statistically significant. Catheter retention rate 1.6% vs 9.3% for Onyx LES in RCT. Significantly shorter fluoroscopy time (37 vs 57 min, p<0.0001) and procedural time (2.1 vs 2.6 hrs, p=0.001) compared to Onyx LES. Lower total procedural costs than Onyx LES due to not requiring detachable tip microcatheters or dual lumen balloon catheters.

## Key Differences vs Competitors

- **Indication specificity**: TRUFILL n-BCA is indicated for embolization of cerebral arteriovenous malformations (AVMs), while Onyx HD-500 is specifically indicated for intracranial aneurysms under a Humanitarian Device Exemption, and Onyx LES is indicated for presurgical embolization of brain AVMs.
- **Material mechanism**: TRUFILL n-BCA is an adhesive liquid embolic (n-butyl cyanoacrylate) that polymerizes on contact with ionic fluids, whereas Onyx LES and Onyx HD-500 are non-adhesive EVOH-based systems that precipitate when DMSO dissipates.
- **Compatibility requirements**: TRUFILL n-BCA does not require detachable-tip microcatheters or dual-lumen balloon catheters and is compatible with standard microcatheters like Prowler EX, while Onyx LES requires DMSO-compatible microcatheters such as Apollo Onyx delivery microcatheter (detachable tip).
- **Procedural characteristics**: TRUFILL n-BCA has shown significantly shorter fluoroscopy time (37 vs 57 minutes) and procedural time (2.1 vs 2.6 hours) compared to Onyx LES in RCT data, with lower total procedural costs due to catheter compatibility.
