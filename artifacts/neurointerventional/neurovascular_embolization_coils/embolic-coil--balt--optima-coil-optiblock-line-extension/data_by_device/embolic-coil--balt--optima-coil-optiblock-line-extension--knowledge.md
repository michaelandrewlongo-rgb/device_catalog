# Optima Coil System (OptiBlock Line Extension)

**Manufacturer:** Balt USA, LLC
**Category:** Embolic Coil

## What It Is

Platinum-tungsten detachable embolization coils for endovascular occlusion of intracranial aneurysms and neurovascular malformations. The system consists of an implant coil affixed to a delivery pusher for insertion through a microcatheter hub. Coils are available in complex (3D) and helical secondary shapes across a range of diameters and lengths. Sequential coil deployment creates blood stasis within the aneurysm sac, progressively thrombosing the aneurysm until occlusion is achieved. Detachment is thermal via the Xcel Detachment Controller (handheld, battery-powered).

The OptiBlock Line Extension (K242582) is a line extension of the Optima Coil System (K223386) that adds new diameter/length combinations and introduces a smaller 0.010" primary wind OD, along with minor adjustments to helical proximal loop diameters for small-aneurysm coil models.

**OptiBlock-specific changes vs predicate (K223386):**
- New coil models with additional diameter/length combinations
- Proximal helical loop diameter reduced by 1 mm (for nominal 3-5 mm loops) or 2 mm (for nominal 6-14 mm loops)
- 0.0015" gaps added to primary wind of distal helical loops for secondary diameters 3-3.5 mm
- New smaller primary wind OD of 0.010"

## Indications

Intended for:
- Endovascular embolization of intracranial aneurysms
- Neurovascular abnormalities including arteriovenous malformations and arteriovenous fistulae
- Vascular occlusion within the neurovascular system to permanently obstruct blood flow to an aneurysm or vascular malformation
- Arterial and venous embolizations in the peripheral vasculature

## Use Notes

**FDA Recalls (active at time of data extraction, 5 events):** Recall actions involving label/pouch mix-ups -- pouch label does not match carton label due to inadequate manufacturing line clearance. Verify labeling consistency between pouch and carton before use.

## Key Differences vs Competitors

- **Primary coil wind OD**: OptiBlock offers 0.010"-0.014" (new 0.010" option), while Axium Detachable Coil requires minimum microcatheter ID of 0.0165" for bare coils (coil OD not specified).
- **Detachment mechanism**: OptiBlock uses thermal detachment via Xcel Detachment Controller, whereas Axium uses mechanical detachment via Instant Detacher and Cerenovus systems use hydraulic detachment.
- **Stretch-resistance**: OptiBlock uses polyolefin Engage thread (0.0018" or 0.0022"), while Cerenovus systems use polypropylene stretch-resistant suture (diameter not specified).
- **Indications scope**: OptiBlock is indicated for intracranial aneurysms, neurovascular malformations, and peripheral vasculature, similar to Axium and Cerenovus systems, but Eclipse 2L is a balloon catheter for temporary occlusion/balloon-assisted embolization, and Prestige Plus is specifically for peripheral vasculature only.
- **Active FDA recalls**: OptiBlock has 5 active recalls for label/pouch mix-ups, Cerenovus systems have 1 active recall for detachment failure, while Axium systems show no active recalls in provided data.
