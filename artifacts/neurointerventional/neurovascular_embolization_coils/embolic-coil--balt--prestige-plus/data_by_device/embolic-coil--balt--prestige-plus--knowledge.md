## Key Differences vs Competitors

- **Indications**: Prestige Plus is indicated for arterial and venous embolizations in the peripheral vasculature only. In contrast, most competitors (Optima Coil System, Axium systems, CODMAN/DELTAMAXX/ORBIT GALAXY G2, HydroCoil Embolic System, i-ED COIL) are indicated for both neurovascular (intracranial aneurysms, AVMs, fistulae) AND peripheral embolizations. Eclipse 2L is a balloon catheter for temporary occlusion, not a permanent embolic coil.
- **MRI Compatibility**: Prestige Plus labeling "does not contain MRI Safety Information" (status not specified). Several competitors (Optima Coil System, Axium systems, i-ED COIL, HydroCoil Embolic System) are explicitly "MR Conditional."
- **Active Recalls**: Prestige Plus has no mentioned recalls. Optima Coil System has 5 active recalls for labeling mix-ups, CODMAN/DELTAMAXX/ORBIT GALAXY G2 has 1 recall for potential detachment failure, and INZONE DETACHMENT SYSTEM has 1 recall for missing temperature labels.
- **Device Type/Technology**: Prestige Plus is a peripheral coil system. Eclipse 2L is a double lumen balloon catheter for temporary occlusion/balloon-assisted embolization. HydroCoil Embolic System uses hydrogel-coated coils that expand for higher packing density. Other systems (Optima, Axium, CODMAN group, i-ED COIL) are detachable coil systems primarily for neurovascular use with peripheral indications.
