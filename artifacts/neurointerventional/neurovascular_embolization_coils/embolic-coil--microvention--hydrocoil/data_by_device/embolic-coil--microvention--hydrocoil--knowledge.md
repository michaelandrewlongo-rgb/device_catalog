# HydroCoil Embolic System (HES) -- MicroVention (HydroFrame 10)

FDA 510(k) K161367 | Cleared August 31, 2016 | Class II

## What It Is

Hydrogel-coated platinum embolic coils designed for higher packing density than bare platinum coils. Each coil consists of a platinum-tungsten alloy primary coil wound around a hydrophilic acrylic copolymer (hydrogel) core. On contact with blood, the hydrogel expands, increasing the effective volume of the coil mass within the aneurysm. The coils assume a 3D spherical shape in various loop sizes and lengths.

## Sizing/Specs

- **Primary coil wire OD:** 0.00150", 0.00175", 0.00200", 0.00225"
- **Coil implant diameter:** 1-12 mm (predicate 2-12 mm; line extension adds 1-9 mm sizes)
- **Coil restrained length:** 2-43 cm (predicate); 2-36 cm (line extension)
- **Coil shape:** 3D spherical
- **Coil gap:** Closed
- **Delivery pusher length:** 195 cm

## Materials

| Component | Material |
|---|---|
| Main coil wire | Platinum/Tungsten alloy (Pt/W 92/8) |
| Coil-to-pusher coupler | Platinum/Iridium (Pt/Ir 90/10) |
| Adhesive | UV curing adhesive |
| Implant-to-pusher filament | Polyolefin elastomer |
| Stretch-resistant member | PET or Polyolefin elastomer (Engage) |
| Hydrogel core | Hydrophilic acrylic copolymer |

## Indications

- Endovascular embolization of intracranial aneurysms
- Other neurovascular abnormalities (AVMs, arteriovenous fistulae)
- Vascular occlusion to permanently obstruct blood flow to aneurysm or vascular malformation
- Arterial and venous embolizations in the peripheral vasculature

## Compatible With

- **Delivery pushers:** V-Trak or V-Trak Advanced Delivery Pusher (attached via polyolefin elastomer filament)
- **Detachment controller:** V-Grip Detachment Controller (handheld, battery-powered; heats filament to detach)
- **Catheter compatibility:** 10-system microcatheters (minimum ID 0.0165")
- **MRI compatible:** Yes

## Deployment/Use

1. Advance coil through compatible microcatheter to target
2. Insert proximal end of V-Trak pusher into V-Grip Detachment Controller
3. Activate controller -- electrical current heats polyolefin elastomer filament, detaching implant
4. Hydrogel begins expanding on contact with blood; reposition window exists before full expansion

## Complications to Know

Not specifically enumerated in this 510(k). Standard embolic coil risks apply. The hydrogel expansion is time-dependent -- coil must be repositioned before hydrogel fully expands or retrieval becomes difficult.
