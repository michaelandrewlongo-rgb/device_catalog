# PENUMBRA COIL SYSTEM/PENUMBRA COIL 400

**Manufacturer:** Penumbra
**Category:** Embolic Coil
510(k) K120330

## What It Is

PC400 is a .020-inch coil designed for aneurysms and fistulas; comes in complex and helical shapes

## Sizing/Specs

Materials: Stretch-resistant bare platinum

## Indications

5 10(k) Number (if known): K120330
Device Name: Penumbra Coil System
The Penumbra Coil System is indicated for the embolization of:
- Intracramial aneurysms
- Other neurovascular abnormalities such as arteriovenous malformations and
arteriovenous fistulae
- Arterial and venous embolizations in the peripheral vasculature
