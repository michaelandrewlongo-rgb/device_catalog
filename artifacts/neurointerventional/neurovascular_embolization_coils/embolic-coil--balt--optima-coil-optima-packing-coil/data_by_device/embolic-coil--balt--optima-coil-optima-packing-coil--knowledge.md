# Optima Coil System (Optima Packing Coil System)

**Manufacturer:** Balt
**Category:** Embolic coil

## What It Is

Device Description:
The Optima Coil System is a series of specialized coils that are inserted into the vasculature
under angiographic visualization to embolize intracranial aneurysms and other vascular
anomalies. The system consists of an embolization coil implant comprised of platinum and
tungsten, affixed to a delivery pusher to facilitate insertion into the hub of a microcatheter. The
system is available in various shapes, lengths, and sizes. The devices are to be placed into
aneurysms to create blood stasis, reducing flow into the aneurysm and thrombosing the
aneurysm. Upon positioning coils into the aneurysm, the coils are detached from the delivery
pusher in a serial manner until the aneurysm is occluded.
Indications for Use:
The Optima Coil System is intended for the endovascular embolization of intracranial
aneurysms and other neurovascular abnormalities such as arteriovenous malformations and
arteriovenous fistulae. The Optima Coil System is also intended for vascular occlusion of
blood vessels within the neurovascular system to permanently obstruct blood flow to an
aneurysm or other vascular malformation and for arterial and venous embolizations in the
peripheral vasculature.
Intended Use:
The Optima Coil System is intended for use in the peripheral and neuro-vasculature to
endovascularly obstruct or occlude blood flow in vascular abnormalities of the neurovascular
and peripheral vessels.

## Key Differences vs Competitors

- **Coil Material & Design**: Optima Coil System uses a 92/8 platinum/tungsten alloy coil with helical secondary shapes (loose/tight combinations) and up to 5 joints. Competitors vary: Axium systems (Medtronic) use platinum/tungsten; CODMAN/DELTAMAXX/ORBIT GALAXY G2 (Cerenovus) use bare platinum with polypropylene suture; HydroCoil Embolic System (MicroVention) uses hydrogel-coated platinum-tungsten alloy with 3D spherical shape; i-ED COIL (Kaneka) material not specified.
- **Detachment Mechanism**: Optima uses a pusher assembly with XCEL detachment controller (mechanical/thermal). Competitors differ: Axium Prime uses Axium I.D. Instant Detacher; CODMAN/DELTAMAXX/ORBIT GALAXY G2 use hydraulic detachment; HydroCoil uses V-Grip Detachment Controller (thermal); i-ED COIL uses ELECTRO DETACH GENERATOR v4 (electrolytic).
- **Indications & Scope**: Optima is indicated for intracranial aneurysms, neurovascular abnormalities (AVMs/fistulae), and peripheral vasculature embolization. This matches Axium systems, HydroCoil, and i-ED COIL. Eclipse 2L (Balt) is a balloon catheter for temporary occlusion/balloon-assisted embolization, not a permanent coil. Prestige Plus (Balt) is for peripheral vasculature only.
- **MRI Compatibility & Recalls**: Optima is MR Conditional but has 5 active FDA recalls for labeling issues. Competitors: Axium systems and HydroCoil are MR Conditional; i-ED COIL is MR Conditional; CODMAN/DELTAMAXX/ORBIT GALAXY G2 have 1 active recall for detachment failure; Eclipse 2L and Prestige Plus labeling lack MRI safety info.
- **Key Differentiators**: Optima offers a range of coil lengths (5-65 cm) and primary wind diameters (0.012”-0.014”), with features to reduce resistance in tortuous vessels. HydroCoil uniquely uses hydrogel expansion for higher packing density. CODMAN/DELTAMAXX/ORBIT GALAXY G2 offer complex framing/filling/finishing coils. Eclipse 2L is not a coil but a balloon catheter for adjunctive use.
