# InZone Detachment System

**Manufacturer:** Stryker
**Category:** Embolic Coil
510(k) K160096

## What It Is

Stryker Neurovascular's InZone® Detachment System is a sterile, handheld, single-patient use device designed for use with Stryker Neurovascular Detachable Coils. The device consists of an enclosure with a detachment button, five LED indicator lamps, a funnel inset at its distal end, and a cable connection port. The device comes pre-loaded with two AAAA (1.5 VDC) batteries. Detachment of the coil from its delivery wire is accomplished by means of an electrolytic process wherein the body's electrolytes serve as the electrolytic carrier between positive and negative electrodes.

## Indications

The InZone Detachment System is intended for use with all versions of Stryker Neurovascular detachable coils in the
embolization of intracranial aneurysms and other vascular malformations of the neuro and peripheral vasculature.
