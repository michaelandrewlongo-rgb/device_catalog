# AXS Vecta 71/74 Intermediate Catheter

**Manufacturer:** Stryker Neurovascular
**Category:** Distal Access Catheter

## What It Is

Intermediate catheter designed for dual-purpose use: device delivery to intracranial targets and direct thrombus aspiration during mechanical thrombectomy. The Vecta 71/74 designation refers to the catheter's 0.071 inch inner diameter and variable outer profile (6.3F distal, 6.5F proximal). The tapered outer profile reduces distal stiffness while maintaining proximal trackability. Available in three working lengths.

## Use Notes

- Classified as a device delivery and thrombus aspiration catheter; supports both intermediate access platform use and direct aspiration (ADAPT-style) technique.
- The 0.071 inch inner diameter accommodates standard stent retrievers (e.g., Trevo, Solitaire) deployed through a co-axial microcatheter.
- Excessive aspiration may cause patient complications; aspiration pressure and duration should be controlled per institutional protocol.
- Source: Stryker Neurovascular product page and EVToday device guide.

## Key Differences vs Competitors

- **Primary function**: AXS Vecta 71/74 is designed for both device delivery to intracranial targets and direct thrombus aspiration (ADAPT-style). In contrast, the AXS Catalyst series is specifically designed for continuous aspiration thrombus removal, and the AXS Vecta 46 is indicated for device insertion/guidance and as a retrieval conduit, not as a standalone aspiration device.
- **Inner diameter**: AXS Vecta 71/74 has a 0.071 inch inner diameter. The inner diameters for AXS Catalyst 5, 6, and 7 are not specified.
- **Outer diameter profile**: AXS Vecta 71/74 has a tapered outer profile (6.3F distal, 6.5F proximal). Outer diameters for the AXS Catalyst series and AXS Vecta 46 are not specified.
- **Compatible aspiration system**: AXS Vecta 71/74's compatible aspiration pump is not specified. The AXS Catalyst Distal Access Catheter requires the Medela Dominant Flex Pump as part of the AXS Universal Aspiration System.
- **FDA recall history**: FDA recall history is mentioned for the AXS Vecta 46 but is not specified for the AXS Vecta 71/74 or the AXS Catalyst series.
