## Key Differences vs Competitors

- **Distal access capability**: AXS Catalyst 5 is specifically designed for more distal access and smaller vessels (e.g., MCA M2), whereas AXS Catalyst 6 is for larger vessel targets, AXS Catalyst 7 for proximal large vessel targets, and CEREBASE DA Guide Sheath focuses on distal access with geometric anchoring.
- **Indications specificity**: AXS Catalyst 5 has only a warning about excessive aspiration, while AXS Catalyst Distal Access Catheter has specific vessel indications (ICA, MCA M1/M2, basilar artery), and CEREGLIDE 71 is optimized for acute ischemic stroke intervention with direct aspiration and stent retriever passes.
- **MRI compatibility**: AXS Catalyst 5 is MR Conditional, similar to AXS Catalyst 6, but AXS Catalyst 7, AXS Offset, and CEREBASE DA Guide Sheath have no specified MRI safety information.
- **Trackability comparison**: CEREGLIDE 71 is described as more trackable than Sofia Plus, React 71, Catalyst 6, and Large Bore Catheter, while CEREBASE DA Guide Sheath offers better trackability and support than NeuronMax, Infinity, Shuttle, Fubuki, and Ballast when placed distally.
