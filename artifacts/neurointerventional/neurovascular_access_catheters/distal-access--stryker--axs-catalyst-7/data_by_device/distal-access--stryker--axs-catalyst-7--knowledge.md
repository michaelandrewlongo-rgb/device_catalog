## Key Differences vs Competitors

- **MRI Safety**: AXS Catalyst 7 labeling does not contain MRI safety information, while AXS Catalyst 5 and Catalyst 6 are MR Conditional.
- **Indications**: AXS Catalyst 7 is indicated only with a warning about excessive aspiration, whereas the AXS Catalyst Distal Access Catheter is indicated for revascularization in specific intracranial vessels (ICA, MCA M1/M2, basilar artery).
- **Sizing Role**: According to the sizing rule for the Catalyst series, Catalyst 7 is for the largest lumen and proximal large vessel targets, while Catalyst 5 is for more distal access/smaller vessels (e.g., MCA M2) and Catalyst 6 is for larger vessel targets or when greater lumen is needed.
- **Inner Diameter**: CEREGLIDE 71 has a larger inner diameter than Catalyst 7, Catalyst 6, and Sofia Plus (per its use notes).
- **Compatibility**: AXS Catalyst 7 compatibility is not specified, while the AXS Catalyst Distal Access Catheter requires the Medela Dominant Flex Pump and is compatible with specific AXS system components.
