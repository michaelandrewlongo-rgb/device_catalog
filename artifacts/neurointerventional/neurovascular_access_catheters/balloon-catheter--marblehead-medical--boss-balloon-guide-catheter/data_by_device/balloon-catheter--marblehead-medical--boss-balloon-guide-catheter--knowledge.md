# BOSS Balloon Guide Catheter

**Manufacturer:** Marblehead Medical, LLC
**Category:** Balloon Guide Catheter

## What It Is

Sterile, single-use balloon guide catheter for neurointerventional and peripheral vascular procedures. The BOSS is a 90 cm, variable-stiffness catheter with a braid-reinforced shaft incorporating a coaxial central lumen (stainless steel coil reinforced) and a separate annular inflation lumen. A bifurcated dual-port luer hub at the proximal end separates the central port (for interventional device passage) from the inflation port (syringe-driven balloon inflation/deflation). A radiopaque marker band is located at the distal tip, distal to the balloon. The distal shaft carries a hydrophilic coating to reduce friction against the vessel wall.

The system is packaged with a 6F dilator, allowing direct arterial access without a separate introducer sheath.

Primary predicate: 8F FlowGate2 Balloon Guide Catheter (K153729). Secondary predicate: Neuron MAX System (K111380). Cleared under 510(k) K200910.

## Indications

Indicated for:
- Facilitating insertion and guidance of an intravascular catheter into a selected blood vessel in the peripheral and neurovascular systems
- Temporary vascular occlusion via balloon inflation during neurointerventional procedures
- Conduit for retrieval devices (stent retrievers, aspiration catheters)

## Key Differences vs Competitors

- **Packaging/access**: BOSS includes a 6F dilator for direct arterial access without a separate introducer sheath; this accessory is not specified for Branchor, EMBOGUARD, or Scepter devices.
- **Indication scope**: BOSS and Scepter C/XC/Mini are indicated for both peripheral and neurovascular systems; Branchor and EMBOGUARD are indicated only for the neurovascular system.
- **Guidewire compatibility**: BOSS central lumen size is not specified; Scepter C/XC accepts 0.014" guidewires, Scepter Mini accepts 0.008" guidewires, and Branchor/EMBOGUARD guidewire compatibility is not specified.
- **Balloon characteristics**: BOSS balloon material is not specified; Branchor uses polyurethane elastomer, EMBOGUARD has an ultra compliant balloon, and Scepter devices have compliant balloons with specific size ranges.
- **Working lengths**: BOSS is 90 cm; Branchor offers 90 cm and 100 cm, EMBOGUARD offers 85 cm and 95 cm, and Scepter device lengths are not specified.
