# CEREGLIDE 92 Catheter System with TruCourse

**Manufacturer:** Cerenovus (J&J)
**Category:** Distal access catheter

## What It Is

Large-bore intermediate catheter system featuring INNERGLIDE 9 Delivery Aid (co-packaged). Engineered for optimized delivery to the M1 segment with TruCourse technology for improved trackability. Features BRITE-LINE for full-length catheter visibility under fluoroscopy. Largest inner diameter with lowest distal profile of 8F systems.

## Sizing/Specs

Distal ID: 0.092 inch; Proximal OD: 0.108 inch; Guide Compatibility: 8F (long guide sheaths >= 0.113 inch ID); Usable Length: 114 cm; Product codes: IG9DA, SBC92114UG, SBC92122UG, SBC92130UG, SBC92135UG; INNERGLIDE 9 compatible with guidewires up to 0.024 inch

## Indications

Designed for acute ischemic stroke intervention with distal M1 access. Optimized for aspiration thrombectomy with reduced relay when used with CEREGLIDE 71.

## Compatible With

INNERGLIDE 9 Delivery Aid (co-packaged), CEREGLIDE 71 Intermediate Catheter, EMBOTRAP III Revascularization Device, Prowler EX Microcatheter; Compatible with commonly sold 8F long guide sheaths >= 0.113 inch ID

## Use Notes

Lower track forces than Zoom 88. Largest ID with lowest distal profile vs Zoom 88, Sofia 88, Millipede 088. Designed to diminish flow in M1 and for distal placement in M1 for reduced relay with CEREGLIDE 71.

## Key Differences vs Competitors

- **Largest inner diameter with lowest distal profile**: CEREGLIDE 92 has a distal ID of 0.092 inch, which is larger than CEREBASE DA's 0.090 inch and not specified for the Catalyst series. It is described as having the largest ID with lowest distal profile compared to Zoom 88, Sofia 88, and Millipede 088.
- **Optimized for specific procedural use**: Designed for distal M1 access and aspiration thrombectomy with reduced relay when used with CEREGLIDE 71, whereas CEREBASE DA is a guide sheath for distal access with geometric anchoring, and the Catalyst series is indicated for thrombus removal in multiple vessels including M1 and M2.
- **Unique compatibility and co-packaging**: Co-packaged with INNERGLIDE 9 Delivery Aid and compatible with CEREGLIDE 71, EMBOTRAP III, and Prowler EX, while CEREBASE DA is compatible with short sheaths and the Catalyst series requires specific aspiration pumps and tubing systems.
- **Trackability features**: Features TruCourse technology for improved trackability and lower track forces than Zoom 88, whereas CEREBASE DA offers better trackability than NeuronMax, Infinity, and others, and Catalyst devices have hydrophilic coating but no direct trackability comparisons provided.
- **Length and guide compatibility**: Usable length of 114 cm and requires 8F long guide sheaths (≥0.113 inch ID), compared to CEREBASE DA's 80-95 cm lengths with 8F short sheath compatibility, and Catalyst devices with not specified lengths but different vessel target rules (Catalyst 5 for M2, Catalyst 6 for larger vessels, Catalyst 7 for proximal targets).
