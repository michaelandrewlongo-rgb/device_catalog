# Esperance pHLO Aspiration System

**Manufacturer:** phenox Ltd. (Wallaby Medical)
**Category:** Aspiration Catheter

## What It Is

Kit-based aspiration thrombectomy system for acute ischemic stroke, consisting of three components: the Esperance pHLO Aspiration Catheter, the Esperance Aspiration Tubing Set, and an introducer sheath. The Aspiration Catheter is a single-lumen, flexible, variable-stiffness composite catheter with coil and braid reinforcement and polymer jackets. A radiopaque marker is located approximately 0.6 mm from the distal tip to facilitate fluoroscopic positioning. The distal 100 cm of the catheter's external surface carries a hydrophilic coating for trackability. A hub with rotating hemostasis valve (RHV) at the proximal end accommodates accessory devices and vacuum connection. The tubing set connects the catheter hub to a compatible aspiration pump canister. Supplied sterile in hoop/pouch/shelf-carton configuration. Predicate: Esperance Aspiration Catheter System (Wallaby Medical, K211697). 510(k) K251357, cleared January 2026.

## Indications

As part of the Esperance pHLO Aspiration System, the Esperance pHLO Aspiration Catheter with a compatible aspiration pump and the Esperance Aspiration Tubing Set is indicated for use in the revascularization of patients with acute ischemic stroke secondary to intracranial large vessel occlusive disease (within the internal carotid, middle cerebral - M1 and M2 segments, basilar, and vertebral arteries) within 8 hours of symptom onset. Patients who are ineligible for thrombolytic drug therapy or who failed thrombolytic drug therapy are candidates for treatment.

## Use Notes

- Requires a compatible aspiration pump (not included); connect via the Esperance Aspiration Tubing Set
- Intended for use in a hospital/healthcare facility by trained physicians only
- Introducer sheath included in kit; removed after femoral insertion
- Non-active device intended for short-term intravascular use

## Key Differences vs Competitors

- **Sizing**: The Esperance pHLO has an outer diameter of 6 Fr (2.1 mm) and inner diameter of 1.8 mm. The AXS Catalyst 7 has a larger inner diameter of 0.068" (1.73 mm), while the APRO Aspiration Catheter comes in two unspecified sizes (APRO 55 and APRO 70). The 072 Aspiration System and AXS Catalyst Distal Access Catheter sizes are not specified.
- **Kit components**: The Esperance pHLO is a kit-based system including the catheter, aspiration tubing set, and introducer sheath. The AXS Catalyst 7 and AXS Catalyst Distal Access Catheter are described as the only intravascular components of their systems, with tubing and pumps remaining outside the sterile field. The APRO Aspiration Catheter includes Alembic aspiration tubing as an accessory.
- **Indications**: The Esperance pHLO, 072 Aspiration System, AXS Catalyst 7, and AXS Catalyst Distal Access Catheter share identical indications for revascularization in acute ischemic stroke within 8 hours of symptom onset. The APRO Aspiration Catheter is indicated more broadly for thrombus removal from cerebral vasculature. The Artemis Neuro Evacuation Device is indicated for aspiration from the Ventricular System and/or Cerebrum, not for stroke thrombectomy.
- **Compatible pumps**: The Esperance pHLO requires a compatible aspiration pump (not included). The AXS Catalyst 7 and AXS Catalyst Distal Access Catheter specify compatibility with the Medela Dominant Flex Pump. Pump compatibility for other devices is not specified.
- **Device type**: The Esperance pHLO, 072 Aspiration System, APRO Aspiration Catheter, AXS Catalyst 7, and AXS Catalyst Distal Access Catheter are all aspiration catheters for thrombectomy. The Artemis is a neuro evacuation device for ventricular/cerebral aspiration, while Ballast and Carrier are sheath and delivery catheter systems respectively, not direct aspiration thrombectomy devices.
