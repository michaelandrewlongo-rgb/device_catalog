# Riptide Aspiration System

**Manufacturer:** Medtronic
**Category:** Aspiration catheter

## What It Is

Aspiration system designed to remove occlusive thrombus from cerebral vasculature during acute ischemic strokes using continuous aspiration. Catheter shaft has hydrophilic coating spanning distal 40 cm to reduce friction. Catheter acts as conduit providing direct aspiration to occlusion site via Riptide aspiration pump. Introduced through a guide catheter into the cerebral vasculature.

## Key Differences vs Competitors

- **Indication specificity**: Riptide is specifically indicated for removing occlusive thrombus from cerebral vasculature during acute ischemic stroke, whereas Ballast, Carrier, and Hybrid are indicated for general intravascular introduction/delivery/guidance, not specifically for thrombus removal.
- **Aspiration mechanism**: Riptide uses continuous aspiration via its dedicated Riptide pump, while devices like Ballast, Carrier, and Hybrid are not aspiration catheters (Ballast is a long sheath, Carrier is a delivery catheter, Hybrid is a guidewire).
- **Compatibility**: Riptide is compatible with the Riptide aspiration pump and a guide catheter, whereas APRO Aspiration Catheter is compatible with Phenom microcatheters, Solitaire X, and Alembic tubing, and React Aspiration Catheter is compatible with Solitaire X for combined approach.
- **Construction features**: Riptide has a 40 cm hydrophilic coating on the distal shaft to reduce friction, while React Aspiration Catheter features coil, braid, and end-to-end nitinol construction for navigability, and APRO has stainless-steel and coil plus braid reinforcements.
- **Procedure location**: Riptide is for cerebral vasculature thrombus removal, while Artemis Neuro Evacuation Device is for aspiration from the Ventricular System and/or Cerebrum through a neuroendoscope, and AXS Universal Aspiration Set's Dominant Flex pump is for general hospital/clinic suction including surgical fluids, tissue, and bodily fluids.
