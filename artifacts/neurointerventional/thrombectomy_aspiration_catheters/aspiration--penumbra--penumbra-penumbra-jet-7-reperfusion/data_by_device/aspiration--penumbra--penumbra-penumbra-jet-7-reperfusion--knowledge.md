# Penumbra JET 7 Reperfusion Catheter with Xtra Flex Technology (JET 7MAX)

**Manufacturer:** Penumbra, Inc.
**Category:** Aspiration Catheter
**FDA Clearance:** K202251 (cleared 2020-08-31)

---

## What It Is

The JET 7 Reperfusion Catheter with Xtra Flex Technology is Penumbra's large-bore aspiration catheter for mechanical thrombectomy in acute ischemic stroke. It is the direct evolution of the JET 7 (K191946, K190010), adding Xtra Flex Technology to the distal catheter shaft to improve navigation through tortuous neurovascular anatomy.

The JET 7 is the flagship of Penumbra's aspiration platform, with a 0.070-inch inner diameter designed for the ADAPT (A Direct Aspiration first Pass Technique) approach. The JET 7MAX configuration includes the MAX Delivery Device, which aids advancement of the catheter to the occlusion and is removed before aspiration begins.

Xtra Flex Technology refers to an enhanced flexibility design in the distal shaft. This distinguishes this device from the preceding JET 7 configurations. It does not refer to the MAX Delivery Device accessory.

## Indications

Acute ischemic stroke secondary to intracranial large vessel occlusive disease within 8 hours of symptom onset. Target vessels include:

- Internal carotid artery (ICA)
- Middle cerebral artery M1 and M2 segments
- Basilar artery
- Vertebral artery

Indicated for patients who are ineligible for or who have failed IV t-PA therapy.

## Use Notes

**Technique:** Direct aspiration thrombectomy (ADAPT). The catheter is advanced to the face of the thrombus under fluoroscopic guidance. The Penumbra Aspiration Pump applies continuous vacuum for clot removal. The MAX Delivery Device, when included, coaxially supports advancement and is withdrawn before aspiration begins.

**Xtra Flex Technology:** Enhanced distal shaft flexibility intended to improve trackability through tortuous anatomy. This is a design change to the catheter itself, not a system-level accessory change.

**Active recalls (Penumbra Reperfusion Catheter family, 6 total):**
- Catheter may fail at the mid-shaft joint and separate; the distal segment could block blood flow and require emergency retrieval.
- Catheter may sustain distal tip damage; tip damage with contrast pressurization may result in vessel injury.

Operators should inspect the catheter prior to use and follow current Penumbra safety communications.

## Key Differences vs Competitors

| Feature | JET 7 Xtra Flex (this device) | JET 7 (K191946/K190010) | Penumbra ACE 68 / ACE 72 |
|---|---|---|---|
| ID | 0.070 in | 0.070 in | 0.068 / 0.072 in |
| Xtra Flex distal shaft | Yes | No | No |
| MAX Delivery Device pairing | Optional (JET 7MAX) | Optional | No |
| Primary design change | Distal flexibility | Baseline JET 7 | Larger bore ACE family |

**JET 7 vs JET 7X:** JET 7X (K191946) was the first iteration pairing the JET 7 catheter with the MAX Delivery Device. This device (K202251) adds Xtra Flex Technology to the catheter shaft itself; the MAX Delivery Device pairing is secondary and reflected in the JET 7MAX product name. These are distinct cleared configurations.
