# Penumbra System JET 7MAX

**Manufacturer:** Penumbra
**Category:** Aspiration catheter

## What It Is

The Penumbra System JET 7MAX is a configuration of the Penumbra System combining the Penumbra JET 7 Reperfusion Catheter with the MAX Delivery Device. The MAX Delivery Device is an optional accessory that aids catheter advancement to the occlusion site and is removed before aspiration begins. Once positioned, the JET 7 delivers continuous aspiration directly to the thrombus via the Penumbra Aspiration Pump, enabling direct clot removal from the neurovasculature.

## Indications

Removal of thrombus from the neurovasculature in patients with acute ischemic stroke secondary to intracranial large vessel occlusion involving the internal carotid artery, middle cerebral artery (M1/M2), basilar artery, or vertebral artery. For use within 8 hours of symptom onset in patients who are ineligible for or who have failed IV t-PA therapy.

## Use Notes

The MAX Delivery Device is used only during catheter advancement to the occlusion. It must be removed before aspiration begins. Aspiration is delivered via the Penumbra Aspiration Pump connected directly to the JET 7 Reperfusion Catheter.

**Active recalls (6 total - Penumbra Reperfusion Catheters):**
- The reperfusion catheter may fail at the mid-shaft joint and break off, potentially causing blocked blood flow requiring emergency intervention.
- The catheter may sustain distal tip damage during use; tip damage with contrast pressurization may result in vessel injury.
