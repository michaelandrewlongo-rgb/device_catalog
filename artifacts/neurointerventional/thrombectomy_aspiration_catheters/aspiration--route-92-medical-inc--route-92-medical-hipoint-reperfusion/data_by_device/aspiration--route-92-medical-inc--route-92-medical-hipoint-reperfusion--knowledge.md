# Route 92 Medical HiPoint Reperfusion System

**Manufacturer:** Route 92 Medical, Inc.
**Category:** Aspiration Catheter

## What It Is

The HiPoint Reperfusion System is a multi-component aspiration platform for mechanical thrombectomy in acute ischemic stroke. The system includes an Aspiration Catheter, a Delivery Catheter (used to facilitate intracranial navigation of the Aspiration Catheter), and is used with the Route 92 Medical Aspiration Tubing Set and the Route 92 Medical Base Camp Sheath System.

The Aspiration Catheter has a distinctive construction: a single-lumen, coil-reinforced polymer distal shaft section connected to a proximal control wire, providing support during advancement through tortuous anatomy. The Delivery Catheter is a single-lumen, variable-stiffness catheter. Both catheters carry hydrophilic coating. Devices are non-pyrogenic and intended for single use only, sterilized by ethylene oxide.

The Route 92 Medical Aspiration Tubing Set uses high-pressure tubing with an on/off switch for vacuum control and a Luer fitting for catheter connection. The compatible aspiration source must be a portable vacuum pump capable of providing constant vacuum at the prespecified performance parameters for the system.

The Base Camp Sheath System (separate component) comprises a Sheath (compatible with 8F or smaller catheters, with a radiopaque distal marker), a Dilator (radiopaque distal tip), a Navigating Catheter (shaped distal end), and an RHV. All catheters in the Base Camp system carry hydrophilic coating.

Two aspiration catheter sizes are available (70 and 88 series), covering a range of vessel sizes from MCA M1 through ICA. The system was evaluated in the SUMMIT MAX randomized controlled trial.

## Indications

The Route 92 Medical HiPoint Reperfusion System and Aspiration Tubing Set, when used with a compatible vacuum pump, is indicated for use in the revascularization of patients with acute ischemic stroke secondary to intracranial large vessel occlusive disease within the internal carotid artery and middle cerebral artery M1 segment within 8 hours of symptom onset. Patients who are ineligible for thrombolytic drug therapy or who failed thrombolytic drug therapy are candidates for treatment.

Note: the cleared indication is limited to ICA and MCA M1 (not M2, basilar, or vertebral arteries), which is narrower than many comparable aspiration systems.

## Use Notes

- Single use only. Do not resterilize.
- The Aspiration Catheter relies on a proximal control wire (not a conventional braided or coiled shaft throughout) for pushability; this is a design differentiator from conventional aspiration catheters.
- The Delivery Catheter is used to facilitate advance of the Aspiration Catheter; it is not interchangeable with standard microcatheters without confirming compatibility.
- Compatible with a portable vacuum pump meeting the system's specified vacuum parameters; the Medela Dominant Flex Pump is not explicitly mandated (unlike AXS Catalyst labeling).
- The Base Camp Sheath System is a separate accessory that provides the access foundation for the HiPoint system and is compatible with 8F or smaller catheters.
- Evaluated in the SUMMIT MAX prospective, multi-center, randomized, controlled, open-label trial.

## Key Differences vs Competitors

- **Indication scope**: The HiPoint system is cleared only for ICA and MCA M1 occlusions within 8 hours of symptom onset, which is narrower than the AXS Catalyst systems, APRO Aspiration Catheter, and Penumbra Reperfusion Catheters, which are also indicated for M2, basilar, and vertebral arteries.
- **Catheter construction**: The HiPoint Aspiration Catheter uses a proximal control wire for support, differing from the stainless-steel and coil-plus-braid reinforcements of the APRO Aspiration Catheter and the variable-stiffness designs of the AXS Catalyst and 072 Aspiration System.
- **Pump compatibility**: HiPoint requires a portable vacuum pump meeting specific parameters but does not mandate the Medela Dominant Flex Pump, unlike the AXS Catalyst systems which specify this pump.
- **System components**: HiPoint is a multi-component platform including a dedicated Delivery Catheter and separate Base Camp Sheath System, whereas the AXS Catalyst 7 and APRO Aspiration Catheter are described as single catheters used with aspiration tubing.
- **Clinical evidence**: HiPoint was evaluated in the SUMMIT MAX randomized controlled trial; clinical trial data for the other devices is not specified in the provided details.
