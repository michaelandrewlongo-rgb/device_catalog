# Zoom 7X Catheter

**Manufacturer:** Imperative Care, Inc.
**Category:** Aspiration catheter
**FDA Clearance:** K243047 (cleared 2025-05-28)

---

## What It Is

The Zoom 7X is a single-lumen, braid- and coil-reinforced neurovascular aspiration catheter designed for direct thrombus removal in acute ischemic stroke. It features CenTRX technology, an asymmetric beveled distal tip that allows atraumatic navigation past vessel branches. The distal section carries a hydrophilic coating to enhance trackability. A radiopaque marker at the distal tip provides fluoroscopic visualization.

The catheter connects to a vacuum source via Zoom Aspiration Tubing or Zoom POD Aspiration Tubing. The standard companion pump is the Zoom Aspiration Pump. The system uses a standard luer fitting for tubing attachment. Accessories include a Rotating Hemostasis Valve (RHV) and two introducer sheath accessories.

Single use only.

## Indications

Indicated for revascularization of patients with acute ischemic stroke secondary to intracranial large vessel occlusive disease involving the internal carotid artery, middle cerebral artery (M1/M2), basilar artery, or vertebral artery within 8 hours of **last known well**.

Candidates include patients ineligible for intravenous thrombolytic therapy or who have not responded to it.

> Note: The indication uses "last known well," not "symptom onset." This is a clinically meaningful distinction. Many older devices specify symptom onset; the last-known-well framing expands eligibility for patients with unwitnessed or wake-up strokes where symptom onset is unknown.

## Compatible With

**Vacuum source requirements:**
- Aspiration pump capable of -20 inHg to maximum vacuum (-29.9 inHg)
- Airflow rating: 0-23 LPM
- IEC 60601-1 compliant
- The Imperative Care Zoom Aspiration Pump meets these criteria

**Tubing:**
- Zoom Aspiration Tubing (connects Zoom 7X to the aspiration pump)
- Zoom POD Aspiration Tubing (alternative tubing configuration)

**Other Zoom catheters compatible with Zoom Aspiration Tubing:**
- Zoom 71, 55, 45, 35
- TracStar LDP Large Distal Platform
- Zoom 88 Large Distal Platform
- Zoom 88 Large Distal Platform Support

## Use Notes

- CenTRX technology: asymmetric beveled distal tip enables atraumatic navigation past branch points without requiring tip reshaping.
- The hydrophilic distal coating is intended to reduce friction during intracranial tracking.
- Sterilization method: [NEEDS CONTENT - not stated in available FDA summary]
- Active FDA recall (as of 2026-04-15): potential for the distal end of the catheter to fracture and become detached. Verify current recall status before use.

---

## Key Differences vs Competitors

The Zoom 7X received 510(k) clearance in May 2025, making it among the most recently cleared large-bore aspiration catheters available. Most direct competitors (Penumbra ACE, Stryker AXS Vecta, Medtronic Riptide) carry indications referencing symptom onset windows. The Zoom 7X's "last known well" framing aligns with current AHA/ASA clinical guidance and may extend the eligible treatment window for certain patient presentations.

The 0.071-inch inner lumen positions the Zoom 7X alongside other 71-equivalent large-bore aspiration catheters (e.g., Zoom 71, Penumbra ACE 68/72) in the high-flow aspiration tier.
