# Esperance 3+ Aspiration Catheter System

**Manufacturer:** Wallaby Medical
**Category:** Aspiration Catheter

## What It Is

Single-use, single-lumen, variable-stiffness composite catheter designed for direct aspiration thrombectomy in acute ischemic stroke secondary to intracranial large vessel occlusion. The shaft is tapered: inner diameter transitions from 0.054" proximally to 0.041" distally; outer diameter transitions from 0.066" proximally to 0.050" distally. The distal shaft carries a 100 cm hydrophilic coating to reduce friction in tortuous anatomy. A radiopaque platinum/iridium marker band provides fluoroscopic tip visualization. The proximal end incorporates a silicone strain relief and standard luer adapter.

Construction materials include a stainless steel braided inner shaft with nitinol coil reinforcement, thermoplastic polyurethane (Tecoflex, Pellethane), polyether block amide (Pebax), polyamide (Vestamid) outer layers, and a PTFE inner liner. The hub is polyamide. The distal tip is straight and steam-shapeable by the user.

The system is supplied as a kit with the Wallaby Aspiration Tubing Set (polyurethane and nylon, 112" length, 0.110" ID, integrated vacuum-control valve), a peel-away introducer sheath, a shaping mandrel, and a rotating hemostasis valve (RHV). The aspiration tubing connects the catheter to a compatible external aspiration pump. Sterilized by ethylene oxide; shelf life 12 months. Single use only.

## Indications

The Esperance 3+ Aspiration Catheter with the Wallaby Aspiration Tubing Set and a compatible aspiration pump is indicated for use in the revascularization of patients with acute ischemic stroke secondary to intracranial large vessel occlusive disease (within the internal carotid, middle cerebral M1 and M2 segments, basilar, and vertebral arteries) within 8 hours of symptom onset. Patients who are ineligible for intravenous tissue plasminogen activator (IV t-PA) or who fail IV t-PA therapy are candidates for treatment.

## Use Notes

- Single use only. Do not resterilize.
- MRI safety information is not included in labeling; treat as MR-unsafe until confirmed.
- Compatible with standard rotating hemostasis valves and a compatible external aspiration pump (not included in kit).
- Tip is steam-shapeable by the user; do not steam more than once.
- Predicate for K240917 clearance was the Penumbra System MAX (K113163).

## Key Differences vs Competitors

- **Tapered shaft design**: Esperance 3+ has a tapered inner/outer diameter (0.054" to 0.041" ID, 0.066" to 0.050" OD), while AXS Catalyst 7 has a constant 0.068" ID, and APRO sizes (55/70) are not specified.
- **Kit components**: Esperance 3+ includes aspiration tubing, peel-away sheath, shaping mandrel, and RHV in the kit; AXS Catalyst catheters require separate aspiration tubing/pump, and 072/APRO kit contents are not specified.
- **Indication specificity**: Esperance 3+, AXS Catalyst, and Penumbra Reperfusion Catheters specify use for intracranial large vessel occlusion within 8 hours; Artemis is for ventricular/cerebral evacuation, and Ballast/Carrier are for general intravascular access.
- **Steam-shapeable tip**: Esperance 3+ allows user steam-shaping; this feature is not specified for AXS Catalyst, 072, APRO, or other listed devices.
- **Hydrophilic coating length**: Esperance 3+ specifies 100 cm coating; AXS Catalyst mentions coating but length not specified, and 072/APRO coating details are not specified.
