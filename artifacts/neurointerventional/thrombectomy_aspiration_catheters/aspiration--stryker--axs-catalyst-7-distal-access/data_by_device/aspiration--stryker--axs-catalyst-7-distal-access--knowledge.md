# AXS Catalyst 7 Distal Access Catheter

**Manufacturer:** Stryker Neurovascular
**Category:** Aspiration Catheter

## What It Is

Sterile, single-lumen, variable-stiffness catheter designed for direct aspiration thrombectomy via continuous aspiration in the neurovasculature. The Catalyst 7 is a larger-bore sibling of the AXS Catalyst 058/060, with a 0.068" inner diameter providing greater aspiration capacity for proximal or large-vessel occlusions. The shaft has a hydrophilic coating to reduce friction during navigation. A radiopaque distal marker enables angiographic visualization. The proximal luer hub accepts an RHV and Tuohy Borst valve with sideport for flushing, device passage, and aspiration.

Used as part of the AXS Universal Aspiration System with the AXS Universal Aspiration Tubing, AXS Universal Liner Set, and Medela Dominant Flex Pump (minimum vacuum: -68 kPa / -20.08 inHg). The catheter is the only intravascularly placed component; tubing and pump remain outside the sterile field. Packaged with an RHV, Tuohy Borst valve with sideport, and two peel-away introducer sheaths. Single use only. Sterilized by ethylene oxide.

## Indications

The AXS Catalyst Distal Access Catheter as part of the AXS Universal Aspiration System is indicated for use in the revascularization of patients with acute ischemic stroke secondary to intracranial large vessel occlusive disease (in the internal carotid, middle cerebral M1 and M2 segments, basilar, and vertebral arteries) within 8 hours of symptom onset. Patients who are ineligible for intravenous tissue plasminogen activator (IV t-PA) or who failed IV t-PA are candidates for treatment.

## Use Notes

- Single use only.
- The AXS Catalyst 7 catheter is the only intravascular component of the AXS Universal Aspiration System; the aspiration tubing and pump remain outside the sterile field.
- Compatible pump: Medela Dominant Flex Pump; minimum vacuum -68 kPa (-20.08 inHg).
- Two peel-away introducer sheaths are included to protect the distal tip during insertion into either the RHV or the Tuohy Borst valve.
- Note: multiple active FDA recalls exist for Stryker stroke procedure packs (Trevo/Catalyst combination packs) related to carton sleeve labeling mismatches; verify pack contents at time of use.

## Key Differences vs Competitors

- **Larger bore for proximal occlusions**: The AXS Catalyst 7 has a 0.068" inner diameter, which is larger than the tapered inner diameters of the Esperance 3+ (0.041" distal / 0.054" proximal) and the unspecified diameters of the APRO, 072, and other Catalyst models, providing greater aspiration capacity for large-vessel occlusions.
- **Specific pump requirement**: Requires the Medela Dominant Flex Pump with a minimum vacuum of -68 kPa (-20.08 inHg), similar to other AXS Catalyst models, while pump compatibility for the 072, APRO, and Esperance 3+ systems is not specified.
- **Included accessories**: Supplied with two peel-away introducer sheaths, whereas the standard AXS Catalyst description mentions one sheath, and sheath inclusion for the 072, APRO, and Esperance 3+ is not specified.
- **Focused neurovascular indication**: Indicated specifically for aspiration thrombectomy in acute ischemic stroke from intracranial large vessel occlusion (ICA, MCA M1/M2, basilar, vertebral), similar to the AXS Catalyst, 072, and Esperance 3+, whereas the Artemis is for ventricular/cerebral evacuation, and Ballast/Carrier are for general device introduction/infusion.
- **Recall note**: Has an associated FDA recall notice for labeling mismatches in procedure packs, a detail not mentioned for the other devices listed.
