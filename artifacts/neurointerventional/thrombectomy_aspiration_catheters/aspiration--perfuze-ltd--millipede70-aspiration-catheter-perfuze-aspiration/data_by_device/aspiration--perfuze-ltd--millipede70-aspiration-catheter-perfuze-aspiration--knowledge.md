# Millipede70 Aspiration Catheter and Perfuze Aspiration Tube Set

**Manufacturer:** Perfuze Ltd.
**Category:** Aspiration Catheter

## What It Is

Large-bore aspiration thrombectomy system for acute ischemic stroke, supplied as two co-labeled components: the Millipede70 Aspiration Catheter and the Perfuze Aspiration Tube Set. The Millipede70 is a sterile, single-use, single-lumen reinforced catheter with variable stiffness, a hydrophilic-coated distal segment, and a distal radiopaque marker for fluoroscopic positioning. A rotating hemostasis valve (RHV) is assembled onto the proximal hub to maintain hemostasis during saline/contrast infusion and device insertion. The Perfuze Aspiration Tube Set is a sterile, single-use flexible braided tube with a rotating male luer-lock connector at the distal end (attaches to the catheter hub) and a suction connector at the proximal end (connects to an aspiration pump canister); a pinch clamp controls vacuum application. The system is designed to interface with a compatible aspiration pump meeting prespecified vacuum performance parameters. 510(k) K252392, cleared August 2025. Predicate: K250012.

## Indications

The Millipede70 Aspiration Catheter, with the Perfuze Aspiration Tube Set and a compatible aspiration pump, is indicated for use in the revascularization of patients with acute ischemic stroke secondary to intracranial large vessel occlusive disease (within the internal carotid, middle cerebral - M1 and M2 segments, basilar, and vertebral arteries) within 8 hours of symptom onset. Patients who are ineligible for intravenous thrombolytic drug therapy or who have not responded to thrombolytic drug therapy are candidates for treatment.

The Perfuze Aspiration Tube Set is indicated to connect the Millipede70 Aspiration Catheter to a compatible aspiration pump.

## Use Notes

- Requires a compatible aspiration pump meeting prespecified vacuum performance parameters (not included); connect via Perfuze Aspiration Tube Set with a legally marketed canister and accessories kit
- Pinch clamp on the tubing set controls vacuum application to the catheter
- Single use only; supplied sterile

## Key Differences vs Competitors

- **Distal inner diameter**: Millipede70 (0.070 in) is larger than AXS Catalyst 7 (0.068 in) and APRO 70 (size not specified), but smaller than the 072 Aspiration System (not specified) and AXS Catalyst Distal Access Catheter (not specified).
- **Indications specificity**: Millipede70 has specific 8-hour window for acute ischemic stroke in defined arteries, similar to AXS Catalyst catheters and Penumbra Reperfusion Catheters, while APRO Aspiration Catheter has broader "removal of thrombus from cerebral vasculature" indication and Artemis is for ventricular/cerebral fluid/tissue evacuation.
- **System components**: Millipede70 includes co-labeled catheter and dedicated tubing set with pinch clamp, while AXS Catalyst catheters require separate aspiration tubing/pump and APRO includes Alembic tubing accessory; 072 Aspiration System compatibility details not specified.
- **Working length**: Millipede70 (136 cm) is longer than AXS Catalyst 7 options (115-132 cm) and APRO (not specified), but shorter than 072 Aspiration System (not specified).
