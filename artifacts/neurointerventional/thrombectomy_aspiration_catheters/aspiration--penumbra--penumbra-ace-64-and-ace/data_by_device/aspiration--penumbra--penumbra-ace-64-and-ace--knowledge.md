# Penumbra System ACE 64 and ACE 68

**Manufacturer:** Penumbra
**Category:** Aspiration catheter

## What It Is

Large-bore aspiration catheters used with the Penumbra Aspiration Pump for direct aspiration clot removal from occluded neurovascular vessels in acute ischemic stroke. The ACE 64 and ACE 68 differ in distal lumen diameter; both share the same proximal shaft dimensions and length options. Cleared together under FDA 510(k) K152541 (2016-01-13).

Both devices are provided sterile, non-pyrogenic, and intended for single use only. Sterilized by ethylene oxide (EO). Shelf life: 36 months.

**Shared construction details:**

- Hydrophilic coating: SRDX Harmony (proprietary)
- Body materials: Pellethane 80A, Tecoflex 80A, Pebax blends (35D-63D distal), Pebax 55D-72D proximal, Grilamid hub
- Reinforcement: Nitinol/stainless steel coil
- Sheath compatibility: 8-F short sheath or 6-F long sheath

**Included accessories:** Peelable PTFE sheath, Rotating Hemostasis Valve (polycarbonate body, silicone o-ring), Shaping Mandrel (0.038 in OD)

## Indications

Acute ischemic stroke secondary to intracranial large vessel occlusive disease. Target vessels:

- Internal carotid artery (ICA)
- Middle cerebral artery, M1 and M2 segments
- Basilar artery
- Vertebral artery

Treatment window: within 8 hours of symptom onset. Intended for patients ineligible for IV t-PA or who have failed IV t-PA therapy.

## Use Notes

- Mode of operation: direct aspiration thrombectomy using the Penumbra Aspiration Pump.
- The broader vessel indication (ICA, M1/M2, basilar, vertebral) was established with K152541. Earlier cleared ACE devices (K142458) were limited to ICA revascularization.

**Active safety recalls (Penumbra Reperfusion Catheter family, 6 recalls):**

- Catheter may fail at the mid-shaft joint and fracture, with the distal segment potentially detaching and blocking blood flow. Emergency retrieval may be required.
- Catheter may sustain distal tip damage. Contrast injection through a damaged tip may cause vessel injury.

Inspect catheter carefully before and during use. Do not advance through resistance.
