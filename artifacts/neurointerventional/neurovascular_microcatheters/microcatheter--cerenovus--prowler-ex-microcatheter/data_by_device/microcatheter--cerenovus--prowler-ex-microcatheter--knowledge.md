# Prowler EX Microcatheter

**Manufacturer:** Cerenovus (J&J)
**Category:** Microcatheter

## What It Is

Variable stiffness, single lumen microcatheter designed to access small, tortuous vasculature. Hydrophilic coating provides lubricity for vessel navigation. PTFE-lined inner lumen facilitates movement of guidewires and other devices. Proprietary braid construction provides balance of stability (braid section) and navigability (coil section).

## Sizing/Specs

Distal tip extension past distal marker: as little as 0.5 mm; TRUELUMEN Technology provides consistent inner diameter throughout length; In-line marker bands

## Compatible With

## Use Notes

TRUELUMEN Technology provides consistent ID throughout catheter length. Shorter distal tip past marker provides more accurate tip position representation under fluoroscopy. Part of the CERENOVUS Stroke Solutions system.

## Key Differences vs Competitors

- **Stiffness Technology**: Prowler EX uses a proprietary braid/coil hybrid construction for variable stiffness, while Echelon (Medtronic) uses progressive picks-per-inch nitinol braid, Excelsior (Stryker) uses graded durometer with stainless steel braid, and Headway Duo (MicroVention) uses a semi-rigid to flexible transition design.
- **Distal Tip Design**: Prowler EX features a short distal tip extension (as little as 0.5 mm past marker) for accurate fluoroscopic positioning, whereas Echelon has a steam-shapeable tip with dual markers, Excelsior variants have steam-shapeable tips, and Headway Duo has dual radiopaque markers.
- **Lumen Technology**: Prowler EX incorporates TRUELUMEN Technology for consistent inner diameter, while other devices' lumen specifications are not specified in the provided data.
- **System Integration**: Prowler EX is specifically designed as part of the CERENOVUS Stroke Solutions system with listed compatible devices (CEREBASE DA, CEREGLIDE 71, EMBOTRAP III, EMBOGUARD), whereas other devices have broader compatibility with various guidewires, coils, and embolic systems.
- **DMSO Compatibility**: Prowler EX compatibility with DMSO-based liquid embolics is not specified, while Echelon and Headway Duo are explicitly DMSO-compatible for liquid embolic delivery (Onyx/Trufill).
