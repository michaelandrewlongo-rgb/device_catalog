# Excelsior XT-27

**Manufacturer:** Stryker
**Category:** Microcatheter

## What It Is

Read Now

## Sizing/Specs

2.54 cm

## Indications

/ indications for use
Stryker Neurovascular Excelsior XT-27 Microcatheter
is intended to assist in the delivery of diagnostic agents
(such as contrast media), therapeutic agents, and non-
liquid interventional devices (such as stents) that are
indicated for use in the neurovasculature and with a
catheter of 0.027 inches in inner diameter.

## Use Notes

MRI: Labeling does not contain MRI Safety Information. Pre-sterilized. Single-use

## Key Differences vs Competitors

- **Inner diameter compatibility for stents**: Excelsior XT-27 (0.027") is specifically indicated for intracranial stents requiring this inner diameter, unlike Excelsior SL-10 (inner diameter not specified), Echelon (0.017" ID for Echelon 14), and Headway Duo (inner diameter not specified).
- **Indications scope**: Excelsior XT-27 is indicated for delivery of diagnostic agents, therapeutic agents, and non-liquid interventional devices (stents) in the neurovasculature only, while Excelsior SL-10 and XT-17 are indicated for peripheral, coronary, and neurovasculature, and Echelon is contraindicated for coronary use.
- **DMSO/liquid embolic compatibility**: Unlike Echelon (DMSO-compatible for Onyx) and Headway Duo (DMSO-compatible for Onyx/Trufill), Excelsior XT-27's labeling does not specify DMSO or liquid embolic compatibility.
- **Maximum infusion pressure**: Excelsior XT-27's maximum infusion pressure is not specified, while Echelon has a specified maximum of 600 PSI.
- **Distal outer diameter profile**: Excelsior XT-27's distal outer diameter is not specified, while Echelon 10 has a low profile 1.7F distal OD and Excelsior SL-10 has a 1.7F/0.031" OD profile.
