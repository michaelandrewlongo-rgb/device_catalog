## Indications

The AXS Lift Intracranial Base Catheter is indicated
for the introduction of interventional devices into the
peripheral and neurovasculature.

## Key Differences vs Competitors

- **Indications**: The AXS Lift™ is indicated for both peripheral and neurovasculature, similar to the Benchmark BMX96 Access System. The Broadway 8, Echo, and Excelsior XT-27 are indicated for neurovasculature only, while other Excelsior models are indicated for peripheral, coronary, and neurovasculature.
- **FDA Recalls**: The AXS Lift™ has no specified recall information, while the Broadway 8 and Echo catheters have 3 active FDA recalls each related to labeling and intended use issues.
- **Device Type**: The AXS Lift™ is an intracranial base catheter, similar to the Echo. The Broadway 8 is a catheter for neurovascular guidance, while the Excelsior family are microcatheters for distal navigation and delivery of agents/devices.
- **Inner Diameter**: The AXS Lift™ inner diameter is not specified. The Echo has a 0.100 inch ID, the Benchmark BMX96 has 0.096 inch ID, and Excelsior models have various IDs (SL-10: 1.00 mm/0.039 inch equivalent, XT-27: 0.027 inch).
- **Compatibility Notes**: The AXS Lift™ has no specified compatibility details, while Excelsior models have detailed compatibility with guide catheters, coils, stents, and contrast media.
