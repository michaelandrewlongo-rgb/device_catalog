# Trevo Trak 21 Microcatheter

**Manufacturer:** Stryker Neurovascular
**Category:** Microcatheter

## What It Is

Single-lumen, braided shaft, variable stiffness microcatheter designed for selective placement of devices and fluids in the neurovasculature. The Trevo Trak 21 features a stainless steel braid over a PTFE inner liner, with a polymeric outer jacket and hydrophilic coating to reduce friction during navigation. Platinum/iridium radiopaque marker bands at the distal end and a radiopaque shaft facilitate fluoroscopic visualization throughout the procedure. The proximal end terminates in a polyurethane Luer hub. A rotating hemostasis valve (RHV) with side-arm adapter is packaged with each microcatheter.

The Trevo Trak 21 is purpose-built as the delivery microcatheter for the Trevo stent-retriever thrombectomy system, enabling advancement through a guide or balloon guide catheter to the target clot over an appropriately sized guidewire.

Key construction features:
- Stainless steel shaft braid for pushability and torque transmission
- PTFE inner layer for low-friction device passage
- Hydrophilic outer coating on distal shaft
- Dual-profile shaft: 2.4F distal / 2.0F taper
- Polyolefin strain relief at hub
- Acrylic (acrylated urethane) adhesive bond construction
- Supplied sterile (EO sterilization), single use only

## Indications

Indicated for selective placement of devices and/or fluids, such as contrast media, into the peripheral, coronary, and neuro vasculature during diagnostic and/or therapeutic procedures. Primary clinical use is as the delivery microcatheter for the Trevo stent-retriever system in acute ischemic stroke thrombectomy.

## Compatible With

- Guide catheters with minimum inner diameter of 0.046" (bench-validated; prior generation required 0.058" ID)
- Appropriately sized steerable guidewires for navigation to target
- Trevo stent-retriever system (primary intended use)

## Use Notes

- Advance over an appropriately sized guidewire under fluoroscopy to desired target location
- RHV with side-arm adapter is included; attach to continuous saline flush to maintain hydrophilic coating
- Single use only; do not resterilize or reprocess
- Active recalls on file (as of data extraction): (1) devices with out-of-US IFU and packaging labels were shipped to US sites in error; (2) the IFU contains an intended use statement not evaluated by FDA -- confirm labeling version before use
- Shelf life is 6 months (shorter than prior predicate generation at 2 years); check expiration before use

## Key Differences vs Competitors

- **Primary Intended Use**: The Trevo Trak 21 is specifically designed as the delivery microcatheter for the Trevo stent-retriever thrombectomy system in acute ischemic stroke. In contrast, the Excelsior family (SL-10, XT-17, XT-27) and Broadway 8 are indicated for general delivery of diagnostic/therapeutic agents (e.g., contrast, coils) or device guidance, not specifically for a dedicated thrombectomy system.
- **Inner Diameter & Compatibility**: The Trevo Trak 21 has a 0.021" inner diameter, making it compatible with the Trevo stent-retriever. The Excelsior XT-27 has a 0.027" ID for compatible stents, the Excelsior SL-10 has a not specified ID, and the AXS Lift, Echo, Broadway 8, Excelsior XT-17 have not specified inner diameters.
- **Shaft Design & Sizing**: The Trevo Trak 21 features a dual-profile tapered shaft (2.4F distal / 2.0F taper) and a 0.021" ID. The Excelsior SL-10 has a 1.7F/0.031" OD profile, the Excelsior XT-17 and XT-27 sizes are not specified beyond length, and the AXS Lift, Echo, and Broadway 8 have no sizing details provided.
- **Recall Status**: The Trevo Trak 21, Broadway 8, and Echo Intracranial Base Catheter all have active FDA recalls related to labeling/IFU issues. The Excelsior family and AXS Lift have no recall information provided in the data.
- **Guide Catheter Requirement**: The Trevo Trak 21 requires a minimum guide catheter inner diameter of 0.046", which is a specified compatibility detail. This requirement is not specified for the AXS Lift, Broadway 8, Echo, or Excelsior family devices.
