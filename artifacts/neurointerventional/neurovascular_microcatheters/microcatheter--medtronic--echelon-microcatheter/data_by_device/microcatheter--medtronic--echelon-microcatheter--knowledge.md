# Echelon Microcatheter

**Manufacturer:** Medtronic
**Category:** Microcatheter

## What It Is

Single-lumen microcatheter designed for introduction over a steerable guidewire into vasculature during neurovascular procedures. Nitinol braid technology with progressive picks-per-inch. Graduated zones and transition points for balance between pushability and softness. DMSO compatible for liquid embolic delivery.

## Sizing/Specs

Models: Echelon 10 (low profile 1.7F distal OD), Echelon 14 (0.017 inch ID); Large 0.017 inch ID; Proximal fluoro-safe marker band; Steam-shapeable flexible tip with dual marker bands; Working length 150 cm

## Indications

Introduction of interventional devices and infusion of diagnostic or therapeutic agents during neurovascular procedures. DMSO-compatible for Onyx liquid embolic delivery.

## Compatible With

Onyx Liquid Embolic System (DMSO compatible), Pipeline Flex, Solitaire X, X-Pedion hydrophilic guidewire

## Use Notes

Nitinol braid provides kink resistance and minimal ovalization for reliable device delivery. Echelon 10 has low profile 1.7F distal OD for accessing small vessels.

## Key Differences vs Competitors

- **Sizing/ID vs. Phenom:** Echelon 14 has 0.017 inch ID, similar to Phenom 17 (0.017 inch ID), but Phenom offers larger ID variants (0.021 inch, 0.027 inch) for stent/flow diverter deployment, which Echelon does not have.
- **Construction vs. Marksman:** Echelon uses nitinol braid technology, while Marksman uses stainless steel braid design; both are noted for resisting ovalization in tortuous anatomy.
- **Compatibility vs. Excelsior Family:** Echelon is specifically DMSO-compatible for Onyx liquid embolic delivery and compatible with Pipeline Flex and Solitaire X; Excelsior SL-10 and XT-17 indications list coil and contrast delivery, with compatibility for occlusion coils and contrast media, but Onyx/Pipeline/Solitaire compatibility is not specified.
- **Profile vs. Echelon 10:** Within its own line, the target Echelon 10 has a low profile 1.7F distal OD for accessing small vessels, a detail not specified for other devices like Marksman or Phenom.
- **Max Infusion Pressure:** Echelon's max infusion pressure is not specified, whereas the Echelon competitor details note a max of 600 PSI (4137 kPa) for infusion, risking rupture above that.

## Also Known As

Echelon 10, Echelon 14
