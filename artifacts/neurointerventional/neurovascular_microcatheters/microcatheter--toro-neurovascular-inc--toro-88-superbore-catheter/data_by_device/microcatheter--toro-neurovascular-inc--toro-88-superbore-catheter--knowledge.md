# Toro 88 Superbore Catheter

**Manufacturer:** Toro Neurovascular, Inc.
**Category:** Microcatheter

## What It Is

Variable stiffness, single-lumen large-bore catheter designed to introduce interventional devices into the neuro, peripheral, and coronary vasculature. The Toro 88 functions as a large distal platform (LDP) or distal access catheter, providing a high inner diameter working channel (0.088") at the distal tip position for delivery of thrombectomy devices, stents, and other large-profile interventional tools.

The shaft is reinforced with stainless steel and nitinol wires embedded in polymers of varying durometers to achieve a graduated stiffness profile: stiff proximally for pushability, progressively softer distally for atraumatic navigation. An internal lubricious liner facilitates advancement over a steerable guidewire or microcatheter. A hydrophilic coating on the distal shaft exterior reduces friction during manipulation. A single radiopaque tip marker enables fluoroscopic visualization. The proximal end includes a Luer-lock hub. Packaged with a split introducer sheath.

Key construction features:
- Distal tip: straight edge, soft and flexible, atraumatic (differs from beveled-tip predicate)
- Shaft reinforcement: stainless steel and nitinol wires in multi-durometer polymers
- Inner liner: lubricious for device delivery
- Hydrophilic coating on distal exterior
- Single radiopaque tip marker (platinum/iridium)
- Supplied sterile (EO sterilization), single use only
- Shelf life: 12 months

The Toro 88 cleared 510(k) K252297 (filed January 2026) as substantially equivalent to the Imperative Care TracStar LDP Large Distal Platform and Zoom 88 Large Distal Platform (K240948).

## Indications

Indicated for the introduction of interventional devices into the peripheral, coronary, and neuro vasculature. Primary clinical context is as a large distal platform for neurointerventional procedures including acute stroke thrombectomy.

## Compatible With

- Steerable guidewires and microcatheters of appropriate outer diameter for intraluminal advancement (0.088" working channel)
- Compatible thrombectomy and interventional devices requiring 0.088" ID delivery

## Use Notes

- The straight distal tip design differentiates the Toro 88 from beveled-tip competitors such as the TracStar LDP; the straight edge may affect seating behavior at vessel origins
- Split introducer sheath is included (vs. RHV supplied with predicate devices); confirm flush/hemostasis setup before use
- Shelf life is 12 months (shorter than the 36-month predicate); check expiration before use
- Low-confidence record (0.5); cleared January 2026 (very recent); manufacturer IFU should be consulted for full procedural guidance
- Single use only; do not resterilize or reprocess

## Key Differences vs Competitors

The Toro 88 competes directly with the Imperative Care TracStar LDP and Zoom 88 Large Distal Platform, and with similar 0.088" large distal platform catheters from other manufacturers:

- **Tip design:** Toro 88 has a straight distal edge; TracStar LDP has a beveled distal edge. Clinical significance of this difference is not established in current sources.
- **Length:** Toro 88 is 118 cm effective length, longer than the Zoom 88 LDP (110 cm) and TracStar LDP (80-105 cm); may offer reach advantage in tall patients or tortuous anatomy.
- **Proximal OD:** Toro 88 proximal OD is 0.108"; TracStar LDP is 0.110" -- marginally smaller.
- **Accessory:** Toro 88 ships with a split introducer sheath rather than a rotating hemostasis valve; workflow may differ.
- **Shelf life:** 12 months vs. 36 months for predicate devices.
