# Marksman Microcatheter

**Manufacturer:** Medtronic (originally Chestnut Medical Technologies / ev3)
**510(k):** K111490, cleared September 16, 2011 (Special 510(k))
**Classification:** Continuous Flush Catheter, 21 CFR 870.1210, Class II, Product Code KRA

---

## What It Is

Variable-stiffness, single-lumen microcatheter designed to access small, tortuous vascular anatomy. The Marksman is a workhorse catheter in neurointerventional practice, widely used as the delivery catheter for the Pipeline Embolization Device (flow diverter). Its PTFE inner liner and hydrophilic outer coating provide low-friction navigation and smooth device passage.

Key features:
- Variable stiffness shaft
- Hydrophilic outer coating (Biocoat) on distal segment for lubricity
- PTFE inner liner to facilitate device passage
- Radiopaque marker at distal tip
- Steam-shapeable distal tip
- Available in various working lengths
- Single use only

---

## Specs

- **Shaft:** Variable stiffness, single lumen
- **Inner liner:** PTFE
- **Outer coating:** Hydrophilic (Biocoat)
- **Tip marker:** Single radiopaque marker at distal tip
- **Tip:** Shapeable
- **Sterilization:** Ethylene Oxide (ISO 11135)

Specific ID/OD dimensions are not provided in this 510(k) summary. The Marksman is commonly known in practice as a 0.027" ID / 2.8F OD catheter (these figures come from commercial labeling, not this 510(k) document).

---

## Compatible With

- Interventional devices delivered through the lumen (flow diverters, stents, coils)
- Guidewires compatible with the lumen
- Diagnostic and therapeutic agents
- Contrast media

The Marksman is the standard delivery catheter for the Pipeline Embolization Device and is also used for stent delivery (e.g., Solitaire, LVIS).

---

## Indications

Introduction of interventional devices and infusion of diagnostic or therapeutic agents into the neuro, peripheral, and coronary vasculature.

---

## Use Notes

- The PTFE liner is specifically designed to reduce friction when advancing devices (flow diverters, stents) through the lumen
- Steam-shape the tip to match the target vessel anatomy before introducing
- Hydrophilic coating requires continuous saline flush to maintain lubricity
- The Biocoat hydrophilic coating is the same technology used on the ev3 Rebar Micro Catheter
- Single use only; do not reprocess
