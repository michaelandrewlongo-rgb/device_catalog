# Socrates 38 Catheter

**Manufacturer:** Scientia Vascular Inc.
**Category:** Microcatheter
**510(k):** K242301 (December 4, 2024)
**Classification:** Catheter, Percutaneous, Neurovasculature; Class II; 21 CFR 870.1250

---

## What It Is

Single-lumen, variable-stiffness, open-ended catheter designed to track through a guide catheter over a steerable guidewire and microcatheter to access the peripheral and neuro vasculature. The Socrates 38 is a distal access / intermediate catheter positioned between a guiding catheter proximally and a microcatheter distally.

Key design features:
- Shaft construction: PEBAX, polyurethane, nitinol, and PTFE lubricious inner liner
- Polycarbonate hub with luer lock; stainless-steel strain relief
- Hydrophilic coating on distal 90 cm of exterior shaft to reduce friction during manipulation in tortuous anatomy
- Single tantalum radiopaque tip marker for fluoroscopic visualization
- Straight tip configuration
- Sterilized with ethylene oxide (EO); single use only
- Packaged with a rotating hemostasis valve (RHV)

The Socrates 38 shares the same design, components, materials, and manufacturing process as the Socrates 38 Aspiration Catheter (K223913). The difference between the two clearances is intended use: the K242301 version is cleared for vascular access (microcatheter/diagnostic agent delivery), not aspiration/revascularization.

## Indications

The Socrates 38 Catheter is indicated for use in facilitating the insertion and guidance of an appropriate microcatheter or diagnostic agents into a selected blood vessel in the peripheral or neuro vasculature.

**Not intended for use in the coronary vasculature.**

---

## Contraindications

Not indicated for use in the coronary vasculature.

---

## Use Notes

- Advance over a steerable guidewire and coaxial microcatheter; do not advance against resistance without fluoroscopic confirmation
- Hydrophilic coating requires hydration; maintain moisture to preserve lubricity throughout procedure
- Single use only; do not reprocess or resterilize
- An active FDA recall exists for this device (as of data extraction date) related to a manufacturing non-conformance that resulted in channels in the packaging pouch seal, creating a potential breach in the sterile barrier. Verify lot-specific recall status before use.
- MRI safety information not available from source; verify with manufacturer prior to use in MR environment

## Key Differences vs Competitors

- **Size and lumen**: Socrates 38 has a 0.038" inner diameter, smaller than large-bore access catheters like the 87 NeuGlide (0.087" ID), 93 NeuFlex (0.093" ID), and Benchmark systems (0.071" to 0.096" ID), positioning it as a distal/intermediate catheter rather than a primary guide catheter.
- **Intended use and positioning**: Unlike the Plato 17 Microcatheter (same manufacturer) which is intended for therapeutic device introduction and embolization material delivery, the Socrates 38 is specifically cleared for vascular access and microcatheter/diagnostic agent delivery only, not for aspiration/revascularization.
- **Design features**: The Socrates 38 has a 90 cm hydrophilic coating on the distal shaft for friction reduction in tortuous anatomy, which is longer than the Benchmark BMX81's 18 cm coating, and includes a single tantalum tip marker versus multiple marker configurations available in some Benchmark systems.
- **Recall status**: Both the Socrates 38 and Plato 17 have active FDA recalls related to packaging seal issues affecting sterile barrier integrity, while recall status for other listed devices is not specified.
- **Vascular territory restrictions**: The Socrates 38 is specifically not intended for use in the coronary vasculature, whereas devices like the 87 NeuGlide, 93 NeuFlex, and Benchmark systems are indicated for peripheral, coronary, and neuro vasculature.
