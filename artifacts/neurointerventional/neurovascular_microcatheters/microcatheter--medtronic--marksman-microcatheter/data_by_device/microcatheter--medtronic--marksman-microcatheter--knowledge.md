# Marksman Microcatheter

**Manufacturer:** Medtronic
**Category:** Microcatheter

## What It Is

Microcatheter intended for introduction of interventional devices and infusion of diagnostic or therapeutic agents. Stainless steel braid design with long length for use with distal access catheters. Coil and braid technologies resist ovalization in tortuous anatomy. Pebax outer jacket with four layers optimizes proximal support and distal flexibility.

## Sizing/Specs

PTFE-lined lumen; Hydrophilic coating; Finely shapeable tip; Radiopaque marker

## Indications

Introduction of interventional devices and infusion of diagnostic or therapeutic agents in neurovascular procedures.

## Compatible With

Solitaire X (mechanical thrombectomy support), Pipeline Flex with Shield Technology (flow diverter delivery), X-Pedion hydrophilic guidewire, distal access catheters

## Use Notes

Designed for support and ease of use in Solitaire X mechanical thrombectomy procedures. Resists ovalization in tortuous anatomy.

## Key Differences vs Competitors

- **Primary Design Focus**: Marksman is specifically designed for support and ease of use in Solitaire X mechanical thrombectomy procedures and resists ovalization in tortuous anatomy. Echelon is designed for aneurysm coiling and DMSO-compatible liquid embolic delivery. Phenom is a comprehensive device deployment catheter with specific models for coiling (Phenom 17), stent/flow diverter deployment (Phenom 21), and large device deployment (Phenom 27). Excelsior family (e.g., SL-10, XT-17) is designed for coil embolization and contrast delivery.
- **Key Construction/Technology**: Marksman uses a stainless steel braid design with coil and braid technologies to resist ovalization and a Pebax outer jacket. Echelon uses a nitinol-braided design with progressive picks-per-inch for graduated zones of pushability and flexibility. Phenom uses a dual-braided proximal shaft and an Elite composite shaft with progressive flexibility. Excelsior uses graded durometer construction with stainless steel wire braid reinforcement.
- **Notable Specifications/Features**: Marksman has a PTFE-lined lumen, hydrophilic coating, finely shapeable tip, and radiopaque marker. Echelon 10 has a low profile 1.7F distal OD, is DMSO-compatible, and has a max infusion pressure of 600 PSI. Phenom models have specific IDs (0.017 in, 0.021 in, 0.027 in) for different device deployments. Excelsior SL-10 has a 1.7F/0.031" OD profile and a steam-shapeable tip.
- **Compatibility Highlights**: Marksman is explicitly noted as compatible with Solitaire X, Pipeline Flex with Shield Technology, X-Pedion guidewire, and distal access catheters. Echelon is compatible with Onyx Liquid Embolic System, Pipeline Flex, Solitaire X, and X-Pedion guidewire. Phenom is compatible with Pipeline Flex, Solitaire X, embolic coils, and intracranial stents. Excelsior SL-10 is compatible with standard guide catheters/guidewires, occlusion coils, and contrast media.
