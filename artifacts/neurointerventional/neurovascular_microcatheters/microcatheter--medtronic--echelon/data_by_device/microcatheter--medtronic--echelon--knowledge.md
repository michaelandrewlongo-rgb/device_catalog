# Echelon Microcatheter

**Manufacturer:** Medtronic (originally ev3 / Micro Therapeutics)
**510(k):** K093750 (multi-device, December 9, 2009); earlier clearances K030688 (Echelon-14), K031992 (Echelon-10), K042187 (tip shape variants), K051990
**Classification:** Continuous Flush Catheter, 21 CFR 870.1210, Class II, Product Code KRA

---

## What It Is

Single-lumen microcatheter for selective catheterization and infusion in the peripheral and neurovasculature. The Echelon is a nitinol-braided microcatheter designed for aneurysm coiling, featuring progressive picks-per-inch braid construction that provides graduated zones of pushability and flexibility. Available in two variants: Echelon 10 (lower profile) and Echelon 14 (more support). DMSO-compatible for use with liquid embolic agents including the Onyx Liquid Embolic System.

Key features:
- Nitinol braid technology with progressive picks-per-inch for balanced pushability and softness
- Graduated stiffness zones with defined transition points
- Steam-shapeable, flexible distal tip
- Dual radiopaque marker bands at distal tip
- Fluoro-safe marker band on shaft
- Hydrophilic outer coating (must keep hydrated)
- Kink resistant with minimal ovalization to maintain lumen integrity
- Available in straight, 45-degree, and 90-degree pre-shaped tip configurations
- DMSO-compatible construction for liquid embolic delivery

## Compatible With

- 0.014" or smaller guidewires (e.g., X-Pedion hydrophilic guidewire)
- Occlusion coils compatible with 0.017" ID
- Embolization materials
- Contrast media
- Onyx Liquid Embolic System (DMSO-compatible; verified for liquid embolic delivery)
- Guide catheters accommodating the 2.1F (Echelon 10) or 2.4F (Echelon 14) OD
- Used as delivery microcatheter in conjunction with Pipeline Flex Embolization Device and Solitaire X Revascularization Device procedures

---

## Indications

Access peripheral and neuro vasculature for introduction of interventional devices and controlled selective infusion of physician-specified therapeutic agents (embolization materials, including DMSO-based liquid embolics) and diagnostic materials (contrast media).

**Contraindicated:**
- Not intended for use in the coronary vasculature
- Contraindicated for neonatal and pediatric use
- Contraindicated when the procedure may compromise the patient's condition

---

## Use Notes

- **Max infusion pressure: 600 PSI (4137 kPa).** Pressures above 600 PSI risk catheter rupture.
- Keep hydrophilic coating hydrated by attaching Y-connector to continuous saline drip
- Manipulate only under fluoroscopy when in the body
- Do not move the catheter without observing tip response on fluoroscopy
- Because the catheter navigates easily into narrow vessels, periodically verify it has not advanced so far as to interfere with removal (slightly withdraw to check)
- If flow becomes restricted, do NOT attempt to clear with high-pressure infusion -- remove and replace
- Never advance or withdraw an intraluminal device against resistance until cause is identified by fluoroscopy
- Ensure embolic material compatibility before use
- Single use only; do not reprocess or re-sterilize

### Choosing Between Echelon 10 and Echelon 14

- **Echelon 10 (2.1 > 1.7F OD):** Lower profile for more distal access in small vessels. Same 0.017" ID and 0.014" guidewire compatibility as the 14.
- **Echelon 14 (2.4 > 1.9F OD):** More proximal support and pushability. Use when you need more backbone to reach the target. Same lumen and guidewire specs.

### Potential Complications

Per safety labeling: hematoma at entry site, vessel perforation, vessel spasm, hemorrhage, pain and tenderness, vascular thrombosis, thrombolytic episodes, neurological deficits including stroke and death, ischemia.
