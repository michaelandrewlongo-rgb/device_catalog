# Excelsior SL-10 Microcatheter

**Manufacturer:** Stryker (originally Boston Scientific Target)
**510(k):** K013789, cleared December 6, 2001 (Special 510(k) device modification)
**Classification:** Percutaneous Catheter, 21 CFR 870.1250, Class II

---

## What It Is

Single-lumen microcatheter for navigating distal vasculature over a guide catheter and guidewire. The SL-10 is one of the most widely used neurovascular microcatheters for coil embolization. It uses graded durometer construction -- flexible at the tip, semi-rigid proximally -- with stainless steel wire reinforcement for torque transmission and kink resistance. Hydrophilic outer coating reduces friction during navigation. Ships with a steam shaping mandrel for tip customization.

Key features:
- 1.7 French (predicate) / 2 French design variants
- One or two radiopaque tip markers for fluoroscopic visualization
- Luer fitting hub for accessory attachment
- Hydrophilic outer coating
- Steam-shapeable distal tip
- Strain reliefs at proximal hub for kink resistance

---

## Compatible With

- Standard guide catheters and guidewires compatible with the 1.7F / 0.031" OD profile
- Occlusion coils delivered through the lumen
- Contrast media for diagnostic angiography
- Steam shaping mandrel (included)

---

## Indications

Delivery of diagnostic agents (contrast media) and therapeutic agents (occlusion coils) into the peripheral, coronary, and neurovasculature.

---

## Use Notes

- Tip is steam-shapeable using the included mandrel for custom curves to match target anatomy
- Hydrophilic coating must be kept hydrated for lubricity -- attach Y-connector to continuous saline drip
- Predicate equivalence to Tracker-10 and Excelsior 1018 microcatheters established
- Single use only; do not reprocess
