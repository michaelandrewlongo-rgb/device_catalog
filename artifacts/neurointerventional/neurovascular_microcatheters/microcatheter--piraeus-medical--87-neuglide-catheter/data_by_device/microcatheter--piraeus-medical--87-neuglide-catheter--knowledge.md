## What It Is

Single-lumen guide catheter for vascular access in the peripheral, coronary, and neuro vasculature. The 87 NeuGlide is a large-bore neurointerventional access catheter with a 0.087" inner diameter (6F-compatible), designed for intermediate-to-distal access in the neurovasculature.

Key design features:
- Hollow cylindrical tube bonded proximally to a standard luer fitting
- Shaft wall constructed from metal coils/braids and medical-grade polymers
- Hydrophilic coating on distal shaft to enhance tracking through tortuous vasculature
- Soft atraumatic straight distal tip to facilitate smooth navigation past vessel branch points
- Single radiopaque distal tip marker for fluoroscopic confirmation of tip position
- Compatible with 0.035" or smaller guidewires
- Packaged with a loading tool to facilitate insertion into a short introducer sheath
- Sterilized with ethylene oxide (EO), SAL 10^-6; single use only

The 87 NeuGlide is the predicate for the 93 NeuFlex Catheter (K251044). The two catheters share identical technological characteristics except for lumen and outer diameter -- the NeuGlide provides a slightly smaller profile (0.087" ID / 0.100" OD vs 0.093" ID / 0.110" OD). The predicate for the NeuGlide was the Imperative Care TracStar Large Distal Platform (K203764).

## Indications

The 87 NeuGlide Catheter is indicated for the introduction of interventional devices into the peripheral, coronary, and neuro vasculature.

---

## Use Notes

- Use with support/diagnostic catheters to assist accessing target vasculature
- Advance under fluoroscopy using standard percutaneous interventional technique
- Required accessory devices (not supplied): guidewires, support/diagnostic catheters, introducer sheaths, rotating hemostasis valves (RHVs)
- Loading tool is packaged with the device to aid insertion into a short sheath
- MRI safety information not included in labeling; verify with manufacturer prior to MR environment use
- Single use only; pre-sterilized
- 6-month shelf life (verified by accelerated aging per ASTM F1980)

## Key Differences vs Competitors

- **Size profile**: The 87 NeuGlide (0.087" ID / 0.100" OD) is slightly smaller than its sibling 93 NeuFlex (0.093" ID / 0.110" OD) but larger than the Benchmark BMX81 (0.081" ID) and Benchmark Intracranial Access System (0.071" ID). The Broadway 8 Catheter and Benchmark BMX96 Access System have larger inner diameters (0.096" and 0.096" respectively), but the Broadway 8 has an active recall.
- **System design**: Unlike the multi-component Benchmark systems (BMX81, BMX96, Intracranial Access System) which include delivery catheters, select catheters, and dilators, the 87 NeuGlide is a single-lumen guide catheter designed for use with separate support/diagnostic catheters and accessories.
- **Indications**: The 87 NeuGlide is indicated for peripheral, coronary, and neuro vasculature, similar to most competitors. However, the Echelon Microcatheter is specifically contraindicated for coronary and pediatric use and is designed for infusion of embolic materials, unlike the guide/access catheters.
- **Guidewire compatibility**: The 87 NeuGlide is compatible with 0.035" or smaller guidewires, similar to the 93 NeuFlex. The Benchmark BMX81 and BMX96 systems are compatible with 0.038" or smaller guidewires, while the Echelon Microcatheter requires 0.014" or smaller guidewires.
- **Sheath compatibility**: The 87 NeuGlide requires a 7F introducer sheath, while the 93 NeuFlex requires an 8F sheath. Sheath requirements for other devices are not specified.
