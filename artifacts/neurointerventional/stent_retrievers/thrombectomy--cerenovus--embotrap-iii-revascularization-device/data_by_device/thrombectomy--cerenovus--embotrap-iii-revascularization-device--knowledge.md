# EMBOTRAP III Revascularization Device

**Manufacturer:** Cerenovus (J&J)
**Category:** Thrombectomy device

## What It Is

Stent retriever for mechanical thrombectomy with innovative dual-layer design. Inner channel secures and stabilizes the clot to maintain engagement while allowing reperfusion. Articulating outer cage maintains wall apposition while engaging and gripping the clot. Distal closed end mesh maintains clot control during retrieval.

## Sizing/Specs

Vessel Size Range: 1.5-6.5 mm; Device Lengths: 22 mm, 37 mm, 45 mm; Working Length: 194 cm; Product codes: ET309522 (5x22), ET309537 (5x37), ET309645 (6x45)

## Indications

Indicated for neurovascular revascularization in acute ischemic stroke. Vessel indication of 1.5mm to 6.5mm.

## Compatible With

Prowler EX Microcatheter, EMBOGUARD Balloon Guide Catheter, CEREBASE DA Guide Sheath, CEREGLIDE 71 Intermediate Catheter (CERENOVUS Stroke Solutions system)

## Use Notes

Distal mesh cell sizes of EMBOTRAP III 5x22mm and 5x37mm are 25% smaller than EMBOTRAP II. MASTRO I is a living systematic review and meta-analysis comparing EmboTrap, Solitaire, and Trevo stent retrievers for mechanical thrombectomy in acute ischemic stroke.

## Key Differences vs Competitors

- **Dual-layer design vs. single-layer competitors**: EMBOTRAP III features an inner channel for clot stabilization and an articulating outer cage for wall apposition, while Solitaire X uses a parametric overlap design and Trevo NXT ProVue uses a tapered nitinol core wire with a laser-cut section. Penumbra System operates on a direct aspiration principle, not a stent retriever design.
- **Vessel size range**: EMBOTRAP III covers 1.5-6.5 mm, Solitaire X covers 1.5-5.5 mm, Trevo NXT ProVue vessel size range not specified, and Penumbra System is indicated for specific intracranial arteries (internal carotid, M1/M2, basilar, vertebral).
- **Device length options**: EMBOTRAP III offers 22 mm, 37 mm, and 45 mm lengths; Solitaire X offers 20 mm, 24 mm, and 40 mm usable lengths; Trevo NXT ProVue offers 32 mm, 28 mm, 41 mm, and 37 mm retriever sizes with shaped section lengths of 32-44 mm.
- **Compatibility ecosystem**: EMBOTRAP III is designed for the Cerenovus Stroke Solutions system (Prowler EX, EMBOGUARD, CEREBASE DA, CEREGLIDE 71), Solitaire X is compatible with Medtronic components (Phenom catheters, React catheters, APRO catheters), and Trevo NXT ProVue is compatible with Trevo and Excelsior catheters. Penumbra System uses its own reperfusion catheters and separators.
- **Clinical evidence context**: EMBOTRAP III is referenced in the MASTRO I systematic review comparing it with Solitaire and Trevo; Solitaire X is noted as the "most-published stent retriever" with over 200 publications; Trevo NXT ProVue and Penumbra System clinical evidence base not specified.
