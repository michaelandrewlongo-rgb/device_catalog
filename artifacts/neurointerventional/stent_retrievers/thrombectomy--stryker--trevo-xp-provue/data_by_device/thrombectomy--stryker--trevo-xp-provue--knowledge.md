## Key Differences vs Competitors

- **Working length**: Trevo XP (180-190 cm depending on size) is shorter than EMBOTRAP III (194 cm) and Trevo NXT ProVue (200 cm), but longer than Solitaire X (usable lengths 20-40 mm, working length not specified).
- **Sheath compatibility**: Trevo XP requires 8F or 9F balloon guide catheters, while EMBOTRAP III is designed for use with the Cerenovus Stroke Solutions ecosystem (including EMBOGUARD Balloon Guide Catheter) and Solitaire X is compatible with various aspiration catheters and microcatheters.
- **Indication limitations**: Trevo XP has specific limitations for large core infarcts (ASPECTS ≤7) and posterior circulation occlusions, whereas EMBOTRAP III and Solitaire X have vessel diameter indications (1.5-6.5 mm and 1.5-5.5 mm respectively) without the same specified limitations.
- **Device design approach**: Trevo XP is a stent retriever with variable working lengths by size, while EMBOTRAP III features a dual-layer design for clot stabilization, Solitaire X uses a parametric overlap design for dynamic expansion/compression, and the Penumbra System operates on direct aspiration principles rather than stent retrieval.
