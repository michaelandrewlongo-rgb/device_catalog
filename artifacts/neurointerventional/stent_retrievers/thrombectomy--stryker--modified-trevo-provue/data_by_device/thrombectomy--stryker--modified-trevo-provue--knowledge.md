# Modified Trevo ProVue Retriever

**Manufacturer:** Stryker Neurovascular
**Category:** Thrombectomy Device

FDA 510(k) K132641 | Cleared January 13, 2014 | Class II

## What It Is

Self-expanding stent retriever for mechanical thrombectomy in acute ischemic stroke. The device consists of a flexible, tapered nitinol core wire with a laser-cut nitinol shaped section at the distal end. A radiopaque platinum coil at the distal tip and a radiopaque shaped section provide full-length fluoroscopic visualization under fluoroscopy. The hydrophilic coating reduces friction during delivery. A torque device and insertion tool are included. The proximal end is compatible with the Abbott guidewire extension to allow catheter exchange while maintaining retriever position in the vessel.

The "Modified" designation refers to a modification of the platinum wire weave in the shaped section relative to the cleared Trevo ProVue (K122478), with no change to design, materials, indications, or function.

## Indications

Intended to restore blood flow in the neurovasculature by removing thrombus in patients experiencing ischemic stroke within 8 hours of symptom onset. Patients who are ineligible for intravenous tissue plasminogen activator (IV t-PA) or who fail IV t-PA therapy are candidates for treatment.

## Use Notes

Mechanical thrombectomy via stent retriever technique. The device features 360 degrees of consistently large cells, a soft distal tip, and full-length radiopacity to facilitate clot integration and fluoroscopic monitoring during retrieval. Compatible with Abbott guidewire extension for catheter exchange without loss of position.

Sterilized with 100% ethylene oxide (EtO). Single use only.

## Key Differences vs Competitors

- **Modification vs. Standard Models**: The 'Modified Trevo ProVue Retriever' is specified as having a modified platinum wire weave in its shaped section compared to the cleared Trevo ProVue (K122478), with no change to design, materials, indications, or function. This distinguishes it from other Trevo models (NXT ProVue, NXT, XP) where such a modification is not specified.
- **Indications Time Window**: The target device is indicated for use within 8 hours of symptom onset. This matches the 'extended' indication for the Trevo NXT ProVue Retriever and the indications for the ERIC Retrieval Device and Penumbra System, but differs from the Trevo NXT ProVue's 'standard' indication which starts within 6 hours.
- **Device Design & Mechanism**: Unlike the EMBOTRAP III's specified dual-layer design (inner channel and outer cage) or the ERIC device's design with retrieval spheres on a pusher wire, the target device is a standard self-expanding stent retriever with a nitinol shaped section and full-length radiopacity.
- **Working Length**: The target device has a working length of 200 cm. This is longer than the Solitaire 4's specified 180 cm and the EMBOTRAP III's 194 cm, but matches the Trevo NXT ProVue's overall length of 200 cm (its working length is not specified).
- **Sheath Compatibility**: The target device is compatible with 8 or 9 Fr balloon guide catheters. This matches the compatibility specified for the Trevo XP and Solitaire 4 devices. Compatibility for other devices (e.g., EMBOTRAP III, ERIC, Penumbra System) is either not specified or described in terms of specific system components.
