# ASAHI CHIKAI Neurovascular Guide Wire (Round Curve)

**Manufacturer:** Asahi Intecc Co., Ltd.
**Category:** Neurovascular Guidewire

## What It Is

Single-lumen steerable neurovascular guidewire with a round curve distal tip configuration. The shaft consists of a stainless steel core wire encased in a dual-coil assembly: an inner coil and outer coil (platinum-nickel and stainless steel), soldered to the core. The outer coil is radiopaque. The distal 30 cm of the outer coil has an external hydrophilic coating for low-friction navigation. On the 300 cm length, the proximal shaft is coated with PTFE. The round curve tip is designed to conform passively to vessel curvature rather than requiring active steering, distinguishing it from the straight-tip CHIKAI predicate. Maximum shaft diameter is 0.014 inches (0.36 mm).

## Indications

Intended for use in the neurovasculature to facilitate placement and exchange of therapeutic devices such as cerebral catheters during intravascular therapy. Indicated for use only in the neurovasculature.

## Use Notes

- The round curve tip is passive: it is designed to deflect and follow vessel curves without requiring active torque shaping.
- Keep the hydrophilic distal coating hydrated throughout the procedure.
- The 200 cm version uses an external extension wire connected at the proximal end for over-the-wire exchanges.
- Single use only.

## Key Differences vs Competitors

- **Distal tip design**: The ASAHI CHIKAI Neurovascular Guide Wire (Round Curve) features a passive round curve tip designed to conform to vessel curvature without active steering, unlike the straight-tip ASAHI CHIKAI 10 (which requires manual shaping) and the CHIKAI Nexus petit (which offers multiple tip shapes including round-curve).
- **Diameter and access**: At 0.014 inches maximum diameter, it is larger than the ASAHI CHIKAI 10 (0.010 inches) and CHIKAI Nexus petit (0.013 inches), making it less suitable for very distal small vessels like M2/M3 branches, but similar in diameter to the CHIKAI Nexus 014 and TRAXCESS 14 GUIDEWIRE.
- **Core construction**: Uses a stainless steel core wire with dual-coil assembly, differing from the CHIKAI Nexus petit's hybrid Nitinol/stainless steel core and the Traxcess .007 Mini Guidewire's Nitinol distal core, which offer different flexibility and torque control properties.
- **Indications specificity**: Indicated exclusively for neurovasculature use, unlike the Hybrid, Synchro 2 Guidewire, Traxcess .007 Mini Guidewire, and TRAXCESS 14 GUIDEWIRE, which are indicated for both neuro and peripheral vasculature.
- **Safety profile**: No active FDA recalls mentioned, unlike the Synchro 2 Guidewire, Traxcess .007 Mini Guidewire, and TRAXCESS 14 GUIDEWIRE which have active recalls for coating issues.
