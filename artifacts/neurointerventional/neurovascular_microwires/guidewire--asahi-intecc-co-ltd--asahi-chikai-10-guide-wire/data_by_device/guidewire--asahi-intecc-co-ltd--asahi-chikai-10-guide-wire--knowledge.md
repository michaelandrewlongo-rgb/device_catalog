# ASAHI CHIKAI 10 Neurovascular Guide Wire

**Manufacturer:** Asahi Intecc Co., Ltd.
**Category:** Neurovascular Guidewire

## What It Is

Single-lumen steerable neurovascular guidewire in the CHIKAI family, distinguished by a reduced 0.010 inch (0.26 mm) shaft diameter compared to the standard CHIKAI (0.014 inch). The shaft is constructed from a stainless steel core wire with a dual-coil assembly of platinum-nickel and stainless steel, soldered to the core. The distal tip is radiopaque and available in a straight configuration that can be manually shaped to conform to vessel anatomy. A hydrophilic coating covers the distal segment for low-friction advancement. On the 300 cm length, the proximal shaft is PTFE-coated. The 200 cm version includes an external extension wire for over-the-wire exchanges.

The reduced diameter (0.010 inch vs. 0.014 inch) is intended to access smaller or more distal intracranial vessels where a standard 0.014 inch wire would be oversized.

## Indications

Intended for use in the neurovasculature to facilitate placement and exchange of therapeutic devices such as cerebral catheters during intravascular therapy. Indicated for use only in the neurovasculature.

## Use Notes

- The 0.010 inch diameter is appropriate for more distal intracranial access than the standard 0.014 inch CHIKAI, particularly for M2/M3 or posterior circulation vessels with small lumens.
- Tip can be manually shaped prior to introduction; straight configuration is the default.
- Keep the hydrophilic distal coating hydrated throughout the procedure.
- The 200 cm version uses an external extension wire at the proximal end.
- Single use only.
