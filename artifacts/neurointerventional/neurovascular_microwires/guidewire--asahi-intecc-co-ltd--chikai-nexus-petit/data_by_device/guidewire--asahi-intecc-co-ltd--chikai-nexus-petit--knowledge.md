# CHIKAI Nexus petit

**Manufacturer:** Asahi Intecc Co., Ltd.
**Category:** Neurovascular guidewire

## What It Is

The CHIKAI Nexus petit is a hybrid-core neurovascular guidewire designed for navigation in the neurovasculature during intravascular therapy. It is intended to facilitate placement and exchange of therapeutic devices such as cerebral catheters.

The core construction uses a hybrid taper: a Nitinol-to-stainless steel (SUS) conjoined core wire connected via a Nitinol pipe. This design combines the torque transmission of stainless steel with the kink resistance and deflection control of Nitinol across the wire's working length.

Key structural details:

- **Distal coil:** Platinum-nickel (Pt-Ni) alloy coil soldered with gold-tin (Au-Sn) alloy for radiopacity and flexibility at the atraumatic tip
- **Proximal coil section:** Nitinol tube covering the stainless steel-to-Nitinol junction; inner connection made with Asahi's proprietary stainless-steel coil, soldered with Au-Sn alloy and silver
- **Distal coating:** Polyurethane base layer over the distal coil, with hydrophilic coating applied from the distal tip proximally over the polyurethane layer
- **Shaft coating:** Hydrophilic coating extends from the distal tip to the proximal shaft, reducing friction during microcatheter navigation

The wire is tapered from a maximum nominal OD of 0.33 mm (0.013") at the shaft to 0.16 mm (0.006") at the distal tip. The radiopaque distal tip section extends 8 cm from the tip.

Packaged accessories include a shaping mandrel, torque device, and introducer.

## Use Notes

- The hybrid Ni-Ti/stainless steel core provides a balance of torque control (from the stainless steel segment) and distal flexibility/kink resistance (from the Nitinol segment).
- Three tip-shape options (Straight, Round-curve, J-shape) allow selection based on target vessel anatomy and operator preference. A shaping mandrel is included for custom reshaping if required.
- Hydrophilic coating extends along the full working shaft; maintain wire hydration per IFU to preserve lubricity.
- The torque device (included) attaches to the proximal end to facilitate controlled wire rotation during navigation.
- For neurovascular use only; not indicated in the peripheral vasculature.

## Key Differences vs Competitors

- **Core construction**: CHIKAI Nexus petit uses a hybrid Nitinol-to-stainless steel conjoined core for torque transmission and kink resistance, while most competitors (ASAHI CHIKAI 10, ASAHI CHIKAI Neurovascular Guide Wire, ASAHI CHIKAI Neurovascular Guide Wire (Round Curve), CHIKAI Nexus 014, Hybrid, Synchro 2 Guidewire) use stainless steel cores, and Traxcess .007 Mini Guidewire uses a distal Nitinol core with proximal stainless steel.
- **Shaft diameter**: At 0.33 mm (0.013"), CHIKAI Nexus petit is thinner than the standard 0.014" (0.36 mm) devices (ASAHI CHIKAI Neurovascular Guide Wire, ASAHI CHIKAI Neurovascular Guide Wire (Round Curve), CHIKAI Nexus 014, TRAXCESS 14 GUIDEWIRE) but thicker than the 0.010" (0.26 mm) ASAHI CHIKAI 10; Traxcess .007 Mini Guidewire diameter is not specified.
- **Indications**: CHIKAI Nexus petit is indicated for neurovasculature use only, whereas Hybrid, Synchro 2 Guidewire, Traxcess .007 Mini Guidewire, and TRAXCESS 14 GUIDEWIRE are indicated for both neuro and peripheral vasculature.
- **Tip shape options**: CHIKAI Nexus petit offers three tip shapes (Straight, Round-curve, J-shape), while ASAHI CHIKAI 10 has a straight tip, ASAHI CHIKAI Neurovascular Guide Wire (Round Curve) has a round curve tip, and tip shapes for other devices are not specified.
- **Recall status**: CHIKAI Nexus petit has no mentioned recalls, while Synchro 2 Guidewire, Traxcess .007 Mini Guidewire, and TRAXCESS 14 GUIDEWIRE have active FDA recalls related to coating issues.
