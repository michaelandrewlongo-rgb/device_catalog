# Transend Guidewires

**Manufacturer:** Stryker Neurovascular
**Category:** Neurovascular Guidewire

## What It Is

The Transend is a family of neurovascular guidewires designed for access and navigation through microcatheters in the neurovasculature. They are used in both hemorrhagic stroke (HEM) and acute ischemic stroke (AIS) procedures.

Key construction features:

- **Scitanium corewire:** Engineered to enhance torque transmission and distal support, allowing tip orientation for precise vessel selection and positioning.
- **Shapeable/reshapeable tip:** 2 cm tapered-ribbon corewire tip permits repeated tip shaping and reshaping at the point of use.
- **ICE hydrophilic coating:** Applied to the distal 39 cm segment (61 cm on the 0.010" variant), intended to facilitate advancement through a microcatheter and tortuous neurovasculature.

Three wire families cover different diameter and length requirements: 0.014" standard (182–205 cm), 0.014" extended/exchange (300 cm), and 0.010" (200 cm).

Note: The Stryker product specifications table spells the product as "Transcend" (with a 'c'); the product page title and URL use "Transend" (no 'c'). Both refer to the same product family.

## Use Notes

- Tip is reshapeable at point of use using the tapered-ribbon corewire tip design (2 cm shapable tip).
- ICE hydrophilic coating (distal 39 cm for 0.014"; distal 61 cm for 0.010") reduces friction during navigation through microcatheters and tortuous anatomy.
- Scitanium corewire designed for torque transmission — use tip orientation for vessel selection.
- 300 cm variants (Extra Support, Floppy) are intended for exchange procedures.
- IFU reference: AP001666 v2.0 (January 2025).

## Key Differences vs Competitors

- **Diameter options**: Transend offers 0.014" and 0.010" diameters, while competitors like Asahi CHIKAI 10 (0.010"), CHIKAI Nexus petit (0.013"), and Microvention Traxcess (.007") provide different micro-diameter options not available in the Transend family.
- **Core material**: Transend uses a proprietary Scitanium corewire for torque transmission, whereas competitors employ various core constructions including stainless steel (Asahi CHIKAI), hybrid Nitinol/stainless steel (CHIKAI Nexus petit), and Nitinol/stainless steel combinations (Traxcess).
- **Coating length**: Transend's ICE hydrophilic coating covers 39 cm (0.014") or 61 cm (0.010") of the distal segment, while coating lengths for most competitors are not specified except for Asahi CHIKAI Neurovascular Guide Wire (Round Curve) with 30 cm outer coil length.
- **Recall status**: Transend has no active recalls mentioned, unlike Stryker Synchro 2 Guidewire and Microvention Traxcess .007 Mini Guidewire which both have active FDA recalls related to coating issues.
- **Tip design flexibility**: Transend features a 2 cm reshapeable tapered-ribbon tip that can be repeatedly shaped at point of use, while some competitors offer pre-shaped configurations (Asahi CHIKAI Round Curve) or multiple tip-shape options (CHIKAI Nexus petit with Straight, Round-curve, J-shape).
