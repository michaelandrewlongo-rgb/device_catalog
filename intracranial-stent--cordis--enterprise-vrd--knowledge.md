# Enterprise Vascular Reconstruction Device (VRD) -- Codman & Shurtleff / Cordis

## What It Is

Self-expanding, closed-cell design intracranial stent used as an adjunct (scaffold) to support coil embolization of wide-necked intracranial aneurysms. The stent is placed across the aneurysm neck in the parent artery to prevent coil herniation into the parent vessel during and after embolization. Approved under Humanitarian Device Exemption (HDE H060001).

## Sizing/Specs

- **Design:** Closed-cell, self-expanding nitinol braid
- **Delivery catheter compatibility:** Designed for delivery through standard neurovascular microcatheters
- **Specific dimensions:** Not available from the provided source PDF. Refer to current IFU for diameter and length options.

## Indications

- Support neurovascular embolization coils for the treatment of wide-necked intracranial aneurysms
- HDE indication (per cross-reference in Surpass SSED): specifically for unruptured wide-necked intracranial aneurysms

## Compatible With

- Standard neurovascular embolic coils (used as adjunct/scaffold)
- Microcatheter-based delivery system
- Specific catheter compatibility: refer to current IFU

## Deployment/Use

- Delivered via microcatheter across aneurysm neck
- Self-expanding design -- deployed by retracting delivery sheath/catheter
- Coils then placed through stent interstices into aneurysm sac
- Requires dual antiplatelet therapy (aspirin + clopidogrel) peri-procedurally

## Complications to Know

- In-stent thrombosis/stenosis
- Thromboembolic events
- Vessel perforation or dissection
- Stent migration
- Requirement for long-term antiplatelet therapy
- Safety and effectiveness not established in patients with intracranial stents and/or coils who then receive Onyx (noted in Onyx HD-500 precautions in the source PDF)
