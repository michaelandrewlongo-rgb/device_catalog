# SHUNTCHECK

**Manufacturer:** NeuroDx Development
**Category:** CSF shunt/diagnostic

## What It Is

ShuntCheck software - Installed on Windows computer; guides user through test procedure, gathers and analyzes thermal data, presents results screen with Flow Confirmed or Flow Not Confirmed plus a time-temperature graph

Indications For Use:
ShuntCheck is an aid to the detection of flow in implanted cerebrospinal fluid (CSF)
shunts. ShuntCheck includes Micro-Pumper, a component which may be used to
temporarily increase CSF flow in suspected non-flowing, patent shunts during the
ShuntCheck test. ShuntCheck cannot alone diagnose CSF shunt function or
malfunction. The clinical diagnosis of CSF shunt function or malfunction, incorporating
the flow information from ShuntCheck, should be made only by a qualified
neurosurgeon.

## Key Differences vs Competitors

- **Device Type**: SHUNTCHECK is a non-invasive diagnostic software system for detecting CSF shunt flow, while all other devices listed are implantable CSF shunt valves or drainage systems (e.g., adjustable valves like proGAV 2.0, M.blue, CERTAS Plus, Strata; fixed gravitational units like ShuntAssistant 2.0; or drainage systems like EVAC).
- **Primary Function**: SHUNTCHECK is an aid to detect flow in implanted shunts and cannot alone diagnose shunt function/malfunction, requiring clinical interpretation by a neurosurgeon. In contrast, competitor devices are therapeutic implants intended to actively shunt or drain CSF (e.g., "shunt cerebrospinal fluid from the lateral ventricles" or "drainage of subdural hematoma").
- **Use Setting**: SHUNTCHECK is used externally with a Windows computer and includes a Micro-Pumper component for temporary flow increase during testing. Competitor devices are surgically implanted (except EVAC, which is for drainage access) and function continuously post-implantation.
- **Regulatory/Recall Status**: SHUNTCHECK has no specified recalls in the provided data. In comparison, Aesculap devices (proGAV 2.0, M.blue) have 6 active FDA recalls each, while recall status is not specified for other competitors like CERTAS Plus, Strata, Miethke devices, or EVAC.
