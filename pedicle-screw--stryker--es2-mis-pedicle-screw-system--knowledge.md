# ES2 MIS Pedicle Screw System

**Manufacturer:** Stryker
**Category:** Pedicle screw system

## What It Is

Low-profile minimally invasive pedicle screw system. Integrated blade design with 15 mm of built-in reduction threads. Ring allows precise rod contouring with secure fixation at various points along the blade. Three cutting flutes facilitate insertion. Cortical cancellous thread pattern matches pedicle anatomy for strong purchase.

## Sizing/Specs

Accepts 5.5 mm and 6.0 mm pre-cut straight and pre-bent titanium rods.

## Indications

MIS thoracolumbar spinal fixation.

## Compatible With

Power and navigation capabilities. Compatible with Spine Guidance 5 and Mako Spine with Copilot features. LITe Pedicle Access System.

## Use Notes

Streamlined instrumentation designed for efficiency in MIS procedures. One system addresses multiple pathologies. Now sold by VB Spine.
