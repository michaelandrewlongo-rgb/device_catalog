# Phenom Microcatheter

**Manufacturer:** Medtronic
**Category:** Microcatheter

## What It Is

Comprehensive device deployment microcatheter for treating hemorrhagic and acute ischemic stroke. Dual-braided proximal shaft, soft rounded distal tip for trackability. Elite composite shaft with progressive flexibility for superior pushability. Inner lumen engineered to prevent ovalization.

## Sizing/Specs

Phenom 17: OD 2.2>1.8 Fr, ID 0.017 inch, length 150 cm, max guidewire 0.014 inch, tip shapes: straight/J/45/90; Phenom 21: OD 2.6>2.3 Fr, ID 0.021 inch, lengths 150-160 cm, max guidewire 0.018 inch; Phenom 27: OD 3.1>2.8 Fr, ID 0.027 inch, length 150 cm, max guidewire 0.025 inch; All have 6 cm soft distal segment and 15 cm flexible single coil segment

## Indications

Device deployment during hemorrhagic and acute ischemic stroke procedures. Phenom 17 for coiling, Phenom 21 for stent/flow diverter deployment, Phenom 27 for large device deployment.

## Compatible With

Pipeline Flex (delivery), Solitaire X (delivery), embolic coils, intracranial stents

## Use Notes

Phenom 17: designed for coiling with low coil delivery force and large ID. Phenom 21: for stent and flow diverter deployment. Phenom 27: for large device deployment with 0.027 inch ID.
