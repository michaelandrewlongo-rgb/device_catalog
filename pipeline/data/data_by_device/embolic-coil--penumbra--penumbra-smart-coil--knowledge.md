# Penumbra Smart Coil

**Manufacturer:** Penumbra
**Category:** Embolic Coil
510(k) K160832

## What It Is

Embolization coil with complex and WAVE shapes, engineered with varying levels of softness

## Sizing/Specs

Materials: Stretch-resistant bare platinum

## Indications

The Smart Coil is indicated for the embolization of:
• Intracranial aneurysms
• Other neurovascular abnormalities such as arteriovenous malformations and arteriovenous fistulae
• Arterial and venous embolizations in the peripheral vasculature

## Use Notes

Method of Detachment: Instant mechanical detachment
Coil Type: Plus standard, standard, soft, extra soft, wave extra soft

[FDA RECALL] 1 active recall(s).
  - The diameter of a component of the device is out of tolerance specification; the Delivery Pusher pull wire which secures the coil in place can prolapse and allow premature detachment of the coil.
