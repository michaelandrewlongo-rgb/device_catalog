# ES2 MIS Pedicle Screw System

**Manufacturer:** Stryker
**Category:** Pedicle screw system

## What It Is

Low-profile minimally invasive pedicle screw system. Integrated blade design with 15 mm of built-in reduction threads. Ring allows precise rod contouring with secure fixation at various points along the blade. Three cutting flutes facilitate insertion. Cortical cancellous thread pattern matches pedicle anatomy for strong purchase.

## Sizing/Specs

Accepts 5.5 mm and 6.0 mm pre-cut straight and pre-bent titanium rods.

## Indications

MIS thoracolumbar spinal fixation.

## Compatible With

Power and navigation capabilities. Compatible with Spine Guidance 5 and Mako Spine with Copilot features. LITe Pedicle Access System.

## Use Notes

Streamlined instrumentation designed for efficiency in MIS procedures. One system addresses multiple pathologies. Now sold by VB Spine.

## Key Differences vs Competitors

- **MIS Focus vs. Broader Platforms**: The ES2 MIS is specifically designed for minimally invasive thoracolumbar procedures, whereas the Xia 3, CD HORIZON, EXPEDIUM VERSE, and Reline systems are comprehensive platforms supporting both open and MIS approaches for a wider range of pathologies including deformity correction.
- **Integrated Reduction vs. Separate Components**: The ES2 features 15 mm of built-in reduction threads on its blade, while systems like the Serrato, CD HORIZON, and CREO MIS offer separate reduction screw types or require additional instrumentation for reduction maneuvers.
- **Simplified Inventory vs. Extensive Options**: The ES2 accepts only 5.5 mm and 6.0 mm rods, contrasting with systems like the CD HORIZON and EXPEDIUM VERSE which offer multiple rod diameter platforms (e.g., 4.75mm, 5.5mm, 6.0mm) and a wider array of screw types and materials.
- **Target Anatomy vs. Interbody Devices**: The ES2 is a pedicle screw system for posterior fixation, while the Tritanium TL is an interbody cage designed for PLIF/TLIF procedures, serving a different primary function in spinal fusion constructs.
- **Streamlined Design vs. Radical Simplification**: The ES2 is described as streamlined for efficiency, but the Neo system takes simplification further by consolidating its entire screw inventory to only 14 references and using single-use instruments, a logistical approach not specified for the ES2.
