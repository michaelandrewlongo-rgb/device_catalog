# X-MESH Expandable Anterior Cervical Corpectomy Cage (Osimplant)

## What It Is

The X-MESH is an expandable titanium mesh corpectomy cage designed for anterior cervical and thoracic vertebral body replacement. It uses a plate-mesh combination design with an in-situ continuous distraction (expansion) mechanism, allowing the surgeon to precisely adjust height after insertion. The cage is hollow, enabling bone graft packing inside and around the implant. It provides 8 degrees of total lordotic angulation (4 degrees from the top endplate + 4 degrees from the bottom endplate).

### Material
- Titanium alloy (Ti-6Al-4V / TiAl6V4)

### Design Features
- Hollow mesh body for bone graft filling
- Plate-mesh combination
- Endplate spikes for anchoring into adjacent vertebral endplates
- In-situ expansion mechanism (continuous distraction)
- Single or paired implantation possible

## Indications

- Single-stage trauma surgery (cervical/thoracic burst fractures, corpectomy defects)
- Tumor surgery (vertebral body replacement after tumor resection)
- Lateral mass hypoplasia
- Fixation or fusion after laminectomy
- Poor bone quality (rheumatoid arthritis, osteoporosis)
- Kyphotic deformity correction
- One or more vertebral body substitution
- Anterior cervical and thoracic spine reconstruction
- Can also be used as intersomatic implant in thoracic and lumbar spine

## Compatible With

- Bone graft (autograft/allograft) for filling the cage interior and exterior
- Anterior cervical plating systems (used as standalone VBR or combined with plate)
- Posterior supplemental fixation as needed

## Use Notes

- The 13-70mm height range covers single-level and multi-level corpectomy defects
- 8 degrees of built-in lordosis eliminates the need to manually angle the cage in many cases
- In-situ expansion allows insertion at a compressed height and then distracting to fill the defect, reducing the risk of endplate damage during insertion
- Spike endplates provide immediate fixation into adjacent vertebral bone
- Can be packed with bone graft both inside (through the hollow mesh body) and outside to maximize fusion surface area
- Available in 4 footprint diameters to match vertebral body width
- Consider supplemental posterior fixation for multi-level corpectomies or compromised posterior elements
- Turkish manufacturer; verify local regulatory clearance status

## Contraindications

- Active systemic or local infection at the surgical site (unless corpectomy is performed for debridement)
- Patient anatomy too small to accommodate the smallest footprint diameter (12mm)
- Severe osteoporosis where endplates cannot support the cage (though the manufacturer lists poor bone quality as an indication, clinical judgment should prevail regarding endplate integrity)
- Known titanium allergy or hypersensitivity (Ti-6Al-4V)
- Conditions precluding adequate surgical exposure or patient positioning
- Inadequate supplemental fixation for multi-level corpectomies
- Use in lumbar corpectomy is off-label unless specifically cleared for that indication; verify regulatory status

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Type** | Expandable mesh | Non-expandable cylindrical mesh | Non-expandable cylindrical mesh | Expandable VBR | Expandable VBR |

| **Endplate fixation** | Spike endplates | Press-fit only | Press-fit only | Integrated endplates | Integrated endplates |

- The X-MESH competes primarily in the expandable VBR segment against DePuy CONCORDE, Globus RISE, and Stryker VLIFT
- Expansion mechanism eliminates the need for intraoperative cutting (required for SYNMESH, Pyramesh)
- 8-degree lordosis is a fixed angle; competitors like CONCORDE and RISE offer adjustable lordosis
- Spike endplates reduce migration risk compared to press-fit-only designs
- Primary limitation is market availability and regulatory clearance outside Turkey; verify local clearance status before use

## Contraindications

- Active systemic or local infection at the surgical site (unless the corpectomy is for infection debridement, with risk-benefit consideration)
- Allergy or sensitivity to titanium alloy (Ti-6Al-4V)
- Insufficient bone quality to support the cage endplate spikes (severe osteoporosis increases subsidence risk even with expandable design)
- Patient conditions precluding general anesthesia or major spine surgery
- Pathology requiring reconstruction beyond the available height range (13-70 mm)
- Inadequate supplemental fixation options for multi-level corpectomies

## Key Differences vs Competitors

| Feature | Osimplant X-MESH | DePuy SYNMESH | Medtronic Pyramesh | DePuy VLIFT | Globus FORTIFY |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Expansion mechanism | In-situ continuous distraction | None (cut to length) | None (cut to length) | In-situ expansion (ratchet) | In-situ expansion |
| Height range | 13-70 mm (4 sizes) | Cut to length | Cut to length | Refer to technical documentation | Refer to technical documentation |
| Lordosis | 8 degrees built-in (lordotic version) | None (straight) | None (straight) | Lordotic options available | Lordotic options available |
| Shape | Plate-mesh combination | Cylindrical mesh | Cylindrical or rectangular | Rectangular | Rectangular |
| Endplate fixation | Spikes | Press-fit only | Press-fit only | Endplate teeth | Endplate teeth |
| Footprint options | 12, 14, 16, 18 mm | Multiple diameters | Multiple diameters | Multiple widths | Multiple widths |
| Regulatory | Turkish manufacturer; verify local clearance | Widely cleared (FDA, CE) | Widely cleared (FDA, CE) | Widely cleared (FDA, CE) | Widely cleared (FDA, CE) |

- X-MESH is one of few expandable cages offering built-in lordosis (8 degrees), which is advantageous in cervical and thoracic kyphosis correction.
- The in-situ expansion mechanism reduces the risk of endplate damage during insertion compared to non-expandable cages that require press-fit under distraction.
- Endplate spikes provide immediate anchoring, unlike smooth-ended mesh cages (SYNMESH, Pyramesh) that rely on press-fit alone.
- The main limitation is regulatory: as a Turkish-manufactured device, availability and clearance status vary by market. Verify FDA/CE status before use.
- Non-expandable cages (SYNMESH, Pyramesh) have a longer track record and lower cost but require more precise intraoperative measurement and carry higher subsidence risk in osteoporotic bone.
