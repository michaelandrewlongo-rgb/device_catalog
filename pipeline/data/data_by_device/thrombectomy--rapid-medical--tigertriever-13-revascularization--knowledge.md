# Tigertriever 13 Revascularization Device

**Manufacturer:** Rapid Medical
**Category:** Thrombectomy / Stent Retriever
**Model:** TRPP7144
**FDA Clearance:** K220808 (cleared 2022-07-25)

---

## What It Is

The Tigertriever 13 is a stent retriever for mechanical thrombectomy in large intracranial vessel occlusion. Its defining feature is a manually adjustable braided nitinol mesh controlled by a handle-mounted slider, giving the physician active, real-time control over mesh expansion diameter during deployment and retrieval. This distinguishes it from conventional self-expanding stent retrievers, which expand passively upon unsheathing.

The device consists of a braided nitinol mesh connected to a stainless steel shaft via a nitinol core wire. Pulling the slider compresses the mesh axially, expanding it radially into the clot. All mesh wires are completely radiopaque, providing continuous fluoroscopic visibility throughout the procedure.

## Indications

Restoration of blood flow by thrombus removal from large intracranial vessels in patients experiencing ischemic stroke within 8 hours of symptom onset. Indicated for patients who are ineligible for IV t-PA or who fail IV t-PA therapy.

---

## Contraindications

Not for use outside the labeled 8-hour symptom onset window. Single-use only. Requires physician training in neuro-interventional catheterization and ischemic stroke treatment.

---

## Compatible With

- Microcatheter with minimum 0.0165 inch (0.42 mm) inner diameter
- Compatible with standard 0.017-inch microcatheters

---

## Use Notes

**Deployment:** Advance through microcatheter across the occlusion. Pull the handle slider to expand the mesh into the clot. The physician controls expansion degree in real time, adjusting to match the local vessel diameter rather than relying on passive self-expansion.

**Visualization:** All mesh wires are completely radiopaque. Fluoroscopic monitoring of mesh position and expansion is continuous throughout deployment and retrieval.

**Retrieval:** Once the mesh has engaged and encapsulated the thrombus, withdraw the device as a unit.

**Sterility:** Supplied sterile, ethylene oxide sterilization. Shelf life 2.5 years. Single-use only.

**Active recalls:** None.

## Key Differences vs Competitors

**vs. Trevo XP ProVue / Solitaire X (self-expanding stent retrievers):**
- Tigertriever 13 uses manually controlled expansion via a slider handle; competitors expand passively upon microcatheter withdrawal
- Fully radiopaque mesh provides complete fluoroscopic visualization; competitor devices have partially radiopaque frame wires only
- Physician actively modulates expansion force and diameter to match vessel; competitors apply fixed radial force from device memory

**vs. Tigertriever 17 (larger sibling):**
- Smaller expanded OD (2.5 mm vs. 3.0 mm): suited for smaller or more distal vessel targets
- Shorter overall length (208 cm vs. 228 cm)
- Works through a smaller microcatheter (0.0165 inch vs. 0.017 inch ID)
