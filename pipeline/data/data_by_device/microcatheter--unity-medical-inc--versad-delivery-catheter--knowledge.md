# VersaD Delivery Catheter

**Manufacturer:** Unity Medical, Inc.
**Category:** Microcatheter

## What It Is

Single-lumen, variable stiffness delivery microcatheter with a long, tapered distal tip delineated by radiopaque markers. The VersaD is designed specifically to facilitate insertion and guidance of microcatheters and catheters into selected blood vessels in the neuro and peripheral vascular systems when used in conjunction with a compatible guide catheter. It is not a stand-alone navigating microcatheter; its role is as an intermediate delivery or support catheter between the guide and the working microcatheter.

Key construction features:
- Catheter shaft: stainless steel hypotube with varying durometers of Pebax polymers
- Inner liner: lubricious for catheter-over-catheter advancement
- Hydrophilic coating on the distal 90 cm of the shaft exterior
- Radiopaque markers delineate the tapered distal tip
- Proximal Luer hub (Pebax)
- Radiopaque markers: Pebax/20% BaSO4 composition
- Packaging includes three wire insertion tools and Instructions for Use
- Sterilization: Ethylene Oxide (EO); single use only

The VersaD cleared 510(k) K242051 as substantially equivalent to the Route 92 Medical 088 Access System delivery catheter (K200121).

## Indications

Intended for use with compatible guide catheters in facilitating the insertion and guidance of catheters into selected blood vessels in the neuro and peripheral vascular systems. This is a prescription device (21 CFR 801 Subpart D).

## Compatible With

- Compatible guide catheters (required for use; the VersaD is not a standalone navigating catheter)
- Downstream microcatheters and catheters that fit within the 0.019" inner diameter lumen

## Use Notes

- The VersaD is a delivery or intermediate support catheter, not a standalone navigating microcatheter; it must be used in conjunction with a guide catheter
- Three wire insertion tools are included in packaging
- Single use only; do not resterilize or reprocess
