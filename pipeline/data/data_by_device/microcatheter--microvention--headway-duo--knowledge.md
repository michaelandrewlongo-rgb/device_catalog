# Headway Duo Microcatheter

**Manufacturer:** MicroVention (Terumo)
**510(k):** K120917, cleared August 2, 2012
**Classification:** Diagnostic Intravascular Catheter, 21 CFR 870.1200, Class II, Product Code DQO

---

## What It Is

Single-lumen microcatheter designed for navigating small, tortuous vasculature over a steerable guidewire. The Headway Duo is a versatile platform used for both coil embolization and liquid embolic delivery in the neurovasculature. It features a semi-rigid proximal section transitioning to a flexible distal tip, with dual radiopaque markers at the distal end for fluoroscopic positioning.

Key features:
- Dual radiopaque distal markers
- Hydrophilic polymer outer coating for lubricity
- Luer fitting hub for accessory attachment
- Available in lengths up to 157 cm and 168 cm
- DMSO-compatible (verified for liquid embolic use)

---

## Specs

- **Lengths:** up to 157 cm and 168 cm
- **Construction:** Semi-rigid proximal section, flexible distal tip
- **Coating:** Hydrophilic polymer outer surface
- **Markers:** Dual radiopaque markers at distal end
- **Hub:** Standard luer fitting

Specific ID/OD dimensions are not provided in the 510(k) summary. The predicate device is the Headway 17 Microcatheter (K083343), suggesting a 0.017" class ID.

---

## Compatible With

Verified compatible with:
- **Occlusion coils**
- **Onyx Liquid Embolic System** (DMSO-compatible)
- **Trufill Liquid Embolic System** (DMSO-compatible)
- **PVA particles**
- Standard steerable guidewires
- Contrast media

---

## Indications

**General intravascular use:** Peripheral and coronary vasculature for infusion of diagnostic agents (contrast media) and therapeutic agents (embolization materials).

**Neurovascular use:** Infusion of diagnostic agents and therapeutic agents that have been cleared or approved for use in the neurovasculature and are compatible with the inner diameter of the Headway Duo.

---

## Use Notes

- The DMSO compatibility makes this catheter suitable for Onyx/liquid embolic delivery, not just coiling
- Dual markers at the tip give better orientation for aneurysm work than single-marker designs
- Hydrophilic coating must be kept hydrated during use
- Single use only
