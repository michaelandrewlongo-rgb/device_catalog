# BENCHMARK BMX81 Access System

**Manufacturer:** Penumbra, Inc.
**Category:** Microcatheter (Guide Catheter / Intracranial Access)
**510(k):** K221822 (November 4, 2022)
**Classification:** Catheter, Percutaneous, Neurovasculature; Class II; 21 CFR 870.1250; Product Codes QJP, DQY

---

## What It Is

Three-component intracranial access system consisting of the BMX81 Delivery Catheter, Penumbra Select Catheter, and a Dilator. The BMX81 is the larger-bore variant of the Benchmark platform, providing a 7F / 0.081" inner diameter delivery catheter for cases requiring greater lumen capacity than the standard 6F Benchmark (0.071" ID).

Components:

**BMX81 Delivery Catheter:**
Single-lumen, variable-stiffness guide catheter with a radiopaque markerband at the distal end and a Luer hub proximally. Features a hydrophilic coating on the distal 18 cm of the catheter shaft. Constructed with laser-cut stainless steel hypotube for the shaft. Can be used alone over a 0.038" guidewire or in combination with the Penumbra Select Catheter.

**Penumbra Select Catheter:**
Single-lumen, braid-reinforced, variable-stiffness 5F coaxial support catheter with a radiopaque distal end and Luer hub. Available tip shapes: SIM, H1, or BER. Identical to the Neuron 5F Select Catheter (K083125) and the Select Catheter included in the prior Benchmark system (K212838).

**Dilator:**
Single-lumen, radiopaque catheter with a tapered distal end for percutaneous insertion. Facilitates atraumatic entry of the BMX81 Delivery Catheter from skin through subcutaneous tissue into the vessel.

All three components are single-use, ethylene oxide sterilized.

## Indications

The BENCHMARK BMX81 Access System is indicated for the introduction of interventional devices into the peripheral, coronary, and neuro vasculature.

---

## Compatible With

- 0.038" or smaller guidewires (BMX81 Delivery Catheter)
- Penumbra Select Catheter (coaxial use inside Delivery Catheter)
- Off-the-shelf accessory devices (guidewires, rotating hemostasis valves, introducer sheaths sized appropriately for the BMX81 OD)

---

## Use Notes

- The Penumbra Select Catheter is used coaxially inside the BMX81 Delivery Catheter to facilitate selective catheterization, then withdrawn once the Delivery Catheter is positioned at the target
- The Dilator is used for percutaneous vascular access only; it is removed before the clinical procedure proceeds
- Hydrophilic coating is limited to the distal 18 cm; maintain hydration of that segment during use
- Single use only; do not reprocess or resterilize
- Intended for use by physicians trained in interventional techniques

## Key Differences vs Competitors

- **Larger inner diameter than most Benchmark variants:** The BMX81 has a 0.081" ID, which is larger than the standard 6F Benchmark system (0.071" ID) but smaller than the BMX96 (0.096" ID).
- **Unique construction vs. other Benchmark systems:** It uses a laser-cut stainless steel hypotube shaft, whereas the standard Benchmark Intracranial Access System uses a coil-reinforced construction.
- **Smaller inner diameter than some large-bore competitors:** Its 0.081" ID is smaller than the 87 NeuGlide (0.087" ID) and 93 NeuFlex (0.093" ID) catheters from Piraeus-Medical.
- **Different form factor vs. microcatheters:** It is a multi-component access system with a dilator and select catheter, unlike single-lumen microcatheters like the Medtronic Echelon, which are designed for distal selective catheterization and infusion.
- **No active recalls mentioned:** Unlike the Stryker Broadway 8 Catheter, which has active FDA recalls noted, no recalls are mentioned for the BMX81.
