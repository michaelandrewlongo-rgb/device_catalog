# Solitaire 2 and Solitaire Platinum Revascularization Device

**Manufacturer:** Medtronic
**Category:** Thrombectomy Device

## What It Is

Self-expanding nitinol stent retriever for mechanical thrombectomy in acute ischemic stroke due to large intracranial vessel occlusion. The distal nitinol stent section is designed to integrate with and capture thrombus; retraction withdraws the thrombus-laden stent from the vessel. The device consists of a nitinol shaped stent section on a nitinol push wire, with 90% platinum/10% iridium radiopaque markers at the proximal and distal ends and, on selected SKUs, at intermediate positions along the stent body. Supplied sterile in a dispenser coil within a Tyvek pouch; sterilized with ethylene oxide; single use only. This submission (K181060) added a compatible aspiration device (Medtronic Riptide Aspiration System) as an alternative aspiration source to the previously labeled 60 cc syringe. All stent sizes, marker spacing, device materials, and packaging remain unchanged.

## Indications

**Solitaire 2:**
Indicated for use to restore blood flow in the neurovasculature by removing thrombus for the treatment of acute ischemic stroke to reduce disability in patients with a persistent, proximal anterior circulation, large vessel occlusion, and smaller core infarcts who have first received IV t-PA. Endovascular therapy should start within 6 hours of symptom onset. Also indicated to restore blood flow by removing thrombus in patients experiencing ischemic stroke within 8 hours of symptom onset; patients ineligible for IV t-PA or who fail IV t-PA therapy are candidates.

**Solitaire Platinum:**
Indicated to restore blood flow by removing thrombus from a large intracranial vessel in patients experiencing ischemic stroke within 8 hours of symptom onset. Patients ineligible for IV t-PA or who fail IV t-PA therapy are candidates.

**Vessels:** Internal carotid artery (ICA), M1 and M2 segments of the middle cerebral artery, basilar artery, and vertebral arteries.

## Use Notes

- Designed for use in the neurovasculature to restore blood flow by removing thrombus from large intracranial vessel occlusions
- Aspiration source: 60 cc syringe or compatible aspiration device (including Riptide Aspiration System per K181060 labeling update)
- Single use only; do not resterilize

## Key Differences vs Competitors

- **Stent size range**: Target device offers 4mm and 6mm diameters with lengths up to 40mm; Solitaire X adds a 3mm diameter for smaller vessels (1.5-3.0mm); EMBOTRAP III offers 5mm and 6mm diameters with lengths up to 45mm; ERIC device sizing not specified.
- **Minimum vessel diameter**: Target device minimum not specified; Solitaire X and EMBOTRAP III both specify 1.5mm minimum; ERIC device minimum not specified.
- **Working length**: Target device has 180cm; EMBOTRAP III has 194cm; Solitaire X and ERIC device working lengths not specified.
- **Design features**: Target device uses standard stent retriever design; EMBOTRAP III features dual-layer architecture with inner channel and outer cage; ERIC device uses retrieval spheres on pusher wire; Solitaire X uses parametric overlap design.
- **Clinical evidence**: Target device evidence base not specified; Solitaire X described as "most-published stent retriever" with over 200 publications; EMBOTRAP III referenced in MASTRO I systematic review; ERIC device evidence not specified.
