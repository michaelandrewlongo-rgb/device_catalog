# DUREPAIR DURA REGENERATION MATRIX

**Manufacturer:** Medtronic
**Category:** Dural substitute

## What It Is

Device Description:
Durepair® Dura Regeneration Matrix is a collagen implant for the repair of defects in the
dura mater. Durepair is supplied sterile, in a double-peel package, and is intended for single
(one-time) use-only. Durepair is available in a variety of sizes intended to be cut by the
surgeon to the desired shape.
Indications for Use:
Durepair is indicated as a dura substitute for the repair of the dura mater.
Summary of Technological Characteristics Compared to the Predicate Device:
The proposed Durepair Dura Regeneration Matrix incorporates the same basic
technological characteristics as the predicate device. The manufacturing process change
relative to the predicate device did not affect the device design, indications of use, material
or the fundamental technology of the device.

## Key Differences vs Competitors

- **Material Composition**: Durepair is a collagen matrix from processed fetal bovine materials, while ArtiFascia is a synthetic non-woven fiber and film composite; Collagen Dura Membrane material is not specified.
- **Sizing Options**: Durepair offers multiple sizes (1"x1", 1"x3", 2"x2", 3"x3", 4"x5", 5"x8") and can be cut to size; ArtiFascia is available in one size (3cm x 4cm); Collagen Dura Membrane sizes are not specified.
- **Active Recalls**: Durepair has 1 active recall for potential endotoxin issues; Collagen Dura Membrane has 2 active recalls for packaging sterility issues; ArtiFascia has no active recalls noted.
- **MRI Compatibility**: ArtiFascia is specified as MR Safe; Durepair and Collagen Dura Membrane MRI compatibility is not specified.
- **Indication Specificity**: ArtiFascia is indicated for defects ≤25cm²; Durepair and Collagen Dura Membrane have no size limitation specified in their indications.
