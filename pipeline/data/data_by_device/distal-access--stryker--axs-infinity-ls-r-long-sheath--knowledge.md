## Indications

The AXS Infinity LS Long Sheath is indicated for the
introduction of interventional devices into the peripheral,
coronary, and neuro vasculature.

## Use Notes

Type: Braided

## Key Differences vs Competitors

- **Primary function**: The AXS Infinity LS Long Sheath is a general-purpose sheath for introducing devices into peripheral, coronary, and neuro vasculature, while competitors like the AXS Catalyst series and CEREGLIDE 71 are specifically optimized for thrombus removal and stroke intervention via aspiration.
- **Device type and construction**: The Infinity LS is a braided long sheath, whereas the AXS Catalyst catheters are variable stiffness aspiration catheters with hydrophilic coatings, and the CEREBASE DA is a guide sheath with geometric anchoring and a Dex tip for tracking.
- **Indications specificity**: The Infinity LS has broad vascular indications, while the AXS Catalyst 5/6/7 are indicated for revascularization in specific intracranial vessels (ICA, MCA M1/M2, basilar artery), and the CEREGLIDE 71 is optimized for acute ischemic stroke intervention with direct aspiration and stent retriever compatibility.
- **Key competitive notes**: The CEREBASE DA claims better trackability and support than the Infinity LS when placed distally, and the CEREGLIDE 71 claims to be more trackable than the Catalyst 6, with a larger inner diameter than the Catalyst 6 and 7.
