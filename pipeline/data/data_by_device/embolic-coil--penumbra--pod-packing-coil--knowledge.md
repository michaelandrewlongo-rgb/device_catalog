# POD Packing Coil

**Manufacturer:** Penumbra, Inc.
**Category:** Embolic Coil

FDA 510(k) K170852 | Cleared July 19, 2017 | Class II

## What It Is

The POD Packing Coil is a bare platinum embolization coil within the Penumbra Occlusion Device (POD) system, designed for endovascular embolization of intracranial aneurysms and other vascular abnormalities. The coil is attached to a delivery pusher and enclosed in an introducer sheath. Detachment is mechanical, using a pull wire within the Delivery Pusher; the Detachment Handle is packaged separately and is reusable within a single procedure for multiple coil detachments.

The POD Packing Coil (K170852) is the packing coil variant within the POD family, cleared as substantially equivalent to the POD System (K141134, predicate) and the Penumbra Smart Coil (K160832, reference device). The subject device uses a two-dimensional wave secondary shape, distinguishing it from the complex and helical shapes of the predicate and reference devices.

**System components:**
- **Coil:** Implantable platinum coil; excludes the treatment area from blood flow to promote stasis and thrombosis
- **Delivery Pusher:** Shaft with radiopaque positioning marker, Distal Detachment Tip (DDT), and pull wire
- **Introducer Sheath:** Covers the entire coil and distal flexible segment of the delivery pusher; friction-locked until use
- **Detachment Handle:** Packaged separately; intended for use in multiple detachments in a single procedure

## Indications

POD is indicated for the endovascular embolization of:
- Intracranial aneurysms
- Other neurovascular abnormalities such as arteriovenous malformations and arteriovenous fistulae
- Arterial and venous embolizations in the peripheral vasculature

## Use Notes

The POD Packing Coil is positioned as a packing/filling coil within the POD system. Its two-dimensional wave secondary shape distinguishes it from the complex three-dimensional shapes of the POD System predicate, and is optimized for dense aneurysm packing after an initial framing coil has been placed. Detachment is mechanical (pull-wire), not electrolytic, which is a key workflow difference from electrolytic systems (e.g., Stryker Target coils with InZone detachment).

Note: One FDA recall has been reported for the POD system relating to a delivery pusher pull wire diameter tolerance issue that could allow premature coil detachment. Confirm current lot status before use.

## Key Differences vs Competitors

- **Coil shape and role**: The POD Packing Coil uses a two-dimensional wave secondary shape specifically for dense packing after framing, unlike the complex/helical shapes of the Penumbra Coil 400, Penumbra LP Coil, Penumbra Smart Coil, and POD System, or the helical/3D shapes of the Axium family.
- **Detachment mechanism**: Uses a mechanical pull-wire system (with separate reusable handle) similar to other Penumbra coils, while the Axium family uses a separate mechanical Instant Detacher unit; both differ from electrolytic systems like Stryker Target coils.
- **Recall status**: Shares the same active FDA recall for delivery pusher pull wire tolerance issues as the Penumbra Coil 400, Penumbra LP Coil, Penumbra Smart Coil, and POD System; recall status for Axium devices is not specified.
- **Coil material composition**: Uses platinum/tungsten (92% Pt, 8% W) with adhesive, PET, and titanium, while other Penumbra devices may use different platinum alloys (e.g., 90% platinum/10% iridium in LP Coil) or unspecified materials; Axium coil materials are not specified.
- **Indications scope**: Has identical neurovascular and peripheral vascular indications to all other Penumbra coils and the Axium family, while the Ruby Embolization Platform's indications are not specified for embolic coil use.
