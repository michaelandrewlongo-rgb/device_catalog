# ENTERPRISE 2 Vascular Reconstruction Device

**Manufacturer:** Cerenovus (J&J)
**Category:** Intracranial stent

## What It Is

Self-expanding intracranial stent (vascular reconstruction device) for coil-assisted embolization of wide-neck aneurysms. Closed-cell design providing stability, conformability, and ease of use. Can be recaptured and redeployed after partial deployment. Radiopaque markers for visibility during and after deployment.

## Sizing/Specs

Parent Vessel Diameter: 2.5-4.0 mm; Stent Lengths: 16 cm, 23 cm, 30 cm, 39 cm; Product codes: ENF401600, ENF401612, ENF402300, ENF402312, ENF403000, ENF403012, ENF403900, ENF403912; Compatible with Prowler Select Plus 0.021 inch ID microcatheters

## Indications

Authorized for use with embolic coils for treatment of wide-neck intracranial saccular or fusiform aneurysms arising from parent vessel diameter 2.5-4.0 mm. Wide-neck defined as neck width >= 4 mm or dome-to-neck ratio < 2. HUMANITARIAN DEVICE (USA ONLY).

## Compatible With

Prowler EX Microcatheter, Prowler Select Plus (0.021 inch ID), TRUFILL n-BCA Liquid Embolic System, embolic coils

## Use Notes

Higher radial resistive force than Neuroform, LVIS Jr, and Solitaire 2/FR stents. Higher chronic outward force than LVIS Jr and Solitaire 2/FR. Higher point load force than Neuroform, LVIS Jr, and Solitaire. Better wall apposition in curved vessels and excellent kink resistance vs ENTERPRISE 1. Less foreshortening than LVIS Jr and Solitaire 2/FR.

## Key Differences vs Competitors

- **Radial/Chronic/Point Load Forces**: ENTERPRISE 2 has higher radial resistive force than Neuroform Atlas, LVIS Jr, and Solitaire 2/FR; higher chronic outward force than LVIS Jr and Solitaire 2/FR; and higher point load force than Neuroform Atlas, LVIS Jr, and Solitaire. This is not specified for LEO+, Enterprise VRD, LVIS/LVIS Jr, or Neuroform Atlas.
- **Deployment & Recapture**: ENTERPRISE 2 can be recaptured and redeployed after partial deployment. LVIS/LVIS Jr can also be resheathed/recaptured before full deployment. Recapture capability is not specified for LEO+, Enterprise VRD, or Neuroform Atlas.
- **Parent Vessel Diameter Range**: ENTERPRISE 2 is indicated for vessels 2.5-4.0 mm. LVIS/LVIS Jr and Neuroform Atlas cover a wider range (2.0-4.5 mm). Vessel diameter range is not specified for LEO+ or Enterprise VRD.
- **Design & Wall Apposition**: ENTERPRISE 2 has a closed-cell design with better wall apposition in curved vessels and excellent kink resistance vs ENTERPRISE 1. LVIS/LVIS Jr is closed-cell, Neuroform Atlas is open-cell. Design details are not specified for LEO+ or Enterprise VRD.
- **Regulatory Status**: ENTERPRISE 2 is a Humanitarian Device (USA ONLY). Enterprise VRD is also approved under a Humanitarian Device Exemption (HDE). Regulatory status is not specified for LEO+, LVIS/LVIS Jr, or Neuroform Atlas.

## Also Known As

Enterprise 2, Enterprise VRD 2, ENTERPRISE2
