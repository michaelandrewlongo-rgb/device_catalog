# Spartan eCoil System

**Manufacturer:** Spartan Micro Inc
**Category:** Embolic Coil
510(k) K180653

## What It Is

The Spartan eCoil™ System is a mechanically
detachable embolic coil system that consists of an
embolic coil (the Spartan eCoil™) attached to a
delivery wire with a fluoroscopic marker band and the
system is housed in an introducer sheath. The system
is provided sterile and requires no additional
equipment to be employed by the user.

## Indications

The Spartan eCoil™ System is indicated for the endovascular embolization of intracranial aneurysms and other
neurovascular abnormalities, such as arteriovenous malformations and arteriovenous fistulae. The Spartan eCoil™ System
is also indicated for arterial and venous embolizations in the peripheral vasculature.
