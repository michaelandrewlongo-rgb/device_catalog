# AXS Catalyst Distal Access Catheter

**Manufacturer:** Stryker Neurovascular
**Category:** Distal access / aspiration catheter
**Regulatory Class:** Class II, 21 CFR 870.1250 (Percutaneous Catheter)
**Product Code:** NRY
**510(k) Numbers:** K173841 (Catalyst 5 and 6, cleared 2018-03-18); K183464 (Catalyst 7, cleared 2019-03-13)

## What It Is

Sterile, single-lumen, variable stiffness catheter designed for use in the removal of thrombus from the neurovasculature using continuous aspiration. Used as the intravascular component of the AXS Universal Aspiration System. The catheter shaft has a hydrophilic coating to reduce friction during use. A radiopaque platinum/iridium marker band at the distal end provides fluoroscopic visualization. A luer hub on the proximal end allows attachments for flushing and aspiration.

The catheter is advanced into the neurovasculature over an appropriately sized guidewire using a compatible sheath or guide catheter. A peel-away sheath is provided in the package to facilitate introduction of the catheter tip into the sheath or guide catheter valve. The catheter delivers aspiration from the Medela Dominant Flex Pump directly to the site of occlusion via the AXS Universal Aspiration Tubing. Aspiration is applied through a Rotating Hemostasis Valve (RHV) or Tuohy Borst Valve with Sideport attached to the proximal hub.

The AXS Catalyst Distal Access Catheter is the only intravascular component of the AXS Universal Aspiration System. The aspiration tubing, liner set, and pump are disposed of or cleaned per institutional procedures following the procedure.

**Packaged with:** Rotating Hemostasis Valve (RHV), Tuohy Borst Valve with Sideport, two peel-away introducer sheaths.

**System components (not intravascular):**
- AXS Universal Aspiration Tubing (Medela AG; EO Sterilized; Non-reusable; ID: 0.218"; Length: 300 cm; Flow clamp)
- AXS Universal Liner Set (3 individually packaged poly-bags, each containing one canister liner and one ClotFinder specimen cup; offered with and without desiccant; Class I Exempt; Non-Sterile)
- Medela Dominant Flex Pump (K170329; Adjustable vacuum pressure dial; Three selectable air flow rates: 40, 50, 60 L/min; Minimum vacuum pressure: -68 kPa [-20.08 inHg]; Non-Sterile; Reusable; Cleanable)

## Indications

Indicated for use in the revascularization of patients with acute ischemic stroke secondary to intracranial large vessel occlusive disease in the following vessels:
- Internal carotid artery
- Middle cerebral artery - M1 and M2 segments
- Basilar artery
- Vertebral arteries

Within 8 hours of symptom onset. Patients who are ineligible for intravenous tissue plasminogen activator (IV t-PA) or who failed IV t-PA are candidates for treatment.

Used as part of the AXS Universal Aspiration System.

## Use Notes

- The catheter is advanced by a physician trained in interventional endovascular procedures.
- Attach the RHV or Tuohy Borst Valve with Sideport to the proximal hub prior to use. Use the RHV and Tuohy Borst valve for flushing, insertion of catheters, and aspiration.
- The peel-away introducer sheaths protect the distal tip during insertion into the RHV or Tuohy Borst; remove after insertion and do not leave in the patient.
- Under fluoroscopic guidance, advance the catheter through the vasculature to the site of occlusion over a guidewire.
- Connect the aspiration system via the RHV or Tuohy Borst. Turn on the vacuum. Open the aspiration tubing clamp to apply aspiration.
- The IFU warning states that excessive aspiration may cause patient complications.
- Upon completion, dispose of the catheter, aspiration tubing, and liner set per institutional procedures. Clean the Medela Dominant Flex Pump per pump instructions.
