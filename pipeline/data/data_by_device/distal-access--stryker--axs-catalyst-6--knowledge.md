## Key Differences vs Competitors

- **Sizing/Indications**: Unlike the AXS Catalyst Distal Access Catheter, which has specific vessel indications (ICA, MCA M1/M2, basilar artery) and a sizing rule (Catalyst 5 for distal/small vessels, Catalyst 6 for larger lumen needs, Catalyst 7 for largest lumen/proximal targets), the AXS Catalyst 6 has only a caution about excessive aspiration as its indication.
- **MRI Compatibility**: The AXS Catalyst 6 is MR Conditional, similar to the AXS Catalyst 5, while the AXS Catalyst 7, AXS Offset, and AXS Infinity LS Long Sheath have labeling that does not contain MRI safety information.
- **Trackability vs Competitors**: According to provided data, the CEREGLIDE 71 Intermediate Catheter is described as more trackable than the Catalyst 6, among others, and has a larger inner diameter than the Catalyst 6 and Catalyst 7.
- **Compatibility/System Role**: The AXS Catalyst Distal Access Catheter lists specific compatible components (Medela Dominant Flex Pump, AXS Universal Aspiration Tubing, etc.) and is described as part of the AXS Universal Aspiration System, while such compatibility details are not specified for the AXS Catalyst 6.
- **Device Type Comparison**: The AXS Catalyst 6 is a distal access catheter, whereas the CEREBASE DA Guide Sheath is a guide sheath for distal access with geometric anchoring, and the AXS Offset is a delivery assist catheter intended to assist in delivering devices like distal access catheters.
