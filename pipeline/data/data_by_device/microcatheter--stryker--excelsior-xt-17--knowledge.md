## Indications

/ indications for use
Stryker Neurovascular’s Excelsior XT-17 Microcatheters
are intended to assist in the delivery of diagnostic
agents, such as contrast media, and therapeutic agents,
such as occlusion coils, into the peripheral, coronary and
neuro vasculature.

## Use Notes

MRI: Labeling does not contain MRI Safety Information. Pre-sterilized. Single-use

## Key Differences vs Competitors

- **Inner diameter compatibility**: Excelsior XT-17 is indicated for delivery of diagnostic agents and occlusion coils into peripheral, coronary, and neurovasculature, similar to Excelsior SL-10 and Excelsior SL-10 Microcatheter. However, Excelsior XT-27 is specifically indicated for non-liquid interventional devices like stents requiring 0.027" inner diameter, while Echelon Microcatheter is compatible with occlusion coils through 0.017" ID and Headway Duo is DMSO-compatible for liquid embolic delivery.
- **Coronary use restrictions**: Unlike Excelsior XT-17, which is indicated for coronary vasculature, Echelon Microcatheter is contraindicated for coronary use, and Magic Infusion Catheter is not intended for coronary vasculature.
- **Specialized features**: Excelsior XT-17 lacks specified DMSO compatibility for liquid embolic delivery, unlike Echelon Microcatheter and Headway Duo which are explicitly DMSO-compatible for systems like Onyx. Magic Infusion Catheter is specifically intended for regional infusion of contrast materials in neurovasculature, not for therapeutic agent delivery like occlusion coils.
- **MRI safety information**: Like Excelsior SL-10, Excelsior XT-27, and Magic Infusion Catheter, Excelsior XT-17 labeling does not contain MRI safety information, whereas this is not specified for Echelon Microcatheter and Headway Duo.
