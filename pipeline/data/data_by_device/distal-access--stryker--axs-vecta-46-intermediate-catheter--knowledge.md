# AXS Vecta 46 Intermediate Catheter

**Manufacturer:** Stryker Neurovascular
**Category:** Distal access / intermediate catheter

## What It Is

The AXS Vecta 46 is a single-lumen, flexible, variable-stiffness intermediate catheter designed for neurovascular access. It is advanced coaxially into the neurovasculature over a microcatheter or guidewire, inside a compatible sheath or guide catheter, by a physician trained in interventional endovascular procedures.

Construction features include:

- Nitinol shaft reinforcement for pushability through tortuous anatomy
- PTFE inner layer for low-friction device delivery
- Hydrophilic coating on the distal 25 cm to reduce friction at the working end
- Platinum/iridium radiopaque distal marker band
- Polycarbonate Luer hub at the proximal end
- Packaged with one hemostasis valve and one peel-away introducer sheath

The catheter functions as an intermediate platform for delivering interventional devices (microcatheters, stent retrievers, coils) to selected neurovascular targets, and as a conduit for retrieval devices. The 46 designation refers to the 0.046" inner diameter (ID), situating this catheter between smaller microcatheters and larger guide catheters in the access stack.

510(k) K202752 updated the device to the QJP product code, adding neurovascular intermediate catheter indication use.

## Indications

Indicated for use in facilitating the insertion and guidance of appropriately sized interventional devices into a selected blood vessel in the neurovascular system. Also indicated for use as a conduit for retrieval devices.

## Compatible With

- Compatible sheath or guide catheter (required for introduction)
- Appropriately sized microcatheter or guidewire (coaxial technique)
- One peel-away introducer sheath included in package

Note: Simulated use validation was performed in a neurovascular in vitro model replicating ICA and MCA anatomy.

## Use Notes

- Intermediate catheter for neurovascular access; not a standalone aspiration device in this clearance (QJP product code; distinct from aspiration indications).
- Coaxial stack: guide catheter > AXS Vecta 46 > microcatheter/guidewire.
- The hydrophilic distal coating reduces friction during navigation through tortuous neurovascular anatomy.
- FDA recall history: 3 active recalls documented, including distribution of OUS-labeled IFU and packaging in the US market. Verify IFU version before use.

## Key Differences vs Competitors

- **Primary function**: AXS Vecta 46 is indicated as an intermediate catheter for device delivery and retrieval conduit, while AXS Catalyst catheters are specifically indicated for thrombus removal via continuous aspiration as part of the AXS Universal Aspiration System.
- **Inner diameter**: AXS Vecta 46 has 0.046" ID, smaller than AXS Vecta 71/74 (0.071" ID) and not specified for Catalyst models (though Catalyst sizing guidance suggests 5 for distal/small vessels, 6 for larger lumen, 7 for largest lumen).
- **Aspiration capability**: AXS Vecta 46 is not cleared as a standalone aspiration device, whereas AXS Catalyst catheters are designed for aspiration thrombectomy and AXS Vecta 71/74 supports both device delivery and direct aspiration (ADAPT-style).
- **Compatibility requirements**: AXS Vecta 46 requires a compatible sheath/guide catheter and works coaxially with microcatheters/guidewires; AXS Catalyst catheters require the Medela Dominant Flex Pump and AXS Universal Aspiration Tubing for aspiration function.
- **Recall status**: AXS Vecta 46 has 3 active FDA recalls documented (OUS-labeled IFU/packaging issues); recall status not specified for other devices.
