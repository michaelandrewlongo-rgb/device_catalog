# INZONE DETACHMENT SYSTEM

**Manufacturer:** Boston Scientific
**Category:** Embolic Coil
510(k) K103008

## What It Is

Boston Scientific's InZoneTM Detachment System is a sterile, handheld, single-patient use device designed for use with Boston Scientific Detachable Coils. The device consists of an enclosure with a detachment button, five LED indicator lamps, a funnel inset at its distal end, and a cable connection port. The device comes pre-loaded with two AAAA (1.5 VDC) batteries.

## Indications

The InZone Detachment System is intended for use with all Boston Scientific Detachable Coils in the embolization of intracranial aneurysms and other vascular malformations of the neuro and peripheral vasculature.
