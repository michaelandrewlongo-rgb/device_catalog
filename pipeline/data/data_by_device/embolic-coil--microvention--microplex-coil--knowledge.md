# MicroPlex Coil System

**Manufacturer:** MicroVention
**Category:** Embolic coil

## What It Is

The MicroPlex Coil System (MCS) is a family of detachable platinum embolization coils for endovascular occlusion of intracranial aneurysms and other neurovascular lesions. Two primary variants exist:

- **MCS Cosmos 10 (bare platinum):** A bare platinum-tungsten alloy coil with a polyethylene terephthalate (PET) stretch-resistant member and a 3D spherical primary shape. The PET filar provides stretch resistance throughout the implant.
- **HES HydroFrame 10 (hydrogel-coated):** Identical base construction to the Cosmos 10 but incorporates an inner hydrophilic acrylic copolymer hydrogel core running parallel to the stretch-resistant member. The hydrogel expands after deployment, improving volumetric fill of the aneurysm sac.

Both variants attach to a V-Trak delivery pusher. Detachment is electrolytic, activated via the handheld battery-powered V-Grip Detachment Controller (sold separately). The 3D spherical shape creates an initial frame within the aneurysm to scaffold subsequent filling coils.

## Indications

Intended for the endovascular embolization of intracranial aneurysms and other neurovascular abnormalities including arteriovenous malformations and arteriovenous fistulae. Also indicated for vascular occlusion of blood vessels in the neurovascular system to permanently obstruct blood flow to an aneurysm or vascular malformation, and for arterial and venous embolization in the peripheral vasculature.

## Use Notes

- The HydroFrame 10 hydrogel core expands after deployment, providing improved volumetric packing density compared to bare platinum coils of equal restrained dimensions.
- The Cosmos 10 and HydroFrame 10 are both framing-type coils by design (3D spherical primary shape).
- FDA recall history: 16 active recalls documented in FDA database, with reported issues including missing implant coils in packaging. Verify lot-specific device integrity per institutional QA protocols.

## Key Differences vs Competitors

- **Hydrogel coating availability:** The target device (MicroPlex Coil System) offers both bare platinum (Cosmos 10) and hydrogel-coated (HydroFrame 10) variants. The competitor Axium family from Medtronic also offers bare platinum and bioabsorbable polymer-coated variants (e.g., Axium Nylon), but specific hydrogel coating is not specified for Axium in the provided data.
- **Detachment mechanism:** The target device uses an electrolytic detachment system (V-Grip Detachment Controller). The competitor Axium family uses a mechanical detachment system (Instant Detacher).
- **Recall status:** The target device has 16 active FDA recalls documented for missing implant coils in packaging. No recall information is provided for the Axium competitor devices.
- **Coil size range:** The target device's specified implant diameter is 2.0-2.5 mm with restrained lengths of 2-4 cm (Cosmos 10) or 2.4 cm (HydroFrame 10). The Axium competitor offers a broader specified range: loop outer diameters of 1.5-25 mm and lengths of 1-50 cm depending on the variant.
- **Delivery pusher length:** The target device uses a 185 cm delivery pusher. The competitor HydroCoil Embolic System (also by MicroVention) uses a 195 cm pusher, while pusher length is not specified for the Axium devices.
