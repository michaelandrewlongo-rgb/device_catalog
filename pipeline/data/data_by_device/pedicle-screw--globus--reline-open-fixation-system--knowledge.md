# Reline Open Fixation System

**Manufacturer:** Globus Medical
**Category:** Pedicle screw system

## What It Is

Comprehensive fixation system from occiput to pelvis. Open, MIS (MAS), and hybrid approaches in single platform. Variants: Reline MAS (minimally invasive), Reline Open, Reline 3D (deformity with VBD derotation), Reline Fenestrated (cement for tumors), Reline Traction (optimized insertional torque), Reline ASF (revision/extension), Reline Cervical.

## Sizing/Specs

Multiple rod diameters and materials.

## Indications

Posterior spinal fixation occiput to pelvis. Degenerative, deformity, tumor, trauma, revision.

## Compatible With

Reline Dual-Headed Modular Connector. ExcelsiusGPS.

## Key Differences vs Competitors

- **Approach versatility**: Reline Open Fixation System uniquely integrates open, MIS (MAS), and hybrid approaches in a single platform with specialized variants for deformity (3D), tumors (Fenestrated), revision (ASF), and cervical applications, whereas competitors like CREO MIS, ES2, and EXPEDIUM VERSE focus primarily on MIS or open/MIS hybrid without such comprehensive variant specialization.
- **Anatomic range**: Reline Open Fixation System is indicated for posterior spinal fixation from occiput to pelvis, covering the full spinal column, while most competitors (CREO MIS, ES2, EXPEDIUM VERSE, Neo) are limited to thoracolumbosacral or non-cervical regions.
- **Compatibility ecosystem**: Reline Open Fixation System is compatible with the Reline Dual-Headed Modular Connector and ExcelsiusGPS navigation, creating a proprietary Globus ecosystem, whereas other systems like CD HORIZON integrate with Medtronic's SEXTANT and StealthStation, and ES2 works with Stryker's Spine Guidance 5 and Mako Spine.
- **Revision capability**: Unlike the Revere Addition Revision Fixation System which is revision-only and attaches to existing Globus hardware, Reline Open Fixation System includes revision capability (Reline ASF variant) within its primary platform while maintaining full construct replacement flexibility.

## Also Known As

Reline, Reline MAS, Reline 3D
