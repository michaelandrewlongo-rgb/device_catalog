# CREO MIS Spinal Fixation System (Globus Medical)

**Manufacturer:** Globus Medical
**Category:** Pedicle Screw System (Minimally Invasive)

## What It Is

The CREO MIS is Globus Medical's minimally invasive percutaneous pedicle screw system, designed for posterior thoracolumbosacral fixation through small incisions. The system uses cannulated polyaxial screws with extended tabs (screw extenders) for MIS insertion over guidewires, enabling percutaneous placement without open exposure of the posterior elements. It employs the Globus non-threaded locking cap closure mechanism (90-degree rotation capture, then set screw final tightening) shared with the REVERE system. The system is designed primarily for 1-2 level MIS TLIF and short-segment fixation.

## Indications

- Degenerative disc disease
- Spondylolisthesis
- Trauma (fracture or dislocation)
- Spinal stenosis
- Tumor
- Pseudarthrosis
- Failed previous fusion
- Thoracic, lumbar, and sacral spine
- Posterior percutaneous approach, as an adjunct to fusion with autograft and/or allograft

## Contraindications

- Active infection at the operative site
- Severe osteoporosis or osteopenia that would compromise implant fixation
- Patient conditions precluding benefit of spinal fixation (e.g., morbid obesity, pregnancy, mental illness, substance abuse affecting compliance)
- Inadequate tissue coverage of the implant
- Known allergy to titanium or cobalt-chromium alloy
- Any condition that would compromise the ability of the implant to provide stability
- Complex deformity requiring open exposure, hooks, or direct vertebral rotation (not appropriate for MIS percutaneous approach)

## Compatible With

- **Globus RISE interbody cages** (expandable TLIF cages)
- **Globus CALIBER interbody cages** (TLIF/PLIF cages)
- **Globus REVERE Stabilization System** (shares the non-threaded locking cap; CREO hardware is compatible with Revere Addition revision extension clamps)
- **Globus CREO open system** (if intraoperative conversion to open is needed)
- Guidewires (K-wires) for cannulated screw placement
- Autograft and/or allograft

## Use Notes

- Cannulated design allows guidewire-based placement: place K-wire into the pedicle under fluoroscopy, then pass the screw over the wire. This reduces the need for open pedicle exposure.
- Extended tabs (screw extenders) function as temporary towers for percutaneous rod passage and set screw insertion; they are removed after final tightening.
- The non-threaded locking cap eliminates cross-threading, which is particularly valuable in MIS cases where visualization is limited and debris can contaminate screw heads.
- Designed primarily for short-segment (1-2 level) fixation; for long constructs or deformity, consider the Globus REVERE or CREO open system.
- CoCr rods provide higher fatigue strength than titanium, which may be advantageous in single-level constructs with high biomechanical demand.
- Fluoroscopic guidance is essential throughout the procedure; confirm screw position with AP and lateral views before rod placement.
- If intraoperative findings require conversion to open surgery, the CREO screw heads are compatible with Globus open rod and connector systems.

## Key Differences vs Competitors

- **vs DePuy Synthes VIPER PRIME:** Both are percutaneous MIS pedicle screw systems. CREO uses the non-threaded locking cap (faster, no cross-threading); VIPER PRIME uses standard set screws. VIPER PRIME offers fenestrated screws for cement augmentation (with CONFIDENCE cement) that CREO does not. VIPER PRIME has broader cross-compatibility with the EXPEDIUM/VERSE open system.
- **vs Medtronic SEXTANT / CD HORIZON:** SEXTANT is Medtronic's MIS percutaneous system, used with CD HORIZON screws. SEXTANT integrates with StealthStation navigation; CREO MIS does not have a dedicated navigation interface. CREO's non-threaded locking cap is faster to close than CD HORIZON's break-off set screws.
- **vs NuVasive Precept:** NuVasive's percutaneous MIS system. Both are cannulated guidewire-based designs. CREO MIS is well-integrated with Globus interbody cages (RISE, CALIBER). Following the NuVasive-Globus merger, these platforms now share a parent company.
- **vs Stryker ES2:** Stryker's MIS platform. CREO MIS's non-threaded locking cap is a differentiating feature for speed and reliability of closure in the MIS setting.
- **Globus ecosystem advantage:** CREO MIS screws are compatible with Globus REVERE revision connectors (Addition/RESCUE clamps), providing a pathway for future revision extension without full hardware removal.
