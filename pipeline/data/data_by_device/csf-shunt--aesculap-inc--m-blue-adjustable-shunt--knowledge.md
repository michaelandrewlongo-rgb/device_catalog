# M. Blue Adjustable Shunt System

**Manufacturer:** Aesculap (B. Braun)
**Category:** CSF shunt/diagnostic

## What It Is

DEVICE DESCRIPTION
M.blue is an adjustable valve that combines an adjustable gravitational unit and a fixed
differential pressure unit. The M.blue valve can be set for a range of pressures and is offered in
four pressure level settings. The M.blue valve is comprised of a titanium housing enclosed by a
thin titanium membrane with a curved wave profile design. The gravitational unit of the M.blue
valve contains a tantalum weight, leaf spring and ball mechanism that is mechanically controlled
by internal magnets. The differential pressure unit (ball-in-cone) of the M.blue valve contains a
sapphire ball and titanium spring.
Manual devices are available to locate, verify the pressure setting and to set or change the
pressure pre and postoperatively. These manual accessories are for external use by the
Healthcare provider.

## Key Differences vs Competitors

- **Valve Type**: M.blue combines an adjustable gravitational unit with a fixed differential pressure unit in a single titanium housing, offering posture-dependent pressure regulation. In contrast, proGAV 2.0 uses separate adjustable differential pressure and fixed gravitational units, while CERTAS Plus uses a ruby ball-in-cone mechanism with spring design, and Strata valves use a rotor with magnet and spring system in silicone housing.
- **Pressure Settings**: M.blue offers adjustable gravitational unit (0–40 cmH₂O) and four fixed differential pressure settings (0, 5, 10, 15 cmH₂O). proGAV 2.0 has adjustable differential pressure settings (range not specified), CERTAS Plus offers 8 programmable settings (7 active drainage settings plus a "virtual off"), and Strata valves have adjustable performance levels (specific settings not specified).
- **Materials & Design**: M.blue features titanium housing with thin curved wave profile, titanium alloy rotor, neodymium ferrite boron magnets, alpha sapphire ball, tantalum weight, and titanium micro spring. proGAV 2.0 uses titanium housing with leaf spring and ball mechanism, CERTAS Plus uses ruby ball-in-cone mechanism, and Strata uses silicone housing with plastic base.
- **Adjustment Features**: M.blue provides audible and tactile feedback during adjustment, uses 4 magnets for control, and includes Active-Lock mechanism to prevent inadvertent adjustment. proGAV 2.0 also features Active-Lock mechanism, CERTAS Plus requires Codman CERTAS Tool Kit for noninvasive adjustment, and Strata valves use magnetic adjustment without specified feedback mechanism.
- **Dimensions**: M.blue measures 4.2 mm height × 16.6 mm diameter × 25.0 mm length. proGAV 2.0 dimensions not specified, CERTAS Plus comes in Inline, Inline Small, and Right Angle configurations (specific dimensions not provided), and Strata dimensions not specified.
