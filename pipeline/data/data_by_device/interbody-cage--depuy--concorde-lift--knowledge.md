# CONCORDE LIFT Expandable Lumbar Interbody Fusion Device -- DePuy Synthes

**Manufacturer:** DePuy Synthes (Johnson & Johnson)
**Category:** Interbody Cage (TLIF, Expandable)

## What It Is

An in-situ expandable interbody fusion cage designed for transforaminal lumbar interbody fusion (TLIF). The CONCORDE LIFT features a PEEK-OPTIMA body with titanium endplates, combining the radiolucent properties of PEEK for postoperative imaging with the endplate engagement characteristics of titanium. The cage inserts at a minimal contracted height through a unilateral posterior approach and then expands vertically in the disc space using a controlled expansion mechanism. This allows the surgeon to distract the disc space and restore lordosis without pre-distraction or sequential trialing. The expansion mechanism provides continuous, controlled height restoration, filling the disc space from within. The device has an open graft window for bone packing to support fusion. Requires supplemental posterior pedicle screw fixation.

## Sizing / Specs

- **Material:** PEEK-OPTIMA body with titanium alloy endplates
- **Expansion range:** Inserts at approximately 8mm contracted height; expands up to approximately 15mm (size-dependent); refer to surgical technique guide for complete expansion matrix
- **Footprint:** Anatomically shaped for unilateral TLIF insertion
- **Lordotic options:** Multiple lordotic angle options available to address sagittal alignment
- **Lengths:** Multiple length options for endplate coverage; refer to surgical technique guide
- **Widths:** Multiple width options; refer to surgical technique guide
- **Graft window:** Open central window for autograft, allograft, or bone graft substitute packing
- **Radiopaque markers:** Titanium endplates and markers visible on fluoroscopy; PEEK body is radiolucent between the endplates
- **Approach:** TLIF (transforaminal lumbar interbody fusion), unilateral posterior insertion
- **Levels:** L2-S1

## Indications

- Degenerative disc disease (DDD) at one or two contiguous levels from L2 to S1
- Lumbar spondylolisthesis (up to Grade 1)
- Lumbar spinal stenosis with instability requiring fusion
- Skeletally mature patients who have failed at least 6 months of conservative treatment
- Cases where controlled in situ distraction and lordosis restoration are desired
- MIS or open TLIF approach

Must be used with supplemental posterior fixation (pedicle screws and rods) and bone graft (autograft and/or allograft).

## Contraindications

- Active infection at the operative site (local or systemic)
- Severe osteoporosis or osteopenia that would preclude adequate endplate support
- Known allergy or sensitivity to PEEK or titanium
- Inadequate bone stock, bone quality, or anatomy to support the implant
- Conditions that preclude the potential benefit of spinal fusion
- Spondylolisthesis grade 3 or higher (relative)
- Morbid obesity or conditions preventing adequate surgical exposure
- Patient non-compliance with postoperative restrictions
- Prior fusion at the treated level (relative)

## Compatible With

- **Pedicle screw systems:** DePuy Synthes VIPER PRIME, EXPEDIUM VERSE, and MATRIX posterior fixation systems; compatible with standard 5.5mm rod systems
- **Retractor systems:** DePuy Synthes Pipeline system and standard MIS tubular retractors for MIS TLIF approach
- **Bone graft:** Autograft (local laminectomy bone, iliac crest), allograft, bone graft substitutes, and biologics packed through the open graft window
- **Navigation:** Compatible with standard fluoroscopy-guided insertion; titanium endplates and markers visible on AP and lateral fluoroscopy
- **Instrumentation:** Dedicated CONCORDE LIFT inserter, expansion driver, trial instruments

## Use Notes

- The CONCORDE LIFT is an expandable cage. The primary advantage is insertion at a contracted profile to minimize nerve root retraction, followed by controlled in situ expansion.
- The PEEK-OPTIMA body provides a radiolucent window between the titanium endplates. This allows visualization of bone graft incorporation on lateral radiographs, an advantage over all-titanium expandable cages where fusion assessment on plain films is more difficult.
- The expansion mechanism eliminates the need for sequential trialing. The cage is inserted at minimum height and expanded to the desired disc height under fluoroscopic guidance.
- Pack bone graft into the central graft window before insertion. Additional graft can be placed in the disc space anteriorly before cage insertion.
- Confirm cage position and expansion height on AP and lateral fluoroscopy before completing posterior fixation.
- The titanium endplates engage the vertebral endplates for resistance to migration and expulsion.
- For repositioning, the cage must be fully contracted before manipulation.
- Standard TLIF approach: prone positioning, facetectomy, discectomy, endplate preparation, cage insertion at contracted height, in situ expansion, posterior fixation.
- Avoid overexpansion. Controlled expansion prevents endplate fracture, but patients with poor bone quality require conservative expansion targets.

## Key Differences vs Competitors

- **vs Globus RISE:** Both are expandable TLIF cages. CONCORDE LIFT uses PEEK body with titanium endplates (partial radiolucency); RISE is all-titanium (no radiolucent window). RISE uses a drive screw mechanism with revolution-based height control; CONCORDE LIFT uses a controlled expansion mechanism. RISE offers multiple widths (8mm, 10mm, 12mm); refer to surgical technique guide for CONCORDE LIFT width options.
- **vs Medtronic Elevate:** Both are expandable TLIF cages with PEEK/titanium hybrid construction. Elevate has a bullet-tip self-distracting design and offers Standard and Extra-Lordotic variants with up to 13 degrees lordosis. CONCORDE LIFT emphasizes controlled distraction and lordosis restoration through its expansion mechanism.
- **vs Stryker Tritanium TL:** Tritanium TL is a static (non-expandable) 3D-printed porous titanium cage designed for osseointegration. CONCORDE LIFT is expandable and uses PEEK/titanium. Tritanium TL requires pre-distraction and sequential trialing; CONCORDE LIFT inserts at contracted height. Tritanium TL prioritizes biological fixation via porous surface; CONCORDE LIFT prioritizes insertion mechanics and lordosis restoration.
- **vs PEEK static cages (e.g., NuVasive CoRoent):** CONCORDE LIFT is expandable, eliminating the need for pre-distraction. Static PEEK cages require sequential trialing and disc space preparation to the target height before insertion.
- **vs lateral cages (NuVasive CoRoent XL):** CONCORDE LIFT is a posterior TLIF cage with a smaller footprint. Lateral cages have much larger footprints inserted via transpsoas approach. Different approach corridors, complication profiles, and biomechanical characteristics.
