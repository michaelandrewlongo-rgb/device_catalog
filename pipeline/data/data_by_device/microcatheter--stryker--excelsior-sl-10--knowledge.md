## Sizing/Specs

1.00 mm; 2.54 cm

## Indications

/ indications for use
Stryker Neurovascular Excelsior SL-10 Microcatheter is
intended to assist in the delivery of diagnostic agents,
such as contrast media, and therapeutic agents, such
as occlusion coils, into the peripheral, coronary, and
neurovasculature.

## Use Notes

MRI: Labeling does not contain MRI Safety Information. Pre-sterilized. Single-use

## Key Differences vs Competitors

- **Indications scope**: Excelsior SL-10 is indicated for peripheral, coronary, and neurovasculature use, while Echelon (Medtronic) is contraindicated for coronary use and Magic (Balt) is not intended for coronary use.
- **Compatibility with liquid embolics**: Excelsior SL-10's labeling does not specify DMSO/liquid embolic compatibility, whereas Echelon (Medtronic) and Headway Duo (Microvention) are explicitly DMSO-compatible for Onyx/Trufill delivery.
- **Inner diameter specifications**: Excelsior SL-10's inner diameter is not specified, while Echelon 14 (Medtronic) has 0.017" ID and Excelsior XT-27 has 0.027" ID for stent delivery.
- **Pressure limitations**: Excelsior SL-10 has no specified maximum infusion pressure, while Echelon (Medtronic) has a maximum of 600 PSI to prevent rupture.
