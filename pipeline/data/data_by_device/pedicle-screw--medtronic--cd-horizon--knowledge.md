# CD HORIZON Spinal System (Medtronic / Sofamor Danek)

## What It Is

The CD HORIZON is Medtronic's flagship posterior thoracolumbosacral pedicle screw and rod system, encompassing multiple sub-platforms (SOLERA, SOLERA VOYAGER, LONGITUDE, LEGACY). The system consists of rods, hooks, screws (including Awl Tap Screws/ATS and Cannulated Multi-Axial Screws/MAS), CROSSLINK plates, staples, and connecting components that can be rigidly locked into a variety of configurations tailored to each case. It is compatible with SEXTANT percutaneous instrumentation, IPC POWEREASE powered instruments, and StealthStation navigated instruments. The SPIRE plate provides single-level non-pedicle spinous process fixation (T1-S1).

## Sizing/Specs

### Screw Sub-Systems (by rod diameter)
- **SOLERA 5.5/6.0mm:** Standard platform, accepts 5.5mm and 6.0mm rods. Includes Awl Tap Screws (ATS), Capped Rods, Percutaneous Rods
- **SOLERA VOYAGER 4.75mm:** Smaller profile, 4.75mm rod diameter. Includes ATS screws
- **SOLERA VOYAGER 5.5/6.0mm:** Combined VOYAGER and SOLERA sizing. Includes Cannulated Multi-Axial Screws (MAS), ATS, Capped Rods, Percutaneous Rods, Tab Extenders, Extender Cap

### Rods
- 5.5mm and 6.0mm diameter (SOLERA)
- 4.75mm diameter (SOLERA VOYAGER)
- 3.5mm diameter (pediatric indications)
- PEEK rods available (not for DDD in LEGACY/3.5mm)
- Prebent rods available
- Can connect to VERTEX Reconstruction System via VERTEX rod connector for occipitocervical extension

### Materials
- Titanium alloy (ASTM F136), commercially pure titanium
- PEEK rods
- Stainless steel instruments

## Indications

**Adult (skeletally mature):**
- Degenerative disc disease
- Spondylolisthesis
- Trauma (fracture or dislocation)
- Spinal stenosis
- Curvatures (scoliosis, kyphosis, lordosis)
- Tumor
- Pseudarthrosis
- Failed previous fusion
- Posterior, non-cervical fixation (with or without SEXTANT)
- Hooks may also be used in anterolateral thoracic/lumbar approaches

**Pediatric:**
- Adolescent idiopathic scoliosis (3.5mm rods, posterior approach only)
- Spondylolysis/spondylolisthesis
- Fracture caused by tumor and/or trauma
- Pseudarthrosis
- Posterior pedicle screw fixation only, with autograft/allograft

**SPIRE Plate:**
- Single-level posterior non-pedicle supplemental fixation (T1-S1)
- DDD, spondylolisthesis, trauma, tumor
- Attaches to spinous processes

## Contraindications

- Active infection at the operative site
- Severe osteoporosis or osteopenia that would compromise implant fixation
- Patient conditions precluding benefit of spinal fixation (e.g., morbid obesity, pregnancy, mental illness, substance abuse affecting compliance)
- Inadequate tissue coverage of the implant
- Known allergy to titanium or implant materials
- Any condition that would compromise the ability of the implant to provide stability
- Fever or elevated WBC count suggestive of active systemic infection
- PEEK rods: not indicated for degenerative disc disease in the LEGACY 3.5mm platform
- Pediatric cases: PEEK rods, Shape Memory Alloy Staples, SPIRE Plates, and DYNALOK bolts are excluded

## Compatible With

- **SEXTANT** percutaneous MIS instrumentation
- **IPC POWEREASE** powered instrument system (for drilling, tapping, screw driving)
- **StealthStation** surgical navigation (navigated taps and screwdrivers)
- **VERTEX Reconstruction System** (via rod connector, for occipitocervical constructs)
- **CROSSLINK** plates (transverse connectors)
- **CD HORIZON LEGACY 3.5mm** rods and PEEK rods (same indications except DDD)

## Use Notes

- Awl Tap Screws (ATS) combine pedicle access and screw insertion into one step, potentially reducing operative time
- Cannulated Multi-Axial Screws (MAS) allow K-wire guided placement
- Percutaneous rods and capped rods available for MIS approaches
- Tab Extenders and Extender Cap provide extended towers for percutaneous rod passage
- Navigated driver is compatible with both StealthStation and IPC POWEREASE simultaneously
- Cadaver validation confirmed ATS devices can be used percutaneously

## Key Differences vs Competitors

- **vs DePuy Synthes EXPEDIUM VERSE:** CD HORIZON is the older, broader platform family (encompasses SOLERA, VOYAGER, LEGACY sub-systems). EXPEDIUM VERSE is a more unified single platform with integrated MIS capability. CD HORIZON requires the separate SEXTANT system for percutaneous work. CD HORIZON has more sub-system options (4.75mm VOYAGER, 3.5mm LEGACY for pediatric) giving broader sizing flexibility.
- **vs Stryker Xia 3:** Xia 3 is more deformity-focused with SUK DVR and 8 rod reduction options. CD HORIZON is a broader platform covering degenerative, trauma, tumor, and deformity. CD HORIZON integrates with StealthStation navigation and IPC POWEREASE powered instruments, which Xia 3 does not. Both accept 5.5/6.0mm rods.
- **vs Globus REVERE:** REVERE uses a non-threaded locking cap (faster, no cross-threading). CD HORIZON uses break-off set screws (built-in torque confirmation). REVERE has the Addition/RESCUE revision extension system. CD HORIZON has CROSSLINK plates, VERTEX connector for occipitocervical extension, and SPIRE spinous process plate.
- **vs NuVasive Reline:** Reline is an open-only thoracolumbosacral system. CD HORIZON covers open + MIS (via SEXTANT) + navigation (StealthStation). CD HORIZON has broader pediatric clearances and the 4.75mm VOYAGER option for smaller anatomy.
- **vs Neo Medical Neo PSS:** Neo offers radical simplification (14 screws, 1 kg instrument set). CD HORIZON is the opposite: maximum system depth and configurability across multiple sub-platforms, at the cost of inventory complexity.
