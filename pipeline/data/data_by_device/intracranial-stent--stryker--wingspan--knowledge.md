# Wingspan Stent System (Stryker Neurovascular)

**Manufacturer:** Stryker Neurovascular
**Category:** Intracranial Stent

## What It Is

[NEEDS CONTENT - scraper returned placeholder only; enrich from IFU PDF: Wingspan-Gateway_DFU_US_Mar19.pdf]

The Wingspan Stent System is used with the Gateway PTA Balloon Catheter for treatment of intracranial atherosclerotic disease. The stent is self-expanding and designed to maintain lumen diameter in intracranial vessels following balloon angioplasty. Unlike stent-assisted coiling devices (e.g., LVIS, Neuroform Atlas), the Wingspan is indicated specifically for intracranial atherosclerotic stenosis, not aneurysm treatment.

## Indications

The Wingspan Stent System with Gateway PTA Balloon Catheter is authorized by federal law for use in improving cerebral artery lumen diameter in patients 22 to 80 years old with:

- Recurrent stroke (2 or more strokes) refractory to a comprehensive regimen of medical therapy
- Atherosclerotic disease of intracranial vessels with 70-99% stenosis
- Vessels accessible to the system
- Most recent stroke occurred more than 7 days prior to treatment
- Modified Rankin Score (mRS) of 3 or less at the time of treatment
