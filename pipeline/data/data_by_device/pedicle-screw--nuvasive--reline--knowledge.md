# Reline Spinal Fixation System (NuVasive)

**Manufacturer:** NuVasive (now part of Globus Medical following the 2023 merger)
**Category:** Pedicle Screw System

## What It Is

The Reline Spinal Fixation System is NuVasive's open posterior thoracolumbosacral pedicle screw and rod platform. The system features polyaxial and monoaxial screws with a low-profile top-loading tulip design and a top-loading set screw closure. It is designed for ease of rod capture and reduction across degenerative, deformity, trauma, and tumor indications. The system accepts titanium and cobalt-chromium rods in 3.5mm, 5.5mm, and 6.0mm diameters.

## Sizing/Specs

### Screw Types
- Polyaxial screws
- Monoaxial screws
- Reduction screws (extended tulip for rod reduction)

### Rod Diameters and Materials
- 3.5mm diameter (titanium, pediatric/smaller constructs)
- 5.5mm diameter (titanium alloy, CoCr)
- 6.0mm diameter (titanium alloy, CoCr)

### Materials
- Titanium alloy screws (Ti-6Al-4V)
- Cobalt-chromium rods (ASTM F1537)
- Titanium alloy rods

### Closure
- Top-loading set screw
- Low-profile tulip design for reduced soft tissue irritation

## Indications

- Degenerative disc disease
- Spondylolisthesis
- Trauma (fracture or dislocation)
- Spinal stenosis
- Curvatures (scoliosis, kyphosis, lordosis)
- Tumor
- Pseudarthrosis
- Failed previous fusion
- Thoracic, lumbar, and sacral spine
- Posterior approach, as an adjunct to fusion with autograft and/or allograft

## Contraindications

- Active infection at the operative site
- Severe osteoporosis or osteopenia that would compromise implant fixation
- Patient conditions precluding benefit of spinal fixation (e.g., morbid obesity, pregnancy, mental illness, substance abuse affecting compliance)
- Inadequate tissue coverage of the implant
- Known allergy to titanium or cobalt-chromium alloy
- Any condition that would compromise the ability of the implant to provide stability
- Fever or elevated WBC count suggestive of active systemic infection

## Compatible With

- **NuVasive Precept MIS System** (percutaneous screws for MIS approaches, separate instrumentation)
- **NuVasive XLIF interbody cages** (lateral interbody fusion)
- **NuVasive ALIF and TLIF interbody cages**
- **NuVasive Pulse navigation/monitoring platform**
- Autograft and/or allograft

*Note: Following the 2023 NuVasive-Globus Medical merger, cross-compatibility with Globus systems should be verified per current IFU documentation.*

## Use Notes

- Low-profile tulip design facilitates rod capture, particularly in patients with limited posterior soft tissue coverage
- Reduction screws available for cases where the rod does not seat easily into the screw head
- The 3.5mm rod option provides a lower-stiffness construct for pediatric cases or dynamic stabilization strategies
- CoCr rods offer higher fatigue strength than titanium for long constructs or heavy-demand patients
- Open system only; for MIS percutaneous approaches, use the NuVasive Precept system
- Rod contouring follows standard principles: avoid reverse bending, use incremental small bends
- Following the NuVasive-Globus merger, the Reline system may be phased into the Globus portfolio; verify current availability with the distributor

## Key Differences vs Competitors

- **vs Medtronic CD HORIZON / SOLERA:** SOLERA is a broader ecosystem with navigation (StealthStation), powered instruments (POWEREASE), and multiple sub-systems. Reline is simpler and more focused. SOLERA has color-coded screws and break-off torque-confirming set screws. Reline is known for ease of rod capture with its low-profile tulip.
- **vs DePuy Synthes EXPEDIUM VERSE:** VERSE offers integrated open + MIS in one platform; Reline is open-only (Precept covers MIS separately). VERSE has broader pediatric indications (neuromuscular, congenital scoliosis). Reline's low-profile tulip and ease of use are its primary differentiators.
- **vs Stryker Xia 3:** Xia 3 is deformity-focused with SUK DVR, uniplanar screws, and 8 rod reduction options. Reline is a general-purpose platform without specialized deformity correction instrumentation.
- **vs Globus REVERE:** REVERE uses a non-threaded 90-degree locking cap (no cross-threading risk). Reline uses standard set screws. REVERE has the Addition/RESCUE revision extension system. Following the NuVasive-Globus merger, these platforms now share a parent company and may eventually converge.
- **vs Neo Medical Neo PSS:** Neo offers radical inventory simplification (14 screws, single-use instruments, 1 kg kit). Reline is a conventional full-tray system with broader screw and rod options. Neo targets ambulatory surgery centers; Reline targets traditional hospital ORs.
