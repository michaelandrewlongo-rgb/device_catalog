# MicroPlex Coil System - Cosmos 10 / HydroFrame 10

**Manufacturer:** MicroVention, Inc.
**Category:** Embolic Coil

## What It Is

Platinum-alloy detachable embolization coils for endovascular occlusion of intracranial aneurysms and neurovascular malformations. Two coil types share the same 3D spherical framing geometry and are delivered via the same V-Trak pusher system with battery-powered V-Grip Detachment Controller (sold separately):

- **MCS Cosmos 10:** Bare platinum alloy coil with a PET stretch-resistant member and 3D spherical secondary shape. Designed to frame the aneurysm sac.
- **HES HydroFrame 10:** Platinum alloy coil with an additional hydrophilic acrylic copolymer hydrogel core running parallel to the stretch-resistant member. The hydrogel expands after deployment to improve aneurysm filling density beyond what the bare platinum scaffold alone provides.

Both variants include a PET filar for stretch resistance, are available in multiple loop sizes and lengths, and detach upon activation of the V-Grip Detachment Controller.

## Indications

Intended for endovascular embolization of:
- Intracranial aneurysms
- Arteriovenous malformations (AVMs)
- Arteriovenous fistulae (AVFs)
- Vascular occlusion within the neurovascular system to permanently obstruct blood flow to an aneurysm or vascular malformation
- Arterial and venous embolization in the peripheral vasculature

## Use Notes

**FDA Recalls (active at time of data extraction, 16 events):** Multiple recall actions involving missing implant coils in packaged units. Verify contents against label before opening for patient use. Do not use if the implant coil is absent from the dispenser package.

## Key Differences vs Competitors

- **Hydrogel core availability**: The target device offers both bare platinum (MCS Cosmos 10) and hydrogel-expandable (HES HydroFrame 10) variants in the same 3D spherical framing geometry, whereas competitor devices like the Axium family offer hydrogel as an optional feature (e.g., Axium Nylon/PGLA coils) but not as a core variant with identical framing shape.
- **Coil implant diameter range**: The target device's specific configuration has a narrow implant diameter range of 2.0-2.5 mm (cleared under K090891), while the HydroCoil Embolic System (HES) by MicroVention offers a broader range of 1-12 mm, and Axium coils range from 1.5-25 mm depending on variant.
- **Delivery pusher length**: The target device uses a 185 cm pusher, which is shorter than the HydroCoil Embolic System's 195 cm pusher but comparable to other MicroPlex variants; Axium devices use a composite delivery wire with length not specified.
- **Detachment mechanism**: The target device requires the V-Grip Detachment Controller (battery-powered, heats filament), whereas Axium coils use a mechanical Instant Detacher (I.D.) unit without electrolytic current.
- **Recall status**: The target device has 16 active FDA recalls for missing implant coils in packaged units, a warning shared across multiple MicroPlex/HydroFrame entries but not mentioned for Axium devices in the provided data.
