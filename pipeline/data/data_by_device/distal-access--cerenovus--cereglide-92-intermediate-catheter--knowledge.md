# CEREGLIDE 92 Catheter System with TruCourse

**Manufacturer:** Cerenovus (J&J)
**Category:** Distal access catheter
510(k) K230726 | Class 2 | 2023-11-29

## What It Is

Large-bore intermediate catheter system featuring INNERGLIDE 9 Delivery Aid (co-packaged). Engineered for optimized delivery to the M1 segment with TruCourse technology for improved trackability. Features BRITE-LINE for full-length catheter visibility under fluoroscopy. Largest inner diameter with lowest distal profile of 8F systems.

## Sizing/Specs

Distal ID: 0.092 inch; Proximal OD: 0.108 inch; Guide Compatibility: 8F (long guide sheaths >= 0.113 inch ID); Usable Length: 114 cm; Product codes: IG9DA, SBC92114UG, SBC92122UG, SBC92130UG, SBC92135UG; INNERGLIDE 9 compatible with guidewires up to 0.024 inch

## Indications

Designed for acute ischemic stroke intervention with distal M1 access. Optimized for aspiration thrombectomy with reduced relay when used with CEREGLIDE 71.

## Compatible With

INNERGLIDE 9 Delivery Aid (co-packaged), CEREGLIDE 71 Intermediate Catheter, EMBOTRAP III Revascularization Device, Prowler EX Microcatheter; Compatible with commonly sold 8F long guide sheaths >= 0.113 inch ID

## Use Notes

Lower track forces than Zoom 88. Largest ID with lowest distal profile vs Zoom 88, Sofia 88, Millipede 088. Designed to diminish flow in M1 and for distal placement in M1 for reduced relay with CEREGLIDE 71.

## Key Differences vs Competitors

- **Largest inner diameter with lowest distal profile among 8F systems**: CEREGLIDE 92 (distal ID: 0.092 inch) has a larger inner diameter than CEREBASE DA (ID: 0.090 inch) and CEREGLIDE 71 (compatible with base catheters ≥0.085 inch ID). Inner diameters for AXS Catalyst 5 and Catalyst 6 are not specified.
- **Optimized for distal M1 placement with reduced relay**: Designed specifically for distal M1 access and reduced relay when used with CEREGLIDE 71, while CEREBASE DA is engineered for distal cervical positioning and CEREGLIDE 71 is optimized for direct aspiration and stent retriever passes. Use indications for AXS Catalyst devices are not specified.
- **Co-packaged delivery aid and specific guide compatibility**: Includes INNERGLIDE 9 Delivery Aid and requires 8F long guide sheaths (≥0.113 inch ID), whereas CEREBASE DA is compatible with 8F short sheaths and CEREGLIDE 71 requires base catheters ≥0.085 inch ID. Guide compatibility for AXS Catalyst devices is not specified.
- **Lower track forces than key competitor**: Has lower track forces than Zoom 88, while CEREGLIDE 71 has lower track forces than Zoom 71 and CEREBASE DA offers better trackability than NeuronMax, Infinity, Shuttle, Fubuki, and Ballast. Trackability comparisons for AXS Catalyst devices are not specified.
