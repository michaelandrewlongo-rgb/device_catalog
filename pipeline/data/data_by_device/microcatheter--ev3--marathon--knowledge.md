# Marathon Flow Directed Micro Catheter

**Manufacturer:** Medtronic (Micro Therapeutics, Inc. d/b/a ev3 Neurovascular)
**510(k):** K202318, cleared September 14, 2020; earlier clearances K093750, K034036
**Classification:** Continuous Flush Catheter, 21 CFR 870.1210, Class II, Product Code KRA, QJP

---

## What It Is

Single-lumen, flow-directed, endhole microcatheter designed for sub-selective infusion of embolization materials and contrast media in tortuous, distal vessels. The Marathon is the primary flow-directed microcatheter in the ev3/Medtronic neurovascular portfolio, used for liquid embolic delivery (Onyx, NBCA) in AVM embolization and other distal embolization procedures. Unlike over-the-wire microcatheters, the Marathon advances by blood flow carrying the flexible distal segment forward.

Key features:
- Flow-directed navigation (highly flexible distal shaft carried by blood flow)
- Semi-rigid proximal shaft for pushability
- Single radiopaque marker at distal end
- Outer coating for lubricity
- Standard luer adapter hub
- Can be used with stylet, guidewire, or introducer sheath to increase distal rigidity during introduction into the guiding catheter

## Compatible With

- Stylets, guidewires, or introducer sheaths (for initial introduction into guide catheter)

- Contrast media
- Guiding catheters accommodating the 2.7F proximal OD
- Standard luer accessories

---

## Indications

Access the peripheral and neuro vasculature for the controlled selective infusion of physician-specified therapeutic agents such as embolization materials and of diagnostic materials such as contrast media.

---

## Use Notes

- The Marathon is a **flow-directed** catheter, not a standard over-the-wire microcatheter. It relies on blood flow to carry the flexible distal tip forward into distal vessels.
- Use a stylet, guidewire, or introducer sheath to stiffen the distal segment during introduction into the guiding catheter, then remove for flow-directed navigation.
- The 1.3F distal tip and 0.013" ID make this one of the smallest-profile neurovascular microcatheters available -- suitable for reaching very distal AVM feeders.
- PTFE inner lining facilitates movement of devices through the lumen.
- The K202318 clearance was for a hub material change (Polypropylene to Trogamid) and adhesive change; no change to dimensions, performance, or indications.
- Single use only; do not reprocess or re-sterilize.
