# ShuntAssistant 2.0 (Miethke / B. Braun / Aesculap)

## What It Is

The ShuntAssistant 2.0 (SA 2.0) is a fixed-pressure gravitational CSF shunt valve designed to prevent overdrainage in the upright position. It is an inline cylindrical device made of titanium that uses a tantalum ball and sapphire ball mechanism inside a titanium housing. In the horizontal (supine) position, the gravitational unit is fully open and provides no resistance -- opening pressure is determined entirely by whatever upstream differential pressure valve it is paired with. As the patient moves toward upright, the tantalum ball activates and progressively increases the opening pressure, counteracting the hydrostatic siphon effect. The SA 2.0 is not a standalone valve; it is designed to be combined in series with a separate differential pressure valve (fixed or adjustable) as overdrainage protection.

## Settings

- 6 fixed pressure levels (selected at ordering, not adjustable after implantation):
  - 10 cmH2O
  - 15 cmH2O
  - 20 cmH2O
  - 25 cmH2O
  - 30 cmH2O
  - 35 cmH2O
- The pressure level represents the additional resistance the valve contributes in the fully upright (90 degree) position
- In supine position, the SA 2.0 contributes 0 cmH2O (fully open)

### Manufacturer Pressure Recommendations (non-binding)

| Patient Group | SA 2.0 Pressure |
|---|---|

*For patients with limited mobility or high BMI, choose a lower pressure level.

## Adjustment Method

- **Not adjustable** after implantation. The pressure level is fixed at manufacture and identified by X-ray coding on the device body.
- Pressure level is identifiable on X-ray via integrated radiopaque coding markers on the valve housing.

## MRI Safety

- **MR Conditional**
- Titanium housing prevents external and subcutaneous pressure influences
- The fixed gravitational pressure level does not change with MRI exposure (no magnets involved in pressure regulation; only tantalum and sapphire balls)
- Refer to the IFU of the specific SA 2.0 variant for detailed MR conditions

## Indications

- CSF shunting for hydrocephalus as an anti-overdrainage component
- Designed for combination with differential pressure valves (fixed or adjustable) in a two-valve shunt system
- Suitable for newborns, pediatric, and adult patients
- Available in VP configurations; LP variants also available (SA 2.0 LP Straight and SA 2.0 LP U-form for lumboperitoneal shunting)

## Contraindications

- Not for use as a standalone shunt valve (must be paired with a differential pressure valve)
- Standard shunt contraindications: active CNS infection, bacteremia, septicemia in the implantation region
- Must be implanted parallel to the patient's body axis for correct gravitational function

## Key Differences vs Competitors

- **Gravitational-only device**: Unlike M.blue or proGAV 2.0, the SA 2.0 is not a complete valve system; it is a modular gravitational add-on that must be combined with a separate DP valve
- **Fixed pressure**: No postoperative adjustment capability, unlike M.blue (0-40 cmH2O adjustable) or the Medtronic Strata Delta Chamber (which is also fixed but has a different mechanism)
- **Cylindrical inline form factor**: Slim titanium cylinder (12 mm long, 4.2 mm outer diameter) that sits inline with the catheter, unlike the disc-shaped M.blue or the flat-profile Strata
- **LP variants available**: Straight and U-form LP configurations for lumboperitoneal shunting, which is not available in most competing gravitational units
- **6 pressure levels**: More granular than the Strata Delta Chamber (which has no selectable gravitational levels) but less flexible than M.blue continuous adjustment
- **No proprietary adjustment tools needed**: Since it is fixed, no tools are required postoperatively (unlike M.blue, proGAV 2.0, or Strata systems)
