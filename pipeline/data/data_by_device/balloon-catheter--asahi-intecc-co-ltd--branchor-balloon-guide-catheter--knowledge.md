# Branchor Balloon Guide Catheter

**Manufacturer:** Asahi Intecc Co., Ltd. (Asahi Intecc USA, Inc.)
**Category:** Balloon Guide Catheter

## What It Is

Sterile, single-use balloon guide catheter for neurointerventional procedures, manufactured by Asahi Intecc (Japan). The Branchor is a variable-stiffness catheter with a braid-reinforced coaxial lumen, a polyurethane elastomer balloon at the distal end, and a branched connector (dual-port hub) at the proximal end. A radiopaque marker at the distal balloon tip facilitates fluoroscopic positioning. The outer shaft carries a hydrophilic coating for trackability when wet. The shaft lumen is lined with PTFE (except the connector section) to facilitate passage of guidewires and coaxial devices.

The subject device (K221951) is a line extension of the predicate Branchor (K203723) cleared to allow use with a power injector in addition to manual syringe injection. Design, materials, and accessories are otherwise identical to the predicate.

**Accessories included:** Peel-away sheath, 1 mL syringe for balloon inflation/deflation, three-way stopcock.

## Indications

Indicated for:
- Facilitating insertion and guidance of an intravascular catheter into a selected blood vessel in the neurovascular system
- Injection of contrast media (including via power injector)
- Temporary vascular occlusion via balloon inflation during neurointerventional procedures
- Conduit for retrieval devices (stent retrievers, aspiration catheters)

## Use Notes

The Branchor is labeled for use only by physicians adequately trained in neurointerventional procedures. Balloon dimensions and recommended inflation volume are specified on the product label; refer to labeling for procedural inflation parameters. The 3.00 mm shaft OD and 2.28 mm shaft ID are consistent across 90 cm and 100 cm working length configurations.

Power injector compatibility was the sole design change from predicate K203723; all other technological characteristics, materials, and sterilization are unchanged.

## Key Differences vs Competitors

- **Power injector compatibility**: Branchor is explicitly cleared for both manual syringe and power injector contrast injection (K221951 line extension), while EMBOGUARD, BOSS, Scepter C/XC, and Scepter Mini do not specify power injector compatibility in the provided data.
- **Shaft dimensions**: Branchor has a 3.00 mm outer diameter and 2.28 mm inner diameter, whereas EMBOGUARD has 0.112 inch (2.84 mm) OD and 0.087 inch (2.21 mm) ID, and BOSS, Scepter C/XC, and Scepter Mini do not specify shaft OD/ID.
- **Working lengths**: Branchor offers 90 cm and 100 cm lengths; EMBOGUARD offers 85 cm and 95 cm; BOSS specifies 90 cm; Scepter C/XC and Scepter Mini lengths are not specified.
- **Indication scope**: Branchor and EMBOGUARD are indicated for neurovascular use only; BOSS and both Scepter models are indicated for both neurovascular and peripheral vascular use.
- **Guidewire compatibility**: Branchor, EMBOGUARD, and BOSS do not specify guidewire size; Scepter C/XC accepts 0.014" guidewires, while Scepter Mini accepts only 0.008" guidewires for smaller vessels.
