# Phenom Microcatheter

**Manufacturer:** Medtronic (originally Cathera, Inc.)
**510(k):** K151638, cleared November 13, 2015
**Classification:** Continuous Flush Catheter, 21 CFR 870.1210, Class II, Product Code KRA

---

## What It Is

Variable-stiffness, single-lumen microcatheter for navigating small, tortuous vasculature. The Phenom is designed for delivery of interventional devices (coils, stents, flow diverters) and infusion of diagnostic/therapeutic agents. It is available in a range of lengths, stiffness profiles, and inner/outer diameters. The outer coating enhances vessel navigation, and an inner liner reduces friction for device passage.

Key features:
- Variable stiffness shaft; dual-braided proximal shaft with elite composite shaft design providing progressive flexibility
- Inner lumen engineered to prevent ovalization
- Outer surface coated for navigation
- Inner liner for device passage
- Radiopaque marker(s) at distal tip
- Soft rounded distal tip for trackability (6 cm soft distal segment)
- 15 cm flexible single coil segment proximal to distal soft zone
- Available in multiple length, stiffness, and diameter configurations (Phenom 17, 21, 27)
- Steam-shapeable (tip shapeability tested)
- Single use only

## Specs

Three standard configurations are available:

| Model | OD (proximal > distal) | ID | Working Length | Max Guidewire | Tip Shapes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Phenom 17 | 2.2 > 1.8 Fr | 0.017 in | 150 cm | 0.014 in | Straight, J, 45°, 90° |
| Phenom 21 | 2.6 > 2.3 Fr | 0.021 in | 150–160 cm | 0.018 in | (not specified in source) |
| Phenom 27 | 3.1 > 2.8 Fr | 0.027 in | 150 cm | 0.025 in | (not specified in source) |

All variants share:
- 6 cm soft distal segment
- 15 cm flexible single coil segment

Additional tested characteristics (per 510(k) submission):
- Dimensional inspection (OD, ID, length, distal tip configuration)
- Device compatibility with guide catheter, guidewire, and RHV
- Kink resistance
- Burst pressure
- Flexural fatigue
- Torque strength
- Outer surface coating lubricity and durability
- Chemical compatibility with saline and contrast medium
- Corrosion resistance

**Sterilization:** Ethylene Oxide (ISO 11135)

- Therapeutic agents
- Interventional devices (embolic coils, intracranial stents, flow diverters)

## Indications

Introduction of interventional devices and infusion of diagnostic or therapeutic agents into the neuro, peripheral, and coronary vasculatures. Clinically applied for device deployment during hemorrhagic and acute ischemic stroke procedures.

---

## Use Notes

- **Phenom 17:** Designed for coiling procedures; 0.017 in ID optimized for low coil delivery force and accommodates standard coil systems
- **Phenom 21:** For stent and flow diverter deployment (e.g., intracranial stents, Pipeline Flex)
- **Phenom 27:** For large device deployment requiring 0.027 in ID working channel
- Performance was evaluated in neurovascular anatomy as the worst-case (most demanding) representation of cardiac and peripheral vascular anatomies
- Design variations in flexibility, coated segment length, and lubricity are optimized for specific applications (infusion vs. device delivery)
- Single use only; do not reprocess
