# CREO 5.5 Stabilization System

**Manufacturer:** Globus Medical
**Category:** Pedicle screw system / Thoracolumbar

## What It Is

Complete thoracolumbar posterior stabilization system with low-profile design and non-threaded locking mechanism. The CREO 5.5 is the full-system platform integrating multiple procedure-specific variants under one implant family. Non-threaded locking cap eliminates cross-threading risk. Available in titanium and cobalt-chromium (CREO CoCr). Procedurally integrated with ExcelsiusGPS robotic navigation.

Variants:
- **CREO MIS** — percutaneous, cannulated, extended-tab screws for MIS TLIF
- **CREO MCS** — cortical screw trajectory
- **CREO AMP** — advanced modular platform with QUAD connector options for complex constructs
- **CREO NXT** — non-threaded CoCr option for deformity
- **CREO DLX** — accepts 6.0/6.35 mm rods for high-demand deformity
- **CREO ONE Robotic Screw** — ExcelsiusGPS-integrated robotic placement
- **CREO Side Loading** — side-loading head for difficult-access levels

## Sizing/Specs

- Standard rod diameter: 5.5 mm
- DLX rod diameter: 6.0 mm / 6.35 mm
- Materials: Ti-6Al-4V titanium alloy and cobalt-chromium alloy rod options
- Non-threaded locking closure (90-degree rotation capture)

## Indications

Thoracolumbar posterior stabilization for degenerative, deformity, tumor, and trauma indications.

## Compatible With

- ExcelsiusGPS navigation and robotic guidance
- Bendini rod bending system
- CREO Derotation Instruments
- CREO AMP QUAD cross-connectors

## Use Notes

Non-threaded locking cap reduces closure time and eliminates cross-threading. CREO ONE Robotic Screw is designed for autonomous screw placement with ExcelsiusGPS. CREO AMP QUAD connectors enable accessory fixation to adjacent CREO constructs for complex deformity. CREO MIS screws are compatible with Globus REVERE Addition revision extension clamps.

For percutaneous MIS TLIF-specific technique and instrumentation details, see the dedicated entry: `pedicle-screw--globus--creo-mis--knowledge.md`.

## Key Differences vs Competitors

- The CREO 5.5 system features a non-threaded locking mechanism, which is a key differentiator compared to most other systems that typically use threaded caps (e.g., CD HORIZON, EXPEDIUM VERSE, Everest Deformity). This design aims to reduce closure time and eliminate cross-threading.
- CREO 5.5 is deeply integrated with the ExcelsiusGPS robotic navigation system, including the CREO ONE Robotic Screw for autonomous placement, offering a higher level of robotic integration than systems like Reline Open Fixation System or CD HORIZON which are "Compatible With" navigation but don't specify autonomous screw placement.
- The CREO 5.5 system offers a specific variant, CREO DLX, which accepts larger rod diameters (6.0 mm / 6.35 mm) for high-demand deformity cases, while most other systems (e.g., ES2 MIS, CREO MIS) primarily use 5.5 mm rods, or offer 6.0 mm as an alternative (e.g., Everest Deformity, CD HORIZON SOLERA).
- CREO 5.5 includes the CREO AMP variant with QUAD connectors for complex constructs, providing advanced modularity for accessory fixation not explicitly detailed in other systems like ES2 MIS or CREO MIS.
- Unlike revision-specific systems like Revere Addition, CREO 5.5 is a primary stabilization system with variants for MIS, cortical, and deformity, but its hardware is compatible with Revere Addition for future revisions.
