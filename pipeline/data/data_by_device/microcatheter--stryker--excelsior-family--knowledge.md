# Excelsior Microcatheter Family

**Manufacturer:** Stryker Neurovascular (originally Boston Scientific Target for legacy variants)
**Category:** Microcatheter
**Variants:** SL-10, XT-17, XT-27, 1018
**510(k) (SL-10):** K013789, cleared December 6, 2001 (Special 510(k) device modification)
**Classification:** Percutaneous Catheter, 21 CFR 870.1250, Class II

---

## What It Is

The Excelsior family is a line of single-lumen neurovascular microcatheters from Stryker for navigating distal vasculature over a guide catheter and guidewire. All variants share a common design philosophy: graded durometer construction (flexible at the tip, semi-rigid proximally), stainless steel wire braid reinforcement for torque transmission and kink resistance, hydrophilic outer coating to reduce friction, and a steam-shapeable distal tip.

Variants are differentiated by inner diameter and target application:

- **Excelsior 1018** -- Legacy 2F workhorse; the original platform, predicate for the SL-10. Designed for coil embolization. DFU revised June 2013.
- **Excelsior SL-10** -- Reduced-profile 1.7F successor to the 1018. The most widely used neurovascular microcatheter for aneurysm coil embolization. DFU revised March 2019.
- **Excelsior XT-17** -- Intermediate 0.017" ID catheter. Bridges the SL-10 and XT-27 in lumen size; used for coil delivery in larger targets and selected stent-assisted procedures. DFU revised July 2019.
- **Excelsior XT-27** -- Larger 0.027" ID catheter. Primary delivery catheter for intracranial stents, flow diverters, and WEB devices requiring a larger lumen. Also used for coil embolization of larger aneurysms. DFU revised May 2019.

Key shared features:
- One or two radiopaque tip markers for fluoroscopic visualization
- Luer fitting hub for accessory attachment
- Hydrophilic outer coating (must be kept hydrated)
- Steam-shapeable distal tip with included mandrel
- Strain reliefs at proximal hub for kink resistance
- Single-use only

## Indications

**Excelsior SL-10 and 1018 (coil/contrast catheters):**
Delivery of diagnostic agents (such as contrast media) and therapeutic agents (such as occlusion coils) into the peripheral, coronary, and neurovasculature.

**Excelsior XT-17:**
Delivery of diagnostic agents (such as contrast media) and therapeutic agents (such as occlusion coils) into the peripheral, coronary, and neurovasculature.

**Excelsior XT-27:**
Delivery of diagnostic agents (such as contrast media), therapeutic agents, and non-liquid interventional devices (such as stents) that are indicated for use in the neurovasculature and with a catheter of 0.027 inches in inner diameter.

Note: The XT-27 indication explicitly includes stent delivery and is scoped to the neurovasculature; the SL-10/XT-17/1018 indications also cover peripheral and coronary use.

## Compatible With

- Standard guide catheters and distal access catheters matched to each variant's OD profile
- Occlusion coils delivered through the lumen (all variants)
- Intracranial stents requiring 0.027" ID microcatheter (XT-27 specifically; consult each stent's IFU for required catheter ID)
- Contrast media for diagnostic angiography
- Steam shaping mandrel (included with each device)
- Standard neurovascular guidewires (e.g., Synchro-14 for SL-10/1018, Synchro-14 or larger for XT-17/XT-27)

---

## Use Notes

- Tip is steam-shapeable using the included mandrel; customary shapes include straight, 45-degree, and 90-degree curves to match target anatomy
- Hydrophilic coating must be kept hydrated for lubricity -- attach Y-connector to continuous saline flush; do not allow to dry
- Single use only; do not reprocess or resterilize
- SL-10: predicate equivalence to Tracker-10 and Excelsior 1018 established (510(k) K013789)
- XT-27: the named 0.027" ID matches the delivery lumen required by several Stryker stent and flow diverter systems (verify compatibility in each stent IFU)
- Introduce over a compatible guidewire through a suitably sized guide or intermediate catheter; do not advance without wire visualization

## Key Differences vs Competitors

| Feature | Stryker SL-10 | Stryker XT-17 | Stryker XT-27 | Medtronic Echelon-10 | Medtronic Marksman | MicroVention Headway 17 | MicroVention Headway 27 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ID | 0.016" | 0.017" | 0.027" | 0.017" | 0.027" | 0.017" | 0.027" |
| Primary use | Coil embolization | Coil; stent-assisted | Stent/FD delivery | Coil embolization | Stent/FD delivery | Coil; stent-assisted | Stent/FD delivery |
| Tip shape | Steam-shapeable | Steam-shapeable | Steam-shapeable | Steam-shapeable | Steam-shapeable | Steam-shapeable | Steam-shapeable |
| Coating | Hydrophilic outer | Hydrophilic outer | Hydrophilic outer | Hydrophilic | Hydrophilic | Hydrophilic | Hydrophilic |

The SL-10 is the smallest-profile catheter in this set at 1.7F / 0.031" OD; this makes it preferred for highly tortuous distal anatomy where the Echelon-10 (similar ID but slightly different OD) is the primary competitor. The XT-27 and Marksman/Headway-27 are functionally equivalent 0.027" workhorse catheters for stent and flow diverter delivery; preference is largely institutional and operator-dependent.
