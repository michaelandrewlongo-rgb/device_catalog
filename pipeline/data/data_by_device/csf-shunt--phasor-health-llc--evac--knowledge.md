Indications for Use (Describe)
Phasor EVACTM is indicated when access to and evacuation of a cranial subacute or chronic
hematoma or hygroma is necessary. The EVAC system is intended for drainage of subdural fluid
accumulations such as hygromas and chronic or subacute hematomas to an external suction
reservoir. The EVAC system is also intended for draining air and fluids from the subdural space
immediately following craniotomy procedures performed to remove a chronic or subacute subdural
hematoma.

## Key Differences vs Competitors

- **Primary function differs**: EVAC is designed for drainage of subdural fluid accumulations (hygromas, chronic/subacute hematomas) and air/fluid after craniotomy procedures, while all other listed devices (proGAV 2.0, M.blue, CERTAS Plus, Strata, etc.) are CSF shunt systems for hydrocephalus management.
- **Application site differs**: EVAC targets subdural space drainage to an external suction reservoir, whereas competitor devices shunt cerebrospinal fluid from ventricles to peritoneum or lumbar space.
- **Adjustability differs**: EVAC appears to be a non-adjustable drainage system (no pressure settings specified), while most competitors offer programmable/adjustable pressure settings (proGAV 2.0, M.blue, CERTAS Plus, Strata).
- **MRI compatibility**: EVAC labeling does not contain MRI safety information (similar to SHUNTCHECK), while MRI compatibility for other devices is not specified.
- **Recall status**: EVAC has no specified recalls, while Aesculap devices (proGAV 2.0, M.blue) have 6 active recalls related to labeling and catheter deflector issues.
