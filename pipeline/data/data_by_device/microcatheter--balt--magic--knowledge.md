## Indications

MAGIC Infusion Catheter (K202366)
The MAGIC Infusion Catheter is intended for regional infusion of contrast materials into selected vessels in the neurovasculature. The MAGIC Infusion Catheter may be used for controlled, regional infusion into selected vessels and is not intended for use in the coronary vasculature.

## Key Differences vs Competitors

- **Indications**: Magic is specifically indicated for regional infusion of contrast materials into selected neurovasculature vessels. In contrast, the Echelon and Excelsior families (except some Excelsior models) are indicated for both diagnostic (contrast) and therapeutic (e.g., occlusion coils, embolic materials) agent delivery in neuro and/or peripheral vasculature. The Headway Duo is indicated for both diagnostic and therapeutic agent infusion in peripheral, coronary, and neurovasculature.
- **Compatibility & Construction**: Magic's compatibility details (e.g., guidewire size, specific therapeutic agents) and construction materials are not specified. Competitors provide details: Echelon is nitinol-braided and DMSO-compatible for liquid embolics like Onyx; Excelsior models use stainless steel braid reinforcement and are steam-shapeable; Headway Duo is DMSO-compatible for liquid embolics and has dual radiopaque markers.
- **Key Use Specifications**: Magic's maximum infusion pressure and detailed sizing/specs (e.g., inner/outer diameter, length) are not specified. For competitors: Echelon has a max infusion pressure of 600 PSI and specified models (Echelon 10/14); Excelsior models have specified sizing (e.g., SL-10 has 1.7F OD); Headway Duo's sizing is not specified.
- **MRI Safety Information**: Magic, Excelsior SL-10, Excelsior XT-17, and Excelsior XT-27 labeling does not contain MRI safety information. MRI safety information for Echelon and Headway Duo is not specified.
