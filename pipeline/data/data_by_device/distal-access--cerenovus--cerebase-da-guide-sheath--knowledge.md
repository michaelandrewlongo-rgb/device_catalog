# CEREBASE DA Guide Sheath

**Manufacturer:** Cerenovus (J&J)
**Category:** Distal access catheter

## What It Is

Guide sheath engineered for distal access with geometric anchoring for procedural support. Features Dex tip for enhanced tracking through challenging anatomy. Large inner diameter (0.090 inch) in an 8F short sheath compatible system.

## Sizing/Specs

Inner Diameter: 0.090 inch; Outer Diameter: 8F; Short Sheath Compatibility: 8F; Usable Lengths: 80 cm, 90 cm, 95 cm; Product codes: GS9080SD, GS9090SD, GS9095SD

## Use Notes

Better trackability and more support than NeuronMax, Infinity, Shuttle, Fubuki, and Ballast when placed in a distal cervical position.

## Key Differences vs Competitors

- **Device Type & Primary Function**: CEREBASE DA is a guide sheath for distal access with geometric anchoring, while CEREGLIDE 71/92 and AXS Catalyst are intermediate catheters or distal access catheters optimized for aspiration thrombectomy in stroke intervention.
- **Inner Diameter & Size Profile**: CEREBASE DA has a 0.090 inch ID in an 8F short sheath system. CEREGLIDE 71 has an ID not specified but is compatible with base catheters ≥0.085 inch. CEREGLIDE 92 has a 0.092 inch distal ID. AXS Catalyst sizes are not specified.
- **Key Design & Performance Claims**: CEREBASE DA offers better trackability and more support than NeuronMax, Infinity, Shuttle, Fubuki, and Ballast in distal cervical placement. CEREGLIDE 71 is more trackable than Sofia Plus, React 71, Catalyst 6, and Large Bore Catheter, with lower track forces than Zoom 71. CEREGLIDE 92 has lower track forces than Zoom 88 and the largest ID with lowest distal profile vs Zoom 88, Sofia 88, Millipede 088.
- **Indications & Procedural Role**: CEREBASE DA is a guide sheath for distal access support. CEREGLIDE 71/92 are for acute ischemic stroke intervention with aspiration thrombectomy. AXS Catalyst is for thrombus removal in neurovasculature using continuous aspiration.
- **Compatibility Notes**: CEREBASE DA is compatible with 8F short sheaths. CEREGLIDE 71 is compatible with CEREBASE DA and other CERENOVUS system components. CEREGLIDE 92 is compatible with 8F long guide sheaths ≥0.113 inch ID. AXS Catalyst requires compatible sheath/guide catheter and specific aspiration pump.
