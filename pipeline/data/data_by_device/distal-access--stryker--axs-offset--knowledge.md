# AXS Offset

**Manufacturer:** Stryker
**Category:** Distal access catheter

## What It Is

Read Now

## Sizing/Specs

80cm; 30cm

## Indications

The AXS Offset Delivery Assist Catheter is intended to assist in the delivery of interventional devices, such as
distal access catheters, in the neurovasculature.

## Use Notes

MRI: Labeling does not contain MRI Safety Information. Pre-sterilized. Single-use

## Key Differences vs Competitors

- **Primary function differs**: AXS Offset is a delivery assist catheter for interventional devices, while competitors like AXS Catalyst catheters are designed for thrombus removal via aspiration, and CEREBASE DA/CEREGLIDE 71 are guide sheaths/intermediate catheters for distal access and stroke intervention.
- **MRI safety varies**: AXS Offset labeling lacks MRI safety information, whereas AXS Catalyst 5 and 6 are MR Conditional, and Catalyst 7 also lacks MRI safety info (similar to Offset). Competitor MRI status is not specified.
- **Key specifications differ**: AXS Offset has specified lengths (80cm; 30cm) but inner/outer diameters are not specified, unlike CEREBASE DA (0.090 inch ID, 8F OD) and CEREGLIDE 71 (compatible with bases ≥0.085 inch ID).
- **Indications are device-specific**: AXS Offset assists device delivery in neurovasculature, while AXS Catalyst catheters are indicated for revascularization in acute ischemic stroke, and CEREGLIDE 71 is optimized for stroke intervention with aspiration/stent retriever compatibility.

## Also Known As

AXS Offset, Vascular guide-catheter, single-use
