# Esperance Distal Access Catheter

**Manufacturer:** Wallaby Medical
**Category:** Distal Access Catheter

## What It Is

Single-use, single-lumen, variable-stiffness composite catheter for distal access in the peripheral and neurovasculature. Available in 5F and 6F outer profiles. Each size has a semi-rigid proximal shaft that transitions to a flexible distal shaft, facilitating advancement through tortuous anatomy. The distal shaft has an external hydrophilic coating to reduce friction. The distal tip is radiopaque for fluoroscopic visualization. The proximal end includes a strain relief and a standard Luer adapter. Supplied with a peelable introducer sheath and a shaping mandrel.

The Esperance Distal Access Catheter shares its predicate lineage with the React 71 Catheter (Medtronic/ev3) and is the non-aspiration companion to the Esperance Aspiration Catheter System from the same manufacturer.

## Indications

Indicated for the introduction of interventional devices into the peripheral and neurovasculature.

## Use Notes

- Supplied with a peelable introducer sheath to facilitate insertion through a hemostatic valve; remove the introducer sheath once the distal shaft is inside the patient.
- The shaping mandrel allows pre-procedural distal tip shaping before introduction.
- Three working lengths (115, 125, 131 cm) accommodate different patient anatomy and access routes.
- The 5F/0.055 inch ID version is sized for microcatheter delivery in distal intracranial targets. The 6F/0.071 inch ID version accommodates larger devices or provides more aspiration capacity.
- Single use only.

## Key Differences vs Competitors

- **Indications**: The Esperance Distal Access Catheter is indicated for the introduction of interventional devices into the peripheral and neurovasculature, while the AXS Catalyst Distal Access Catheter is specifically indicated for revascularization in acute ischemic stroke secondary to intracranial large vessel occlusive disease.
- **Aspiration capability**: The Esperance is described as a non-aspiration companion to the Esperance Aspiration Catheter System, whereas the AXS Catalyst Distal Access Catheter is designed for continuous aspiration as part of the AXS Universal Aspiration System.
- **MRI compatibility**: MRI compatibility is not specified for the Esperance, while the AXS Catalyst 5 and 6 are MR Conditional, and MRI safety information is not contained in labeling for the AXS Catalyst 7.
- **Compatibility requirements**: The Esperance has no specified compatibility requirements beyond standard use, while the AXS Catalyst Distal Access Catheter requires specific compatible components including the Medela Dominant Flex Pump, AXS Universal Aspiration Tubing, and AXS Universal Liner Set.
- **Recall history**: The Esperance has no specified recall history, while the AXS Vecta 46 has an FDA recall history of 3 events.
