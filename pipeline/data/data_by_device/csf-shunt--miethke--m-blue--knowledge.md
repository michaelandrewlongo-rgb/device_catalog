# M.blue Adjustable Shunt System (Miethke / B. Braun / Aesculap)

## What It Is

The M.blue is a posture-dependent CSF shunt valve made from titanium that combines two functional units in a single disc-shaped housing: an adjustable gravitational unit and a fixed differential pressure unit. In the horizontal position, the gravitational unit is open and contributes no resistance, so only the fixed differential pressure unit governs drainage. When the patient moves upright, the gravitational unit closes proportionally via a tantalum weight acting on a sapphire ball-in-cone mechanism, increasing total opening pressure to prevent overdrainage. The adjustable gravitational unit uses a rotor with magnets whose pretension on a torsion spring can be changed transcutaneously, allowing the upright-position opening pressure to be tuned without surgery. The differential pressure unit uses a separate micro-coil spring and sapphire ball mechanism that is fixed at implantation.

## Settings

### Adjustable Gravitational Unit
- Range: 0 to 40 cmH2O (continuously variable)
- Factory preset: 20 cmH2O
- Adjustment: maximum change of 16 cmH2O per single adjustment step (larger changes require staged adjustments)

### Fixed Differential Pressure Unit (selected at ordering)
- Available levels: 0, 5, 10, or 15 cmH2O
- Not adjustable after implantation
- Default recommendation: 5 cmH2O

### Combined Opening Pressure
- Horizontal (supine): equals the differential pressure unit setting only (gravitational unit is open)
- Vertical (upright): equals differential pressure unit + gravitational unit setting
- Example: DP unit = 5, gravitational unit = 20 gives 5 cmH2O supine, 25 cmH2O upright

## Adjustment Method

### Tools Required
Three instruments are available (collectively called "M.blue plus Instruments"):

1. **M.blue plus Compass** -- Locates the valve and reads the current gravitational unit setting. Open the compass to expose a circular template for valve localization. Close it to read the current pressure on a float gauge (0-40 cmH2O blue inner scale). Must be centered over the valve. Keep the M.blue plus adjustment ring at least 30 cm away during reading to avoid magnetic interference.

2. **M.blue plus Adjustment Ring** -- Magnetic ring that changes the gravitational unit opening pressure. Insert the ring into the scale ring with the line marking aligned to the desired value. Place over the valve and press lightly on the diaphragm center with index finger to release the rotor brake. The valve provides tactile and audible click feedback when the brake releases. Maximum 16 cmH2O change per adjustment step; stage larger changes in two steps.

3. **M.blue Check-mate** -- Sterile, single-use tool for intraoperative pressure level changes and verification. Place centrally over the valve; it auto-aligns via magnets. Pressure stage must point toward the proximal catheter (leading to valve). Press lightly on the adjustable unit to release the rotor brake and set the pressure.

### Adjustment Procedure
1. Localize valve with Compass
2. Read current pressure on Compass float gauge
3. Set Adjustment Ring to desired value, insert into scale ring
4. Place over valve, press center diaphragm to release rotor brake
5. Re-read pressure with Compass to verify
6. If large change needed, adjust in staged increments of no more than 16 cmH2O each

### Post-adjustment Verification
- Always re-check with Compass after adjustment
- X-ray confirmation may still be needed if scalp thickness is 5 mm or greater
- Adjustment can be difficult if swelling is present in the first few days postoperatively

### Caution
- The adjustment ring emits a magnetic field. Keep metallic objects and magnetic storage media away.
- M.blue plus Instruments must not be used near cardiac pacemakers or other active implants due to MRI instrument magnets.

## MRI Safety

- **MR Conditional** per ASTM F2503-13
- Safe at static magnetic field of 1.5T or 3T only
- Maximum spatial gradient: 500 Gauss/cm
- Maximum whole-body SAR: 4 W/kg for 15 minutes of scanning in First Level Controlled Operating Mode
- Temperature rise: maximum 4.2 degrees C after 15 minutes continuous scanning (per pulse sequence)
- Image artifact: approximately 20 mm from the implant using gradient echo at 3T
- **The valve setting is NOT altered by MRI exposure** -- confirmed per IFU. However, the setting should be verified with the M.blue plus Compass before and after MRI as a precaution.
- If a magnetic field and pressure are applied to the valve simultaneously (during MRI), it is not possible to rule out valve adjustment -- this triggers the brake mechanism and creates artifacts larger than the valve itself.
- Do not take the M.blue plus Instruments (Compass, Adjustment Ring, Check-mate) into the MR environment. They are MR Unsafe.
- Catheters, reservoirs, deflectors, and connectors in the system are MR safe.

## Indications

- CSF shunting for hydrocephalus in adults and pediatric patients
- Posture-dependent hydrocephalus where overdrainage in the upright position is a concern
- Available in VP shunt configurations with optional Sprung Reservoir or Control Reservoir for pediatric applications

## Contraindications

- Active or suspected CNS infection (meningitis, ventriculitis, peritonitis, bacteremia, septicemia) in the region affected by implantation
- Implantation near cardiac pacemakers or other active implants (magnets in the adjustment instruments may interfere)
- Do not implant in areas where detection or palpation of the valve is difficult (e.g., underneath heavily scarred tissue)
- Position-dependent: the valve is position-dependent and must be implanted parallel to the body axis

## Key Differences vs Competitors

- **Dual-mechanism in single housing**: Unlike the Medtronic Strata (differential pressure only with optional Delta Chamber) or proGAV 2.0 (separate adjustable unit + gravitational unit), M.blue integrates both adjustable gravitational and fixed differential pressure units in one disc
- **Continuously variable gravitational unit**: 0-40 cmH2O in a continuous range, vs. ShuntAssistant 2.0 which has only 6 fixed gravitational pressure levels (10-35 cmH2O)
- **Titanium construction**: Unlike silicone-bodied valves (Strata, CERTAS Plus), M.blue uses a titanium housing
- **Setting survives MRI**: The valve setting is not altered by MRI at up to 3T, unlike Medtronic Strata valves which may change settings after MRI exposure
- **No siphon-control chamber**: Unlike the Strata with its Delta Chamber, M.blue relies on the gravitational mechanism alone for upright overdrainage protection
- **Magnetic adjustment tools required**: Like proGAV 2.0, uses proprietary Miethke instruments; unlike Strata which uses its own StrataVarius system
- **Fixed DP unit**: The differential pressure component (0, 5, 10, or 15 cmH2O) is chosen at ordering and cannot be changed postoperatively, unlike CERTAS Plus where all settings are reprogrammable
