# Penumbra Reperfusion Catheter RED 43

**Manufacturer:** Penumbra  
**Category:** Aspiration Catheter  
**FDA Clearance:** K222808 (cleared 2022-12-20)

---

## What It Is

The RED 43 is the smallest-bore catheter in the Penumbra RED reperfusion catheter family. It is a flexible, single-use aspiration catheter designed for mechanical thrombectomy via continuous aspiration using the Penumbra Aspiration Pump. The "RED" naming convention encodes the inner diameter: RED 43 = 0.043 inch minimum inner diameter.

Its small caliber makes it the only RED-family catheter designed specifically for distal vessel access, including M2/M3 segments and basilar artery branches, where larger-bore aspiration catheters cannot safely navigate.

## Indications

Acute ischemic stroke secondary to intracranial large vessel occlusive disease involving:

- Internal carotid artery
- Middle cerebral artery (M1 or M2 segment)
- Basilar artery
- Vertebral artery

Within 8 hours of symptom onset, in patients who are ineligible for or who have failed IV t-PA.

RED 43 is specifically suited to M2/M3 and basilar branch occlusions where the larger RED variants (RED 62, RED 68, RED 72) cannot reach.

---

## Contraindications

No absolute contraindications specific to RED 43 are documented in available materials. General contraindications for mechanical thrombectomy apply.

**Sheath access:**
- 8-F short sheath, or

## Use Notes

- **Mode of operation:** Direct aspiration thrombectomy. The catheter is advanced to the occlusion site; the Penumbra Aspiration Pump applies continuous negative pressure to aspirate the thrombus.
- **Separator use:** Because of the small 0.043-inch inner diameter, the catheter lumen occludes more readily than larger RED variants. The Separator should be available and is typically needed during the procedure to restore flow through the catheter.
- **Distal reach:** RED 43 is designed for vessel segments inaccessible to larger bore aspiration catheters. It is the primary RED-family option for M2, M3, and distal basilar branch occlusions.
- **Predicate device:** RED 43 (K222808) was cleared as substantially equivalent to RED 62 (K203440). Despite the significant caliber difference, the FDA accepted RED 62 as the predicate.

## Active Recalls

Two categories of recalls affect the Penumbra Reperfusion Catheter family (6 total active recalls):

1. **Mid-shaft joint failure:** The reperfusion catheter may fail at the mid-shaft joint, causing the distal segment to break off. A retained fragment in the neurovasculature can block blood flow and may require emergency treatment.
2. **Distal tip damage:** The catheter may sustain damage to the distal tip. If the damaged tip is pressurized with contrast, vessel injury may result.

Verify lot numbers against current Penumbra recall notices before clinical use.

## Key Differences vs Competitors

| Device | Min ID | Distal Access Role |
|---|---|---|
| Penumbra RED 43 | 0.043 in (1.09 mm) | Primary distal/M2-M3 option in RED family |
| Penumbra RED 62 | 0.062 in (1.57 mm) | Standard proximal to mid vessel |
| Penumbra RED 68 | 0.068 in (1.73 mm) | Larger vessel, higher flow aspiration |
| Penumbra RED 72 | 0.072 in (1.83 mm) | Largest bore RED variant |
| Medtronic Riptide React 68 | 0.068 in | Large vessel, not designed for distal reach |
| Stryker AXS Vecta 71/74 | 0.071-0.074 in | Large bore, proximal aspiration |

RED 43 has no direct competitor in the distal aspiration catheter class from the major vendors listed above. Its closest functional comparators are intermediate/distal access catheters (e.g., Stryker Trevo TRAK 21, Sofia series) used in similar anatomical targets, though those devices differ in mechanism and role.
