## Key Differences vs Competitors

- **Packaging/Recall Status**: Collagen Dura Membrane has 2 active recalls for outer packaging seal issues compromising sterility, while Durepair has 1 recall for endotoxin testing issues, and ArtiFascia has no recalls noted.
- **Material Composition**: Collagen Dura Membrane is collagen-based (not specified if synthetic or natural), Durepair is collagen-based, and ArtiFascia is synthetic (non-woven fibers with non-porous film).
- **Size Specifications**: Collagen Dura Membrane size not specified, Durepair size not specified but available in variety of sizes, ArtiFascia specifically indicated for defects ≤25cm² with example size 3cm × 4cm.
- **MRI Compatibility**: Collagen Dura Membrane MRI compatibility not specified, Durepair MRI compatibility not specified, ArtiFascia is MR Safe.
- **Indication Specificity**: All three devices are indicated as dura substitutes for dura mater repair, but only ArtiFascia specifies a maximum defect size (≤25cm²).
