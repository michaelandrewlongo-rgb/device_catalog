## Key Differences vs Competitors

- **Device Design**: Trevo NXT ProVue Retriever uses a flexible, tapered nitinol core wire with a laser-cut nitinol shaped section, while EMBOTRAP III features a dual-layer stent retriever design with an inner channel for clot stabilization and an articulating outer cage, and Solitaire X uses a parametric overlap design for dynamic expansion and compression.
- **Sizing/Specs**: Trevo NXT ProVue Retriever sizes are 3x32 mm, 4x28 mm, 4x41 mm, and 6x37 mm with a 200 cm overall length. EMBOTRAP III sizes are 5x22 mm, 5x37 mm, and 6x45 mm with a 194 cm working length. Solitaire X sizes are 3 mm, 4 mm, and 6 mm diameters with usable lengths of 20 mm, 24 mm, and 40 mm. Penumbra System sizing is not specified.
- **Indications**: Trevo NXT ProVue Retriever is indicated for thrombus removal in acute ischemic stroke within 6 hours (standard) or 8 hours (extended) of symptom onset for proximal anterior circulation LVO. EMBOTRAP III and Solitaire X are indicated for neurovascular revascularization in acute ischemic stroke, with vessel ranges of 1.5-6.5 mm and 1.5-5.5 mm respectively. Penumbra System is indicated for revascularization within 8 hours of symptom onset for intracranial LVO in specific arteries.
- **Compatible Systems**: Trevo NXT ProVue Retriever is compatible with Trevo Pro 14, Trevo Trak 21, Trevo Pro 18, and Excelsior XT-27 microcatheters. EMBOTRAP III is designed for use with the Cerenovus Stroke Solutions ecosystem (Prowler EX Microcatheter, EMBOGUARD Balloon Guide Catheter, etc.). Solitaire X is compatible with Phenom catheters, React catheters, and APRO aspiration catheters. Penumbra System compatibility is not specified.
- **Clinical Evidence/Notes**: Solitaire X is noted as the most-published stent retriever with over 200 publications. EMBOTRAP III references the MASTRO I systematic review comparing it with Solitaire and Trevo. Trevo NXT ProVue Retriever and Penumbra System do not have specific clinical evidence notes provided in the data.
