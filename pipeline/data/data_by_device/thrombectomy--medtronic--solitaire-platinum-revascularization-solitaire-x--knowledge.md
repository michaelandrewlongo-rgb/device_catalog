# Solitaire Platinum / Solitaire X Revascularization Device

**Manufacturer:** Medtronic (ev3 Neurovascular)
**Category:** Thrombectomy Device

FDA 510(k) K193576 | Cleared November 20, 2020 | Class II

## What It Is

Self-expanding nitinol stent retriever for mechanical thrombectomy in acute ischemic stroke. The distal nitinol portion of the Solitaire Revascularization Device integrates with thrombus after deployment, then withdraws clot on retrieval. Platinum/Iridium radiopaque markers are positioned at the proximal and distal ends and along the circumference of the working length for full fluoroscopic visualization. Devices are supplied sterile and are for single use.

The K193576 clearance represents a labeling modification to the Solitaire Platinum and Solitaire X platforms, reducing the minimum recommended vessel diameter for the 4 mm device from 2.0 mm to 1.5 mm, supported by retrospective STRATIS registry analysis.

**Applicable vessels:** Internal carotid artery (ICA), M1 and M2 segments of middle cerebral artery (MCA), basilar artery, vertebral arteries.

**Materials:**
- Stent: Nitinol
- Pushwire: Nitinol
- Markers: 90% Platinum / 10% Iridium
- Pushwire shrink tubing: PTFE
- Introducer sheath: PTFE/Grilamid

**Sheath compatibility:** 8 or 9 Fr balloon guide catheter
**Pushwire working length:** 180 cm

**Sterilization:** Ethylene oxide; single use; stored in dispenser coil, pouch, and shipping carton.

## Indications

1. **Standard window (within 6 hours):** Restore blood flow by removing thrombus for treatment of acute ischemic stroke in patients with a persistent, proximal anterior circulation large vessel occlusion and smaller core infarcts who have first received IV t-PA. Endovascular therapy should begin within 6 hours of symptom onset.

2. **Extended window (within 8 hours):** Restore blood flow by removing thrombus from a large intracranial vessel in patients experiencing ischemic stroke within 8 hours of symptom onset. Patients ineligible for IV t-PA or who fail IV t-PA therapy are candidates.

3. **Late window (6-16 hours):** Restore blood flow by removing thrombus for treatment of acute ischemic stroke in patients with a persistent, proximal anterior circulation large vessel occlusion of the ICA or MCA-M1 with smaller core infarcts (< 70 cc by CTA or MRA; < 25 cc by MR-DWI). Endovascular therapy should begin within 6-16 hours of time last seen well, for patients ineligible for or who fail IV t-PA.

## Use Notes

Designed for use in the ICA, MCA-M1, MCA-M2, basilar, and vertebral arteries. The K193576 labeling modification specifically expands the recommended minimum vessel diameter for the 4 mm device to 1.5 mm (from 2.0 mm), enabling use in smaller M2 and distal vessel targets supported by STRATIS registry data.

## Key Differences vs Competitors

- **Vessel diameter range**: Solitaire Platinum/X 4mm device (1.5-4.0mm) has a smaller minimum vessel diameter than Solitaire 4 (not specified), Solitaire Platinum (not specified), and Modified Trevo ProVue (not specified), matching EMBOTRAP III (1.5-6.5mm) and Solitaire X (1.5-5.5mm) for small vessel capability.
- **Indication windows**: Solitaire Platinum/X has specific 6-hour, 8-hour, and 6-16 hour windows with detailed patient criteria, while Solitaire 4, Solitaire Platinum, Solitaire X, EMBOTRAP III, and ERIC have less specific or only 8-hour windows.
- **Device design features**: Solitaire Platinum/X uses a parametric overlap design for dynamic expansion/compression during retrieval, while EMBOTRAP III features a dual-layer design with inner channel and outer cage, and ERIC uses retrieval spheres on a pusher wire.
- **Clinical evidence**: Solitaire X is noted as "most-published stent retriever" with over 200 publications, while EMBOTRAP III references MASTRO I systematic review comparing devices, and others have no specified evidence base.
- **Compatibility systems**: Solitaire Platinum/X is compatible with Medtronic's Phenom/React/APRO catheters, EMBOTRAP III with Cerenovus Stroke Solutions ecosystem, and others have limited or not specified compatibility details.
