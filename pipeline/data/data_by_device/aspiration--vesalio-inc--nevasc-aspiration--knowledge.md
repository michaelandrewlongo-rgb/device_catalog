# NeVasc Aspiration System

**Manufacturer:** Vesalio, Inc.
**Category:** Aspiration Catheter

## What It Is

The NeVasc Aspiration System is a three-component system for direct aspiration thrombectomy in acute ischemic stroke: (1) NeVasc Aspiration Catheter, (2) Peel-Away Introducer Sheath, and (3) Vesalio Aspiration Tubing Set (supplied separately).

The NeVasc Aspiration Catheter is a single-lumen, reinforced, flexible, variable-stiffness composite catheter. The distal shaft has an external hydrophilic coating (50 cm length) to reduce friction in tortuous neurovasculature. The distal end is radiopaque for fluoroscopic visualization. The proximal end features a luer hub for attachment of accessories. Construction materials include stainless steel, PTFE, polyurethane, nitinol, tungsten, and platinum/iridium.

The Vesalio Aspiration Tubing Set is a hollow polymer tube bonded to a standard male rotator fitting on one end (connects to an RHV or the catheter's luer hub) and a hose fitting on the other end (connects to a compatible aspiration pump). An in-line flow switch provides vacuum control. Aspiration tubing ID is 0.110", length 107".

The peel-away introducer sheath facilitates insertion of the catheter distal tip into a vascular access sheath.

All system components are provided sterile, non-pyrogenic, and single use only. Sterilized by ethylene oxide.

## Indications

The NeVasc Aspiration Catheter, as part of the NeVasc Aspiration System with the Vesalio Aspiration Tubing Set and a compatible aspiration pump, is indicated for use in the revascularization of patients with acute ischemic stroke secondary to intracranial large vessel occlusive disease (within the internal carotid, middle cerebral M1 and M2 segments, basilar, and vertebral arteries) within 8 hours of symptom onset. Patients who are ineligible for thrombolytic drug therapy or who failed thrombolytic drug therapy are candidates for treatment.

The Vesalio Aspiration Tubing Set is indicated to connect the NeVasc Aspiration Catheter to a compatible aspiration pump.

## Use Notes

- Single use only. Do not resterilize.
- The Aspiration Tubing Set is packaged and supplied separately from the catheter.
- An in-line flow switch on the tubing set provides vacuum on/off control.
- Compatible pump specifications not specified in source; use a pump capable of generating adequate continuous vacuum for neuroaspiration.

## Key Differences vs Competitors

- **System components**: NeVasc includes a peel-away introducer sheath and separately supplied aspiration tubing with in-line flow switch, while the 072 Aspiration System and APRO Catheter descriptions do not specify inclusion of these accessories. The AXS Catalyst 7 and AXS Catalyst Distal Access Catheter specify the catheter as the only intravascular component, with tubing/pump outside the sterile field.
- **Catheter sizing**: NeVasp offers working lengths of 115, 125, 132, and 140 cm with a minimum ID of 0.070", compared to AXS Catalyst 7's 0.068" ID and 115/125/132 cm lengths, and APRO's 55 and 70 sizes (specific dimensions not specified).
- **Indications specificity**: NeVasc, AXS Catalyst catheters, and Penumbra Reperfusion Catheters specify use for acute ischemic stroke within 8 hours in specific vessels (ICA, MCA M1/M2, basilar, vertebral), while APRO's indication is broader ("removal of thrombus from cerebral vasculature") and Artemis is for ventricular/cerebral fluid/tissue evacuation.
- **Compatibility/pump requirements**: NeVasc requires a compatible aspiration pump (specs not specified), while AXS Catalyst catheters specify compatibility with Medela Dominant Flex Pump (-68 kPa minimum). APRO is compatible with Alembic aspiration tubing and Phenom microcatheters/Solitaire X.
