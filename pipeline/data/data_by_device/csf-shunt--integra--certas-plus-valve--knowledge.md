# CERTAS Plus Programmable Valve (Integra / Codman)

## What It Is

The Codman CERTAS Plus Programmable Valve is an implantable CSF shunt valve for hydrocephalus management. It uses a ruby ball-in-cone pressure-regulating mechanism with a spring design. The valve offers 8 programmable pressure settings that can be adjusted noninvasively after implantation using the Codman CERTAS Tool Kit. One of the 8 settings is a "virtual off" position (~400 mmH2O) that effectively stops flow without requiring surgical valve removal.

## Sizing/Specs

### Configurations
- **Certas Plus Inline** (standard, original clearance K152152)
- **Certas Plus Inline Small** (smaller/curved housing, K182265)
- **Certas Plus Right Angle** (90-degree proximal connector, K182265)

### Pressure Settings
- 8 settings total
- 7 active drainage settings: 25 to 215 mmH2O operating pressure range
- 1 "virtual off" setting: minimum opening pressure ~400 mmH2O

### Valve Mechanism
- Ruby ball-in-cone design
- Synthetic ruby ball and matching cone for precise CSF flow regulation
- Pressure-inducing spring design
- As differential pressure increases, ball displaces from cone, increasing flow to re-establish selected pressure

### Materials
- Silicone housing
- Synthetic ruby valve components
- Polyethersulfone (PES) needle guard
- Metallic internal components (unchanged across all configurations)

### Key Dimensions
- Inline Small: smaller silicone housing and reservoir, curved housing profile (designed for pediatric and small-anatomy patients)
- Right Angle: 90-degree proximal connector (reduces catheter stress in certain anatomical configurations)

### Fixation
- Inline/Inline Small: suture flange
- Right Angle: bone screw fixation compatible

## Indications

- Management of hydrocephalus
- Provides constant intraventricular pressure regulation and CSF drainage
- All ages (adult and pediatric)

## Compatible With

- **Codman CERTAS Tool Kit** (required for noninvasive pressure adjustment and setting indication/verification)
- **SiphonGuard** anti-siphon device (available with or without)
- **Unitized catheters** (available with or without pre-attached ventricular/peritoneal catheters)
- Standard shunt catheters via barbed connectors

## Use Notes

- The "virtual off" setting (setting 8, ~400 mmH2O) is clinically useful for: temporarily halting drainage, diagnostic workup of shunt dependency, managing overdrainage without reoperation
- Pressure can be changed noninvasively at any time post-implantation; no surgery required for pressure adjustment
- The Inline Small configuration is useful in pediatric patients and adults with thin scalp tissue due to its lower profile and curved housing
- The Right Angle configuration reduces catheter kinking at the ventricular catheter junction and provides bone screw fixation for secure anchoring to the skull
- MRI: the valve has been tested under MR imaging conditions per ISO 7197:2009. The metallic components are identical across all configurations. Valve setting should be verified and potentially reprogrammed after MRI exposure, as the magnetic field may alter the setting
- The Inline Small valve is more flexible than the standard Inline; the Right Angle valve has equivalent flexibility
- Predecessor: Codman Hakim Programmable Valve (K053107), which also had a right-angle configuration

## Key Differences vs Competitors

- **8 programmable settings with "virtual off"**: CERTAS Plus offers 8 settings (7 active + 1 virtual off at ~400 mmH2O). The virtual off position is unique among common programmable valves -- it allows flow cessation without surgery, useful for shunt dependency workup. Medtronic Strata has only 5 performance levels and no virtual off. Miethke proGAV 2.0 has 21 DP settings (0-20 cmH2O in 1 cmH2O steps) but no virtual off equivalent.
- **Ruby ball-in-cone mechanism**: Uses synthetic ruby components for precision flow regulation, vs. sapphire ball mechanisms in Miethke valves (M.blue, proGAV 2.0) and the spring/rotor design in Strata.
- **No integrated gravitational/anti-siphon unit**: CERTAS Plus is a pure differential pressure valve. It can be paired with a SiphonGuard device but has no built-in posture-dependent mechanism. Miethke M.blue and proGAV 2.0 integrate gravitational units for overdrainage protection. Strata (with Delta Chamber) includes integrated siphon control. For patients at high overdrainage risk, CERTAS Plus alone may be insufficient without an add-on device.
- **Setting may change with MRI**: Like Medtronic Strata, the CERTAS Plus setting should be verified and potentially reprogrammed after MRI exposure. Miethke proGAV 2.0's Active-Lock mechanism resists setting changes up to 3T. M.blue's setting is also confirmed stable through MRI.
- **Silicone housing with multiple form factors**: Available in Inline, Inline Small (low-profile/curved for pediatric), and Right Angle (90-degree proximal connector with bone screw fixation). Strata offers similar size variants (Regular, Small, Bur Hole) but in a different form factor. Miethke valves are titanium-bodied with fewer housing options.
- **Codman CERTAS Tool Kit required**: Proprietary programming tool, similar in concept to the Strata Adjustment Tools/StrataVarius and Miethke Soft Touch/M.blue plus Instruments. All three valve families require manufacturer-specific tools.
- **No LP variant**: Unlike Medtronic Strata NSC LP and Miethke SA 2.0 LP, the CERTAS Plus is not available in a lumboperitoneal configuration.
- **Wider pressure range**: Operating range of 25-215 mmH2O (~2.5-21.5 cmH2O) across active settings provides broader coverage than the Strata's 5 performance levels, though the proGAV 2.0's adjustable unit (0-20 cmH2O plus gravitational) achieves comparable combined range.
