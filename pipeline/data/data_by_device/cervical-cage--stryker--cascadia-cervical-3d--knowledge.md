# Cascadia Cervical 3D Interbody System

**Manufacturer:** Stryker
**Category:** Interbody cage / Cervical
510(k) K160547 | Class 2 | 2016-03-24

## What It Is

Lamellar 3D titanium cervical interbody cage for ACDF. Lamellar 3D Titanium Technology with 300-500 um longitudinal channels and transverse windows creating interconnected lattice for bony integration. Reverse hourglass design for large graft volume. Bulleted nose for ease of insertion. 3-5 um surface roughness for direct bony ongrowth.

## Sizing/Specs

Full range of sizes. Sagittal lordosis profiles: 0, 7, and 12 degrees.

## Indications

Anterior cervical discectomy and fusion (ACDF).

## Use Notes

Roughened titanium surfaces shown to demonstrate increased protein expression vs smooth titanium. Now sold by VB Spine.

## Key Differences vs Competitors

- The Cascadia Cervical 3D Interbody System features Lamellar 3D Titanium Technology with 300-500 um longitudinal channels and transverse windows for bony integration, and a 3-5 um surface roughness for direct bony ongrowth. In contrast, the Zero-P uses a PEEK-OPTIMA spacer with an integrated titanium alloy plate, the COALITION is a 3D-printed porous titanium cage, the CAPSTONE is a PEEK-OPTIMA polymer spacer, and the Tritanium C is a hollow, ring-shaped titanium alloy cage with serrations.
- The Cascadia system offers sagittal lordosis profiles of 0, 7, and 12 degrees. The Zero-P offers convex, lordotic, and parallel endplate geometries. The COALITION and CAPSTONE offer lordotic options, but specific angles are not specified for COALITION, and CAPSTONE offers 0 and 6 degrees. Tritanium C offers multiple lordotic angles, but specific values are not specified.
- The Cascadia system is indicated for Anterior cervical discectomy and fusion (ACDF). The Zero-P, COALITION, and CAPSTONE have more detailed indications including specific levels and conditions like degenerative disc disease, radiculopathy, and myelopathy. Tritanium C also has detailed indications for DDD at one or two contiguous levels from C2-T1.
- The Cascadia system's compatibility with supplemental fixation is not specified. The Zero-P has integrated fixation, the COALITION has integrated fixation with an optional supplemental plate, and the CAPSTONE and Tritanium C both require supplemental anterior plate fixation.
- The Cascadia system is noted for its reverse hourglass design for large graft volume and bulleted nose for ease of insertion. The Zero-P's primary advantage is its zero-profile design to reduce dysphagia. The COALITION's primary selling point is its integrated fixation. The CAPSTONE's PEEK construction allows for artifact-free imaging. The Tritanium C's porous surface is designed for deep osseointegration.
