## What It Is

Single-lumen guide catheter for vascular access in the peripheral, coronary, and neuro vasculature. The 93 NeuFlex is a large-bore neurointerventional access catheter positioned at the larger end of the guide catheter spectrum (0.093" ID, 6F-compatible).

Key design features:
- Hollow cylindrical tube bonded proximally to a standard luer fitting
- Shaft wall constructed from metal coils/braids and medical-grade polymers
- Hydrophilic coating on distal shaft to enhance tracking through tortuous vasculature
- Soft atraumatic straight distal tip to facilitate navigation past vessel branch points
- Single radiopaque distal tip marker for fluoroscopic confirmation of tip position
- Compatible with 0.035" or smaller guidewires
- Packaged with a loading tool to facilitate insertion into a short introducer sheath
- Sterilized with ethylene oxide (EO), SAL 10^-6; single use only

The 93 NeuFlex is the larger-diameter companion to the 87 NeuGlide Catheter (K240971), sharing the same basic technological design with a wider inner lumen (0.093" vs 0.087") to accommodate larger interventional devices.

## Indications

The 93 NeuFlex Catheter is indicated for the introduction of interventional devices into the peripheral, coronary, and neuro vasculature.

---

## Use Notes

- Use with support/diagnostic catheters to assist accessing target vasculature
- Advance under fluoroscopy using standard percutaneous interventional technique
- Required accessory devices (not supplied): guidewires, support/diagnostic catheters, introducer sheaths, rotating hemostasis valves (RHVs)
- Loading tool is packaged with the device to aid insertion into a short sheath
- MRI safety information not included in labeling; verify with manufacturer prior to MR environment use
- Single use only; pre-sterilized

## Key Differences vs Competitors

- **Largest inner diameter among single-lumen guide catheters**: The 93 NeuFlex (0.093" ID) has a larger lumen than the 87 NeuGlide (0.087"), Benchmark BMX81 (0.081"), Benchmark Intracranial Access System (0.071"), and Echelon Microcatheter (0.017" ID). Only the Benchmark BMX96 (0.096") and Broadway 8 Catheter (size not specified) may have comparable or larger diameters.
- **Simpler system design compared to multi-component competitors**: Unlike the BENCHMARK BMX81, BMX96, and Benchmark Intracranial Access Systems (which include delivery catheters, select catheters, and dilators), the 93 NeuFlex is a standalone single-lumen catheter requiring separate accessory devices.
- **Different primary application focus than microcatheters**: While indicated for similar vascular territories, the 93 NeuFlex is a large-bore guide catheter for device introduction, whereas the Echelon Microcatheter is designed for selective catheterization, infusion (including DMSO-based embolics), and aneurysm coiling with specific pressure limitations (600 PSI max).
- **No current FDA recalls unlike Broadway 8 Catheter**: The Broadway 8 Catheter has 3 active recalls related to labeling issues, while no recalls are mentioned for the 93 NeuFlex.
- **Shorter working lengths than some competitors**: Available in 100 cm and 110 cm lengths, while the BENCHMARK BMX81 offers 95 cm, 105 cm, and 115 cm, and the Echelon Microcatheter has a 150 cm working length.
