# Tigertriever Revascularization Device (21 / 17 / 13)

**Manufacturer:** Rapid Medical
**Category:** Thrombectomy Device

FDA 510(k) K230429 | Cleared April 17, 2023 | Class II

## What It Is

The Tigertriever is an adjustable stent retriever (stentriever) for mechanical thrombectomy in acute ischemic stroke. Unlike conventional self-expanding stent retrievers, the Tigertriever uses a manually controlled braided nitinol mesh: the operator pulls a slider on the handle to actively expand the mesh, allowing controlled, progressive engagement of the clot and real-time adjustment to vessel diameter. The mesh is fully radiopaque, providing continuous fluoroscopic visualization of its conformation within the vessel. The closed-end braided mesh design is intended to penetrate and encapsulate thrombus during retrieval.

Three sizes are available: Tigertriever 21 (large vessel), Tigertriever 17 (medium vessel), and Tigertriever 13 (small vessel/distal).

**Materials:**
- Mesh: Nitinol
- Radiopaque markers: 90% Platinum / 10% Iridium; FluoroSafe markers on shaft (added in K230429 generation)
- Core wire: Nitinol; shaft: 304 Stainless Steel
- Introducer sheath: PTFE
- Sterilization: Ethylene oxide; single use

## Indications

Intended to restore blood flow by removing thrombus from a large intracranial vessel in patients experiencing ischemic stroke within 8 hours of symptom onset. Patients who are ineligible for intravenous tissue plasminogen activator (IV t-PA) or who fail IV t-PA therapy are candidates for treatment.

## Compatible With

- Tigertriever 21: microcatheter with minimum ID of 0.021"
- Tigertriever 17: microcatheter with minimum ID of 0.0165"
- Tigertriever 13: microcatheter with minimum ID of 0.0165"

## Use Notes

The defining feature of the Tigertriever is active, operator-controlled mesh expansion via the handle slider, as opposed to passive self-expansion. This allows the operator to adjust mesh diameter in real time to conform to vessel diameter and clot burden, and to remove tension from the vasculature before withdrawal. The fully radiopaque mesh enables direct visualization of mesh-clot interaction throughout the procedure.

Durability testing validated up to 3 passes per device.

## Key Differences vs Competitors

- **Active vs. passive expansion**: The Tigertriever's key differentiator is its operator-controlled, manual mesh expansion via a handle slider, allowing real-time adjustment to vessel diameter and clot burden. This contrasts with all listed competitors (EMBOTRAP III, Modified Trevo ProVue, Solitaire 4, Solitaire Platinum/X, and the ERIC device), which are self-expanding stent retrievers or, in the case of the Penumbra System, an aspiration platform.
- **Visualization and sizing approach**: The Tigertriever features a fully radiopaque nitinol mesh for continuous fluoroscopic visualization of mesh-clot interaction. It is offered in three discrete sizes (21, 17, 13) targeting specific vessel categories. In contrast, competitors like the EMBOTRAP III and Solitaire Platinum/X specify vessel diameter ranges (e.g., 1.5-6.5 mm) for their devices, and the ERIC device uses a different retrieval sphere design.
- **Compatibility and procedural notes**: The Tigertriever requires specific microcatheter inner diameters (0.021" for the 21, 0.0165" for the 17 and 13) and is validated for up to 3 passes per device. The EMBOTRAP III is noted for compatibility within the Cerenovus Stroke Solutions ecosystem, the Penumbra System operates on a direct aspiration principle, and the Solitaire Platinum/X has specific vessel size recommendations (e.g., 4 mm device for 1.5-4.0 mm vessels).
