# ArtiFascia

**Manufacturer:** Nurami Medical
**Category:** Dural substitute

## What It Is

ArtiFascia® is an absorbable dural repair graft for the repair of cranial dural defects. ArtiFascia® is a highly flexible, easy to handle, non-friable soft matrix composed of synthetic non-woven fibers and a non-porous film. ArtiFascia® readily conforms to the surface of the wound area and is applied to the dural defect by using sutures.

## Key Differences vs Competitors

- **Material composition**: ArtiFascia is composed of synthetic non-woven fibers and a non-porous film, while COLLAGEN DURA MEMBRANE and DUREPAIR DURA REGENERATION MATRIX are collagen-based implants.
- **Defect size specification**: ArtiFascia is indicated for defects of 25cm² or less, while COLLAGEN DURA MEMBRANE and DUREPAIR DURA REGENERATION MATRIX have no specified defect size limitations in the provided data.
- **Safety profile**: ArtiFascia has no active FDA recalls mentioned, while COLLAGEN DURA MEMBRANE has 2 active recalls for packaging sterility issues and DUREPAIR DURA REGENERATION MATRIX has 1 active recall for endotoxin level concerns.
- **MRI compatibility**: ArtiFascia is specified as MR Safe, while MRI compatibility is not specified for COLLAGEN DURA MEMBRANE or DUREPAIR DURA REGENERATION MATRIX.
