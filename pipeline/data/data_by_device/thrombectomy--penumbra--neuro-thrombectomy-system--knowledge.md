# Penumbra System -- Neuro Thrombectomy

**Manufacturer:** Penumbra, Inc.
**Category:** Mechanical thrombectomy / aspiration thrombectomy system

## What It Is

The Penumbra System is a fully integrated mechanical thrombectomy platform designed specifically for acute ischemic stroke due to intracranial large vessel occlusion (LVO). It received initial 510(k) clearance in December 2007 and was among the first FDA-cleared systems for intracranial mechanical thrombectomy. The system operates on a direct aspiration principle: large-bore reperfusion catheters are advanced to the face of the thrombus and connected to the Penumbra Aspiration Pump, which provides continuous vacuum to aspirate and remove the clot.

The system includes the RED (Reperfusion and Evacuation Device) family of aspiration catheters, a SEPARATOR wire used to macerate thrombus and maintain catheter patency during aspiration, sterile aspiration tubing, and the Penumbra Aspiration Pump (reusable vacuum source). Later generations introduced the ACE (Aspiration Catheter Engine) series of large-bore reperfusion catheters optimized for direct aspiration thrombectomy (DAT) without requiring a separator wire.

## Indications

**Reperfusion Catheters and Separators (Penumbra System):** Revascularization of patients with acute ischemic stroke secondary to intracranial large vessel occlusive disease within the internal carotid artery, middle cerebral artery (M1 and M2 segments), basilar artery, and vertebral arteries within 8 hours of symptom onset. Indicated for patients who are ineligible for IV t-PA or who fail IV t-PA therapy.

**Aspiration Tubing:** Connects Penumbra Reperfusion Catheters to the Penumbra Aspiration Pump as part of the Penumbra System.

**Aspiration Pump:** Vacuum source for Penumbra Aspiration Systems.

## Use Notes

- Single use only. Do not resterilize or reuse. Reuse may result in loss of catheter coating lubrication and inability to access target vasculature.
- Do not use automated high-pressure contrast injection equipment with reperfusion catheters; may damage the device.
- Do not advance or retract any component against resistance without fluoroscopic assessment of the cause. If the cause cannot be determined, withdraw the system as a unit.
- Confirm vessel diameter before selecting catheter size. Do not use in arteries with diameter smaller than or equal to the distal OD of the reperfusion catheter.
- The direct aspiration (ADAPT) technique -- advancing the ACE catheter to the thrombus face and aspirating without a stent retriever -- is a primary use pattern for the ACE series.
- Do not use kinked or damaged devices or open/damaged packages.
