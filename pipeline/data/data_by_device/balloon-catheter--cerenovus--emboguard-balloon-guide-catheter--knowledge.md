# EMBOGUARD Balloon Guide Catheter

**Manufacturer:** Cerenovus (J&J)
**Category:** Balloon catheter

## What It Is

Balloon guide catheter designed for flow arrest during neurovascular interventions. Features ultra compliant balloon for atraumatic conformability and excellent trackability. Designed for seamless integration into the most commonly used stroke intervention set ups.

## Sizing/Specs

Inner Diameter: 0.087 inch; Outer Diameter: 0.112 inch; Working Lengths: 85 cm, 95 cm; Tip Length: 5 mm; Introducer Sheath Compatibility: 8F; Product codes: BG8785U, BG8795U

## Indications

EMBOGUARD Balloon Guide Catheters are indicated for use in facilitating the insertion and guidance of an intravascular catheter into a selected blood vessel in the neurovascular system. The balloon provides temporary vascular occlusion during angiographic procedures. The Balloon Guide Catheter is also indicated for use as a conduit for Retrieval Devices.

## Compatible With

EMBOTRAP III Revascularization Device, CEREBASE DA Guide Sheath, Prowler EX Microcatheter, CEREGLIDE 71 Intermediate Catheter (CERENOVUS Stroke Solutions system)

## Use Notes

Better trackability than Walrus, FlowGate, and NeuronMax. More atraumatic balloon conformability than Walrus when inflated to 7mm in 5mm ID vessel. Better trackability than NeuronMax in model with challenging proximal carotid tortuosity plus 360 degree loop in ICA.

## Key Differences vs Competitors

- **Indications**: EMBOGUARD is indicated for use only in the neurovasculature, while Scepter C/XC and Scepter Mini are indicated for use in both the peripheral and neurovasculature.
- **Guidewire Compatibility**: EMBOGUARD inner diameter (0.087 inch) is compatible with standard guidewires used in stroke intervention setups (compatibility with specific guidewire sizes not specified in provided data). Scepter C/XC is 0.014" guidewire-compatible, and Scepter Mini accepts only a 0.008" guidewire.
- **Balloon Design & Use**: EMBOGUARD features an ultra-compliant balloon designed for flow arrest during neurovascular interventions and is part of the CERENOVUS Stroke Solutions system. Scepter devices are dual-lumen occlusion balloon microcatheters designed for temporary vascular occlusion and balloon-assisted coiling/embolization, with compliant balloons available in multiple specific sizes (e.g., 4x10 mm, 4x11 mm).
- **Key Design Features**: EMBOGUARD has a hydrophilic coating on the distal portion of the shaft and a non-coaxial balloon inflation lumen. Scepter devices have an external hydrophilic coating (covering distal 100 cm for Scepter Mini) and a dual-lumen design with a dedicated inflation lumen and a guidewire/delivery lumen.
