# AXS Catalyst Distal Access Catheter

**Manufacturer:** Stryker Neurovascular
**Category:** Aspiration Catheter

## What It Is

Sterile, single-lumen, variable-stiffness catheter designed for distal intracranial access and direct aspiration thrombectomy in acute ischemic stroke secondary to large vessel occlusion. The catheter shaft has a hydrophilic coating to reduce friction during navigation. A radiopaque marker on the distal end provides angiographic visualization. A luer hub on the proximal end accepts a rotating hemostatic valve (RHV) and Tuohy Borst valve with sideport for flushing, device insertion, and aspiration.

The AXS Catalyst is used as part of the AXS Universal Aspiration System, which also includes the AXS Universal Aspiration Tubing, AXS Universal Liner Set, and the Medela Dominant Flex Pump (minimum vacuum: -68 kPa / -20.08 inHg). The aspiration tubing connects the sterile catheter to the non-sterile pump environment. The AXS Catalyst is the only intravascularly used component of the system; the tubing and pump remain outside the sterile field. Packaged with an RHV, a Tuohy Borst valve with sideport, and a peel-away introducer sheath.

The 058 model (5.3F distal / 5.6F proximal) is designed for smaller vessel targets. Single use only. Sterilized by ethylene oxide.

## Indications

The AXS Catalyst Distal Access Catheter as part of the AXS Universal Aspiration System is indicated for use in the revascularization of patients with acute ischemic stroke secondary to intracranial large vessel occlusive disease (in the internal carotid, middle cerebral M1 and M2 segments, basilar, and vertebral arteries) within 8 hours of symptom onset. Patients who are ineligible for intravenous tissue plasminogen activator (IV t-PA) or who failed IV t-PA are candidates for treatment.

## Use Notes

- Single use only.
- The AXS Catalyst catheter is the only intravascular component of the AXS Universal Aspiration System; the aspiration tubing and pump remain outside the sterile field.
- Compatible pump: Medela Dominant Flex Pump; minimum vacuum -68 kPa (-20.08 inHg).
- Peel-away introducer sheath protects the distal tip during insertion into the RHV or Tuohy Borst valve.
- Note: multiple active FDA recalls exist for Stryker stroke procedure packs (Trevo/Catalyst combination packs) related to carton sleeve labeling mismatches; verify pack contents at time of use.

## Key Differences vs Competitors

- **Size and aspiration capacity**: The AXS Catalyst 058 (5.3F distal) is designed for smaller vessel targets, while its sibling the AXS Catalyst 7 (6.2F distal, 0.068\" ID) provides larger bore for greater aspiration capacity. The Esperance 3+ has a tapered lumen (0.041\" distal ID to 0.054\" proximal ID). The APRO Aspiration Catheter comes in APRO 55 and APRO 70 sizes, but specific diameters are not specified.
- **System integration and compatibility**: The AXS Catalyst is part of the AXS Universal Aspiration System requiring the Medela Dominant Flex Pump. The APRO Aspiration Catheter is compatible with Alembic aspiration tubing and Phenom microcatheters/Solitaire X. The Esperance 3+ is compatible with standard RHVs and external pumps (not included). The 072 Aspiration System requires a compatible suction pump (not specified).
- **Intended use scope**: The AXS Catalyst, AXS Catalyst 7, Esperance 3+, and 072 Aspiration System are specifically indicated for revascularization in acute ischemic stroke from large vessel occlusion. The Artemis Neuro Evacuation Device is for aspiration from the Ventricular System/Cerebrum during neuroendoscopy. The Ballast 088 Long Sheath and Carrier Delivery Catheter are for device introduction and general intravascular use, not specifically for aspiration thrombectomy.
- **Recall status**: The AXS Catalyst has active FDA recalls for Stryker stroke procedure packs (Trevo/Catalyst combination packs) related to carton sleeve labeling mismatches. No recall information is provided for other devices.
