## Indications

/ indications for use
Target Detachable Coils are intended to endovascularly obstruct or occlude
blood flow in vascular abnormalities of the neurovascular and peripheral
vessels.
Target Detachable Coils are indicated for endovascular embolization of:
• Intracranial aneurysms
• Other neurovascular abnormalities such as arteriovenous malformations and
arteriovenous fistulae
• Arterial and venous embolizations in the peripheral vasculature
