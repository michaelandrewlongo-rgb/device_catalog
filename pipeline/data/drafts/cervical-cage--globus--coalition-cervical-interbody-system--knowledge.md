# COALITION Cervical Interbody System

**Manufacturer:** Globus Medical
**Category:** Cervical cage

## What It Is

Interfixated cervical interbody system. HEDRON C-MIS 3D-printed integrated spacer compatible with COALITION MIS instruments. HEDRON IC combinable with COALITION AGX Plate as stand-alone plate-spacer. Inline anchor delivery eliminates angled instruments.
