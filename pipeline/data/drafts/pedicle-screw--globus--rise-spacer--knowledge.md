# RISE Expandable Lumbar Interbody Spacer

**Manufacturer:** Globus Medical
**Category:** Pedicle screw system
510(k) K113447 | Class 2 | 2012-01-26

## What It Is

All-titanium expandable lumbar interbody spacer for TLIF/PLIF. Inserted contracted, continuously expands up to 7mm. RISE IntraLIF variant: percutaneous via 8.5mm cannula with extraforaminal access.

## Sizing/Specs

Expands up to 7mm. IntraLIF: 8.5mm cannula delivery.

## Indications

TLIF, PLIF. IntraLIF for percutaneous approach.
