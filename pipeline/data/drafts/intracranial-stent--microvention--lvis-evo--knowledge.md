## What It Is

The LVIS EVO is the next-generation intracranial stent in the LVIS family from MicroVention/Terumo. It is a self-expanding nitinol single-wire braid stent designed for stent-assisted coiling of wide-necked intracranial aneurysms. The LVIS EVO builds on the original LVIS platform's closed-cell, low-profile design with refinements intended to enhance deliverability in tortuous anatomy and improve neck coverage. Like its predecessors, it features helical radiopaque marker strands woven into the braid for enhanced fluoroscopic visualization.
