# CEREGLIDE 92 Catheter System with TruCourse

**Manufacturer:** Cerenovus (J&J MedTech)
**Category:** Distal access / large-bore intermediate catheter

## What It Is

Large-bore intermediate catheter system co-packaged with the INNERGLIDE 9 Delivery Aid. Engineered for distal access to the M1 segment in acute ischemic stroke intervention. TruCourse technology provides improved trackability through tortuous cervical and intracranial vasculature. BRITE-LINE construction delivers full-length catheter visibility under fluoroscopy. Positioned by the manufacturer as the largest inner diameter with the lowest distal profile among 8F-class aspiration/intermediate catheter systems.

Packaged as a system with the INNERGLIDE 9 Delivery Aid, which is used to facilitate delivery and is not intended to remain in the body.

## Use Notes

- Designed to diminish flow in the M1 segment and support distal placement for reduced relay when paired with CEREGLIDE 71.
- Lower track forces compared to Zoom 88 per manufacturer data.
- Largest inner diameter and lowest distal profile among 8F-class systems versus Zoom 88, SOFIA 88, and Millipede 088 per manufacturer claims.
- BRITE-LINE full-length radiopaque construction assists fluoroscopic catheter position confirmation throughout the entire shaft, not only at the distal tip.
- INNERGLIDE 9 accepts guidewires up to 0.024 inch for delivery assistance.
