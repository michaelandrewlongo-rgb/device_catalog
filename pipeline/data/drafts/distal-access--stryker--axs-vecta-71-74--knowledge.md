# AXS Vecta 71/74 Intermediate Catheter

**Manufacturer:** Stryker Neurovascular
**Category:** Distal Access Catheter

## What It Is

Intermediate catheter designed for dual-purpose use: device delivery to intracranial targets and direct thrombus aspiration during mechanical thrombectomy. The Vecta 71/74 designation refers to the catheter's 0.071 inch inner diameter and variable outer profile (6.3F distal, 6.5F proximal). The tapered outer profile reduces distal stiffness while maintaining proximal trackability. Available in three working lengths.

## Use Notes

- Classified as a device delivery and thrombus aspiration catheter; supports both intermediate access platform use and direct aspiration (ADAPT-style) technique.
- The 0.071 inch inner diameter accommodates standard stent retrievers (e.g., Trevo, Solitaire) deployed through a co-axial microcatheter.
- Excessive aspiration may cause patient complications; aspiration pressure and duration should be controlled per institutional protocol.
- Source: Stryker Neurovascular product page and EVToday device guide.
