## Indications

The Synchro SELECT Guidewire series is intended for
general intravascular use, including neurovascular and
peripheral vasculatures. It can be used to selectively
introduce and position catheters and other interventional
devices within the peripheral and neurovasculature.
This device should be used only by physicians trained in
percutaneous, intravascular techniques and procedures.
