## What It Is

The FRED X is the next-generation flow diverter in the FRED family from MicroVention/Terumo. It is a self-expanding nitinol dual-layer flow diverter designed for endovascular treatment of intracranial aneurysms. The FRED X builds on the original FRED platform with design refinements intended to improve deliverability and vessel wall apposition. Like the original FRED, it uses an inner high-density braid for flow diversion at the aneurysm neck and an outer scaffold for radial support. Delivery is via the Headway microcatheter family.
