# Solitaire X Revascularization Device -- Medtronic

**Manufacturer:** Medtronic
**Category:** Thrombectomy | Stent Retriever

510(k) K203358 | Class II | Cleared March 1, 2021

## What It Is

Self-expanding stent retriever for mechanical thrombectomy in acute ischemic stroke (AIS). Uses a parametric overlap design that allows the stent to expand and compress within the vessel during deployment and retrieval, maintaining continuous clot engagement. The nitinol mesh integrates with thrombus upon deployment; the clot is then withdrawn through the access system. Most-published stent retriever in the category with over 200 publications supporting its use.

## Indications

Neurovascular mechanical thrombectomy for restoration of blood flow in adult patients with acute ischemic stroke due to large vessel occlusion (LVO). Indicated for vessel diameters 1.5--5.5 mm.

## Use Notes

- Parametric overlap design is the defining feature of the Solitaire platform; the stent compresses and expands dynamically as it traverses tortuous anatomy and captures clot
- Most-published stent retriever in the thrombectomy category, with clinical evidence base exceeding 200 publications
- Available as a "Power Package" bundled with compatible Medtronic access components (brochure on file)
- Standard technique: navigate microcatheter past thrombus, advance retriever, retract microcatheter to deploy stent across occlusion, allow integration time, withdraw as a unit with aspiration applied through access catheter
