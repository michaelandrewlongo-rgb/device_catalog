# iFuse INTRA Ti

**Manufacturer:** SI-BONE
**Category:** Sacroiliac Fusion

## What It Is

3D-printed titanium intra-articular SI joint fusion implant with self-cutting blades. Designed for placement within the articular cartilage of the SI joint (intra-articular approach, not ligamentous). Features bone anchors for initial fixation, fenestrations for bone through-growth, iFuse Lattice Technology for bone ingrowth and ongrowth, and cutting blades that pierce SI joint cortices at implantation. Distinct from iFuse INTRA (allograft-based) — iFuse INTRA Ti uses 3D-printed titanium with the iFuse lattice surface. MIS delivery via percutaneous posterior incision.
