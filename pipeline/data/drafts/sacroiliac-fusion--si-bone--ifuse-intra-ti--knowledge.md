# iFuse INTRA Ti

**Manufacturer:** Si-Bone
**Category:** Sacroiliac Fusion

## What It Is

3D-printed titanium intra-articular SI joint fusion implant with self-cutting blades. Designed for placement in the articular portion of the SI joint (not ligamentous). Features bone anchors for fixation and stability, fenestrations for bone through-growth, iFuse Lattice Technology for bone ingrowth/ongrowth, and cutting blades that pierce SI joint cortices for initial fixation.
