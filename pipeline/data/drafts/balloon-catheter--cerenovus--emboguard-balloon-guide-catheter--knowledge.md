# EMBOGUARD Balloon Guide Catheter

**Manufacturer:** Cerenovus (J&J)
**Category:** Balloon catheter

## What It Is

Balloon guide catheter designed for flow arrest during neurovascular interventions. Features ultra compliant balloon for atraumatic conformability and excellent trackability. Designed for seamless integration into the most commonly used stroke intervention set ups.

## Sizing/Specs

Inner Diameter: 0.087 inch; Outer Diameter: 0.112 inch; Working Lengths: 85 cm, 95 cm; Tip Length: 5 mm; Introducer Sheath Compatibility: 8F; Product codes: BG8785U, BG8795U
