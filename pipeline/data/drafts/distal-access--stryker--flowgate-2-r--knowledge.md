## Indications

FlowGate2 Balloon Guide Catheters are indicated for use
in procedures for facilitating the insertion and guidance
of an intravascular catheter into a selected blood vessel in
the neuro vascular system. The Balloon Guide Catheter is
also indicated for use as a conduit for Retrieval devices.
The balloon provides temporary vascular occlusion during
these and other angiographic procedures.
