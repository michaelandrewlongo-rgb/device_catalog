# Mojave PL 3D Expandable Interbody System

**Manufacturer:** Stryker
**Category:** Pedicle screw system
510(k) K163364 | Class 2 | 2017-03-15

## What It Is

Expandable posterior lumbar interbody fusion device with independent control of anterior and posterior height. Lamellar 3D Titanium Technology with 500 um longitudinal channels and transverse windows for bony integration. Lockable at any desired height and lordosis to restore sagittal balance. Graft chamber allows bone graft packing prior to insertion. Collapsed insertion profile minimizes risk of ligament, nerve root, and endplate damage.

## Sizing/Specs

Comprehensive height and lordotic expansion ranges. Collapsed insertion profile expands to desired height intraoperatively.

## Indications

Posterior lumbar interbody fusion (PLIF).

## Use Notes

All-in-one instrumentation for fast and simple insertion and expansion. Now sold by VB Spine.
