# LEO+ Intracranial Stent

**Manufacturer:** Balt
**Category:** Intracranial stent (self-expanding, for stent-assisted coiling)
**Source URL:** https://baltgroup.com/products/leo

## What It Is

Self-expanding braided nitinol intracranial stent designed for stent-assisted coiling of wide-necked intracranial aneurysms. The LEO+ provides neck bridging to retain coil mass within the aneurysm sac during packing and serves as a scaffold for neointimal coverage of the aneurysm neck over time.

The braid design allows the stent to conform to curved intracranial vessel anatomy.
