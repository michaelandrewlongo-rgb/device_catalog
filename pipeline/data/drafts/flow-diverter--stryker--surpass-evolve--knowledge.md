# Surpass Evolve Flow Diverter

**Manufacturer:** Stryker Neurovascular
**Category:** Flow diverter
**Source URL:** https://www.stryker.com/us/en/neurovascular/products/surpass-evolve.html

## What It Is

Self-expandable braided flow diverter for endovascular treatment of intracranial aneurysms. The braided implant is positioned across the aneurysm neck in the parent artery, diverting blood flow away from the aneurysm sac to promote progressive thrombosis and endothelialization over time. Successor to the Surpass Streamline; designed with a lower-profile delivery system and a 64-wire cobalt-chromium braid interwoven with platinum-tungsten radiopaque markers. Delivered preloaded through a dedicated microcatheter (Excelsior XT-27).

The system consists of the flow diverter implant, a pusher, and a compatible delivery microcatheter. The AXS Infinity LS or AXS Cat serves as the recommended intermediate/guide catheter.
