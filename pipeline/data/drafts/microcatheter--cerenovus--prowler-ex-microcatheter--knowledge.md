# Prowler EX Microcatheter

**Manufacturer:** Cerenovus (J&J)
**Category:** Microcatheter

## What It Is

Variable stiffness, single lumen microcatheter designed to access small, tortuous vasculature. Hydrophilic coating provides lubricity for vessel navigation. PTFE-lined inner lumen facilitates movement of guidewires and other devices. Proprietary braid construction provides balance of stability (braid section) and navigability (coil section).

## Sizing/Specs

Distal tip extension past distal marker: as little as 0.5 mm; TRUELUMEN Technology provides consistent inner diameter throughout length; In-line marker bands
