# Carrier Delivery Catheter

**Manufacturer:** Balt
**Category:** Microcatheter / delivery catheter
**Source URL:** https://baltgroup.com/products/carrier

## What It Is

Flexible delivery catheter for intravascular use across peripheral, coronary, and neurovascular territories. In the neurovascular setting, the Carrier functions as an intermediate or guide-extension catheter to facilitate coaxial delivery of distal access catheters and other interventional devices. It accepts contrast injection for vessel opacification and serves as a conduit for device delivery.
