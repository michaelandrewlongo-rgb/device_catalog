## Indications

/ Indications for use
The Surpass Streamline Flow Diverter is indicated for
use in the endovascular treatment of patients (18 years
of age and older) with unruptured large or giant saccular
wide-neck (neck width ≥ 4 mm or dome-to-neck ratio
< 2) or fusiform intracranial aneurysms in the internal
carotid artery from the petrous segment to the terminus
arising from a parent vessel with a diameter ≥ 2.5 mm
and ≤ 5.3 mm.
