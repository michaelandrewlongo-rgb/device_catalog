## Indications

/ indications for use
Stryker Neurovascular’s Excelsior XT-17 Microcatheters
are intended to assist in the delivery of diagnostic
agents, such as contrast media, and therapeutic agents,
such as occlusion coils, into the peripheral, coronary and
neuro vasculature.
