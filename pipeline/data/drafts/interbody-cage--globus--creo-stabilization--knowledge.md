# CREO 5.5 Stabilization System

**Manufacturer:** Globus Medical
**Category:** Interbody cage
510(k) K143633 | Class 2 | 2015-01-30

## What It Is

Complete thoracolumbar stabilization system with low-profile design. Non-threaded locking mechanism. Available in titanium and cobalt chromium (CREO CoCr). Multiple variants: CREO MIS (percutaneous), CREO MCS (cortical screw), CREO AMP (advanced modular platform), CREO NXT (non-threaded CoCr deformity), CREO DLX (6.0/6.35mm rod), CREO ONE Robotic Screw, CREO Side Loading. Procedurally integrated with ExcelsiusGPS.

## Sizing/Specs

5.5mm rod (standard). 6.0/6.35mm rod (DLX). Ti and CoCr alloy options.

## Indications

Thoracolumbar stabilization for degenerative, deformity, tumor, and trauma.

## Compatible With

ExcelsiusGPS navigation/robotics. Bendini rod bending. CREO Derotation Instruments. CREO AMP QUAD connectors.
