# Solitaire X Revascularization Device

**Manufacturer:** Medtronic
**Category:** Thrombectomy device
510(k) K203358 | Class 2 | 2021-03-01

## What It Is

Stent retriever designed for effective clot retrieval during thrombectomies for acute ischemic stroke (AIS) patients. Most-published stent retriever with over 200 publications. Parametric overlap design allows stent to expand and compress in vessel during deployment and retrieval.

## Sizing/Specs

Sizes: 3mm (vessel 1.5-3.0mm), 4mm (vessel 1.5-4.0mm), 6mm (vessel 2.0-5.5mm); Microcatheter ID: 0.017 inch (3mm) or 0.021 inch (4mm/6mm); Body markers: 3-4; Usable lengths: 20mm, 24mm, 40mm; Product codes: SFR4-3-20-10, SFR4-3-40-10, SFR4-4-20-05, SFR4-4-20-10, SFR4-4-40-10, SFR4-6-20-10, SFR4-6-24-06, SFR4-6-40-10

## Indications

Neurovascular mechanical thrombectomy for acute ischemic stroke (AIS). Vessel sizes 1.5-5.5 mm.

## Compatible With

APRO 55/70 aspiration catheters, Phenom 17/21/27 catheters, React catheters

## Use Notes

Most-published stent retriever. Overlap parametric design fundamental to Solitaire portfolio.
