# iFuse 3D

**Manufacturer:** Si-Bone
**Category:** Sacroiliac Fusion

## What It Is

Minimally invasive sacroiliac joint fusion implant with 3D-printed porous titanium (FuSIon 3D) surface. Triangular cross-section provides 6X more rotational resistance than screws. Patented fenestrated design allows bone through-growth while withstanding SI joint loads. Threaded base allows implant adjustments during placement. Self-harvests bone during press-fit implantation.

## Indications

Minimally invasive surgical fusion of the sacroiliac (SI) joint in patients who have failed non-surgical treatment.

## Use Notes

Lateral transiliac approach. 5-year prospective multicenter trial (103 patients/12 sites): 88% patient satisfaction, 87% bridging bone within SI joint, 40% decrease in opioid use, 54-point VAS improvement, 25-point ODI improvement.
