## Indications

FlowGate2 Balloon Guide Catheters are indicated for use in procedures for facilitating the insertion and guidance of an intravascular catheter into a selected blood vessel in the neurovascular system. The Balloon Guide Catheter is also indicated for use as a conduit for retrieval devices. The balloon provides temporary vascular occlusion during these and other angiographic procedures.
