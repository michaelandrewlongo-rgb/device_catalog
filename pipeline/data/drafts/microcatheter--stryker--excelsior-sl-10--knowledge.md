## Indications

Stryker Neurovascular Excelsior SL-10 Microcatheter is
intended to assist in the delivery of diagnostic agents,
such as contrast media, and therapeutic agents, such
as occlusion coils, into the peripheral, coronary, and
neurovasculature.
