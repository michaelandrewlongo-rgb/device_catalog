# Reline Open Fixation System

**Manufacturer:** Globus Medical
**Category:** Pedicle screw system

## What It Is

Comprehensive fixation system from occiput to pelvis. Open, MIS (MAS), and hybrid approaches in single platform. Variants: Reline MAS (minimally invasive), Reline Open, Reline 3D (deformity with VBD derotation), Reline Fenestrated (cement for tumors), Reline Traction (optimized insertional torque), Reline ASF (revision/extension), Reline Cervical.

## Sizing/Specs

Multiple rod diameters and materials.

## Indications

Posterior spinal fixation occiput to pelvis. Degenerative, deformity, tumor, trauma, revision.

## Compatible With

Reline Dual-Headed Modular Connector. ExcelsiusGPS.
