# Riptide Aspiration System React 68 Catheter

**Manufacturer:** Medtronic (Micro Therapeutics, Inc. d/b/a ev3 Neurovascular)
**Category:** Aspiration Catheter

## What It Is

Single-lumen, flexible, variable-stiffness composite aspiration catheter for large vessel occlusion stroke thrombectomy. The shaft uses a nitinol structure jacketed with a durable polymer outer layer and lined with a PTFE inner liner, providing proximal stiffness and distal flexibility. The distal tip contains an encapsulated platinum-iridium radiopaque markerband for fluoroscopic visualization. The distal shaft has a hydrophilic coating. The proximal hub is a clear thermoplastic elastomer design with strain relief. Introduced through a split y-introducer sheath included with the system.

The React 68 Catheter is one component of the Riptide Aspiration System; the system also includes aspiration tubing connecting to an external pump. The 68 designation refers to the inner lumen size class, positioned alongside the React 71 as a large-bore aspiration platform for direct aspiration thrombectomy.

## Indications

Intended for use in the revascularization of patients with acute ischemic stroke secondary to intracranial large vessel occlusive disease within the internal carotid artery, middle cerebral artery (M1 and M2 segments), basilar artery, and vertebral arteries, within 8 hours of symptom onset. Patients who are ineligible for intravenous tissue plasminogen activator (IV t-PA) or who fail IV t-PA therapy are candidates for treatment.

## Compatible With

- Phenom microcatheters (for co-axial delivery)
- Solitaire X Revascularization Device (for combined aspiration + stent retriever technique)
- Alembic aspiration tubing (connects catheter to aspiration pump)

## Use Notes

- Navigated to the treatment site and positioned proximal to the site of occlusion before aspiration.
- Introduced through the included split y-introducer sheath.
- Used as part of the complete Riptide Aspiration System including the aspiration pump and tubing.
- Indicated for acute ischemic stroke; not a general-purpose aspiration or access catheter.
