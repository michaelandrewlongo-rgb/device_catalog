## What It Is

The SOFIA Flow Plus is a large-bore aspiration catheter from MicroVention/Terumo designed for direct aspiration mechanical thrombectomy in acute ischemic stroke. It is part of the SOFIA catheter family and represents the high-caliber aspiration variant, offering a larger inner lumen diameter than the standard SOFIA 5F and 6F models to maximize clot engagement and aspiration force. Like all SOFIA catheters, it uses coil and braid reinforcement, a hydrophilic distal shaft coating, and a radiopaque distal tip marker. The distal segment is steam-shapeable.

The SOFIA Flow Plus is intended for use with standard thrombectomy techniques including direct aspiration first pass (ADAPT) and combined aspiration with stent retriever approaches.
