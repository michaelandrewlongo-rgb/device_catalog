# iFuse INTRA X

**Manufacturer:** Si-Bone
**Category:** Sacroiliac Fusion

## What It Is

Allograft implant for intra-articular SI joint stabilization and fusion. Percutaneous delivery through a single needle-like incision. Sterile single-use instrument set. Designed for reproducible intra-articular placement.
