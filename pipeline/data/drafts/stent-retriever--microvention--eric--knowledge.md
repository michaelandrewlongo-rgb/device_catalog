**Category:** Stent retriever / thrombectomy device
**Source:** https://www.terumoneuro.com/products/eric

## What It Is

The ERIC (Endovascular Revascularization Catheter) is a self-expanding nitinol stent retriever from MicroVention/Terumo designed for mechanical thrombectomy in acute ischemic stroke caused by large vessel occlusion (LVO). It is deployed into and through a thrombus, expands to engage the clot within its struts, and is then withdrawn while aspirating proximally to restore flow. The ERIC uses a continuous closed-cell braid design to achieve circumferential clot engagement. Radiopaque markers at the proximal and distal ends allow fluoroscopic positioning.
