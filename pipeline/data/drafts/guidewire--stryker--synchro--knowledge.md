## What It Is

The Synchro guidewire family consists of two variants -- Synchro 2 and Synchro SELECT -- designed for intravascular navigation in neurovascular and peripheral procedures. Both are steerable microguidewires used to selectively introduce and position microcatheters and other interventional devices.

## Indications

**Synchro 2:** The Synchro2 Guidewire series is intended for general intravascular use, including neurovascular and peripheral vasculatures. It can be used to selectively introduce and position catheters and other interventional devices within the peripheral and neurovasculature. This device should be used only by physicians trained in percutaneous, intravascular techniques and procedures.

**Synchro SELECT:** The Synchro SELECT Guidewire series is intended for general intravascular use, including neurovascular and peripheral vasculatures. It can be used to selectively introduce and position catheters and other interventional devices within the peripheral and neurovasculature. This device should be used only by physicians trained in percutaneous, intravascular techniques and procedures.
