# ENTERPRISE 2 Vascular Reconstruction Device (Cerenovus)

**Manufacturer:** Cerenovus (Johnson & Johnson MedTech)
**Category:** Intracranial Stent

## What It Is

The ENTERPRISE 2 Vascular Reconstruction Device is a self-expanding nitinol intracranial stent for coil-assisted embolization of wide-neck intracranial aneurysms. It uses a closed-cell design that provides stability, conformability, and vessel wall apposition. The device can be recaptured and redeployed after partial deployment, allowing repositioning before full deployment. Radiopaque markers are integrated for fluoroscopic visibility during and after deployment.

It is designated as a Humanitarian Device in the United States (HDE authorization), which limits its use to facilities with Institutional Review Board oversight.

## Indications

- Authorized for use with embolic coils for treatment of wide-neck intracranial saccular or fusiform aneurysms
- Parent vessel diameter 2.5-4.0 mm
- Wide-neck defined as neck width >= 4 mm or dome-to-neck ratio < 2
- **HUMANITARIAN DEVICE (USA ONLY):** Authorized by Federal law for use only in the treatment of wide-neck intracranial saccular or fusiform aneurysms not amenable to treatment by surgical clipping or by endovascular coiling without stent assistance. The effectiveness of this device for this use has not been demonstrated.

## Use Notes

**Mechanical performance vs. comparable devices (per manufacturer data):**
- Higher radial resistive force than Neuroform, LVIS Jr., and Solitaire 2/FR
- Higher chronic outward force than LVIS Jr. and Solitaire 2/FR
- Higher point load force than Neuroform, LVIS Jr., and Solitaire
- Better vessel wall apposition in curved vessels vs. ENTERPRISE 1
- Excellent kink resistance vs. ENTERPRISE 1
- Less foreshortening than LVIS Jr. and Solitaire 2/FR

**Recapture:** Device can be recaptured and redeployed after partial deployment, permitting repositioning before full deployment and detachment. Cannot be repositioned after full detachment.

| Foreshortening | Less than LVIS Jr. and Solitaire 2/FR | More than Enterprise 2 | [NEEDS CONTENT] |

The ENTERPRISE 2 offers higher radial and outward force profiles than LVIS Jr. and Solitaire 2/FR, which may benefit cases requiring firm vessel wall apposition. The closed-cell design provides consistent neck coverage similar to LVIS. The 0.021" delivery requirement (vs. 0.017" for LVIS Jr. and Neuroform Atlas) may limit use in smaller or more tortuous access anatomy.
