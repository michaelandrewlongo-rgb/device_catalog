# Neuroform EZ Stent System

**Manufacturer:** Stryker Neurovascular
**Category:** Intracranial Stent

FDA HDE H020002 | Original approval September 11, 2002 (supplements through S064, August 2025) | Class III
**Note: Humanitarian Device Exemption (HDE). IRB approval required prior to use.**

## What It Is

The Neuroform EZ Stent System is a self-expanding, open-cell nitinol intracranial stent pre-loaded on an enhanced Stent Delivery Wire. It is engineered for conformability, flexibility, and accessibility in the neurovasculature. The Neuroform EZ is the current generation of the Neuroform stent platform — among the first intracranial stents to receive FDA authorization for stent-assisted coiling of wide-neck intracranial aneurysms. The "EZ" designation reflects design improvements in deliverability relative to earlier Neuroform generations.

The open-cell nitinol design provides wall apposition in tortuous anatomy and allows post-stent microcatheter re-access through the stent interstices, supporting both jailing and sequential coiling techniques.

This device operates under a Humanitarian Device Exemption (HDE H020002), not a 510(k) clearance. IRB approval is required at each institution prior to patient treatment.

## Indications

Intended for use with embolic coils for the treatment of wide-neck, intracranial, saccular aneurysms arising from a parent vessel with a diameter of ≥2 mm and ≤4.5 mm that are not amenable to treatment with surgical clipping. Wide-neck aneurysms are defined as having a neck >4 mm or a dome-to-neck ratio <2.

*(Source: HDE H020002 Approval Order Statement, confirmed in FDA HDE database)*
