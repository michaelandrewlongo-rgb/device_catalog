# CEREGLIDE 71 Intermediate Catheter

**Manufacturer:** Cerenovus (Johnson & Johnson MedTech)
**Category:** Distal access / intermediate catheter

## What It Is

Intermediate catheter engineered for acute ischemic stroke intervention. Incorporates TruCourse Technology, which the manufacturer describes as providing 1:1 responsiveness and an angle-cut polymer jacket for smooth transitions between catheter segments. Features a 55 cm hydrophilic coating on the distal shaft and is designed to maintain lumen integrity during intra-procedural use (aspiration and stent retriever passes).

Positioned within the Cerenovus Stroke Solutions system alongside the EMBOTRAP III stent retriever, EMBOGUARD Balloon Guide Catheter, CEREBASE DA Guide Sheath, and Prowler EX Microcatheter.

## Use Notes

- More trackable than Sofia Plus, React 71, Catalyst 6, and Large Bore Catheter per manufacturer bench data.
- Lower track forces than Zoom 71 per manufacturer bench data.
- More kink-resistant during intra-procedural use than Sofia Plus and React 71 per manufacturer bench data.
- Larger inner diameter than Sofia Plus, Catalyst 6, Catalyst 7, and React 68.
- 55 cm hydrophilic coating on distal shaft facilitates navigation in tortuous intracranial anatomy.
- Designed to support up to 3 stent retriever passes without loss of lumen integrity.
