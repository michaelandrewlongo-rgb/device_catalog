# Cascadia TL 3D Interbody System

**Manufacturer:** Stryker
**Category:** Pedicle screw system
510(k) K150481 | Class 2 | 2015-08-20

## What It Is

Lamellar 3D titanium posterior lumbar interbody cage. Lamellar 3D Titanium Technology incorporates 300-500 um longitudinal channels with transverse windows creating an interconnected lattice for bony integration. Approximately 70% porosity. Reverse hourglass design allows large graft volume. Bulleted nose for ease of insertion. 3-5 um surface roughness for direct bony ongrowth.

## Sizing/Specs

Full range of implant sizes to accommodate vertebral anatomy.

## Indications

Transforaminal lumbar interbody fusion (TLIF).

## Use Notes

Roughened titanium surfaces shown to demonstrate increased protein expression vs smooth titanium. Now sold by VB Spine.
