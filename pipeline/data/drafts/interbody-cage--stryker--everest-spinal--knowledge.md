# Everest Deformity Spinal System

**Manufacturer:** Stryker
**Category:** Interbody cage
510(k) K161369 | Class 2 | 2016-07-21

## What It Is

Top-loading pedicle screw system for complex spinal deformity correction. Accepts 5.5 mm and 6.0 mm diameter titanium and cobalt chrome rods. Mixed-metal (Ti/CoCr) tulip minimizes head splay with improved biomechanical performance vs all-titanium screws. Dual-lead thread pattern for faster insertion. Tapered distal third for increased pullout strength.

## Sizing/Specs

Accepts 5.5 mm and 6.0 mm rods. Titanium and cobalt chrome rod options. Multiple screw types available.

## Indications

Complex spinal deformity correction.

## Compatible With

BaseCamp deformity rod reducer for sequential reduction and correction. Compatible with Spine Guidance 5 and Mako Spine with Copilot features.

## Use Notes

Modified square thread set screw design facilitates introduction. BaseCamp allows placement of multiple tubes for sequential reduction. Now sold by VB Spine.
