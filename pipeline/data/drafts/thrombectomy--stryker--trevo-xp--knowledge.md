# Trevo XP ProVue Retriever

**Manufacturer:** Stryker Neurovascular
**Category:** Thrombectomy Device

## What It Is

Self-expanding nitinol stent retriever for mechanical thrombectomy in acute ischemic stroke. Designed for large vessel occlusion (LVO) revascularization via clot integration and retrieval. Key design features include 360 degrees of consistently large cell openings along the full stent length, a soft atraumatic distal tip, and full-length radiopacity. Delivered through a compatible microcatheter, deployed across the thrombus by retracting the microcatheter, then withdrawn with the captured clot. Intended for use with aspiration (balloon guide catheter or aspiration catheter) and syringe or pump.

## Indications

1. **Standard window (within 6 hrs):** Restore blood flow in acute ischemic stroke with persistent proximal anterior circulation LVO and smaller core infarcts in patients who have first received IV t-PA. Endovascular therapy should start within 6 hours of symptom onset.

2. **Extended window (within 8 hrs):** Restore blood flow by removing thrombus in patients experiencing ischemic stroke within 8 hours of symptom onset. Patients ineligible for IV t-PA or who fail IV t-PA therapy are candidates.

3. **Late window (6-24 hrs):** Restore blood flow in patients with persistent proximal anterior circulation LVO of the ICA or MCA-M1 with smaller core infarcts (0-50 mL for age <80 years; 0-20 mL for age >=80 years). Endovascular therapy within 6-24 hours of last seen well. For patients ineligible for or who fail IV t-PA therapy.

## Use Notes

- Mode of operation: mechanical thrombectomy via stent integration and retrieval
- Full-length radiopacity enables fluoroscopic visualization throughout the retrieval stroke
- 360-degree large cell design intended to maximize clot integration and en bloc retrieval
