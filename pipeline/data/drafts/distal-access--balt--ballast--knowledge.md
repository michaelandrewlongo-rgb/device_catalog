## What It Is

Flexible long sheath designed to provide stable proximal access and support for delivery of interventional devices into the peripheral, coronary, and neurovasculature. In the neurovascular setting, the Ballast 088 functions as a large-bore guiding sheath or distal access platform, enabling coaxial delivery of aspiration catheters, microcatheters, and other endovascular devices. The 088 designation refers to the inner diameter.
