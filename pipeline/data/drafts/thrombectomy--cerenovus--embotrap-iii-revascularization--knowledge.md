# EMBOTRAP III Revascularization Device

**Manufacturer:** Cerenovus (J&J)
**Category:** Thrombectomy device
510(k) K211338 | Class 2 | 2021-07-30

## What It Is

Stent retriever for mechanical thrombectomy with innovative dual-layer design. Inner channel secures and stabilizes the clot to maintain engagement while allowing reperfusion. Articulating outer cage maintains wall apposition while engaging and gripping the clot. Distal closed end mesh maintains clot control during retrieval.

## Sizing/Specs

Vessel Size Range: 1.5-6.5 mm; Device Lengths: 22 mm, 37 mm, 45 mm; Working Length: 194 cm; Product codes: ET309522 (5x22), ET309537 (5x37), ET309645 (6x45)

## Indications

Indicated for neurovascular revascularization in acute ischemic stroke. Vessel indication of 1.5mm to 6.5mm.

## Compatible With

Prowler EX Microcatheter, EMBOGUARD Balloon Guide Catheter, CEREBASE DA Guide Sheath, CEREGLIDE 71 Intermediate Catheter (CERENOVUS Stroke Solutions system)

## Use Notes

Distal mesh cell sizes of EMBOTRAP III 5x22mm and 5x37mm are 25% smaller than EMBOTRAP II. MASTRO I is a living systematic review and meta-analysis comparing EmboTrap, Solitaire, and Trevo stent retrievers for mechanical thrombectomy in acute ischemic stroke.
