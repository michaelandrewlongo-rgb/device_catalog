## What It Is

The BOBBY is a balloon guide catheter (BGC) from MicroVention/Terumo designed to provide proximal flow arrest during mechanical thrombectomy for acute ischemic stroke. Balloon guide catheters arrest antegrade flow in the target vessel (typically the ICA or vertebral artery) when the balloon is inflated, enabling aspiration of thrombus in a "no-flow" or "reverse-flow" environment that reduces distal embolization during clot retrieval. The BOBBY provides a large inner working lumen for coaxial delivery of aspiration catheters, microcatheters, and stent retrievers while the balloon is inflated.

## Use Notes

- Inflate balloon only when positioned in a straight segment of vessel with adequate diameter; avoid inflating over bifurcations
- Deflate balloon immediately if resistance is felt during inflation or if patient develops neurological changes
- Maintain continuous heparinized saline flush through the inner lumen at all times
- Do not leave balloon inflated longer than necessary; prolonged inflation in the ICA may cause ischemia to ipsilateral territories
