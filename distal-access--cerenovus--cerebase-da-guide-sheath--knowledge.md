# CEREBASE DA Guide Sheath

**Manufacturer:** Cerenovus (J&J)
**Category:** Distal access catheter

## What It Is

Guide sheath engineered for distal access with geometric anchoring for procedural support. Features Dex tip for enhanced tracking through challenging anatomy. Large inner diameter (0.090 inch) in an 8F short sheath compatible system.

## Sizing/Specs

Inner Diameter: 0.090 inch; Outer Diameter: 8F; Short Sheath Compatibility: 8F; Usable Lengths: 80 cm, 90 cm, 95 cm; Product codes: GS9080SD, GS9090SD, GS9095SD
