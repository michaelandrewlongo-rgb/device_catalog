# iFuse Bedrock Granite

**Manufacturer:** Si-Bone
**Category:** Sacroiliac Fusion

## What It Is

Spinopelvic fixation implant designed to address the 24% lumbopelvic fixation failure rate. FDA Breakthrough Device Designation. Features robust cannulated core with large neck and shank designed for spinopelvic stresses, FuSIon 3D microporous lattice surface mimicking cancellous bone, OMNICapture tulip and screw set with negative-rake square threads to mitigate tulip splay and expulsion, and IntelliHarvest macroporous graduated fenestrations for bone self-harvesting.
