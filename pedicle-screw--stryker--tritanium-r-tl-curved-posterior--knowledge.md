# Tritanium TL Curved Posterior Lumbar Cage

**Manufacturer:** Stryker
**Category:** Pedicle screw system
510(k) K173476 | Class 2 | 2018-01-18

## What It Is

Hollow curved posterior lumbar interbody cage built using AMagine additive manufacturing technology. Features Tritanium In-Growth Technology porous structure designed for bone in-growth and biological fixation. Unique randomized pore shape and sizing mimic cancellous bone with interconnected pore structure from endplate to endplate. Curved shape with roughened Tritanium surface featuring multi-directional teeth provides tactile feedback during placement.

## Sizing/Specs

Available in a variety of heights, lengths, and lordotic angles. Two inserter and two tamp options for open and MIS procedures.

## Indications

Posterior lumbar interbody fusion (PLIF/TLIF).

## Compatible With

Tritanium PL (complementary posterior lumbar solution). Anterior Placement System.

## Use Notes

Anterior Placement System enables apophyseal placement. Designed for versatility in open and MIS procedures. Imaging-compatible. Developed to minimize subsidence. Now sold by VB Spine.
