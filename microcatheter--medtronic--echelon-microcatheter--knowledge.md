# Echelon Microcatheter

**Manufacturer:** Medtronic
**Category:** Microcatheter

## What It Is

Single-lumen microcatheter designed for introduction over a steerable guidewire into vasculature during neurovascular procedures. Nitinol braid technology with progressive picks-per-inch. Graduated zones and transition points for balance between pushability and softness. DMSO compatible for liquid embolic delivery.

## Sizing/Specs

Models: Echelon 10 (low profile 1.7F distal OD), Echelon 14 (0.017 inch ID); Large 0.017 inch ID; Proximal fluoro-safe marker band; Steam-shapeable flexible tip with dual marker bands; Working length 150 cm

## Indications

Introduction of interventional devices and infusion of diagnostic or therapeutic agents during neurovascular procedures. DMSO-compatible for Onyx liquid embolic delivery.

## Compatible With

Onyx Liquid Embolic System (DMSO compatible), Pipeline Flex, Solitaire X, X-Pedion hydrophilic guidewire

## Use Notes

Nitinol braid provides kink resistance and minimal ovalization for reliable device delivery. Echelon 10 has low profile 1.7F distal OD for accessing small vessels.
