# CEREGLIDE 71 Intermediate Catheter with TruCourse

**Manufacturer:** Cerenovus (J&J)
**Category:** Distal access catheter
510(k) K221930 | Class 2 | 2023-03-09

## What It Is

Intermediate catheter engineered for stroke intervention with TruCourse Technology for smoother navigation. Features 55cm hydrophilic coating, 1:1 responsiveness, angle cut polymer jacket for smooth transitions, and improved lumen integrity during intra-procedural use.

## Sizing/Specs

Usable Length: 115 cm; Product codes: EVT110, NIC71115U, NIC71132U, NIC71137U; Compatible with base catheters >= 0.085 inch ID

## Indications

Optimized for effective direct aspiration and up to 3 stent retriever passes. Designed for acute ischemic stroke intervention.

## Compatible With

EMBOTRAP III Revascularization Device, EMBOGUARD Balloon Guide Catheter, CEREBASE DA Guide Sheath, Prowler EX Microcatheter (CERENOVUS Stroke Solutions system)

## Use Notes

More trackable than Sofia Plus, React 71, Catalyst 6, and Large Bore Catheter. Lower track forces than Zoom 71. More kink-resistant during intra-procedural use than Sofia Plus and React 71. Larger inner diameter than Sofia Plus, Catalyst 6, Catalyst 7, React 68.
