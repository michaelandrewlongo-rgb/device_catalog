# Reflex Hybrid Anterior Cervical Plate System (Stryker)

**Manufacturer:** Stryker Spine
**Category:** Cervical Plate

## What It Is

The Reflex Hybrid is an anterior cervical plate system for fixation after discectomy and/or corpectomy with interbody fusion (ACDF or ACCF). It features a semi-constrained design with variable-angle screws that allow angulation of approximately +/-15 degrees from the fixed trajectory. The plate uses a rotational locking mechanism to secure screws after placement, preventing backout.

Key design features:
- Titanium alloy plate construction
- Low-profile design to minimize anterior hardware bulk and reduce dysphagia risk
- Variable-angle screw holes with rotational lock engagement
- Self-drilling, self-tapping screws in 4.0mm and 4.5mm diameters
- Available in multiple plate lengths for 1-level through 4-level constructs
- Implanted via standard open anterior cervical approach from C2 to T1

The "Hybrid" designation refers to the combination of variable-angle screw placement with a positive-locking mechanism, providing the surgeon trajectory flexibility while maintaining construct rigidity after locking.

## Sizing/Specs

### Plate Lengths
- Available for 1-level, 2-level, 3-level, and 4-level constructs
- Multiple lengths within each level range to accommodate anatomical variation
- Plate should span from mid-vertebral body to mid-vertebral body across the fused segment(s)
- Refer to surgical technique guide for complete plate length matrix

### Screws
- **4.0mm diameter:** Standard screws, self-drilling, self-tapping
- **4.5mm diameter:** Rescue/emergency screws for use when 4.0mm has stripped bone
- **Screw lengths:** 12mm, 14mm, 16mm, 18mm (refer to surgical technique guide for complete availability)
- Variable-angle trajectory: approximately +/-15 degrees angulation from the neutral axis

### Materials
- **Plate:** Titanium alloy
- **Screws:** Titanium alloy, self-drilling, self-tapping
- **Locking mechanism:** Rotational lock integrated into plate screw holes

### Design Features
- Low-profile plate to reduce soft tissue irritation and dysphagia
- Pre-contoured lordotic shape
- Rotational screw-locking mechanism: after screw insertion, a locking element is engaged to prevent screw backout
- Screw holes allow variable angulation before locking

## Indications

- Anterior cervical fusion from C2 to T1
- Anterior cervical discectomy and fusion (ACDF), single or multi-level
- Anterior cervical corpectomy and fusion (ACCF)
- Degenerative disc disease
- Trauma (fracture, dislocation)
- Tumor
- Deformity
- Failed previous fusion
- Patients must be skeletally mature

## Contraindications

- Active infection at the operative site
- Severe osteoporosis or inadequate bone stock for screw purchase
- Known allergy to titanium or other implant materials
- Conditions precluding benefit of spinal implant surgery
- Skeletal immaturity
- Mental or neuromuscular disorders creating unacceptable risk of fixation failure or noncompliance

## Compatible With

- **Cervical interbody cages:** Any ACDF cage (PEEK or titanium) cleared for anterior cervical use
- **Corpectomy cages:** Expandable or static corpectomy devices for ACCF reconstruction
- **Bone graft:** Autograft, allograft, or bone graft substitutes within the interbody space
- **Cervical retractor systems:** Standard anterior cervical retractor setups (Caspar-type distraction pins, self-retaining retractors)
- **Imaging:** Intraoperative fluoroscopy (AP and lateral) for plate and screw positioning
- **Dedicated instrument set:** Stryker Reflex Hybrid instrument tray including drill guides, screwdrivers, and locking tools

## Use Notes

- The variable-angle screw design (+/-15 degrees) provides flexibility to adjust screw trajectory based on anatomy, avoiding endplate violation or adjacent disc penetration. After optimal trajectory is achieved, the rotational lock secures the screw in place.
- Self-drilling, self-tapping screws simplify the workflow: pilot holes can be drilled directly through the plate guide, and screws cut their own threads. In very dense bone, pre-tapping may still be advisable.
- The 4.5mm screw is a rescue option for stripped 4.0mm screw holes. Always have rescue screws available.
- Low-profile design reduces the anterior plate prominence, which is a factor in postoperative dysphagia. However, any anterior plate adds profile compared to zero-profile devices.
- Plate should not overhang adjacent disc spaces. Overhang increases risk of adjacent-segment ossification and dysphagia.
- The plate is pre-contoured with lordotic shape. Select plate length so that screws land in the mid-vertebral body, away from endplates.
- Engage the rotational locking mechanism after all screws are placed and positions confirmed. Confirm locking status before wound closure.
- For corpectomy constructs, ensure the plate length spans the full corpectomy defect with screws in intact vertebral bodies above and below.
- Do not mix implant components with other manufacturers' systems.

## Key Differences vs Competitors

- **vs. Atlantis Vision Elite (Medtronic):** The Atlantis uses Nitinol locking caps that rotate over screw heads to prevent backout. The Reflex Hybrid uses a rotational locking mechanism within the screw hole. The Atlantis supports up to 5-level constructs (plate lengths to 110mm); the Reflex Hybrid covers up to 4-level constructs. The Atlantis offers both self-drilling and self-tapping screws; the Reflex Hybrid uses self-drilling, self-tapping screws.
- **vs. Vectra (DePuy):** The Vectra offers four screw insertion options (variable-angle cancellous, variable-angle self-tapping, fixed-angle cancellous, fixed-angle self-tapping). The Reflex Hybrid uses a single screw type (self-drilling, self-tapping) with variable angulation. The Vectra emphasizes screw versatility for different bone qualities; the Reflex Hybrid emphasizes simplicity with fewer instrument options and a streamlined workflow.
- **vs. Zero-profile devices (Zero-P, COALITION):** Zero-profile cages with integrated fixation eliminate the anterior plate entirely, reducing dysphagia risk and simplifying the construct. The Reflex Hybrid is a traditional plate providing broader construct flexibility (multi-level, corpectomy, variable screw angles). Traditional plates remain the standard for multi-level and corpectomy cases where zero-profile devices may not provide sufficient stability.
- **Low-profile advantage:** The Reflex Hybrid's low-profile design positions it as a dysphagia-conscious plate option while retaining the construct versatility of a traditional anterior plate system.
