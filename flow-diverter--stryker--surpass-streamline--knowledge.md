# Surpass Streamline Flow Diverter -- Stryker Neurovascular

PMA P170024 | FDA Approved July 13, 2018 | Class III

## What It Is

Self-expandable braided flow diverter for endovascular treatment of large/giant wide-necked intracranial aneurysms. The braided implant is placed across the aneurysm neck in the parent artery to divert blood flow away from the aneurysm sac. Over time, endothelialization of the device and thrombosis within the sac produce progressive aneurysm occlusion. Unlike stent-assisted coiling, this is a standalone device -- no coils are placed in the aneurysm. Shipped preloaded in a dedicated delivery catheter system.

## System Components

1. **Surpass Flow Diverter (Implant):** Braided cobalt chromium with interwoven platinum-tungsten wires for fluoroscopic visualization
2. **Delivery Catheter:** PTFE-lined lumen, nitinol wire reinforcement, hydrophilic coating on distal 80 cm, radiopaque marker at distal tip
3. **Pusher:** Dual-segment catheter with PTFE-coated proximal shaft, stainless steel hypotube distal shaft, two radiopaque markers, and stainless steel recapture feature

- **Parent vessel range:** 2.5-5.3 mm diameter
- **MRI:** "MR Conditional" per ASTM testing
- **Sterilization:** 100% EtO, SAL 10^-6
- **Shelf life:** 1 year (validated for 3 years in accelerated aging)

## Indications

Endovascular treatment of patients (18 years and older) with unruptured large or giant saccular wide-neck (neck width >= 4 mm or dome-to-neck ratio < 2) or fusiform intracranial aneurysms in the internal carotid artery from the petrous segment to the terminus, arising from a parent vessel with diameter >= 2.5 mm and <= 5.3 mm.

## Contraindications

- Parent vessel size outside 2.5-5.3 mm range
- Antiplatelet and/or anticoagulation therapy (aspirin + clopidogrel) is contraindicated
- Patients who have not received dual antiplatelet agents prior to procedure
- Active bacterial infection
- Anatomy not appropriate for endovascular treatment (severe vessel tortuosity/stenosis, intracranial vasospasm unresponsive to medical therapy)

## Deployment/Use

1. Dual antiplatelet therapy required pre-procedure (aspirin + clopidogrel)
2. Access via standard femoral approach with guide catheter
3. Navigate delivery catheter (with preloaded implant) to target site across aneurysm neck
4. Deploy implant by retracting delivery catheter while stabilizing pusher
5. Implant self-expands to vessel wall diameter
6. Recapture feature on pusher allows partial retrieval if repositioning needed
7. Withdraw delivery system after deployment
8. Mean 1.1 devices implanted per subject in SCENT trial (range 0-2)

## Clinical Evidence (SCENT Trial)

- **Design:** Prospective, multicenter, non-randomized, 236 enrolled (180 mITT), 25 US + 1 Netherlands sites
- **Primary safety endpoint:** Disabling stroke (mRS >= 3 at 90+ days) or neurological death within 12 months: **6.1% (11/180)**
- **Primary effectiveness endpoint:** Complete (100%) aneurysm occlusion (Raymond-Roy Class I) without significant in-stent stenosis or retreatment at 12 months: **62.8% (113/180)**, p < 0.001
- **Minor stroke rate:** 6.7% (12/180) at 12 months
- **Age effect:** Effectiveness 70.2% for age <65 vs. 50.0% for age >=65 (statistically significant)
- **Apposition effect:** Raymond-Roy I occlusion 79.2% with full wall apposition vs. 50.0% without

## Complications to Know

- **Ischemic stroke:** 6.1% (11/180) serious; most common neurological SAE
- **Subarachnoid hemorrhage:** 2.8% (5/180) serious
- **Hemorrhagic stroke:** 2.2% (4/180) serious
- **Headache:** 30% of patients (mostly non-serious)
- **Visual complications:** 8.9% visual impairment, 5.0% diplopia (due to ICA/ophthalmic location)
- **Thrombosis in device:** 3.3% (6/180)
- **Device migration/fracture/misplacement:** Listed as possible
- **Neurological death:** 2.8% (5/180)
- **Decreased effectiveness in patients >= 65 years** -- labeling warning added
- **Decreased effectiveness in giant aneurysms** (46.2% vs. 64.1% for large) -- not statistically significant but trend present

## Antiplatelet Protocol (from SCENT trial)

- Pre-procedure: Dual antiplatelet (ASA + clopidogrel) with Plavix resistance testing
- Intra-procedure: Heparinization
- Post-procedure: Continue dual antiplatelet per institutional protocol
- Duration: Per treating physician, typically 3-12 months then ASA alone

## Also Known As

- Surpass Streamline
- Surpass Flow Diverter
- Intracranial Aneurysm Flow Diverter

## Key Comparator

Pipeline Embolization Device (Medtronic, PMA P100018): Similar indication, ICA petrous to superior hypophyseal only. PUFS trial showed 70.8% occlusion at 1 year and 5.6% primary safety event rate. Surpass covers ICA from petrous to terminus (wider anatomic range).
