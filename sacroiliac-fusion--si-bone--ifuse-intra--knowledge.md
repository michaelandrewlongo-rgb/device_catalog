# iFuse INTRA

**Manufacturer:** Si-Bone
**Category:** Sacroiliac Fusion

## What It Is

Triangular allograft implant for minimally invasive SI joint stabilization and fusion. Designed for intra-articular placement with procedural decortication. Decreases SI joint motion. Allograft-based (bone graft, not titanium).
