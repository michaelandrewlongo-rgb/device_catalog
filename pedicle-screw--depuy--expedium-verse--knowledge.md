# EXPEDIUM VERSE Spine System (DePuy Synthes)

## What It Is

The EXPEDIUM VERSE is DePuy Synthes' versatile thoracolumbosacral pedicle screw and rod system, supporting both open and MIS percutaneous posterior approaches. It is the evolution of the EXPEDIUM platform with added MIS capability. The system includes longitudinal rods, monoaxial screws, polyaxial screws, uniplanar screws, reduction screws, cable/wire screws, bolts, slotted connectors, wires, hooks, reduction hooks, transverse connectors (including SFX Cross Connector System), dual rod connectors, sacral extenders, lateral connectors, and washers.

## Sizing/Specs

## Indications

**Adult (skeletally mature) - Open:**
- Degenerative disc disease
- Spondylolisthesis
- Trauma (fracture or dislocation)
- Spinal stenosis
- Curvatures (scoliosis, kyphosis, lordosis)
- Tumor
- Pseudarthrosis
- Failed previous fusion
- Thoracic, lumbar, and sacral spine
- Noncervical pedicle fixation and nonpedicle fixation

**Adult - MIS Percutaneous:**
- Same indications as open approach
- Noncervical pedicle and nonpedicle fixation

**Pediatric (broadened indications, K200245):**
- Progressive spinal deformities: adolescent idiopathic scoliosis, neuromuscular scoliosis, congenital scoliosis
- Spondylolisthesis/spondylolysis
- Fracture caused by tumor and/or trauma
- Pseudarthrosis
- Failed previous fusion
- Posterior approach only, with autograft/allograft

Note: The 2020 clearance (K200245) expanded the pediatric indications beyond adolescent idiopathic scoliosis to include neuromuscular scoliosis, congenital scoliosis, and additional pediatric pathologies. This makes the EXPEDIUM VERSE one of the more broadly indicated systems for pediatric spine deformity.

## Contraindications

- Active infection at the operative site
- Severe osteoporosis or osteopenia that would compromise implant fixation
- Patient conditions precluding benefit of spinal fixation (e.g., morbid obesity, pregnancy, mental illness, substance abuse affecting compliance)
- Inadequate tissue coverage of the implant
- Known allergy to titanium, stainless steel, or cobalt-chromium alloy
- Any condition that would compromise the ability of the implant to provide stability (e.g., severely compromised bone stock, pathologic fracture with insufficient remaining bone)
- Fever or elevated WBC count suggestive of active systemic infection

## Use Notes

- The EXPEDIUM VERSE bridges open and MIS in one system; the same screws can be used with percutaneous MIS instrumentation or in an open approach
- Broad pediatric indications make this suitable for neuromuscular and congenital scoliosis cases, not just AIS
- Multiple screw types (monoaxial, polyaxial, uniplanar, reduction, cable/wire) provide flexibility across degenerative, trauma, tumor, and deformity cases
- Reduction screws and hooks available for deformity correction

## Key Differences vs Competitors

- **vs Medtronic CD HORIZON / SOLERA:** EXPEDIUM VERSE offers integrated open + MIS capability in a single platform; SOLERA requires the separate SEXTANT system for percutaneous work. VERSE has broader pediatric indications (neuromuscular, congenital scoliosis) vs SOLERA's AIS-only pediatric clearance. SOLERA has more granular screw sizing and color-coded diameter identification.
- **vs Stryker Xia 3:** Xia 3 is more deformity-focused with the SUK DVR direct vertebral rotation system and 8 rod reduction options. EXPEDIUM VERSE is more versatile across degenerative, trauma, and deformity. Both accept dual rod diameters (5.5/6.0mm). Xia 3 offers uniplanar screws for DVR that VERSE does not.
- **vs Globus REVERE:** REVERE uses a non-threaded 90-degree locking cap (faster, no cross-threading risk). EXPEDIUM VERSE uses traditional set screw locking. REVERE has a broader revision ecosystem (Addition/RESCUE clamps). VERSE has stronger MIS integration.
- **vs NuVasive Reline:** Reline is open-only; VERSE covers both open and MIS. VERSE has broader pediatric indications. Reline is known for ease of rod capture with its low-profile tulip.
- **vs Neo Medical Neo PSS:** Neo offers radical inventory simplification (14 screw references vs hundreds for VERSE) and single-use sterile instruments. VERSE has far broader system depth (hooks, connectors, cross-links, revision options). Neo is CE-marked but has uncertain US FDA status.
