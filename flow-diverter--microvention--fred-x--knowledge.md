# FRED X

**Manufacturer:** MicroVention (now Terumo Neuro)
**Category:** Flow diverter
**PMA:** P180027 | Class 3 | 2025-09-18

## What It Is

Flow Re-Direction Endoluminal Device (FRED) with X Technology, the next generation of the original FRED flow diverter. X Technology is a proprietary nanoscale surface treatment covalently bonded to the device surface that creates an optimized protective hydration layer. Preclinical data shows reduced material thrombogenicity while maintaining the natural vessel healing response (comparable endothelial cell growth to bare device in-vitro).

Retains the FRED dual-layer braid design (inner high-density flow-diverting mesh, outer structural stent) with precise predictable placement and immediate opening. FRED X adds reduced delivery and retraction forces for easier tracking through tortuous anatomies.

## Indications

Adults ≥22 years old. Internal carotid artery from the petrous segment to the terminus. Wide-necked intracranial aneurysms (neck width ≥4 mm or dome-to-neck ratio <2), saccular or fusiform, arising from a parent vessel with diameter 2.0-5.0 mm.

Canada: intracranial aneurysms not amenable to surgical clipping, parent vessels 2.0-5.0 mm.

## Use Notes

- X Technology surface coating is permanent (covalently bonded nanoscale treatment, not a degradable coating)
- 2.5 and 3.0 mm sizes have no distal lead wire, reducing risk of vessel perforation in small distal targets
- Active FDA recalls (as of 2025): tantalum length/attachment pattern manufacturing issue (may lead to incomplete opening or vessel malapposition); incorrect inner stent length in some units

## Key Differences vs Competitors

- **Dual-Layer Design**: FRED X has an inner high-density flow-diverting mesh plus an outer structural stent, a unique architecture among flow diverters. Pipeline Flex, Surpass Elite, and Silk Vista are all single-layer braided designs.
- **Small-Vessel Optimization**: FRED X 2.5-3.0 mm devices are deliverable through a 0.021" catheter with no distal lead wire. Silk Vista Baby goes smaller (0.017"). Pipeline Flex and Surpass Elite require larger delivery catheters.
- **Surface Technology**: X Technology is a nanoscale covalent surface treatment. Pipeline Flex has phosphorylcholine (Shield Technology). Surpass Elite has BioStealth Surface. Silk Vista has no surface modification.
- **Resheathing**: FRED X specifies resheathing capability. Pipeline Flex and Surpass Elite also support resheathing. Silk Vista resheathing characteristics are not specified.
- **ICA-Specific Indication**: US PMA indication is restricted to internal carotid artery (petrous to terminus). Pipeline Flex has a similar ICA-focused indication.
