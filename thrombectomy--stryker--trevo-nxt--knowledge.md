# Trevo NXT ProVue Retriever -- Stryker Neurovascular

FDA 510(k) K223305 | Cleared March 29, 2023 | Class II

## What It Is

Self-expanding stent retriever for mechanical thrombectomy in acute ischemic stroke. The device consists of a flexible, tapered nitinol core wire with a laser-cut nitinol shaped (stent-like) section at the distal end. Platinum markers and radiopaque wire provide fluoroscopic visualization. The retriever is delivered through a microcatheter, deployed across the thrombus by retracting the microcatheter, then withdrawn with the captured clot.

## Indications

1. **Standard (within 6 hrs):** Restore blood flow by removing thrombus in acute ischemic stroke with persistent proximal anterior circulation LVO, smaller core infarcts, after IV t-PA. Start within 6 hours of symptom onset.

2. **Extended (within 8 hrs):** Restore blood flow by removing thrombus within 8 hours of symptom onset. For patients ineligible for IV t-PA or who fail IV t-PA therapy.

3. **Late window (6-24 hrs):** Restore blood flow in patients with persistent proximal anterior circulation LVO of ICA or MCA-M1 with smaller core infarcts (0-50cc for age <80 years, 0-20cc for age >=80 years). Start within 6-24 hours of last seen well. For patients ineligible for or who fail IV t-PA.

- **Accessories:** Insertion tool and torque device provided in package

## Deployment/Use

1. Navigate microcatheter past thrombus
2. Advance retriever through microcatheter using insertion tool
3. Retract microcatheter to deploy shaped section across thrombus
4. Allow integration time (per IFU)
5. If using aspiration catheter: remove microcatheter, advance aspiration catheter over proximal section of retriever while applying aspiration (60 mL syringe or pump)
6. Withdraw retriever with microcatheter or aspiration catheter as a unit, capturing thrombus

## Complications to Know

Standard mechanical thrombectomy risks: vessel perforation, dissection, vasospasm, distal embolization, hemorrhagic transformation, device fracture. No new safety concerns identified with this labeling update (K223305 is a labeling-only change from predicate K210502 to update catheter compatibility for smaller vessels down to 1.5 mm).
