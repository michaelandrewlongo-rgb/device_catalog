# Rebar Micro Catheter

**Manufacturer:** Medtronic (Micro Therapeutics, Inc. d/b/a ev3 Neurovascular)
**510(k):** K093750 (multi-device clearance, December 9, 2009); earlier clearances K993672 (Rebar-14), K001966 (Rebar-18, Rebar-27), K002723 (Rebar-10)
**Classification:** Continuous Flush Catheter, 21 CFR 870.1210, Class II, Product Code KRA

---

## What It Is

Single-lumen, endhole microcatheter designed for sub-selective infusion of embolization materials and contrast media in tortuous, distal vessels. The Rebar is part of the ev3/Medtronic neurovascular microcatheter family (alongside Marathon and Echelon). Unlike the flow-directed Marathon, the Rebar is an over-the-wire design. It is available in multiple sizes (Rebar-10, Rebar-14, Rebar-18, Rebar-27), covering a range of lumen and profile sizes for different applications.

Key features:
- Over-the-wire design (not flow-directed)
- Semi-rigid proximal shaft, highly flexible distal shaft
- Single or dual radiopaque markers at distal end
- Lubricious outer coating
- Standard luer adapter hub
- Available in multiple size configurations (10, 14, 18, 27)

---

## Specs

Specific dimensional values (ID, OD) are not provided in the K093750 510(k) summary. The Rebar family spans:
- **Rebar-10:** Smallest profile (cleared K002723)
- **Rebar-14:** Mid-range (cleared K993672)
- **Rebar-18:** Larger lumen (cleared K001966)
- **Rebar-27:** Largest lumen in family (cleared K001966)

All variants share the same basic construction: semi-rigid proximal shaft, flexible distal shaft, radiopaque markers, lubricious outer coating, standard luer hub.

---

## Compatible With

- Guidewires compatible with each size variant
- Physician-specified therapeutic agents (embolization materials)
- Contrast media
- Guide catheters

The Rebar's hydrophilic coating uses the same technology later adopted for the Marksman catheter (Biocoat).

---

## Indications

Controlled selective infusion of physician-specified therapeutic agents or contrast media into the vasculature of the peripheral and neuro anatomy.

---

## Use Notes

- The K093750 submission was a Special 510(k) to remove the pediatric and neonatal contraindication from the IFU for the Rebar (and other ev3 microcatheters). No device changes were made.
- The Rebar is an over-the-wire microcatheter, distinguishing it from the flow-directed Marathon in the same product family.
- The Rebar-18 and Rebar-27 are the larger-bore members of the family, used when a wider lumen is needed for larger coils or device delivery.
- Single use only; do not reprocess.
