# Onyx Liquid Embolic System

**Manufacturer:** Medtronic
**Category:** Liquid embolic

## What It Is

Pre-mixed, radiopaque, injectable liquid embolic made of ethylene vinyl-alcohol copolymer (EVOH), dimethyl sulfoxide (DMSO), and micronized tantalum powder (Ta). Designed for complete filling and distal penetration with cohesive deposition providing responsive, controlled embolization.

## Sizing/Specs

Formulations: Onyx 18, Onyx 34 LES; Compositions: EVOH 8% (Onyx 18), EVOH 8% (Onyx 34); DMSO solvent; Micronized tantalum powder for radiopacity

## Indications

Presurgical embolization of brain arteriovenous malformations (AVMs). Surgical adjunct for subacute/chronic subdural hematoma.

## Compatible With

Apollo Onyx delivery micro catheter (detachable tip), Echelon micro catheter, Marathon flow-directed micro catheter, Mirage hydrophilic guidewire

## Use Notes

Cohesive deposition provides responsive controlled embolization. Requires DMSO-compatible microcatheter for delivery. IFU reference: M016938CDOC2 Rev. G (12/2025).
