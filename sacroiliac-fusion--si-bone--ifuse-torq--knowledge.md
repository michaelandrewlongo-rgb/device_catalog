# iFuse TORQ

**Manufacturer:** Si-Bone
**Category:** Sacroiliac Fusion

## What It Is

Threaded sacroiliac joint fusion implant with 3D-printed porous titanium (FuSIon 3D) surface for osseointegration. Features TORQLock hooked thread profile to reduce toggle, IntelliHarvest helical flutes and fenestrations for bone self-harvesting, and EZDrive self-drilling self-tapping tip that decreases surgical steps. Two minimally invasive approach options (lateral transiliac).

## Indications

## Use Notes

Sheep model histology (6-week) showed 11.5 Nm max torque-out for iFuse TORQ vs 0.5 Nm for traditional screws (Technical Study 300940-TS). STACI trial: prospective multicenter study demonstrating safety and effectiveness.
