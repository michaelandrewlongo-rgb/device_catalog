# iFuse TORQ

**Manufacturer:** Si-Bone
**Category:** Sacroiliac Fusion

## What It Is

Threaded sacroiliac joint fusion implant with 3D-printed porous titanium (FuSIon 3D) surface for osseointegration. Features TORQLock hooked thread profile to reduce toggle, IntelliHarvest helical flutes and fenestrations for bone self-harvesting, and EZDrive self-drilling self-tapping tip that decreases surgical steps. Two minimally invasive approach options (lateral transiliac).

## Indications

Minimally invasive surgical fusion of the sacroiliac (SI) joint.

## Use Notes

Sheep model histology (6-week) showed 11.5 Nm max torque-out for iFuse TORQ vs 0.5 Nm for traditional screws (Technical Study 300940-TS). STACI trial: prospective multicenter study demonstrating safety and effectiveness.

## Key Differences vs Competitors

- **Implant design and material**: iFuse TORQ features a threaded titanium implant with 3D-printed porous surface (FuSIon 3D) and TORQLock hooked thread profile, while iFuse Implant System uses triangular cross-section titanium implants, iFuse INTRA/INTRA X are allograft-based triangular implants, and iFuse Bedrock Granite has a cannulated core with FuSIon 3D surface for spinopelvic fixation.
- **Primary indications**: iFuse TORQ is indicated for minimally invasive surgical fusion of the SI joint, similar to iFuse Implant System and iFuse INTRA/INTRA X, whereas iFuse Bedrock Granite targets spinopelvic fixation in adult deformity/revision cases, and iFuse TORQ TNT addresses pelvic fragility fractures with bilateral SI joint involvement.
- **Key mechanical features**: iFuse TORQ includes IntelliHarvest helical flutes for bone self-harvesting and EZDrive self-drilling tip to reduce surgical steps, with sheep model data showing 11.5 Nm max torque-out. iFuse Implant System's triangular geometry resists rotational forces, iFuse Bedrock Granite has OMNICapture tulip with negative-rake threads, and iFuse TORQ TNT is 150% stronger in bending for transsacral placement.
- **Approach and compatibility**: iFuse TORQ offers two minimally invasive lateral transiliac approaches. iFuse Implant System is compatible with iFuse Bedrock Granite for supplemental fixation and uses fluoroscopy/navigation, while iFuse INTRA X features percutaneous delivery through a single needle-like incision, and iFuse TORQ TNT spans the full posterior pelvis (transsacral).
