# RISE TLIF Expandable Lumbar Interbody Spacer -- Globus Medical

## What It Is

An expandable TLIF interbody cage that inserts at a contracted height and then expands in situ to restore disc height. The device enters the disc space through a transforaminal approach at a reduced profile, minimizing nerve root retraction and musculoskeletal resection. Once positioned, a drive screw mechanism expands the implant continuously upward, tensioning the annulus and surrounding ligaments while optimizing endplate-to-endplate fit. The convex endplate surfaces are toothed for fixation. It has an axial graft opening for bone graft delivery. Automatic locking prevents height loss after expansion. Designed for MIS or open TLIF with supplemental posterior fixation (e.g., CREO MIS pedicle screws).

## Sizing / Specs

**Widths:** 8mm, 10mm, 12mm

**Footprint options by width:**
- 8mm: 8x22mm
- 10mm: 10x22mm, 10x26mm, 10x30mm
- 12mm: 12x26mm, 12x30mm

**Expansion ranges (contracted to expanded height):**
- 7-13mm
- 7-14mm
- 8-14mm (10 degree lordotic)
- 8-15mm (10 degree lordotic)
- 9-15mm
- 10-17mm
- 11-17mm
- 10-17mm (15 degree lordotic)
- 11-17mm (15 degree lordotic)

**Maximum expansion:** up to 7mm of height gain from contracted state

**Lordotic options:** 4 degrees, 10 degrees, 15 degrees

**Drive screw revolution reference (approximate):** 1.75 revolutions = 1mm of expansion

**Material:** Titanium alloy with convex toothed endplates

## Indications

- Degenerative disc disease (DDD) requiring transforaminal lumbar interbody fusion
- Lumbar spondylolisthesis
- Lumbar stenosis with instability requiring fusion
- Revision TLIF where disc height restoration is needed
- MIS TLIF where minimizing nerve root retraction is a priority
- Cases where controlled in situ distraction is preferred over pre-distraction

Must be used with supplemental posterior fixation (pedicle screws and rods).

## Contraindications

- Active infection at the operative site (local or systemic)
- Severe osteoporosis or osteopenia that would preclude adequate endplate support
- Known allergy or sensitivity to titanium
- Inadequate bone stock, quality, or anatomy to support the implant
- Conditions that preclude the potential benefit of spinal fusion (e.g., tumor, fracture with gross instability not amenable to TLIF)
- Spondylolisthesis grade 3 or higher (relative; device not validated for high-grade slips)
- Morbid obesity or other conditions that prevent adequate surgical exposure
- Patient non-compliance with postoperative activity restrictions

## Compatible With

- **Pedicle screw systems:** Globus CREO MIS pedicle screw system (primary recommended pairing), REVERE Stabilization System, other posterior fixation systems compatible with standard rod diameters (5.5mm)
- **Retractor systems:** Globus MARS 3V MIS retractor system, third-party MIS tubular retractors
- **Bone graft:** Autograft (local laminectomy bone, iliac crest), allograft (cancellous chips, demineralized bone matrix), bone graft substitutes packed through axial graft opening
- **Biologics:** Compatible with standard bone graft extenders and substitutes; graft chamber accommodates morselized graft
- **Navigation:** Compatible with intraoperative fluoroscopy (AP and lateral); titanium construction allows CT-based navigation if available

## Use Notes

- The RISE is an expandable cage, not a static cage. The primary advantage is reduced insertion profile: the cage enters the disc space at contracted height and expands in situ, minimizing the degree of nerve root retraction needed for insertion.
- Expansion is controlled by a torque-limiting driver. Do not override the torque limiter. If the driver stops, either maximum height or maximum safe endplate force has been reached.
- The drive screw conversion is approximately 1.75 revolutions per 1mm of expansion. Count revolutions carefully, especially when placing bilateral implants that must match in height.
- Lordotic options (4, 10, 15 degrees) should be selected based on preoperative sagittal alignment goals. The 15-degree options are only available in the 10mm and 12mm width families.
- Always pack bone graft into the graft chamber before insertion. Graft-to-endplate contact is essential for fusion.
- If the implant must be repositioned, fully contract it before attempting to move it. Never reposition a partially expanded implant.
- Confirm final position and expansion on both AP and lateral fluoroscopy before completing posterior fixation.
- The 8mm width is suitable for smaller disc spaces or revision settings; 10mm and 12mm widths provide broader endplate coverage.

## Key Differences vs Competitors

- **vs Medtronic Elevate Expandable:** RISE is all-titanium; Elevate uses a PEEK/titanium hybrid. RISE offers three width options (8, 10, 12mm); Elevate is 10mm width only. Elevate offers an Extra-Lordotic variant with up to 13 degrees lordosis; RISE maxes at 15 degrees but through a different geometry. Elevate has a bullet-tip self-distracting nose; RISE relies on pre-distraction with shavers/trials.
- **vs DePuy CONCORDE LIFT:** Both are expandable TLIF cages. CONCORDE LIFT uses PEEK-OPTIMA body with titanium endplates; RISE is all-titanium. RISE offers wider footprint options (up to 12mm width). CONCORDE LIFT has controlled lordosis restoration during expansion.
- **vs static TLIF cages (e.g., Stryker Tritanium TL):** Expandable cages allow smaller insertion profile and in situ height adjustment. Static cages require pre-distraction to final height and rely on trialing for size selection. Static 3D-printed titanium cages (Tritanium TL) may offer superior osseointegration surface texture. Expandable cages add mechanical complexity and cost.
- **vs lateral cages (e.g., NuVasive CoRoent XL):** RISE is a TLIF cage inserted through a posterior approach. Lateral cages have much larger footprints and are inserted via a transpsoas corridor. Different indications, different anatomy, different complication profiles.

## Use Step-by-Step

1. **Position the patient.** Prone on a radiolucent table with fluoroscopy available. Lateral C-arm throughout.

2. **Make the incision.** Incise 4 to 4.5cm lateral to midline, in line with the target disc. Finger dissect between multifidus and longissimus to the facet joint.

3. **Retract.** Use MARS 3V dilators sequentially over the initial dilator. Place the Retractor 3 Blade Frame (closed position) over the cannulas. Articulate all three blades one full turn before removing cannulas to prevent tissue creep. Tighten the Articulating Arm Assembly. Verify with AP fluoroscopy.

4. **Create transforaminal access.** Remove the inferior facet of the cephalad vertebra and the superior facet of the caudal vertebra with an osteotome to create the working window to the disc.

5. **Perform discectomy and endplate preparation.** Use rongeurs, rasps, curettes, and preparation instruments through the annular window. Use Sizer/Shavers (7-17mm, smallest first) to remove superficial cartilaginous endplate layers. Avoid damaging the bony endplate. Thorough prep maximizes fusion potential.

6. **Distract and size the implant.** Assemble the Adjustable Trial Assembly: attach the Removable Drive (Right Hand) to the trial shaft, lock with push button, thread the MIS Handle, attach the Torque-Limiting Palm Handle. Insert the trial at contracted height, expand clockwise to desired height. Read the trial height on the side window. Confirm fit and length with fluoroscopy. Alternatively, use sequential shavers for distraction.

7. **Assemble the implant inserter.** Partially thread the Inserter QC Shaft into the Inserter Base. Slide the Inserter Tube in (flats distal, facing up). Thread the gold knob of the Inserter Base counterclockwise until it stops. Select the correct width Inserter Fork (8mm, 10mm, or 12mm). Insert the fork into the tube, aligning tabs with cleaning holes. Tighten the QC Shaft to lock. Attach the Torque-Limiting Palm Handle aligned to the etched line. Do NOT torque out the Palm Handle during assembly.

8. **Load and attach the implant.** Select the appropriately sized implant and pack autogenous bone graft into the graft chamber. With the Inserter Tube threaded counterclockwise, place the implant between the Inserter Fork tips. Rotate the tube clockwise to secure the implant.

9. **Insert the implant.** Advance the implant into the disc space using the Inserter Assembly. Confirm position with fluoroscopy.

10. **Expand the implant.** Insert the Torque-Limiting Implant Driver through the Inserter Assembly, engaging the drive screw. Rotate clockwise to expand. Gauge expansion by tactile feel (gentle toggling) and by counting drive screw revolutions (approximately 1.75 revolutions = 1mm). Use the arrow on the back of the driver to count revolutions. If inserting two RISE implants, match heights using revolution count and/or fluoroscopy.

11. **Verify final position.** Use fluoroscopy to confirm implant position and expansion height before disengaging.

12. **Remove the inserter.** First remove the Torque-Limiting Implant Driver. Then rotate the gold knob on the Inserter Base counterclockwise until disengaged. Use the Spanner Wrench if needed.

13. **Complete posterior fixation.** Place pedicle screws and rods (e.g., CREO MIS system).

## Pearls / Troubleshooting

- **Insert at minimum height.** The contracted profile is the key advantage. Do not pre-expand before insertion.
- **Count revolutions carefully.** Reference the expansion range chart printed on the implant module. For a 7-14mm implant, 0 revolutions = 7mm; 1.75 rev = 8mm; 3.25 rev = 9mm, and so on up to 11.75 rev = 14mm.
- **The torque-limiting driver will stop you** when you hit either maximum expansion height or maximum safe distraction force on the endplates. Do not override this.
- **For two-implant constructs**, count revolutions for each implant independently and verify matched heights on fluoroscopy.
- **Reposition before expanding.** If the implant is not well positioned, it must be fully contracted before repositioning. Use the Slap Hammer if needed.
- **For removal**, reduce height by inserting the Torque-Limiting Implant Driver and rotating counterclockwise. Then extract with forceps, or rotate the implant 90 degrees and remove.
- **Sizer/Shavers double as distractors.** Start with the smallest and upsize until desired distraction. Be cautious of endplate damage.
- **Use the Spanner Wrench** if the gold knob on the Inserter Base is difficult to loosen during disengagement.
- **Lordotic implants** (10 and 15 degree options) are available for the 10mm and 12mm width families. Choose based on preoperative sagittal alignment goals.
- **Slim inserter design** is compatible with MIS tubular retractor systems.
