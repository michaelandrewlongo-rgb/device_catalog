# Solitaire X Revascularization Device

**Manufacturer:** Medtronic
**Category:** Thrombectomy device

## What It Is

Stent retriever designed for effective clot retrieval during thrombectomies for acute ischemic stroke (AIS) patients. Most-published stent retriever with over 200 publications. Parametric overlap design allows stent to expand and compress in vessel during deployment and retrieval.

## Sizing/Specs

Sizes: 3mm (vessel 1.5-3.0mm), 4mm (vessel 1.5-4.0mm), 6mm (vessel 2.0-5.5mm); Microcatheter ID: 0.017 inch (3mm) or 0.021 inch (4mm/6mm); Body markers: 3-4; Usable lengths: 20mm, 24mm, 40mm; Product codes: SFR4-3-20-10, SFR4-3-40-10, SFR4-4-20-05, SFR4-4-20-10, SFR4-4-40-10, SFR4-6-20-10, SFR4-6-24-06, SFR4-6-40-10

## Indications

Neurovascular mechanical thrombectomy for acute ischemic stroke (AIS). Vessel sizes 1.5-5.5 mm.

## Compatible With

APRO 55/70 aspiration catheters, Phenom 17/21/27 catheters, React catheters

## Use Notes

Most-published stent retriever. Overlap parametric design fundamental to Solitaire portfolio.

## Key Differences vs Competitors

- **Design approach**: Solitaire X uses a parametric overlap design for dynamic expansion/compression during deployment/retrieval, while EMBOTRAP III features a dual-layer architecture with inner channel for clot stabilization and outer cage for wall apposition, and Penumbra System operates on direct aspiration principles rather than stent retrieval.
- **Vessel size range**: Solitaire X covers 1.5-5.5mm vessels, EMBOTRAP III covers 1.5-6.5mm (wider upper range), Trevo NXT ProVue has minimum vessel IDs specified per size but overall range not specified, and Penumbra System indications specify specific arteries but vessel diameter range not specified.
- **Clinical evidence base**: Solitaire X is described as "most-published stent retriever with over 200 publications," while EMBOTRAP III references MASTRO I systematic review comparing it with Solitaire and Trevo, and other devices' publication volumes not specified.
- **Compatibility ecosystem**: Solitaire X works with Medtronic's APRO/Phenom/React catheters, EMBOTRAP III designed for Cerenovus Stroke Solutions ecosystem (Prowler EX, EMBOGUARD, etc.), Trevo NXT ProVue compatible with Trevo/Excelsior catheters, and Penumbra System is a fully integrated platform with proprietary components.
- **Device sizing options**: Solitaire X offers 3 diameters (3/4/6mm) with 3 usable lengths (20/24/40mm), EMBOTRAP III offers 2 diameters (5/6mm) with 3 lengths (22/37/45mm), Trevo NXT ProVue offers 3 diameters (3/4/6mm) with varying lengths per diameter, and Penumbra System sizes not specified.
