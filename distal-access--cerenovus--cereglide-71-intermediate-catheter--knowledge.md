# CEREGLIDE 71 Intermediate Catheter with TruCourse

**Manufacturer:** Cerenovus (J&J)
**Category:** Distal access catheter
510(k) K221930 | Class 2 | 2023-03-09

## What It Is

Intermediate catheter engineered for stroke intervention with TruCourse Technology for smoother navigation. Features 55cm hydrophilic coating, 1:1 responsiveness, angle cut polymer jacket for smooth transitions, and improved lumen integrity during intra-procedural use.

## Sizing/Specs

Usable Length: 115 cm; Product codes: EVT110, NIC71115U, NIC71132U, NIC71137U; Compatible with base catheters >= 0.085 inch ID

## Indications

Optimized for effective direct aspiration and up to 3 stent retriever passes. Designed for acute ischemic stroke intervention.

## Compatible With

EMBOTRAP III Revascularization Device, EMBOGUARD Balloon Guide Catheter, CEREBASE DA Guide Sheath, Prowler EX Microcatheter (CERENOVUS Stroke Solutions system)

## Use Notes

More trackable than Sofia Plus, React 71, Catalyst 6, and Large Bore Catheter. Lower track forces than Zoom 71. More kink-resistant during intra-procedural use than Sofia Plus and React 71. Larger inner diameter than Sofia Plus, Catalyst 6, Catalyst 7, React 68.

## Key Differences vs Competitors

- **Device Type & Primary Function**: CEREGLIDE 71 is an intermediate catheter for stroke intervention, optimized for direct aspiration and stent retriever passes. In contrast, CEREBASE DA is a guide sheath for distal access, providing geometric anchoring and procedural support. CEREGLIDE 92 is a larger-bore intermediate catheter system designed for distal M1 access and optimized for aspiration thrombectomy when used with CEREGLIDE 71.
- **Key Specifications**: CEREGLIDE 71 has a usable length of 115 cm and is compatible with base catheters ≥0.085 inch ID. CEREBASE DA has an inner diameter of 0.090 inch, outer diameter of 8F, and comes in shorter usable lengths (80 cm, 90 cm, 95 cm). CEREGLIDE 92 has a larger distal inner diameter (0.092 inch), a proximal outer diameter of 0.108 inch, a usable length of 114 cm, and requires 8F long guide sheaths ≥0.113 inch ID. Specifications for AXS Catalyst 5 and Catalyst 6 are not specified.
- **Performance & Design Advantages**: CEREGLIDE 71 offers more trackability than Sofia Plus, React 71, Catalyst 6, and Large Bore Catheter; lower track forces than Zoom 71; more kink-resistance than Sofia Plus and React 71; and a larger inner diameter than Sofia Plus, Catalyst 6, Catalyst 7, and React 68. CEREBASE DA provides better trackability and more support than NeuronMax, Infinity, Shuttle, Fubuki, and Ballast when placed distally. CEREGLIDE 92 has lower track forces than Zoom 88 and the largest inner diameter with the lowest distal profile compared to Zoom 88, Sofia 88, and Millipede 088.
- **Indications & Compatibility**: CEREGLIDE 71 is indicated for acute ischemic stroke intervention. It is compatible with specific CERENOVUS system components (EMBOTRAP III, EMBOGUARD, CEREBASE DA, Prowler EX). CEREBASE DA is indicated for distal access procedures. CEREGLIDE 92 is indicated for acute ischemic stroke with distal M1 access and is compatible with INNERGLIDE 9 (co-packaged), CEREGLIDE 71, EMBOTRAP III, and Prowler EX. Indications for AXS Catalyst 5 and Catalyst 6 are not specified beyond a warning about excessive aspiration.
