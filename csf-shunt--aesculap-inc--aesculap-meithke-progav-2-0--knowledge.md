# AESCULAP - MEITHKE PROGAV 2.0 ADJUSTABLE SHUNT SYSTEM

**Manufacturer:** Aesculap (B. Braun)
**Category:** CSF shunt/diagnostic

## What It Is

DEVICE DESCRIPTION
proGAV 2.0 is an adjustable differential pressure valve that can be set for a range of pressures.
The proGAV 2.0 valve is comprised of a titanium housing that contains a leaf spring and ball
mechanism that is mechanically controlled by internal magnets. Manual devices are available to
locate, verify the pressure setting and to set or re-set the pressure pre and postoperatively. These
manual accessories are for external use by the physician. Once verified using the Compass the
setting must be confirmed with an X-ray. The device will be distributed by itself or in
combination with the ShuntAssistant valve or proSA valve. The proGAV 2.0 adjustable
differential pressure valve includes the same legally marketed accessories that are available with
the Miethke Shunt Systems.

INDICATIONS FOR USE
The Miethke proGAV 2.0 Adjustable Shunt System is intended to shunt cerebrospinal fluid
(CSF) from the lateral ventricles of the brain into the peritoneum. Adjustments of the proGAV
2.0 shunt can be verified by using the proGAV 2.0 Compass but must be confirmed by
radiograph (X-ray).

## Key Differences vs Competitors

- **Adjustment mechanism**: proGAV 2.0 uses a magnetically adjustable differential pressure valve (0-20 cmH₂O) with audible/tactile feedback and requires X-ray confirmation after adjustment; competitors like CERTAS Plus offer 8 programmable settings with noninvasive adjustment and no X-ray requirement, while M.blue combines adjustable gravitational and fixed differential pressure units.
- **Anti-siphon/gravitational features**: proGAV 2.0 is typically paired with a separate ShuntAssistant gravitational unit for posture-dependent flow control; M.blue integrates both functions in one device, and Strata valves offer siphon control via Delta Chamber or no siphon control options.
- **MRI compatibility**: proGAV 2.0 is MR Conditional for 3-Tesla systems; CERTAS Plus and Strata valves also offer MRI compatibility, but specific Tesla ratings for competitors are not specified.
- **Recall status**: proGAV 2.0 has 6 active FDA recalls (including labeling issues and deflector movement problems); M.blue has the same recall count, while recall status for CERTAS Plus and Strata is not specified.
- **Indications specificity**: proGAV 2.0 is specifically for ventriculoperitoneal shunting from lateral ventricles to peritoneum; CERTAS Plus is for general hydrocephalus management, Strata includes lumboperitoneal options, and M.blue is for posture-dependent hydrocephalus.
